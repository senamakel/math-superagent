## Summary

Berthé & Imbert, "Diophantine Approximation, Ostrowski Numeration and the Double-Base Number System", DMTCS vol 11:1 (2009) 153–172.

Relevant to PE591: Section 2 gives the **Ostrowski number system** and an **inhomogeneous best-left-approximation algorithm**. Given irrational α∈(0,1) and β∈[0,1], it computes the infinite sequence (k_i, l_i) of **best left α-approximations** of β, i.e. the points k·α − l ∈ (0,β] that are record minima of distance-from-below to β.

Algorithm 2 (verbatim, p.160):
1. (k_0,l_0):=(0,0).
2. repeat: write β − (k_i α − l_i) = c_i·f_{n_i} + f_{n_i+1} + e_i
   - if n_i even: (k_{i+1},l_{i+1}) := (k_i + q_{n_i}, l_i + p_{n_i})
   - if n_i odd:  (k_{i+1},l_{i+1}) := (k_i − c_i q_{n_i} + q_{n_i+1}, l_i − c_i p_{n_i} + p_{n_i+1})
   where (p_n/q_n) are the convergents of α and f_n = |q_n α − p_n| are the convergent errors.
3. Correctness: Prop 4 proves the sequence is strictly increasing toward β and (Prop 4 ineq. (10)) that **no** k between successive (k_i, k_{i+1}) beats k_iα−l_i as a left approximation below β. Complexity: Prop 6 — terminates in **O(log log x)** iterations for bounded-bit integers (the greedy Ostrowski recoding bound).

Key implication for PE591: the best b under a bound B that makes {bα} closest to β from below (or above, by symmetry) is among an O(log B)-sized set of candidates generated by this continued-fraction / Ostrowski machinery. This is one of the O(log)-time routes to the inhomogeneous minimum; Cabanillas–López & Labbé (arXiv:1904.01874) give the cleaner left/right candidate characterization used in the reference solution.
