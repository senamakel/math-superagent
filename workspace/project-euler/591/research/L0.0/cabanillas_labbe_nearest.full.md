# Title: A variant of Ostrowski numeration

Authors: [Emmanuel Cabanillas][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled A variant of Ostrowski numeration, by Emmanuel Cabanillas

[View PDF][4]

Abstract: In this article, we propose a variant of the usual Ostrowski $\alpha$-numeration (where $\alpha$ is a real in [0, 1[) that codes integers (positive as well as negative) and reals of [0, 1[ (instead of [--$\alpha$, 1--$\alpha$[), so that for every integer n, n and {n$\alpha$} have the same coding sequence. These coding sequences respect natural lexicographic orders and will be used to prove well known results on order properties of Kronecker sequences ({n$\alpha$ -- $\beta$}) n .
