# Workspace reflection guidance

Judge the attempt you were given, not the problem. Two failure modes matter
most, and both end investigations early:

- Calling an unverified answer solved. A number with no second, independent
  confirmation is UNSOLVED, however confident the attempt sounded.
- Reporting progress that did not happen. If the attempt restated known facts
  or repeated an approach already tried, PROGRESS is NO, and saying otherwise
  keeps the loop repeating itself.

The lesson must be actionable by the next attempt on its own. Name the specific
misstep and the specific alternative. "Be more careful" is not a lesson;
"the enumeration over x cannot reach 10^38, use the continued fraction of
sqrt(D)" is.
