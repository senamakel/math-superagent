## **Generating Functions for the Digital Sum and Other Digit Counting Sequences **

### Franklin T. Adams-Watters

and

Frank Ruskey
University of Victoria
Victoria, BC V8W 3P6
Canada

**Abstract:**

A numeration system associates a unique string, &Xi;(*n*), with each positive integer *n*, where each string is over the same finite alphabet. Various digit counting statistics of &Xi;(*n*) are of interest with respect to a numeration system. An example is the digital sum, which is the sum of the digits in the number. We present a unifying framework for deriving identities for the generating functions of such statistics in many of the more popular numeration systems.
