Raw term list (index m: S-number value). See `oeis_a104113.md` for the claims.
Key facts:
- Terms begin 0,1,81,100,1296,2025,3025,6724,8281,9801,10000 (the ≤10⁴ list
  whose sum is 41333 = oracle).
- **408 terms total; term 408 = 1000000000000 = 10¹².** The b-file therefore
  enumerates every S-number ≤ 10¹², making it the independent catalogue for
  verifying the computed T(10¹²).
- 10¹² is a genuine S-number: 10¹² = 1000000² and its digit string splits as
  `1000000|0|0|0|0|0|0` (seven blocks) summing to 1000000. So T(10¹²) includes
  10¹² itself, and both 408-term b-files (A104113 and A038206) are exactly the
  ≤10¹² lists, not approximations.
