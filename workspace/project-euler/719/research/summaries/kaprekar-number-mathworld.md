**Definition.** An n-digit number k such that adding the right n digits of k² to
the left n or n−1 digits yields k. Examples: 9²=81 → 8+1; 297²=88209 → 88+209.
First few: 1,9,45,55,99,297,703,… (OEIS A006886).
