## Content (abstract)

k is n-Kaprekar (two-block: k = q + r, k² = q·10^n + r) only if k² ≡ k (mod 9),
forcing k ≡ 0 or 1 (mod 9). Gives a generation scheme for two-block Kaprekar
numbers ≤ 10^6. Two-block only; not the multi-block S-number route to T(10¹²).
