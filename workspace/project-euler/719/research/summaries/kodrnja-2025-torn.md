# Kodrnja — "On Remarkable Properties of Number 2025" — does NOT bear on PE 719

Source: https://doi.org/10.31896/k.29.8 (Iva Kodrnja, *KoG* 29(29), 2025, 74-80;
the accessible capture is the abstract/pages only, paywalled behind the journal).

**What the captured text establishes.** Nothing about the PE 719 / S-number /
split-and-sum-to-root class. The paper studies the *number* 2025 as a perfect
square *year*: figure / trigonal / tetrahedral numbers, counting lattice points
in polygons and polyhedra, the frequency of square years, and the uniqueness of
such a year number. Keywords: figure numbers, trigonal numbers, tetrahedral
numbers.

**Why it was captured and why it does not help.** It was fetched because OEIS
A238237 (two-block torn numbers) cross-references Kodrnja. But the link is to
the KoG *volume* on the special number 2025, not to torn-number/split-and-sum
theory. 2025 = 45² = 20+25 is both a torn number and an S-number, but this paper
does not develop that line; the abstract is about calendar-year/figurate-number
structure. The only shared object is the integer 2025 itself.

**Hypotheses / holds-here.** No theorem of the paper transfers to T(10^12);
the hypotheses it would need (the S-structure) are not addressed. Does not
contradict anything in the library.

**Bearing.** None. Retain only as background that the number 2025 is discussed
for year-regularity; it must not be cited as supporting the two-block / torn-
number / S-number theory held in this library (which is properly sourced to
Iannucci, Dudeney, Javaheri, Black, and the OEIS records). Do not re-fetch —
the abstract-only capture establishes nothing further.
