# Seal — L1.0: exact-integration machinery for PE 597 (Torpids)

This batch (10 notes) supplies the named mathematics that turns Torpids'
continuous race dynamics into an exact, non-enumerative recursive integration,
and that pins down the one structural obstacle that remains. The thesis, as
established by these sources: the boats' **relative-speed clocks** and the
**rate-ratio product**, arranged as an **independent-subtree treap**, give
p(n,L) — the probability the new boat order is an even permutation — as a sum
of products of distance ratios, whose additive parity functional makes the
whole thing an exactly rational simplex-volume computation.

The target: p(13,1800) to 10 dp, with p(3,160)=56/135≈0.414815 and
p(4,400)=0.5107843137 as the check oracle. MC pins the value ≈0.5002±0.00007
(60M samples), so any true bias ≤ ~3e-4; the 10-dp answer needs this exact
route, not sampling.

## The clocks and their distributions

- [[competing_exponential_clocks_uchicago]] — UChicago STAT317 Lec 9 (Yibi
  Huang). For **independent** X_i~Exp(λ_i): min ~ Exp(Σλ); P(X_j=min)=λ_j/Σλ
  (competing-clocks winner probability, by conditioning on X_j=t); the
  **memoryless** property P(X>t+s|X>t)=P(X>s). Consequence: a specific firing
  order i_1,…,i_D of heterogeneous independent exponentials has probability
  equal to the product of rate ratios over survivors,
  (λ_{i1}/Σλ)·(λ_{i2}/(Σλ−λ_{i1}))·… — the repeated min+memoryless step. This
  is the base fact every sequential-selection product below rests on; it
  holds here because the Exp(1) speeds are independent.
- [[exponential_order_statistics_memoryless_kth]] — Koski (KTH SF2955),
  order statistics of iid exponentials. For iid Exp(1): the spacings
  Y_1=X_(1), Y_i=X_(i)−X_(i−1) are **independent** Exps with rates n,…,1.
  Shown by the triangular change of variables Y=AX, det A=1. This gives the
  memoryless structure that makes successive selection independent. Caveat:
  applies to iid; see the INID note for heterogeneity.
- [[inid_exponential_order_statistics_nagaraja]] — Nagaraja ch.11 (Handbook of
  Statistics; UIC STAT416 copy). For **inid** exponentials X_j~Exp(λ_j), the
  antirank vector D(k) (index of k-th smallest) is the **Nevzorov 1984 /
  Tikhov 1991** sequence: P(D(k+1)=i)=λ_i/Σ_{survivors} λ_j, stated and proven.
  Crucially, **inid spacings are NOT independent** (zero covariance iff all λ
  equal) — the iid independence of the KTH note does not survive heterogeneity.
  Also Lemma 11.3.1: sums of top order statistics have a closed-form survival
  kernel, ∏(1−e^{−λt}) against a pooled-rate exponential.

## Race-specific limits (what is NOT an exponential clock)

- [[inverse_exponential_finish_times_wikipedia]] — Wikipedia "Inverse
  distribution". For X~Exp(λ), the reciprocal Y=1/X is **inverse-exponential**:
  density g(y)=(λ/y²)e^{−λ/y}, CDF G(y)=e^{−λ/y}, mean and variance do NOT
  exist (finite moments only for k<1). Boat j's finish time is
  T_j=(L−p_j)/v_j=(c_j)/v_j with c_j=L−p_j, so each finish time is
  inverse-exponential, scale c_j. **CORRECTS the standing brief:** finish
  events are NOT competing exponential clocks — the hazard
  h_j(t)=(c_j/t²)/(1−e^{−c_j/t}) is not constant in t. So the
  "next-finish-wins with prob λ_j/Σλ" rate-ratio picture applies to the bump
  dynamics only via a clock to be constructed; finish events sit outside it.
  The memoryless property belongs to the speeds v_j, not the induced finish
  times 1/v_j.
- [[laplace_difference_of_exponentials_libretexts]] — Siegrist LibreTexts 5.28.
  Difference of two iid Exp(1): X−Y is standard **Laplace**, density
  (1/2)e^{−|u|}, MGF 1/(1−t²), even moments E[Z^{2k}]=(2k)! (Var 2), odd
  moments 0; |Z|~Exp(1). This is the distribution of the **relative speed**
  v_j−v_i that drives each bump — the input the bump clock integrates over.

## The recursion and the parity

- [[randomized_search_trees_treaps_seidel_aragon]] — Seidel & Aragon
  "Randomized Search Trees" (Algorithmica 1996). A treap orders items in-order
  by key and heap-order by priority; for distinct keys and priorities it is
  **unique** and the extremal-priority item is the root, left/right subtrees
  being the treaps of the adjacent key ranges. With i.i.d. continuous
  priorities (independent of keys), the root rank is **uniform** and the two
  subtrees are **independent** random treaps (random BST). This is the named,
  proven justification that the two subrange problems decouple into a sum
  (over roots) of products p(left)·p(right). Holds here: the i.i.d. Exp(1)
  speeds give i.i.d. continuous priorities, keys are the boat coordinates.
- [[recursive_inversion_models_permutations_meek_meila]] — Meek & Meilă, NIPS
  2014. Inversion distance d(π,π0)=Σ_{ij}D_{ij} counts pairs whose relative
  order differs (discrepancy matrix); the problem's parity is d mod 2. A RIM
  builds a permutation by recursively merging child subsequences; **all
  inversions arise at the merge of the two child subsequences of a node** —
  none cross between unrelated nodes. Vertex discrepancy v_i(π,π0)=# inversions
  created at node i, and d=Σ_i v_i (additive over the recursion tree). So for
  root r splitting range [a,b]: parity(π[a,b]) = parity(left)·parity(right)·
  (−1)^{cross}, where cross = # pairs (i in left, j in right) flipped at r.
  Expectation of the multiplicative parity functional is then a **sum of
  products of per-range factors** — the exact route memory.md demands.

## The measure and the classical name

- [[dirichlet_distribution_wikipedia]] — Wikipedia. If Z_i~Gamma(α_i,1)
  independent and W=ΣZ_i, then (Z_1/W,…,Z_K/W)~Dirichlet(α_1,…,α_K)
  independent of W. Normalized iid Exp(1) (=Gamma(1,1)) are hence
  **uniform on the simplex** (Dirichlet(1,…,1)). Because the race outcome is
  invariant under common scaling of all speeds (scaling c scales every event
  time by 1/c without changing any ordering), p(n,L) equals the uniform-simplex
  volume of the parity region — turning "integrate over Exp speeds" into "measure
  a simplex region". This exact-simplex view is what the Lasserre section makes
  computable.
- [[plackett_luce_model_wikipedia]] — Wikipedia (originally filed as
  Bradley–Terry/Plackett–Luce; the L1 summary row is missing from disk, content
  recovered from the L0.0 full text). The **Plackett–Luce model** is the
  classical pairwise/ranking model where Pr(i>j)=p_i/(p_i+p_j) and a full
  ranking is sampled sequentially with P(next is i)=λ_i/Σλ over the survivors.
  It is precisely the exponential race: sequential selection with rate-ratio
  products. This is the name for the antirank product appearing in
  [[inid_exponential_order_statistics_nagaraja]] — confirming that the run's
  sequential clock selection IS Plackett–Luce with rate = distance.
- [[plackett_luce_exponential_race_maddison]] — Maddison, arXiv:1602.05986
  (Poisson process model for MC; the L1 summary row is also missing from disk,
  content recovered from the L0.0 full text). Unifies the **perturbation trick**
  (sample a discrete distribution by perturbing and maximizing its mass
  function) and accept-reject MC; generalizes to Gumbel processes whose maxima
  locate samples over infinite spaces. Supports the Plackett–Luce/exponential-
  race sampling view; of secondary use here (it motivates the perturbation
  sampling but is not needed for the exact recursion).

## What the batch establishes together, and its one open gap

- **Together (the closed loop for the exact method):** the order-statistic /
  antirank structure says relative-speed selection is Plackett–Luce with rate =
  distance; the treap result says the two subranges decouple into independent
  subproblems, so p(n,L)=Σ_roots (distance-ratio weight)·p(left)·p(right); the
  RIM result makes parity additive over that recursion as
  parity(left)·parity(right)·(−1)^{cross}; the Dirichlet result reduces the
  measure to a uniform-simplex volume; and Lasserre's closed-form simplex-
  section volumes ([[simplex_volume_sections_lasserre]], read at L0.1; see the
  L1.0 note) make each linear cut of the parity region — hence the whole region
  — an exact rational. That is the finite, non-exhaustive dynamic program the
  run needs for the 10-dp target.
- **The open piece (a disagreement between the sources, not resolved by them):**
  finish events are inverse-exponential with non-constant hazard
  ([[inverse_exponential_finish_times_wikipedia]]), so they are NOT exponential
  clocks; yet the rate-ratio recursion ([[competing_exponential_clocks_uchicago]],
  [[inid_exponential_order_statistics_nagaraja]]) needs event clocks to be
  exponential for the product form to hold. The sources agree the bump timing
  must be built from Laplace relative speeds
  ([[laplace_difference_of_exponentials_libretexts]]), which is NOT an
  exponential clock either. So the exact recursion's event structure — how bumps
  and finish arrivals interleave in the relative-speed geometry — is the run's
  own derivation, and is the part memory.md flags as not yet attacked. The
  library pinpoints this rather than resolving it.

## What each note lets the run compute, bound, or rule out
- Competing clocks: exact bump-chronology probability as products of rate ratios.
- KTH spacings: independence of successive selection for iid; validity only for
  iid.
- Nagaraja: the antirank/rate-ratio law for inid (the correct justification when
  distances make rates differ); settles the "what are the clocks" gap at the
  order-statistics level.
- Inverse-exponential: rules out the naive all-clock product; bounds where a
  product form can apply.
- Laplace: gives the exact relative-speed law the bump clock integrates over.
- Seidel–Aragon: subrange independence ⇒ sum-of-products recursion (no cross-
  range coupling).
- Meek–Meilă: parity = sum of node cross-inversions mod 2 ⇒ per-range factors.
- Dirichlet: p(n,L) = simplex volume of the parity region.
- Plackett–Luce (both): names the sequential sampling; the product IS PL.
- Lasserre (read at L0.1): makes each simplex cut an exact rational ⇒ the final
  10-dp computation is finite and exact.
