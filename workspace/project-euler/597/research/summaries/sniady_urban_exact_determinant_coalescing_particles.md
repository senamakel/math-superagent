# Śniady & Urbán, "Exact determinant formulas for coalescing particle systems" — summary

- Source: Piotr Śniady & Ákos Urbán, *Exact determinant formulas for coalescing particle systems*, arXiv:2602.10782 (2026). URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.10782 (full text: research/sources/sniady_urban_exact_determinant_coalescing_particles.full.md)
- Content: A ghost-particle construction for coalescing (annihilating/merging) particle systems on a line. When two particles collide, one survivor continues and one *invisible ghost* is created alongside it, so the total particle count is constant throughout — restoring the square-matrix/determinantal structure. The probability of any prescribed coalescence pattern (which initial particles merge into which survivors, ending at given positions) equals a determinant whose entries are transition probabilities; integrating ghost positions gives the coalescence determinant for survivors alone. Assumptions: Markov property and nearest-neighbor transitions (e.g. coalescing random walks). The proof is combinatorial via planar configurations, interval-labelled genealogies, and a sign-reversing involution on "performances".
- Bearing on PE597: the "survivor + invisible ghost" semantics is structurally identical to the torpids rear-removal bump rule (bumped/OUT boat = ghost, continues invisibly and non-interacting; front continues). This is the nearest published *exact signed* machinery for coalescence patterns of a transparent-removal process — a genuinely new angle in the library. Two restrictions stop it being directly applicable: (i) its interactions are nearest-neighbor Markov random walks, whereas torpids boats move at constant iid Exp(1) speeds (ballistic, deterministic, not random-walk); (ii) it treats position-dependent coalescence, not the torpids finish-line-absorption parity. But the ghost/heir determinant idea is exactly the kind of exact mechanism that could carry the parity statistic (each heir/ghost carries a sign ±1, and the torpids parity is #chain-pairs mod 2, a sign-product-like functional). Recorded as an ADJACENT computational-attack lead, not a ready-made formula.
