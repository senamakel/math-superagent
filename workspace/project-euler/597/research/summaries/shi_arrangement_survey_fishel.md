# Fishel, "A survey of the Shi arrangement" — summary

- Source: Susanna Fishel, *A survey of the Shi arrangement*, arXiv:1909.01257 (2019). URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.01257 (full text: research/sources/shi_arrangement_survey_fishel.full.md)
- Content: a gentle, comprehensive introduction to the Shi arrangement and its relatives (Ish, Linial-type), centered on the affine Weyl group and alcove geometry. Establishes the central region-count result: the Shi arrangement in type A, hyperplanes x_i − x_j = 0 and x_i − x_j = 1 for 1 ≤ i < j ≤ n, has exactly (n+1)^{n−1} regions. Includes the affine-permutation/alcover perspective (minimal alcoves = inverses of minimal permutations in a scaled fundamental alcove), Khalid–Lusztig cells, the sign-type characterization (X is an ⊕-sign type iff α<β, X_α=+ ⟹ X_β=+), Zaslavsky's theorem (r(A)=(−1)^n χ_A(−1)), and the Shi∆ lattice decomposition into irreducible Coxeter groups. Also treats the Ish arrangement and its parity with Shi.
- Bearing on PE597: the (n+1)^{n−1} Shi count (16 at n=3, 125 at n=4) is the reference region count for the nearest standard braid deformation; it does not equal the run's parity-cell counts (32 at n=3, 1202 at n=4). Together with Postnikov–Stanley (Linial = alternating trees) and Stanley PNAS 1996, this closes the "parity arrangement known deformation" question. The torpids arrangement is not I-coboundary-type either (it involves inverse-speed ties). See `research/threads/finish_line_as_absorbing_wall.md`.
