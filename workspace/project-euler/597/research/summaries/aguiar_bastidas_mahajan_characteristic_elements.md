# Aguiar, Bastidas & Mahajan, "Characteristic elements for real hyperplane arrangements" (arXiv:1902.07325, 2019)

Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.07325 (full text at `research/sources/aguiar_bastidas_mahajan_characteristic_elements.full.md`).

## What it establishes

Introduces **characteristic elements of the Tits algebra** of a real hyperplane arrangement, which "carry information about the characteristic polynomial." Derives the basic properties and applies them to obtain various charpoly facts, from Zaslavsky's formulas to more recent results of Kung and of Klivans–Swartz. Constructs several examples of characteristic elements, including one in terms of the **intrinsic volumes of the faces** of the arrangement.

## Bearing on PE597

Part of the same family as Klivans–Swartz / Kabluchko / Goregaokar: the characteristic polynomial of an arrangement is not merely a region counter but a linear-functional (characteristic element) whose captures aggregate sums over faces with signs (−1)^{dim} / intrinsic volumes. This is the structural home of "signed sums over arrangement faces collapse to a charpoly-level object." The torpids parity is exactly such a signed sum (parity sign per cell) over simplex-section volumes, so this Tits-algebra / characteristic-element framework is the named algebra where a charpoly-level expression for the parity would live. **Caveat:** the paper treats intrinsic volumes and standard arrangements; it does not provide the inverse-speed torpids arrangement or its parity weight, so it remains a route-principle and the algebra of the intended solution, not a statement that p(13,1800) succumbs directly.
