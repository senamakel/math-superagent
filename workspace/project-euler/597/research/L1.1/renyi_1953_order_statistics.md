## What is known to hang on this name (from elsewhere in this library, not this file)
Rényi's 1953 paper is the origin of the **Rényi representation** of exponential order statistics: for X_1..X_n iid Exp(1), the spacings X_(1), X_(i)−X_(i−1) are independent exponentials with rates n, n−1, …, 1.
