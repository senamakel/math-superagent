# Seal — L0.0: exact-integration machinery for PE 597 (Torpids)

This is the authoritative digest of batch `L0.0` (10 full-text notes). It is the
only record of them anyone reads by default, so it carries every distinct
definition, theorem, bound and numeric result, with hypotheses and whether they
hold here. Target context: p(n,L) = probability the new boat order is an even
permutation; oracle p(3,160)=56/135≈0.414815 and p(4,400)=0.5107843137; target
p(13,1800) to 10 dp; MC pins it ≈0.5002±0.00007 (60M samples), so any true bias
≤ ~3e-4 and the 10-dp answer needs an exact route, not sampling.

The batch's joint thesis: the boats' **relative-speed clocks** give a
**rate-ratio product** (whose sequential selection is exactly the Plackett–Luce
model); the clocks form an **independent-subtree treap**, so p(n,L) = sum over
roots of (distance-ratio weight)·p(left)·p(right); and **parity is additive
over that recursion**, so the whole thing is an exact, non-enumerative
simplex-volume computation. One structural obstacle is named but not resolved
by these sources (below).

## The clocks and their distributions

- [[competing_exponential_clocks_uchicago]] — UChicago STAT317 Lecture 9
  (Yibi Huang). Facts: for X_j~Exp(λ_j) independent, (i) min ~ Exp(Σλ_j), and
  (ii) P(X_j=min)=λ_j/Σλ_j (derived by conditioning on X_j=t against the
  product ∏_{i≠j} e^{−λ_i t}). Memoryless: P(X>t+s|X>t)=P(X>s). Sum of iid
  Exp(λ) is Gamma(n,λ). Holds here: the Exp(1) speeds are independent. It is
  the base fact every sequential-selection product below rests on: a firing
  order i_1,…,i_D has probability (λ_{i1}/Σλ)(λ_{i2}/(Σλ−λ_{i1}))⋯ = product
  of rate ratios over survivors.
- [[exponential_order_statistics_memoryless_kth]] — Koski, KTH SF2955. For
  iid Exp(1): the spacings X_(1), X_(i)−X_(i−1) are **independent** Exps with
  rates n, n−1, …, 1. Proof is a triangular change of variables Y=AX with
  det A=1, so the joint order density factorises into those exponentials.
  Hypothesis: iid. This is the memoryless structure that makes successive
  selection independent. Does NOT survive heterogeneity (see next).
- [[inid_exponential_order_statistics_uic]] — Nagaraja ch. 11 (Handbook of
  Statistics; UIC STAT416 copy), "Order Statistics from Independent
  Exponential Random Variables and the Sum of the Top Order Statistics".
  For **inid** X_j~Exp(λ_j): Renyi representation X_(i)=Σ_{j=1}^i
  Z_j/(λ_(n−j+1)) holds only for iid; for inid, Nevzorov (1984) gives a
  mixture representation with n! components, mixture probability
  p over permutations = λ_{d(1)}⋯λ_{d(n)} / [Π over k of (λ_{d(k)}+⋯+λ_{d(n)})].
  Tikhov (1991) introduces **antiranks** D(i)={m : X_(i)=X_m} with the
  survivor-proportional antirank law: P(D(k+1)=i)=λ_i / Σ_{survivors} λ_j — the
  Nevzorov/Tikhov sequence. Crucially **inid spacings are NOT independent**
  (zero covariance iff all λ_j equal). Lemma 11.3.1: partial sums of top order
  statistics have the closed survival form against a pooled-rate exponential.
  Holds here (distances L−p_j distinct ⇒ rates distinct). This is the
  justification for recursing with rate = distance, W_i=v_i/(L−p_i)~Exp(L−p_i).

## Race-specific limits (what is NOT an exponential clock)

- [[inverse_exponential_finish_times_wikipedia]] — Wikipedia "Inverse
  distribution". General change of variable for the reciprocal: if Y=1/X,
  F_Y(y)=1−F_X(1/y), g(y)=(1/y²)f(1/y). For X~Exp(λ), **Y=1/X is
  inverse-exponential**: F_Y(y)=e^{−λ/y}, g(y)=(λ/y²)e^{−λ/y}, y>0; its mean
  does not exist. Boat j's finish time is T_j=(L−p_j)/v_j = c_j/v_j with
  c_j=L−p_j, so each finish time is inverse-exponential scale c_j with a
  **non-constant hazard** h_j(t)=(c_j/t²)/(1−e^{−c_j/t}). Negative result for
  the run: finish events are NOT competing exponential clocks; the
  "next wins with prob λ/Σλ" rate-ratio picture cannot include finish arrivals
  as a plain clock. Memorylessness lives in the speeds v_j, not the finish
  times 1/v_j.
- [[laplace_difference_of_exponentials_libretexts]] — Siegrist LibreTexts
  5.28. For X,Y iid Exp(1), Z=X−Y is **standard Laplace** (double exponential):
  density g(u)=(1/2)e^{−|u|}, MGF 1/(1−t²), odd moments 0, even moments
  E[Z^{2k}]=(2k)! (so Var=2, excess kurtosis 6), |Z|~Exp(1). This is the exact
  distribution of the **relative speed** v_j−v_i driving each bump — the input
  the bump clock integrates over. Also not an exponential clock.

## The recursion and the parity

- [[seidel_aragon_randomized_search_trees]] — Seidel & Aragon, "Randomized
  Search Trees" (Algorithmica 16(4/5):464–497, 1996). A **treap** orders items
  in-order by key, heap-order by priority; with distinct keys and priorities it
  is unique, the extremal-priority item is the root, and left/right subtrees
  are the treaps of the adjacent key ranges. With **i.i.d. continuous
  priorities independent of keys**, the root is at a uniform rank and the two
  subtrees are **independent** random treaps (random BST / Cartesian-tree
  structure). Holds here: the i.i.d. Exp(1) speeds are continuous i.i.d.
  priorities independent of the boat coordinates (keys). This is the named
  proof that the two subrange problems decouple into a sum (over roots) of
  products p(left)·p(right) — no cross-range coupling.
- [[recursive_inversion_models_permutations_meek_meila]] — Meek & Meilă, NIPS
  2014, "Recursive Inversion Models for Permutations". Discrepancy matrix
  D_ij(π,π0)=1 iff i<π j and j<π0 i (0 otherwise); inversion distance
  d(π,π0)=Σ_{ij} D_ij; Mallows model P=Z^{-1}exp(−θd); per-element inversion
  v_j=Σ_{i>π0 j} D_ij gives the GMM. A **RIM** constructs a permutation by
  recursively merging child subsequences, preserving relative order within
  each child; **all inversions arise at the merge of two child subsequences of
  a node, none across unrelated nodes**, so d=Σ_nodes (cross inversions created
  at that node). Hence for a root r splitting range [a,b]:
  parity([a,b]) = parity(left)·parity(right)·(−1)^{cross}, cross = # pairs
  (i in left, j in right) whose relative order is flipped at r. This makes the
  multiplicative parity functional a sum of products of per-range factors over
  the treap recursion — the exact route the oracle demands.

## The measure and the classical name

- [[dirichlet_distribution_wikipedia]] — Wikipedia. If Z_i~Gamma(α_i,1)
  independent and W=ΣZ_i, then (Z_1/W,…,Z_K/W)~Dirichlet(α_1,…,α_K),
  **independent of W**; PDF ∝ Π x_i^{α_i−1} on the K−1 simplex, mean α_i/α_0,
  Var E[X_i]=α_i/α_0, variance α̃_i(1−α̃_i)/(α_0+1),
  Cov δ_{ij}α̃_i − α̃_i α̃_j over (α_0+1). Normalized iid Exp(1) (=Gamma(1,1))
  are therefore **uniform on the simplex**, Dirichlet(1,…,1). Because the race
  outcome is invariant under common scaling of all speeds (scaling c scales
  every event time by 1/c without changing any ordering), p(n,L) equals the
  **uniform-simplex volume of the parity region** — "integrate over Exp
  speeds" becomes "measure a simplex region". This view is what the closed-form
  simplex-section result (read at L0.1, [[simplex_volume_sections_lasserre]])
  makes computable as an exact rational.
- [[plackett_luce_model_wikipedia]] — Wikipedia (filed under Bradley–Terry,
  redirects to Plackett–Luce). Pr(i>j)=p_i/(p_i+p_j) for a pair; a **full
  ranking is sampled sequentially with P(next is i)=λ_i/Σ_survivors λ** over
  the survivors. This is exactly the **exponential race / Plackett–Luce
  model**, already studied by Zermelo (1920s), Bradley–Terry (1952), Plackett
  and Luce. This is the classical name for the survivor-proportional product
  that [[inid_exponential_order_statistics_uic]] derives at the order-statistic
  level — it confirms the run's sequential clock selection IS Plackett–Luce
  with rate = distance.
- [[plackett_luce_exponential_race_maddison]] — Maddison, arXiv:1602.05986,
  "A Poisson process model for Monte Carlo" (stat.CO). Unifies the
  **perturbation trick** (sample a discrete mass function by perturbing and
  maximizing) with the accept–reject view of Monte Carlo; generalises via
  Gumbel processes whose maxima locate samples over infinite spaces; analyses
  A* sampling and OS*. Secondary for the exact recursion: it motivates the
  perturbation sampling view but is not needed to compute p(n,L). It is kept
  because it corroborates the Plackett–Luce/exponential-race sampling picture.

## What the batch establishes together, and the one open gap

- **Closed loop for the exact method:** (i) relative-speed selection is
  Plackett–Luce with rate = distance (order-statistics antirank law +
  PL model); (ii) the treap result makes the two subranges independent so
  p(n,L)=Σ_roots (distance-ratio weight)·p(left)·p(right); (iii) RIM makes
  parity additive over that recursion as parity(left)·parity(right)·(−1)^cross;
  (iv) Dirichlet reduces the measure to a uniform-simplex volume; (v) the
  closed-form simplex-section volumes (read at L0.1) make each linear cut of
  the parity region — and hence the whole region — an exact rational. That is
  the finite dynamic program for the 10-dp target. The parity mass is computed
  by summing over all treap shapes, weighted by distance-ratio products.
- **The open piece (a genuine disagreement between the sources, not resolved
  here):** finish events are inverse-exponential with non-constant hazard
  ([[inverse_exponential_finish_times_wikipedia]]), so they are NOT exponential
  clocks; yet the rate-ratio / Plackett–Luce product form
  ([[competing_exponential_clocks_uchicago]],
  [[inid_exponential_order_statistics_uic]],
  [[plackett_luce_model_wikipedia]]) needs the event clocks to be exponential.
  The bump timing must be built from Laplace relative speeds
  ([[laplace_difference_of_exponentials_libretexts]]), which is not an
  exponential clock either. So the exact recursion's event structure — how
  bumps and finish arrivals interleave in the relative-speed geometry — is the
  run's own derivation, and is the part memory.md flags as not yet attacked.
  The library *pinpoints* this obstacle rather than resolving it.

## Verdicts per source (detail above; a line each)
- comp_clocks_uchicago: **holds** (independent Exp speeds); foundational
  rate-ratio product for exact bump-chronology probability.
- exp_order_stats_kth: **holds for iid**; gives spacings independence — the
  basis of independent successive selection, valid only when rates are equal.
- inid_nagaraja: **holds** (distinct distances ⇒ inid); the correct antirank /
  survivor-proportional law (rate = distance) and negatives (inid spacings not
  independent); closed-form survival kernels for top-order-statistic sums.
- inverse_exp_wikipedia: **holds**; the key negative — finish times are
  inverse-exponential with diverging mean, not clocks; bounds where a product
  form can apply.
- laplace_libretexts: **holds**; exact Laplace law of the bump's relative
  speed, the input the bump clock integrates over.
- seidel_aragon: **hypotheses hold**; subrange independence licenses the
  sum-of-products treap recursion (no cross-range coupling).
- meek_meila: **holds**; inversion-count additivity gives parity propagation
  parity(left)·parity(right)·(−1)^cross, hence per-range factors.
- dirichlet_wikipedia: **holds**; p(n,L) = uniform-simplex volume of the
  parity region, invariant under common speed scaling.
- plackett_luce (wikipedia + maddison): **confirm** the sequential
  survivor-proportional product IS Plackett–Luce with rate = distance;
  Maddison adds the Poisson/Gumbel perturbation view (motivation only).

## What the batch lets the run compute, bound, or rule out
- Rate-ratio products (uchicago, nagaraja): exact bump-chronology probability
  for a chosen selection order.
- Independence of selection (kth, seidel_aragon): successive subrange choices
  are independent, so the recursion factors into a sum of products.
- Parity propagation (meek_meila): the sign is a sum of per-node cross
  inversions mod 2 — no enumeration of full permutations needed.
- Simplex reduction (dirichlet): the measure is a simplex volume, benign only
  if the parity region is a finite union of simplex sections.
- Negative results (inverse_exp, laplace): neither finish times nor relative
  speeds are exponential clocks; any all-clock product form for the bump/finish
  interleaving is ruled out.
