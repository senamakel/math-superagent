# Title: Isometric group actions on Hilbert spaces: structure of orbits

Authors: [Yves de Cornulier][2], [Romain Tessera][3], [Alain Valette][4]

View a PDF of the paper titled Isometric group actions on Hilbert spaces: structure of orbits, by Yves de Cornulier and 2 other authors

[View PDF][5] [HTML (experimental)][6]

Abstract: Our main result is that a finitely generated nilpotent group has no isometric action on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space with dense orbits. In contrast, we construct such an action with a finitely generated metabelian group.
