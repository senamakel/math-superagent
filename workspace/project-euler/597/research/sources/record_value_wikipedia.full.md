From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

In [statistics][1], a **record value**or **record statistic**is the largest or smallest value obtained from a sequence of random variables. The theory is closely related to that used in [order statistics][2]. [1]

The term was first introduced by K. N. Chandler in 1952. [2]
