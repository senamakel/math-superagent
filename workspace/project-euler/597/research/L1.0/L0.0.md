# Seal — L0.0: exact-integration machinery for PE 597 (Torpids)

This batch (10 notes) supplies the named machinery that turns the race's
continuous dynamics into an exact recursive integration. Its thesis: relative
speed clocks and the rate-ratio product, arranged as an independent-subtree
treap, give p(n,L) as a sum of products of distance ratios.

## The clocks and their distributions
- [[competing_exponential_clocks_uchicago.full]] — min of independent exponentials
  fires with probability proportional to pooled rate; a fixed firing order has
  probability = product of λ_i/Σλ over survivors; memoryless property. Base
  fact for every sequential-selection product.
- [[exponential_order_statistics_memoryless_kth.full]] — order-statistic spacings
  of iid Exp(1) are independent Exps with rates n,n−1,…,1. The memoryless
  structure that makes successive selection independent.
- [[inid_exponential_order_statistics_uic.full]] — for heterogeneous
  (INID) exponentials the successive order statistics obey the Nevzorov/Tikhov
  antirank law: P(next is i)=λ_i/Σ_survivors λ_j; spacings are NOT independent
  when rates differ. Justifies recursing with rate=distance, W_i=v_i/(t−i)~Exp(t−i).

## Race-specific limits
- [[inverse_exponential_finish_times_wikipedia.full]] — finish time (L−p_j)/v_j is
  inverse-exponential, not an exponential clock; finish events cannot be a
  competing-clock term.
- [[laplace_difference_of_exponentials_libretexts.full]] — difference of two iid
  Exp(1) is standard Laplace: the distribution of the relative speed v_j−v_i
  driving each bump.

## The recursion and the parity
- [[seidel_aragon_randomized_search_trees.full]] — treap: min-priority root,
  left/right subtrees are treaps of adjacent ranges; with i.i.d. continuous
  priorities the two subtrees are **independent**. So the two subrange problems
  decouple into a sum (over roots) of products p(left)·p(right).
- [[recursive_inversion_models_permutations_meek_meila.full]] — inversion count is
  additive over the recursion tree; each node contributes only the cross
  inversions between its left/right subranges. Parity propagates as
  parity(left)·parity(right)·(−1)^cross.

## The measure and the classical name
- [[dirichlet_distribution_wikipedia]] — normalized iid Exp(1) are uniform on
  the simplex (Dirichlet(1,…,1)); race outcome is invariant under common speed
  scaling, so p(n,L) is the uniform-simplex volume of the parity region.
- [[plackett_luce_exponential_race_maddison]] — (arXiv:1602.05986, Maddison)
  Poisson-process / Gumbel-perturbation view of discrete sampling; the
  perturbation trick that samples the maximizing item, unifying accept-reject
  and perturbation Monte Carlo.
- [[plackett_luce_model_wikipedia]] — the Plackett–Luce model is exactly the
  exponential race: a full ranking sampled sequentially with
  P(next is i)=λ_i/Σλ, named and classical. Confirms the antirank product above
  IS Plackett–Luce.

## What the batch establishes together
Sequential selection over the relative-speed clocks is the Plackett–Luce
exponential race with rate=distance, giving p(n,L) = Σ_roots (distance-ratio
weight)·p(left)·p(right) with parity propagated additively — the exact,
non-exhaustive recursion targets. Finish times are not clocks and relative
speeds are Laplace, so only the *relative-speed order* acts as clocks, and the
subtree independence makes the recursion a clean dynamic program.
