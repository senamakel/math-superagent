| `simplex_volume_sections_lasserre.md` | Digest of Lasserre, "Volume of slices and sections of the simplex in closed form" (Optimization Letters 2015). Closed-form Laplace-transform volumes of simplex sections under linear cuts — bridges the Dirichlet/simplex-volume view of p(n,L) to an exact rational evaluation for the 10-dp target p(13,1800). URL: https://hal.science/hal-01095071/document |
| `torpids_ballistic_aggregation_arxiv.md` | Summary of Majumdar-Mallick-Sabhapandit, "Statistical properties of the final state in one-dimensional ballistic aggregation" (arXiv:0811.0908, PRE 79 021109 2009). Final 'fan' state of merging sticky particles = convex minorant of the random walk with steps (1,v_i); number of clusters ~ S1(N,k)/N! = cycles of a uniform random permutation (Raney-lemma per-cluster 1/n). Identifies the pure no-finish bumper race's bump partition with these convex-minorant clusters / leaders = right-to-left record minima of speeds. Restriction: no-boundary model only; the finite finish line of PE 597 breaks the fan mapping. Source URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.0908 |
| `torpids_record_runs_platoons_haghighi_talab.md` | Summary of Haghighi-Talab & Wright, "On the distribution of records in a finite sequence of observations, with an application to a road traffic problem" (J. Appl. Prob. 10(3):556-571, 1973). Exact distributional results for lower records in a finite i.i.d. sequence by rank-permutation methods, focused on the 'record run' between successive records. For PE 597: platoon leaders = right-to-left record minima of speeds v_i, each platoon = one record run — the exact combinatorics of the pure no-finish bumper/convoy partition. Restriction: no-passing platoon theory is boundary-free; finite finish line is the extra layer. DOI: https://doi.org/10.2307/3212776 |
