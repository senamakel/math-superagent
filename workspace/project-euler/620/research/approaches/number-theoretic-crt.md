## Research verdict — split

**The *reformulation* (tooth-count congruences as the discreteness) is grounded, and now has a direct off-centre precedent.** Kurasov 2020 (gear eccentric systems, "toothed contours" method) derives universal assembly conditions for the off-centre (eccentric) gear case precisely as integer congruences of central-angle·tooth-count sums equated to an integer multiple of π — eq. (7): `2φv·zv + φn·zn − φC1·zC1 − φC2·zC2 − π·K = 0`, eq. (8): `φv·zv + φn·zn + φC'1·zC1 + φC'2·zC2 − 2π·K' = 0`. That is the same structural object as the thread's W-invariant (s·φ + c·χ − t·γ ≡ 0 mod π/2π) — a signed sum of angle×tooth-count equal to an integer multiple of π — and it applies to *eccentric* (off-centre) systems, matching PE620's off-centre sun. It corroborates the tooth-count-congruence discreteness for the off-centre case, not only the coaxal one. This grounds the *discrete-language* half of the candidate.

**The closed-form / multiplicative half is UNSUPPORTED, and the candidate's own mechanism is self-undermining there.** The candidate asserts g depends on c,s,p,q only through gcd/lcm structures (making it multiplicative and giving a sub-cubic sieve for G(500)). Two problems:

1. None of the found sources (Kurasov 2020, Guo 2011, Simionescu 1998, Xue 2020, Zou 2015, Sun 2017) establishes that the count g is multiplicative in the tooth counts or has a closed CRT/Smith form. Each gives a *per-assembly* congruence, not a seat-count formula. The multiplicativity claim is a conjecture with no precedent and no on-disk derivation.
2. The candidate's mechanism (*"counting tooth-index assignments is equivalent to counting d values"* / *"after fixing the discrete tooth indices, the geometry determines d uniquely"*) is exactly where the off-centre geometry bites: in PE620 the position is *forced by tangency* (two mirror points per type, thread claim `offcentre_two_positions_per_type`) and the free variable is the centre-distance d — not four independent ring-tooth indices that a congruence system can freely range. The tooth-index count and the d-count are not independent; they encode the same one-dimensional discreteness, so a naive product over four Z_c indices would massively overcount. A correct CRT/sieve formulation therefore does NOT decouple into independent congruences the way the candidate sketches.

Net: **grounded** as a reformulation of the discreteness into tooth-count congruences (strongly supported, including by an off-centre source), **but** the CRT/Smith/multiplicativity half that would buy sub-cubic G(500) is unsupported — there is no evidence g is multiplicative, and the candidate's own index↔d identification overcounts. The gcd/lcm-structure conjecture must be tested against a correct g before it pays off.

**Verification requirement (both halves):** the congruences must reproduce g(16,5,5,6)=9, G(16)=9, G(20)=205. Until a correct g under this model matches the oracle, treat the formula as conjectural regardless of how well precedented the reformulation is.
