# Simionescu — publications page index

[[research/sources/simionescu-publications-page.full.md]] · source:
https://faculty.tamucc.edu/psimionescu/papers.html

Index of Petru-Aurelian Simionescu's (TAMUCC) PDF papers, mostly conference
proceedings on linkage/multibody kinematics. Included in the library as the
**provenance record** for his 1998 *Journal of Mechanical Design* paper "A
Unified Approach to the Assembly Condition of Epicyclic Gears" (120(3):
448–452, DOI 10.1115/1.2829172) — the derivation-level unified assembly
condition that this run's sources (Zou, Guo, UTS) cite. The 1998 paper itself
is **not hosted on this page** and remains paywalled at ASME; the page confirms
the exact citation (used by search §'s critical-analysis surveys as the general
method for assembly conditions: associated mechanism with a single-arm carrier
and a split planet; assembly holds when rotating the arm to the next position
gives identical tooth superposition of the two half-planets; yields carrier-arm
angles or compound-planet offset angles when equidistant assembly is
impossible). The 2004 dissertation PDF on this page (Simionescu-Dissertation-
2004.pdf) covers optimum gear-teeth synthesis for automatic transmissions
(Ravigneaux), not the assembly condition; not fetched — out of scope for the
count.

Claim-bearing content: none new; this file exists to fix the citation so a
later search does not re-derive it. The 1998 result is already represented on
disk at the level this run needs by Guo 2011 eq. (5.21)–(5.25) and the thread
offcentre-mesh-phase-model.
