# Vilán-Vilán, Segade-Robleda et al. 2010 — J. Mech. Des. bibliography record

This file is the Uvigo/Scopus **bibliographic record** for:
José A. Vilán Vilán, Abraham Segade Robleda, Marcos López Lago, Enrique
Casarejos Ruiz, "Feasible geometrical configurations for split torque gearboxes
with idler pinions", *ASME J. Mech. Des.* 2010, 132(12), DOI 10.1115/1.4002977.

**The paper itself is ASME-paywalled** (not on disk). Its content — the
curvilinear-quadrilateral / pitch-difference simultaneous-meshing condition for
four-gear (input + two idlers + output) assemblies, and the worked tooth-number
solutions — is carried in the library by:
- `research/summaries/split-torque-gearboxes-core-2012.md` (Segade-Robleda et
  al. 2012 IntechOpen chapter by the same group, which restates and applies the
  2010 method, incl. the exact same condition and a worked z=(30,50,20,12)
  n-set);
- `research/summaries/torque-split-idler-mesh-zhao-li-2018-pdf.md` (Zhao & Li
  2018, which cites and generalises the Vilán 2010 method).

No need to obtain the paywalled 2010 paper: the 2012 chapter is the same group's
open-access account. This record fixes the citation so a later search does not
re-acquire it.
