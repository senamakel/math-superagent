## Why it is cited and what that means for PE620

Guo 2011 (OSU thesis, Ch. 5) cites Müller 1982 as [68], the origin of the
assembly condition `(Z_r + Z_s)·ψ̂ = 2π·n` for a simple planetary stage. That
condition is on disk in full derivable form via Guo's own eq. (5.21)–(5.25)
(claim `assembly_condition_simple_planetary_guo`, status sourced), plus three
design-guide statements (`least_mesh_angle*`) and the worked 68/18 example in
UTS 1165. Nothing that Müller would add is missing from the library because
Guo already carries the derivation to which Müller is upstream.
