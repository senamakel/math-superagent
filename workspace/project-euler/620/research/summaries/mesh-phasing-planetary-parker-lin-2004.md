## What it establishes

The authoritative publication list of Robert G. Parker's Dynamics & Vibrations Lab
(bibliography tier — it confirms citations, it does not provide full texts).

- **Parker & Lin, "Mesh Phasing Relationships in Planetary and Epicyclic Gears",
  ASME Journal of Mechanical Design, 2004, vol. 126, no. 2, pp. 365–370** — the
  foundational mesh-phase paper the library cites. Full text **paywalled**; the
  derivation is carried, in thesis form, by the Guo 2011 dissertation now on disk
  (same research group), and retold in the ISMA 2016 paper (on disk).
- **Cooley & Parker, "A Review of Planetary and Epicyclic Gear Dynamics and
  Vibration Research", Applied Mechanics Reviews, 2014, vol. 66, p. 040804** — the
  survey tier. Full text **paywalled** (ASME).
- Related relevant entries: Parker (2000) JSV 236(4):561–573 (physical explanation
  for planet phasing); Lin & Parker (2000) JSV 233(5):921–928 (structured vibration
  of unequally spaced planets); Guo & Parker (2011) MMT 46:1869–1887 (mesh phase
  relations in compound gears — the journal version of Guo's thesis Ch. 5).

## Implication for PE620

Confirms the canonical citations so the run can cite the primary literature correctly
(Parker & Lin 2004 for mesh phases; Cooley & Parker 2014 for the survey). Since both
full texts are paywalled, the on-disk derivations (Guo 2011 thesis on OhioLINK and the
ISMA 2016 open-access paper) are the accessible primary sources.
