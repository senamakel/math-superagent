In the formula, it is specified that “+” is taken when the planet carrier rotates anticlockwise, and “-” when it is rotated clockwise.

Because three planetary gears are evenly arranged on the planet carrier, the angle between the adjacent planetary gears is 120 degrees. Taking the planetary gear 1 as the reference gear, we can know that ψ 1 = 0, ψ 2 = 2 π / 3, ψ 3 = 4 π / 3. When the planet carrier rotates anticlockwise, the phase relationship among the gears is γ S1 = γ R1 = 0, γ S2 = γ R2 = Zr / 3, γ S3 = γ R3 = 2Zr / 3, that is, the meshing phases of the second and third planetary gear relative to the first planetary gear are respectively ahead of Zr / 3 and 2Zr / 3.

In planetary gear transmission system, many pairs of “sun gear planetary gear” and “planetary gear inner gear ring” gear pairs are meshed at the same time. Although the meshing stiffness of each pair of sun gear planetary gear (or planetary gear inner gear ring) is the same, the meshing phase between different tooth pairs is not always the same.
