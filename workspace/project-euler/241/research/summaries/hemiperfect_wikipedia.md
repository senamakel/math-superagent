## What it claims

In [number theory][1], a **hemiperfect number**is a [positive integer][2] with a half-integer [abundancy index][3]. In other words, *σ*(*n*)/*n*= *k*/2 for an odd integer *k*, where *σ*(*n*) is the [sum-of-divisors function][4], the sum of all positive [divisors][5] of *n*.

The first few hemiperfect numbers are:

2, 24, 4320, 4680, 26208, 8910720, 17428320, 20427264, 91963648, 197064960, ... (sequence [A159907][6] in the [OEIS][7])
