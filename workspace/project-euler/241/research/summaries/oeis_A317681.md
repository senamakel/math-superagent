A317681

a(n) = smallest m such that sigma(m) = n*m/2.

OFFSET

2,2

COMMENTS

Interleaving of [A007539][11] and [A088912][12].

For even n, a(n) is a multiply perfect number; for odd n it is a hemiperfect number.

Note that 1 is the only number with abundancy 1, and 2 is the only number with abundancy 3/2 (in other words, 1 and 2 are solitary numbers; see [A014567][13]). For k >= 4 it is not known whether there are finitely many or infinitely many numbers with abundancy k/2. Also it is not known whether a(n) < a(n+1) always holds.

On the Riemann Hypothesis (RH), a(n) > exp(exp(n/(2*exp(gamma)))), where gamma = 0.5772156649... is the Euler-Mascheroni constant ( [A001620][14]).

FORMULA

a(2n) = [A007539][11] (n), a(2n+1) = [A088912][12] (n), n > 0.

EXAMPLE

a(7) = 4320 since sigma(4320) = 15120 = 7/2*4320 and 4320 is the smallest m such that sigma(m)/m = 7/2.
