# Fang — Searching on the boundary of abundance for odd weird numbers (arXiv abstract)

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12906 — `[[fang_odd_weird_boundary.full]]`
(W. Fang, "Searching on the boundary of abundance for odd weird numbers",
arXiv:2207.12906, 2022).

## What the abstract establishes

No odd weird numbers exist below 10^21 (and none with abundance < 10^14 below 10^28).
The search uses **reverse search** over factorisations — the same tree-search/pruning
family as Alekseyev's (which cites this paper) and the run's DFS.

## What it means for PE 241

Weird numbers (abundant but not pseudoperfect) are an **adjacent, unrelated** family —
no overlap with hemiperfects. This source's value is purely methodological: it is a
second, independent published instance of reverse search on a σ/abundance tree being
scaled to a huge bound with cost in the description, confirming the tree-search method
family the run uses. It adds no bound or value for the hemiperfect sum. Background only;
do not re-read.
