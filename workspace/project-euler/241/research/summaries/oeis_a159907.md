# A159907 — hemiperfect numbers (half-integer abundancy)

Source: https://oeis.org/A159907 — `[[oeis_A088912.full]]` holds the OEIS family; this is the primary hemiperfect list.

## What it establishes

- **Terms:** 2, 24, 4320, 4680, 26208, 8910720, 17428320, 20427264, 91963648, 197064960, 8583644160, 10200236032, 21857648640, 57575890944, 57629644800, 206166804480, 17116004505600, 1416963251404800, ... — i.e. **all** hemiperfect numbers (any k/2), the union over abundancies.
- **Characterization:** A159907 = { n | 2·σ(n) ∈ n·(odd numbers) } = { n | antisigma(n) ≡ 0 (mod n) } where antisigma(n) = n(n+1)/2 − σ(n).
- **All terms are even** (given as a comment; the "odd numbers and higher powers of 2 cannot be in the sequence" note, e.g. 6 is perfect, powers of 2 have abundancy 2−2^{-a} not half-integer).
- The antisigma characterization connects hemiperfects to multiply-antiperfect numbers; no odd multiply-perfect numbers are known, so A242484 and A159907 are believed to coincide beyond n=2.
