## **Variations on Euclid's Formula for Perfect Numbers **

### Farideh Firoozbakht
Faculty of Mathematics & Computer Science
University of Isfahan
Khansar
Iran

Maximilian F. Hasler
Laboratoire CEREGMIA
Univ. Antilles-Guyane
Schoelcher
Martinique

**Abstract:**

We study several families of solutions to equations of the form &sigma;(*n*) = *An*+ *B*(*n*), where *B*is a function that may depend on properties of *n*.
