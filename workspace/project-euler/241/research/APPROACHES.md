## What closed, and why

Do not propose these again. A reason stated precisely is what makes that possible; one left blank makes this row worthless.

- [[exponent-signature-first]] (refuted): The signature-first ordering is not a distinct named method — it is a re-packaging of the same multiplicative monotone tree-search (Goto–Shibata Lemma 2.1). Its central pruning claim ("at most a narrow range of prime assignments achieve T for a fixed signature") is a conjecture unbacked by the literature; Alekseyev's wheel (Theorem 3.3) is the proved-complete alternative. The r=1 and r=2 signature cases are exactly Alekseyev's p^k/pq shortcuts, already inside the standard method. For r≥3 the monotone search over prime tuples is heuristic pruning where the wheel is analytic, and the signature enumeration (a bounded integer partition problem) is interesting but duplicative: the denominator-forcing in the 2-adic split already restricts the search to primes that clear the denominator, which implicitly selects the feasible signatures without pre-enumerating them. Close as subsumed — the useful parts are already inside the adopted approach, and the rest is unproven.
