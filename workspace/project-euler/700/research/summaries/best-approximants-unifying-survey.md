# Dajani–Kraaikamp–Sanderson: unifying theory for convergents and mediants

A survey/paper on the *metric theory* of regular continued fractions, built on
Ito's natural extension of the Farey tent map. It is a **metric/ergodic-theoretic**
source, not an algorithmic one; its value here is as an authoritative
confirmation of the best-approximation facts, not as the method.

## Verified statements (against full text)

- **Best approximants of the first kind** (def. in §1): p/q in lowest terms is a
  best approximant of x iff any other d/e with `|x − d/e| ≤ |x − p/q|` has `e > q`.
  "Every best approximant is either a regular convergent or a mediant" — the
  survey **cites this to Blom [4]** (J. Austral. Math. Soc. 53 (1992)), it does not
  prove it. So attribute: sourced, via [4], not this paper's own result.
- **Best approximants of the second kind** (footnote 2): analogously with
  `|ex − d| ≤ |qx − p|`; these "are classified as rcf-convergents of x, see [54]"
  (Rockett–Szüsz) — again cited, not proved here. Same fact as Cornell Thm 4.14
  (which we hold and which is proved there).
- **Legendre's Theorem** (Theorem 5.14, proved here): if `Θ(x,p/q) = q|qx−p| < 1/2`
  then p/q is a regular convergent of x; the constant 1/2 is optimal. This is the
  continued-fraction recognition theorem — the "if a fraction is within 1/(2q²)
  it's a convergent" fact.
- **Fatou–Grace (Theorem 5.15, proved here):** if `Θ < 1` then p/q is a convergent
  *or a nearest (first or final) mediant*; constant 1 optimal. **Koksma (5.16):**
  if `Θ < 2/3` then p/q is a convergent or a *first* mediant; constant 2/3 optimal.
  These refine Legendre to mediants.
- Approximation coefficients `θ_n = q_n² |x − p_n/q_n|`; the Farey-tent-extended
  framework gives limiting distributions of these coefficients (Doeblin–Lenstra
  conjecture, Cor 5.4, etc.) — metric results not needed for the exact answer.

## What it means for this run

The relevant, correct, load-bearing fact is the one we already hold from Cornell
Thm 4.14 and use in `code/solution.py` via the record-low recurrence: **the
convergents of α are the best approximations of the second kind of α** (minimise
`q|qα−p|`), and Legendre's theorem (5.14) says good rational approximations are
convergents. This survey confirms both at survey-level authority and adds the
sharp-proved versions (Legendre, Fatou–Grace, Koksma) but does **not** change the
method: the Eulercoins are still generated by the O(log M) record-low recurrence
`eu700-record-low-recurrence`, which is already verified by computation. This
source is **not** needed to compute the answer and is read for confirmation only.

Does **not** establish the record-low recurrence `n_{k+2} = ceil(...)*n_{k+1} − n_k`;
that is the smsxgz source, separately verified by brute force.
