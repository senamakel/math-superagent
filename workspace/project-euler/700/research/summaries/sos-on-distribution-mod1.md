# Sós, "On the theory of diophantine approximations. I (on a problem of A. Ostrowski)"

The **foundational primary reference** for the record-low / channel structure of the
sequence of fractional parts `{nα}`. Acta Mathematica Academiae Scientiarum
Hungaricae 8 (1957), pp. 461–472. The results grew out of Sós's 1957 dissertation.

## What is establishable from the local copy

**The full mathematical content is paywalled.** The downloaded text (from the
Springer landing page via DOI 10.1007/BF02020329) carries only the metadata: title,
author (Vera T. Sós), journal/volume/pages, and the bibliography. The ~226 KB raw
download contains no theorems or proofs — only the access page. So this file records
**that the reference exists and is the canonical treatment of the Ostrowski problem**
(discrepancy / record structure of `{nα}`), but **not** a citable statement of any
theorem.

## What the bibliography establishes (metadata, citable)

The paper is positioned in the classical Khintchine–Ostrowski programme on linear
Diophantine approximation:

- A. Ostrowski (1921, Abh. Math. Sem. Hamburg) — original problem on inhomogeneous
  Diophantine approximation, the direct antecedent.
- A. Khintchine (1926, Rend. Palermo) — linear Diophantine-approximation class.
- S. Fukasawa (1926), H. Davenport (1950), A. V. Prasad (1951), J. W. S. Cassels
  (1954) — the circle of results on `|Ix + α − y|` that Sós's paper is part of.

## Why it is in the library despite the paywall

The Three Gap Theorem source (Mayero, `summaries/three-gap-theorem-steinhaus.md`)
and the Baxa–Schoißengeier paper both cite **Sós (1957)** as the origin of the gap /
record-low structure of `{nα}`, which is exactly the structure the PE700 Eulercoins
(the record lows of `A n mod M`) exhibit. Even without the proof text, this file
(when cited) fixes the history and the authoritative name: the modular-distribution /
record-minimum structure of `{nα}` is the Sós / Ostrowski theory. Its 21 citations
were added to `research/FRONTIER.md`, giving the next likely primary leads.
