# Ahmad & Husnine, "Power digraphs modulo n" (DML)

Source: http://hdl.handle.net/10338.dmlcz/141608 · full text: [[ahmad-husnine-power-digraphs-modulo-n.full]]

## What it establishes

Power digraphs G(n,k) — digraphs with vertex set Z_n = {0..n−1} and edges
(a,b) where a^k ≡ b (mod n). The paper finds conditions for the digraph to have
an isolated fixed point or exactly two components, and discusses Fermat-number
primality. Nothing about the multiplicative order of 2, out-shuffles, or the
lcm decomposition of orders.

## Consequence for this problem

**Does not help.** The search abstract suggested it states
`ord_n(a) = lcm(ord_m(a), ord_l(a))` for coprime factors, but the downloaded
full text does **not** contain that statement. Do not cite this as the source
for the CRT/lcm order decomposition. The correct sources for that are Naor
(Princeton group-theory notes, direct-product order = lcm) and Chappelon
Prop 5.
