# Kiriu et al., "Some notes about power residues modulo prime"

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02751 · full text: [[kiriu-power-residues-modulo-prime.full]]

## What it establishes

Classifies odd primes p ≠ q for which x^2 ≡ q (mod p) has a solution, via a
subgroup L_{4q} of U_{4q} (modulo 4q); and asks whether the Z[√2] norm criterion
(x^2 ≡ 2 solvable) extends to Z[ⁿ√2] for n≥2, solving weak versions.

## Consequences for this problem

**Does not help.** The problem needs the multiplicative order of 2 mod n−1 to
be exactly 60, and the CRT/lcm/prime-power decomposition of that order. This
paper is about quadratic residues and norm equations in cyclotomic/radical
extensions — a different question with no bearing on out-shuffle orders or the
enumeration over divisors of 2^60−1. Nothing here bounds, computes, or rules out
anything this run needs.
