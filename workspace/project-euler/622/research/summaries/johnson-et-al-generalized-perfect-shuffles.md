# Johnson et al. — "Generalized perfect shuffles" (MSP, 2021)

Source: https://msp.org/involve/2021/14-5/p04.xhtml · full text: [[johnson-et-al-generalized-perfect-shuffles.full]]

## What it establishes

Johnson, Manny, Van Cott, Zhang, *Involve* 14(5):745–770, 2021. Extends Diaconis–Graham–Kantor's 1983 group-theoretic study of the shuffles from decks split into **two** stacks to **generalized perfect shuffles** splitting m·k cards into m stacks (an in_m-shuffle and out_m-shuffle). Main result: the permutation group generated by these shuffles (together with the m^y-shuffles) is completely determined by k/gcd(y,k) and the parity of y/gcd(y,k), and is **independent of m**.

## Consequences for this problem

Not needed. PE622 is the ordinary two-stack in/out shuffle on an even deck, which the library already has nailed down (Diaconis–Graham–Kantor Lemma 1: out-shuffle order = ord_{n−1}(2); substantiated by Packard, OEIS A002326, Susam, MathWorld). This source generalises to multi-stack decks and derives *group structure* (not the single-shuffle recycle order), with none of it bearing on s(n) = ord_{n−1}(2) or the ord_m(2)=60 sum. The abstract confirms the two-stack case is Diaconis et al. 1983, which is already the library's anchor.
