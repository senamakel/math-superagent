# Wikipedia — Multiplicative order

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplicative_order · full text: [[wikipedia-multiplicative-order.full]]

## What it establishes

Definition: the multiplicative order of a mod n (a coprime to n) is the
smallest positive k with a^k ≡ 1 (mod n), i.e. the order of a in the unit group
(Z/nZ)^×. Properties: ord_n(a) | φ(n); the exact-order minimality.
