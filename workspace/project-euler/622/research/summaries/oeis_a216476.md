# A216476 — Number of iterations of the "Oware" operation

Source: https://oeis.org/A216476 · full text: (OEIS page, self-contained) [[oeis_a216476]]

## What it establishes

A216476(n) = the number of iterations of the "Oware" (mancala-like) digit
redistribution operation until the initial position n (including the pivot
digit) is reproduced. The listed terms (1,1,1,...,4,3,6,10,12,4,8,...) happen
to share early values with A002326 by coincidence of small numbers.

## Consequences for this problem

**Does not help.** This is a digit-iteration game about place-value
redistribution, completely unrelated to the multiplicative order of 2 mod n−1
or the out-shuffle. The superficial overlap of early terms with A002326
(e.g. 3,6,10,12,4,8) is a coincidence and must not be mistaken for the shuffle
order. Do not use or re-read for PE622.

## Status

Irrelevant to PE622. Recorded as a dead end so nobody is misled by the early
term overlap.
