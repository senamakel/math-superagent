## What it establishes

This file is the **same paper** as
`diaconis-graham-kantor-perfect-shuffles.md` (identical abstract, Lemma 1, and
main theorem; only the file naming "Crossing the River" differs — the paper's
actual content is "The Mathematics of Perfect Shuffles", Adv. Appl. Math. 4,
1983, hosted at two URLs). Lemma 1 is identical:
order of out-shuffle = ord of 2 mod 2n−1; order of in-shuffle = ord of 2
mod 2n+1.
