## What it is exactly

a(n) counts the moduli m (including m = 1) whose multiplicative order of 2 is
exactly n:  a(n) = |{ m >= 1 : ord_m(2) = n }|.

## Closed form (the point)

- a(n) = Sum_{d|n} mu(n/d) * tau(2^d - 1)   (Alekseyev 2022; program by Heinz,
  Maple `add(mobius(n/d)*tau(2^d-1), d=divisors(n))`).

**This is exactly this run's C(k) identity** — C(k) = Σ_{d|k} μ(k/d)(τ(2^d−1)−1) —
up to the m=1 term: A059499 counts m=1 too, so A059499(1)=1 while the run's
C(1)=0 (the −1 inside the τ-term excludes m=1). For every k >= 2 the two agree
exactly.

## Holds here

- The count C(k) in this run is over odd m > 1 with ord_m(2) = k. Σ_{d|k}
  μ(k/d)(τ(2^d−1)−1) is a finite sum (divisors of k), needing only the Möbius
  function and divisor counts, both elementary. The core bijection is
  ord_m(2) | d  ⟺  m | 2^d − 1  (Conrad Thm 2.1), valid for gcd(m,2)=1 — true
  here since m = n−1 is odd.

## Independent confirmation of the answer

A059499(60) = 4456. This run's C(60) (the count of moduli m with ord_m(2)=60,
i.e. the number of valid deck sizes reduced by one) is **4456** by three
independent routes (Möbius inversion, direct divisor inclusion-exclusion, and
brute divisor enumeration — see `code/pe622/`). The match at n=60 with the
catalogued A059499 term independently confirms the count half of the answer.
