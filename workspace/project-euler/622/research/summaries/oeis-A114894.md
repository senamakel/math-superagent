# OEIS A114894 — minimal k such that a 2k deck returns after n out-shuffles

Source: https://oeis.org/A114894 · full text: (summary source is self-contained) [[oeis-A114894]]

## What it establishes

a(n) = the minimal k such that a deck of 2k cards is returned to its original
state by n out-shuffles. This is the *least* 2k-deck with s(2k) | n, i.e. the
smallest m = 2k−1 = n−1 relation inverted: it is the minimal k with
n | ord_{2k−1}(2) (equivalently 2k−1 | 2^n−1 analyzed by least k).

## Consequences for this problem

Gives the *smallest* even n with s(n)=60 as a cross-check: A114894(60) would be
the minimal k. Since it is "least k", it does not enumerate all n, so it cannot
directly give the sum — but it confirms the small end of the range and that the
problem is about divisors of 2^n−1. It is the inverse-direction sequence; the
sum needs the full set of n, not the least.

## Does not settle

The sum (needs all n with s(n)=60, not the minimum).

## Status

Catalogued (OEIS term list). Hypothesis (2k deck, out-shuffle) holds. Useful as
a cross-check of the smallest term, not the total.
