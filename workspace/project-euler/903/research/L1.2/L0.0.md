# Seal — L0.0: powers' descents/inversions, conjugacy-class inversion stats, factoradic rank, cycle-type toolkit, homomesy

Context this batch serves: the run has reduced Q(n) exactly (verify_red.py) to
Q(n) = (n!)² + A_n·(n!−1) + (B_n/2)·T(n), T(n)=Σ_{m=1}^{n-1}m(m−1)m!, from
empirically proved gap-affine pair-inversion counts f_n(k)=A_n+(k−1)B_n
(translation-invariant, exactly arithmetic in the gap k, verified n≤11). The
open step is closed forms for A_n,B_n. These ten notes supply the per-gap
inversion MECHANISM and the SUMMATION TOOLS, and two clean negatives — but NONE
of them computes the sum of rank over the cyclic subgroup {π^i}, the genuinely
novel core of the problem. Full texts live under L0.0/; curated one-line
summaries under L1.0/.

## 1. Cambie & Yan, Descents and inversions in powers of permutations
[[../L0.0/cambie_yan_html.full.md]] (arXiv:2408.01211, full text) and its abs
page [[../L0.0/cambie_yan_descents_inversions_powers.full.md]]. Curated:
[[L1.0/cambie_yan_html]].
Uniform π∈S_n, fixed k∈ℤ⁺, valid n ≥ 2k+1. τ(k)=#divisors, σ(k)=Σ_{d|k}d,
τ_o(k)=#odd divisors of k.
- Thm 1.1: E[des(π^k)] = (n−1)/2 − [τ(k)²−τ(k)−τ_o(k)+σ(k)]/(2n).
- Thm 1.2: E[inv(π^k)] = n(n−1)/4 − (τ(k)−1)n/6 − [τ(k)²−τ(k)−τ_o(k)+σ(k)]/12.
- k=2,3 descents = (n−1)/2 − 2/n, confirming Archer–Geary's conjecture (see §2).
- Thms 1.3–1.5: Grassmannian-power counts via Gessel–Reutenauer.
**Meaning for this run (hypothesis n≥2k+1):** the proof (Lemmas 2.1–2.6) shows
that, for FIXED exponent t with n≥2t+1, the count of π with a given pair-inversion
at gap d in π^t is translation-invariant in position and AFFINE in d — this is the
per-exponent, PROVED source of the run's empirical gap-linearity of f_n(k).
**Hypotheses hold here?** Only for exponents t ≤ (n−1)/2. The full rank sum runs
i=1..n! including huge exponents, where π^t is periodic with small period ord(π).
**Bound this lets us make:** each per-exponent slope a_t and intercept enter the
gap-linearity only in the small-exponent regime; the large-exponent regime is the
open part. **Crucial negative (records a disagreement risk, not a contradiction):**
naively summing the per-exponent slopes over t=1..n! for n=3 gives 32, but the true
B_3 = 1 — large exponents behave entirely differently and must be handled via the
period formula, not by extrapolating Thm 1.2. This source gives NO formula for
rank(π^i) or its sum over {<π>}.

## 2. Archer & Geary, Descents in powers of permutations
[[../L0.0/archer_geary_descents_powers.full.md]] (arXiv:2406.09369). Curated:
[[L1.0/archer_geary_descents_powers]].
Enumeration, for special classes, of # of π with π² (or π³) having prescribed
descent counts: Grassmannian (one-descent) π by desc(π²); all π with exactly one
descent in π² (small descents fully, general lower bound); max-descent counts for
square/cube. Poses conjecture E[des(π²)]=E[des(π³)]=(n−1)/2−2/n (small n excluded).
**Role:** provenance and counting-technique source for the descent-in-powers line
that Cambie–Yan confirmed and closed. Contains NO closed form for the cyclic-subgroup
rank sum; does not touch the large-exponent regime. **Hypotheses:** none needed; the
conjecture is now a theorem (CY k=2,3). Supporting/context only.

## 3. Campion-Loth, Levet, Liu, Stucky, Sundaram, Yin — conjugacy-class stats
[[../L0.0/conjugacy_class_statistics.full.md]] (arXiv:2301.00898, abs) and
[[../L0.0/conjugacy_class_statistics_body.full.md]] (full text). Curated:
[[L1.0/conjugacy_class_statistics_body]].
Distribution of permutation statistics on a SINGLE conjugacy class C_λ, elementary
bijective proofs (no character theory).
**Lemma 4.7 (inversion indicator), the proved core:** for 1≤i<j≤n, over C_λ
  Prλ[I_{i,j}=1] = ½ + a_2/[n(n−1)] − a_1(a_1−1)/[2n(n−1)]
     + (j−i−1)·[n − n·a_1 − a_1 + a_1² − 2a_2] / [n(n−1)(n−2)],
where a_1=#fixed points, a_2=#2-cycles of the class. Depends ONLY on n, a_1, a_2 and
the gap d=j−i — NOT on absolute positions (translation invariance) — and is AFFINE
in d. This is the *proved* analogue of the run's f_n(k)=A_n+(k−1)B_n.
**Thm 4.8:** any X=Σ_{i<j}wt(i,j)·I_{i,j} (des, maj, inv) has Eλ[X]=C_1(n,a_1,a_2)·α_n(X)
+C_2(n,a_1,a_2)·β_n(X) with α_n=Σwt, β_n=Σ(j−i−1)wt, both λ-independent; the first
moment of ANY weighted inversion statistic depends only on n,a_1,a_2. E.g.
Eλ[inv]=(3n²−n+2a_2−a_1²+a_1−2n·a_1)/12.
**Higher moments (Thms 1.3,1.5,7.16,7.26, Prop 7.28):** Eλ[X^k] independent of λ when
every part of λ ≥ mk+1 (m=constraint-set size); for symmetric stats each is a poly in
n of degree ≤ mk; Eλ[inv^k] is degree-2k with leading coeff 4^{−k}; class↔S_n averages
via centralizer identity (eq 6.1).
**Meaning:** a concrete summation route to closed forms for A_n,B_n — weight the
per-gap affine probability over cycle types with class sizes n!/∏(k^{a_k}a_k!) and
Ford's factorial moments. **Hypotheses hold:** translation-invariance and
gap-affineness are proved for a single fixed permutation's conjugacy class. **Caveat:**
per-fixed-permutation only; does NOT give Σ over the cyclic subgroup {π^i} — the
unresolved core. Complements Cambie–Yan from the other direction (base-π cycle type
vs exponent).

## 4. Factorial number system (Wikipedia)
[[../L0.0/factorial_number_system_wiki.full.md]]. Curated: [[L1.0/factorial_number_system_wiki]].
Factoradic is mixed-radix (i-th digit from right has base i, place value (i−1)!,
0≤digit<i). Reading digits as a Lehmer/right-inversion code bijects 0..n!−1 onto S_n
in lexicographic order — the factoradic IS the 0-based lex rank;
rank0(σ)=Σ_{i=1..n} c_i(σ)(n−i)! with c_i=right-inversion counts. This is the basis
of rank-as-Lehmer-digits-in-factorial-base (already fact (1) of report_cited_facts)
and of Σ_{σ∈S_n}rank(σ)=n!(n!+1)/2. **No structural identity over cyclic subgroups;**
no shortcut for Σ_{τ∈<π>}rank(τ). Confirms an existing cited belief; no new route.
**Verdict:** basis, not a method.

## 5. Ford, Cycle type of random permutations: A toolkit
[[../L0.0/ford_cycle_type_toolkit.full.md]] (Discrete Analysis 2022:9, DOI
10.19086/da.38090, arXiv:2104.12019). Curated: [[L1.0/ford_cycle_type_toolkit]].
Standard reference for cycle type of uniform σ∈S_n: k-cycles behave like independent
Poisson(1/k) under constraint Σk·C_k=n.
**Exact factorial moments:** E[∏_k (C_k)_{r_k}] = ∏_k k^{−r_k} whenever Σ_k k·r_k ≤ n
((x)_r falling factorial). The calculus that collapses cycle-type sums. Fixed points
C_1→Poisson(1) with explicit error; cycles in arbitrary length-sets I: sieve bounds
and Poisson/CLT when Σ_{k∈I}1/k grows; largest/smallest cycle; fixed-set sizes.
**Meaning:** the summation engine for the cycle-type sums behind A_n,B_n — both
candidate routes (Campion-Loth per-conjugacy-class affine prob + class sizes, and
Pinsky–Schickentanz fixed-point conditioning from L0.2) are cycle-type sums, and this
is the toolkit for evaluating them exactly and replacing with Poisson asymptotics at
n=10⁶. Its bibliography also maps the order-of-permutation literature governing the
n!/ord(π) weights. **Hypotheses:** exact moment identity is unconditional (size
constraint only). **Caveat:** tooling/reference only — no powers π^k, cyclic
subgroups, inversion probabilities, or rank stats; exactness at n=10⁶ still needs the
run's own summation.

## 6. Elder, Lafrenière, McNicholas, Striker, Welch — Homomesies on permutations
[[../L0.0/homomesies_permutations_arxiv.full.md]] (arXiv abs page, 2206.13409; Math
Comp 93 (2024) 921–976, DOI 10.1090/mcom/3866) and
[[../L0.0/homomesies_permutations_arxiv.md]]. Curated: [[L1.0/homomesies_permutations]].
**Homomesy:** statistic X is homomesic under map φ when its average is the SAME on
every φ-orbit (then orbit average = global average). 122–128 instances on S_n; maps:
Lehmer-code rotation, reverse, complement, Foata, Kreweras.
**Thm 4.8:** under Lehmer-code rotation every orbit has size lcm(1..n).
**Thm 4.7:** 45 inversion-linked statistics are homomesic under rotation.
**Meaning for this run (conceptual framework):** the open core is averaging
rank=Lehmer-weighted-sum over the cyclic subgroup {π^i} — an orbit-average question,
and homomesy is exactly the phenomenon that would collapse it to the global average.
**Hypotheses DO NOT hold here:** the map is Lehmer-code rotation, NOT the
permutation-power/cyclic-subgroup action; and rank itself is not among the shown
homomesic statistics (only individual inversion-related ones). So this sharpens but
does not close the core. It phrases the question, supplies the test (is our
orbit-average homomesic?), not a formula.

## 9. What this batch lets the run compute, bound, or rule out
- **Compute (route, not closed form):** A_n,B_n as cycle-type sums — per-conjugacy-class
  affine pair-inversion probability (Campion-Loth Lemma 4.7) weighted by class sizes

- **Rule out:** homomesy under Lehmer-code ROTATION cannot be imported to the

  Gaetz–Ryba file is worthless (misdownload); OEIS negatives elsewhere confirm A_n,B_n
