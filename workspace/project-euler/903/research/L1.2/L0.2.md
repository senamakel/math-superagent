# Seal — L0.2: mechanism/route sources for the open A_n, B_n in PE 903

This batch holds the four posterior-distribution / power-side / per-gap sources
that underpin the run's central reduction
`Q(n) = (n!)² + A_n(n!−1) + (B_n/2)T(n)`, `T(n)=Σ_{m=1}^{n-1}m(m−1)m!`, derived
from the proved gap-affine pair-inversion counts `f_n(k)=A_n+(k−1)B_n` (sources
and all numerical tables in MEMORY.md / verify_red.py). **No source in this batch
computes the rank-sum over the cyclic subgroup {π^i}** — that remains the run's
genuinely novel core. All four are mechanism / route / weight-bounding sources
leading toward closed forms for A_n and B_n. Everything below is sourced.

## 1. Courtois–Bard–Ault — cycle structure of POWERS π^k (*the* power-side mechanism)
[[courtois_bard_ault_ppowers]] · [[courtois_bard_ault_ppowers_body]] ·
full text at [[../L0.3/courtois_bard_ault_ppowers_body.full]] (the L0.2
`courtois_bard_ault_ppowers.full.md` is only the arXiv abstract page and is
superseded; `courtois_bard_ault_ppowers.md` is its front-matter stub).
arXiv:0905.3682 [math.CO], Cryptologia 36 (2012) 240–262; https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.3682.

Analytic combinatorics (Weak/Strong Cycle Structure Theorem: perms with cycles in a
set A have EGF `exp(Σ_{i∈A} z^i/i)`, derivatives of double EGFs give means). New
relative to the rest of the library: a rigorous treatment of *iterates* π^k.

- **Fixed points of a power (Thm 3.11/Cor 3.13):** x fixed by π^k iff x lies in a
  π-cycle of length i dividing k. So #fixed(π^k) = number of points in π-cycles whose
  length divides k.
- **Expected #fixed(π^k) (Thm 3.14):** → τ(k) (divisor function) as n→∞. A
  divisor-function statement complementing Nathanson's fixed-points↔cycle-count
  Möbius identity (see L0.1 seal).
- **Cycle splitting (§3.1):** raising to the k-th power replaces a cycle of length i
  by `gcd(i,k)` cycles each of length `i/gcd(i,k)`. Odd cycles stay whole; even
  (length 2c) cycles split into two (odds/evens). Fixed points of π^k come exactly
  from the divisor-length cycles.
- **Fixed-point-count distribution of π^k (Thm 5.1):** PGF-EGF
  `exp(Σ_{i|k}(y^i−1)/i)`; derangement probability of π^k (App A) = `e^{−σ(k)/k}`
  (σ = sum-of-divisors).
- Applications (distinguishing attacks on ~10^6-fold iterates, τ(10^6)=49,
  highly-composite exponents) are cryptanalytic, not relevant to Q(n).

**Why it matters here:** the run's f_n(k) counts pair-inversions of the *random
power* σ=π^i. How two points of σ sit (fixed / swapped / ordered) is governed
precisely by the gcd/lcm of their π-cycle lengths and divisibility of the exponent.
This paper supplies the power-side splitting facts the conjugacy-class/Ewens sources
treat only for the base permutation π. **It is mechanism, not a Q(n) computation.**

## 2. Pinsky–Schickentanz — Ewens pair-inversion, gap-affine (second independent proof)
[[pinsky_schickentanz_ewens_html]] (curated summary; sup-unpubl. note
`pinsky_schickentanz_ewens_inversions.md` is the superseded abstract-page record) ·
full text at [[pinsky_schickentanz_ewens_html.full]] (102 KB) and the abstract page
at `pinsky_schickentanz_ewens_inversions.full.md`.
arXiv:2510.20654v2 [math.PR/math.CO]; v1 23 Oct 2025, v2 17 Nov 2025 (v2 added the
fixed-point-conditioning part).

Exact formulas under Ewens sampling `P_θ^(n)` ∝ θ^{#cycles} on S_n (uniform = θ=1):

- **Unconditioned pair-inversion probability (Thm 1a, eq 1.1):**
  `P_θ^(n)((i,j) inverted) = n(n−2(j−i)+1)/[2(θ+n−1)] − (n−1)(n−2(j−i))/[2(θ+n−2)]`.
  Depends on the pair only through the gap `j−i` — translation-invariant and
  **affine in the gap**, the same structural feature as `f_n(k)=A_n+(k−1)B_n`.
  θ=1 → 1/2 (uniform: every pair equally likely inverted); θ=0 (single cycle) →
  `1/2 + (j−i−1)/[(n−1)(n−2)]`.
- **Fixed-point-conditioned (Prop 10a, eq 3.2):** exact closed form for
  `P_θ^(n)((i,j) inv | #fixed = m)`, a five-term combination of ratios
  `P_θ^(n−2)(#fixed = m−1,m,m+1,m+2)/P_θ^(n)(#fixed = m)`, each term affine in the gap.
- **Fixed-point count (Prop 4):**
  `P_θ^(n)(#fixed=m) = [n!θ^m/(m!θ^{(n)})]Σ_{k=0}^{n−m}(−θ)^k θ^{(n−m−k)}/(k!(n−m−k)!)`,
  θ^{(r)} the rising factorial — the D_{n,m} probabilities needed to evaluate Prop 10a.
- **Asymptotics (Thm 5,8,9):** conditioned pair prob → `1/2 − (m−1)(j−i)/n² −
  (m²−3m−θ+2)/(2n²) + O(n⁻³)` (leading term depends only on m, not θ); θ→∞ limits
  parity-only; θ→0 depends only on whether m=n−2. Expected #inversions decreasing in
  θ ∀ n, convex in θ for n∉{3,4}.

**Why it matters:** the exact fixed-point-conditioned formula (Prop 10a, θ=1) is a
per-gap, per-m closed form directly usable to sum over cycle types — an alternative,
independent route to A_n and B_n. The θ=0 form gives the exact small-exponent
inversion structure f_n aggregates. It **proves** translation-invariance and
gap-affineness (not just empirically). Agreement recorded: this is a fully independent
derivation of the same mechanism as Campion-Loth et al (L0.0), which is valuable —
two independent proofs of the affine pairwise mechanism underlying f_n(k).

## 3. Sack–Úlfarsson — gap-resolved (k-step) inversion statistics
[[sack_ulfarsson_refined_inversion_statistics]] · [[sack_ulfarsson_refined_inversion_pdf]] ·
full PDF text at `sack_ulfarsson_refined_inversion_pdf.full.md` (58 KB).
arXiv:1106.1995 [math.CO], DOI 10.48550/arXiv.1106.1995.
(Note the redundancy in the batch: `sack_ulfarsson_refined_inversion_statistics.full.md`
is a 449-byte stub pointing to the PDF text, not the paper itself.)

- **k-step inversion** = inversion (a,b) with b−a=k (Def 4.1); `inv(π)=Σ_k inv_k(π)`.
- **k-step inversion distribution (Thm 4.4):**
  `H_{n,k}(x) = I(n,k,0)·A_s(x)^t·A_{s−1}(x)^{k−t}` with Eulerian polynomials A,
  `s=⌊n/k⌋+1`, `t=n mod k`, I a binomial product. Closed distribution of inversions
  at a single fixed gap k — the direct analytic handle on the gap function f_n(k).
- **Non-inversion sum identity (Thm 2.5):**
  `1·π = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 − invsum(π)`; **Cor 2.6** `ninvsum(π∘ρ)=π·ρ^{−1}−1·1^c` —
  a trace-like identity for composed powers.
- **Zone-crossing vectors (Def 3.1, Prop 3.3, Lemma 3.4):** `z_k=#{(a,b): a≤k<b}`
  uniquely determine π, and ninvsum = sum of coordinates; **Thm 3.8** recurrence for
  the ninvsum distribution N_n(q) via inserting n+1 (Lemma 3.6).
- (k1,k2)-step and (≤k)-step inversions (Prop 4.6, 4.8).

**Why it matters:** the k-step inversion distribution *is* the gap-resolved inversion
structure encoded by `f_n(k)=A_n+(k−1)B_n`; supplies Eulerian/zone-crossing generating
machinery for the per-gap counts. **Single permutation π only**, never the cyclic
subgroup {π^i}. Mechanism/route, not the answer.

## 4. Stong — average order of a permutation (weights in the period formula)
[[stong_average_order_permutation]] · abstract page `stong_average_order_permutation.full.md`
and PDF link stub `stong_average_order_permutation.pdf.full.md`.
EJC 5 (1998) #R41, DOI 10.37236/1379; https://doi.org/10.37236/1379.

Let `μ_n = (1/n!)Σ_{σ∈S_n} ord(σ)`. Refining Erdős–Turán / Schmutz / Goh–Schmutz,
Stong proves `log μ_n = C√(n/log n) + O(√n·loglog n / log n)`, C ≈ 2.99047. Background
Erdős–Turán law: for uniform σ, `log ord(σ)` is asymptotically normal with mean
`(log 2)n/2`, var `(log 3)n/3` — the *average* order is dominated by rare very-high-order
perms.

**Why it matters:** the period-mean weight in brute2 is `n!/ord(π)` summed over π;
ord(π) typically ≍ exp((log 2)n/2) so bulk errors decay ~ n!/e^{((log2)/2)n}. Bounds the
order-domain but gives no closed form for A_n, B_n and does not compute Q(10^6).

## Cross-source reading (what the batch does and does not settle)
- **Two fully independent proofs of the same per-gap affine mechanism** (`f_n(k)=A_n+(k−1)B_n`):
  conjugacy-class (Campion-Loth, L0.0) and Ewens (Pinsky–Schickentanz, this batch). A real
  corroboration, not a restatement.
- **Power-side structure** (Courtois–Bard–Ault): what the random power σ=π^i looks like at the
  cycle level (τ(k) fixed points, gcd-splitting, fixed-point EGF) — the missing link between
  the base-π conjugacy/Ewens sources and the empirical f_n.
- **Gap machinery** (Sack–Úlfarsson) and **weight bounds** (Stong) supply tools and
  asymptotics but neither closes A_n, B_n.
- **No source addresses the cyclic-subgroup rank sum Σ_i rank(π^i).** Everyone who needs the
  answer must still derive the A_n, B_n closed forms (the concrete summation route: Prop 10a
  θ=1 + Ford factorial moments; see report_A_n_B_n_closed_forms_sources at L2.0).
- **Hypothesis caveat to remember:** the Cambie–Yan small-exponent closed forms (L0.0/L1.0)
  require n≥2k+1; the large-exponent regime (k>(n−1)/2) is not covered by that theorem, and
  f_n averages over all powers up to n!.
