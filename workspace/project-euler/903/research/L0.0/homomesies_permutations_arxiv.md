# Homomesies on permutations (arXiv:2206.13409) — summary

Full text: `research/L0/homomesies_permutations_arxiv.full.md` (arXiv abs page; abstract only — the PDF is at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.13409, not captured here). Indexed summary: [[../L1/homomesies_permutations]].

Elder, Lafrenière, McNicholas, Striker, Welch. Math. Comp. 93 (2024) 921–976, DOI 10.1090/mcom/3866. Proves 128 instances of **homomesy** (statistic average constant on each orbit of a map) on S_n. Maps: Lehmer-code rotation (a cyclic shift of factoradic digits), reverse, complement, Foata, Kreweras. Under Lehmer-code rotation every orbit has size lcm(1..n) (Thm 4.8) and 45 inversion-linked statistics are homomesic (Thm 4.7), so their orbit average equals the global average.

Relevance to Q(n): the run's open core is averaging the Lehmer/rank statistic over the cyclic subgroup {π^i}. Homomesy is the phenomenon that would make such an orbit-average equal the global average. It is only a route, not the solution: the map here is Lehmer-code rotation, not permutation-power/cyclic-subgroup action, and rank itself is not among the homomesic statistics.
