# Cambie & Yan, "Descents and inversions in powers of permutations" (arXiv:2408.01211) — arXiv abstract page

Superseded listing. Every substantive claim lives in [[cambie_yan_html]] (summary) and L0 `cambie_yan_html.full.md` (full text). This page only records bibliographic data:

- Authors: Stijn Cambie (KU Leuven), Jun Yan (Warwick). math.CO, MSC 05A05.
- v1 2 Aug 2024, v2 23 Aug 2024; DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2408.01211.
- Abstract: explicit formulas for E[des(π^k)] and E[inv(π^k)] over uniform π ∈ S_n for all k ∈ ℤ⁺ with n ≥ 2k+1, in terms of τ(k), σ(k), τ_o(k); confirms Archer–Geary conjectures; plus Grassmannian-power counts.

See [[cambie_yan_html]] for Theorem 1.1/1.2 statements and the caveat that matters for this run: the n ≥ 2k+1 hypothesis excludes exponents t > (n−1)/2 that appear in the full Q(n) sum over i = 1..n!, so these fixed-k closed forms do not directly give A_n, B_n.
