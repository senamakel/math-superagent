# Sack & Úlfarsson, *Refined inversion statistics on permutations*

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1995 (arXiv:1106.1995v2, math.CO, 12 Jan 2012).
Authors: Joshua Sack, Henning Úlfarsson.

Gap-refined inversion statistics on permutations. Relevant to PE 903:
- k-step inversions (inversions (a,b) with b−a=k): distribution H_{n,k} = product of
  Eulerian polynomials with binomial interleaving factor (Thm 4.4).
- Non-inversion sum ninvsum(π)=Σ(b−a) over non-inversions; identity
  1·π = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 − invsum(π) (Thm 2.5); product identity (Cor 2.6).
- Zone-crossing vectors uniquely determine π (Prop 3.3); recurrence for ninvsum
  distribution (Thm 3.8).
- (k1,k2)-step and (≤k)-step inversions (Prop 4.6, 4.8).

Full text (PDF conversion): [[sack_ulfarsson_refined_inversion_pdf.full]].
Note: covers single permutations only — NOT the cyclic subgroup {π^i} of PE 903.
