Decimal expansion of sqrt(3)/4.

OFFSET

0,1

COMMENTS

Area of equilateral triangle of side 1.

Quadratic number with denominator 4 and minimal polynomial 16x^2 - 3. - [Charles R Greathouse IV][12], Jun 30 2021

With offset 1, surface area of a pentagonal bipyramid (Johnson solid J_13) with unit edges. - [Paolo Xausa][13], Aug 04 2025

A lower bound for Bloch constant (see [A085508][14]) found by Ahlfors (1938). - [Amiram Eldar][15], Jul 25 2026
