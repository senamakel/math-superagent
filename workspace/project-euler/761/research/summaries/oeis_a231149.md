# A231149 — Greatest integer k such that n+1 + ... + n+k <= 1 + ... + n.

## Formula

- a(n) = floor((-2*n - 1 + sqrt(8*n^2 + 8*n + 1)/2)).

## Comments

- a(n) is the greatest k such that 2*p(n) >= p(n+k), where p(n) is the n-th partial sum of the sequence c(n) = n.
