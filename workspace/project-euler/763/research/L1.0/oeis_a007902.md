## Formula / asymptotic

a(n) ~ c * d^n, d=2.321642199494..., c=0.122687073421... (Knessl 2006).

## Exact recurrence (Alois P. Heinz, OEIS) — two-index DP, the seed to lift to 3D

G(k,m): k<1 -> 0
  m=0 -> 2*G(k-1,0)+G(k,1)+(k==2 ? 1:0)
  m=1 -> G(k-3,0)+2*G(k-2,1)+G(k-1,2)+G(k-4,1)
  m>=2-> G(k-m-2,m-1)+2*G(k-m-1,m)+G(k-m,m+1)
a(1)=1; a(n)=G(n,0) for n>=2.

Important: NO simple one-index (constant-coefficient or holonomic) recurrence
exists for A007902 — verified.  This is why D(N) for PE763 (d=3) has no small
closed form and needs a real transfer procedure.
