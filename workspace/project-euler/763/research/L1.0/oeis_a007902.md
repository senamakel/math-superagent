# A007902 — Number of pebbling configurations with n pebbles

Source: https://oeis.org/A007902 (OEIS entry, plain text)

## What the sequence is

1,1,2,4,9,20,46,105,243,561,1301,3014,6995,...
Counts graph-pebbling configurations on n pebbles (a graph-theoretic
game/decision sequence, cf. A007901). Asymptotic growth a(n) ~ c*d^n with
d ≈ 2.3216 (Knessl 2006). Addressed by a memoized recurrence over
(p, m) "pebbling" states, not a simple closed form.

## Bearing on D(N)

D(N) = 1,1,3,9,30,99,336,... . A007902 = 1,1,2,4,9,20,... diverges from
D(N) at n=2: a(2)=2 vs D(2)=3. It agrees only through n=1. Not D(N), and
its underlying object (graph pebbling) is unrelated to the 3D amoeba
division rule. Ruled-out hypothesis; the growth constant d≈2.32 is also
far from D(N)'s observed ×~3.4 frontier factor, so even the regime is
different. Do not revisit.
