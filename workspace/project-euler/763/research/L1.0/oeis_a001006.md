# A001006 — Motzkin numbers

Source: https://oeis.org/A001006 (OEIS entry, plain text)

## What the sequence is

The Motzkin numbers: 1,1,2,4,9,21,51,127,323,835,2188,5798,...
Closed forms: g.f. A(x) = (1 - x - sqrt(1-2x-3x^2))/(2x^2);
D-finite recurrence (n+2)a(n) = (2n+1)a(n-1) + (3n-3)a(n-2);
a(n) = Sum_k C(n,2k)*Catalan(k). Many combinatorial interpretations
(chords on a circle, Motzkin paths, Dyck paths with no UUU, etc.).

## Bearing on the amoeba sequence D(N)

D(N) = 1,1,3,9,30,99,336,1134,... (BFS-verified to N=14). Motzkin
diverges from D(N) already at n=2: Motzkin a(2)=2, D(2)=3. Only the first
two terms agree. So **D(N) is not the Motzkin sequence** and no Motzkin
closed form applies.

The single closed form/recurrence that would have turned D(N) into an
evaluation does **not** govern it, because the tail terms do not match.
This is a ruled-out hypothesis, not a usable formula. Do not revisit a
Motzkin closed form for D(N) unless new data change the first terms.
