# A005207 — a(n) = (F(2n-1)+F(n+1))/2 (Fibonacci)

Source: https://oeis.org/A005207 (OEIS entry, plain text)

## What the sequence is

1,1,2,4,9,21,51,127,322,826,2135,...
a(n) = (F(2n-1) + F(n+1))/2 with F the Fibonacci numbers.
Linear recurrence a(n) = 4a(n-1) - 3a(n-2) - 2a(n-3) + a(n-4);
g.f. 1 - x(1-2x-x^2+x^3)/((x^2+x-1)(x^2-3x+1)).
Combinatorial: number of Motzkin n-paths of height <= 3; sums over
a tridiagonal matrix; counts of certain involutions.

## Bearing on D(N)

D(N) = 1,1,3,9,30,99,336,... diverges from A005207 at n=2: A005207 a(2)=2,
D(2)=3. Only the first two terms agree. **Not a candidate fit.** Its linear
recurrence with constant coefficients (exponential growth base ~2.6) shows
a different growth regime from D(N)'s observed ×~3.4 frontier factor, so even
the growth rate is unlike D(N)'s.

Closed form does not apply; this is a ruled-out hypothesis. Do not revisit.
