## Bearing on this run — dead end

A383891 (chunk length of prime-reciprocal partial sums) was filed during the
OEIS hunt for a catalogued match to the run's **distinct-level-histogram-count
sequence** H(N)=1,1,2,3,5,8,13,22,36,60,100,166,277 (N=2..14). Its terms
1,1,2,3,5,8,13,22,36,60,100,168,284,... look like A186085 early, and the OEIS
comment even flags it "looks like ... the 1-dimension sandpile sequence
(A186085)" — but it **diverges** at the 13th term (168 vs the run's 166, 284 vs
277). So A383891 is a coincidental sibling, **not** the histogram-count
sequence; the true match remains A186085. Dead end, kept only to stop a
re-search. No contradiction — the two sequences merely share a Fibonacci-like
head before diverging.
