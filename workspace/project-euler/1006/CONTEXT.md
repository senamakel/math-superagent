## Established

The finite words S_n converge in the prefix sense to the infinite Fibonacci word f fixed by the morphism 0→01, 1→0. The governing theory is Sturmian combinatorics: f is the characteristic Sturmian word of slope α=1/φ²=(3−√5)/2, and every Sturmian word has factor complexity p(k)=k+1. Thus the finite-subword union in the problem is exactly the set of length-k factors of f and has k+1 elements. Mechanical-word theory represents its digits by floor differences d_j(x)=⌊x+(j+1)a⌋−⌊x+ja⌋, equivalently by coding an irrational rotation; the factor set is obtained from the k+1 interval classes cut by the orbit points {−m·a}. The positional theorem of Mohankumar–Sivasankar–Rama Raghavan gives a compact list of those k+1 factors using first-occurrence windows.
