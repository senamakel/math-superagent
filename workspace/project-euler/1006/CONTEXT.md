# Governing theory

The finite words converge prefixwise to the fixed point f of the Fibonacci morphism 0→01, 1→0. The relevant theory is Sturmian word combinatorics: f is the characteristic Sturmian word of irrational slope α=1/φ²=(3−√5)/2. A Sturmian word has exactly k+1 distinct factors of every length k (Morse–Hedlund minimal-complexity theorem), so the union over finite S_n is precisely the k+1 factor set of f. Mechanical-word theory gives these factors as the k+1 binary codings of the orbit partition by {−mα mod 1}, m=0,…,k. This reduces factor generation to irrational-rotation/floor-difference formulas rather than searching finite words.

Sources: https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781107326019.003 (Lothaire, Sturmian words); https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2011.12.047 (Perrin–Restivo, Sturmian words); https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04304 (factor enumeration/location for Fibonacci words). Explicit factor-location formulas are also treated at https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2010.04.013.
