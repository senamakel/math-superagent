## 1 — from steer

Pair-correlation route. Verified outside the container against a brute oracle for k = F_n - 1 up to n=12; reproduce it here before building on it. Write the square of a factor value as a double sum over digit positions: Psi(k) = sum over j,jp of C(j,jp) * 10^(2k-2-j-jp), where C(j,jp) counts the length-k factors carrying a 1 at both positions j and jp. At k = F_n - 1 the k+1 factors are the F_n rotations of the standard word q_n truncated to k letters, so the sum over factors is a sum over rotations and C(j,jp) = A(jp-j), the CYCLIC autocorrelation of q_n, a function of the lag alone. A has an exact closed form: A(d) = max(0, m - t) + max(0, m - (N - t)) with N = F_n = length of q_n, m = number of ones in q_n, t = (d*m) mod N. Checked for every lag at n=3..12, and Psi(F_n - 1) = sum over j,jp of A(jp-j)*10^(2k-2-j-jp) reproduces the brute Psi exactly there. For a fixed lag the inner sum over j is geometric, so Psi collapses to ONE sum over lags of A(d) times an explicit geometric weight, and what is left is sum over d of (a*d mod N) * x^d mod M, a Euclidean/Ostrowski recursion costing O(log N), not 10^18 terms. Directive 2 gives a stronger form of this that removes the k = F_n - 1 restriction; read both before choosing.

## 2 — from steer

Stronger form of directive 1: it removes the k = F_n - 1 restriction and is exact in integer arithmetic. Verified outside the container for k = 3,5,8,10,13,17,21,26,34,40,55 against the brute oracle. Model the k+1 factors as a mechanical word: with slope a taken as the RATIONAL F(n-1)/F(n) for any F(n) much larger than k, cut the unit circle by the k+1 points frac(-m*a), m = 0..k, take the midpoint x of each of the k+1 arcs, and set digit j of that factor to floor(x + (j+1)a) - floor(x + j a). Checked: this reproduces the brute factor set exactly at every k above, non-Fibonacci k included, and rational a makes every quantity an exact integer computation. Now Abel-summate the value. With v(x) = sum over j of digit_j * 10^(k-1-j), telescoping gives v(x) = floor(x + k*a) - 10^(k-1)*floor(x) + 9 * sum over j = 1..k-1 of 10^(k-1-j) * floor(x + j*a). Checked exactly for every representative at every k above, and the sum of v(x)^2 over the k+1 representatives reproduces the brute Psi exactly. So Psi(k) is a SECOND MOMENT of a geometrically weighted floor sum, with no factor enumeration, no k -> k+1 stepping, and no Psi-period.
