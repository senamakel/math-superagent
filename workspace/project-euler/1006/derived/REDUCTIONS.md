## Banked — links established, chain not closed

Identities and algebraic steps this run has established on the way to a target. None of these is a result on its own; together they are the reduction. Read them before deriving one again.

- `pe1006-g4-diagonal-coupling` — Reduce J(a,k)=Σ_{m=0}^k v_a(m)^2 to a fixed-dimensional, O(log k) closed form by separating the digit-position and intercept coordinates.
  - parameter: the pair (h, m) with h=l−m (the diagonal coordinate of the double floor product) and m the rotation-intercept index; equivalently the diagonal-defect matrix C_k(i,j)−T(i−j).
  - lower: the per-intercept side is solved: a single floor sequence g(t)=⌊t·p/q⌋−[t=0] carries the whole word, and the universal-Euclidean monoid (monoid-composition-formulas-verified) closes its weighted moments in O(log max(p,q)).
  - upper: the joint side is blocked: the double sum does not factor over the two coordinates. Under h=l−m the decimal weight w_l=10^{k−1−l}=10^{k−1−h}·10^{−m} keeps a geometric factor 10^{−2m} on m, and the window range l∈[0,k] becomes h∈[−m,k−m], which moves with m.
  - gap: Two facts keep the two sides apart. (1) The coordinates do not separate: Ψ(k)=Σ_{j,l} w_j w_l C_k(j,l) with C_k(j,l)=Σ_{m=0}^k g(j−m)g(l−m), and a fixed-dimensional diagonal state would need C_k Toeplitz (a function of j−l only); the run's mechanically-checked refutation pe1006-pair-correlation-boundary shows this holds only at k=F_n−1. (2) Babichev–Shpakova closes one staircase with two per-intercept endpoint markers at L² marker-slot cost, i.e. per-intercept work = the O(k) this reduction exists to remove. Nothing yet proves a uniform O(1)-parameter encoding of all k+1 marker traces.
  - reason: Exact identity from code/mech/mech_psi.py formulation (B), verified against the mechanical oracle; the Toeplitz-only-at-k=F_n−1 fact is claim dir1-domain-autocorrelation, mechanically checked.
  - refs: research/approaches/pe1006-bivariate-floor-moment-diagonal.md, research/approaches/pe1006-pair-correlation-boundary.md, claim dir1-domain-autocorrelation, claim monoid-composition-formulas-verified
