## Exact regularities over supplied terms

These are finite checked statements, not global theorems:

- `d_j` has 1145 stored terms and agrees term-for-term with the stored OEIS A019587 comparison for `j=1..1145`.
- No exact homogeneous constant-coefficient recurrence of order at most 12 fits the supplied `d_j` terms. Berlekamp–Massey over modulus `101001001` returns complexity 573 on the 1145-term supplied prefix; this is a finite diagnostic, not a proof of non-C-finiteness.
- The supplied V-run starts (1146 records) equal `floor(j*phi^2)` for every stored `j=1..1146`; the 1145 supplied gaps are exactly 2 or 3, with counts 437 and 708 when indexed as gaps from start 2 through start 1146.
- The supplied tables still satisfy exactly: `count(k)=k+1` (`k=1..400`), `c1(k)=1+floor(k/phi^2)` (`k=1..400` in this rerun), and `Lmin(k)=k+NextFib_strict(k)-1` (`k=1..400`; independent workspace artifacts extend this to 6764/10000).
- The stored right-extension terms satisfy the recorded recurrence on their supplied range, and the stored Toeplitz-defect scan has maximum absolute defect 1 through `k=400`. Its zero-defect list is `[1,2,4,7,12,20,33,54,88,143,232,376]`, matching positive `F_n-1` values; the naive comparison including `F_2-1=0` is false solely because the scan starts at k=1.
- Exact low-modulus regularity: `Psi(k) mod 4 = c1(k) mod 4` on the stored tested range. The corresponding mod-8 conjecture is false on the supplied terms.

## Deliberate falsifiers / negative results

- Candidate `d_j` constant-coefficient recurrence: not supported; exact order <=12 search found none. BM complexity 573/1145 is evidence against a short recurrence, not a proof.
- Candidate general Toeplitz/lag-translation invariance: false. The first nonzero defect is reported at `k=6`; only Fibonacci-boundary lengths in the stored zero-defect list pass completely.
- Candidate `Psi mod 8 = c1`: false (the exact small-modulus tool reports a mismatch within `k=1..30`; first mismatch is k=2, since Psi(2)=101 ≡5 mod8 while c1(2)=1).
- Candidate `Psi mod 1000 = c1`: false at k=2 (`101` versus `1`).
- Candidate exact affine/Fibonacci-additive Psi laws: both fail at k=2 from supplied exact terms (`Psi(1)=1`, `Psi(2)=101`).
- Candidate `Psi` constant-coefficient recurrence: no order <=12 exact recurrence on the 25 supplied exact terms; BM complexity on 400 supplied residues is 200 modulo 101001001. This is a finite negative search only.
- The Wythoff script itself still terminates with an `mpf` formatting exception after printing its exact integer checks; its exact outputs are valid, but its density print is not used.
