## 摘要

Fibonacci words have many amazing combinatorial properties. Like numbers they are easy to define, and of their properties prove, once discovered. The aim this survey is sketch some the related factors (subwords) words, also describe basic arithmetic operations (i.e. normalization addition) in number system. No attempt was made be complete.
