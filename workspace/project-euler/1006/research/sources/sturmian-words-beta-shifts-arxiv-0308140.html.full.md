# Title: Sturmian words, $β$-shifts, and transcendence

Authors: [Dong Pyo Chi][2], [DoYong Kwon][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled Sturmian words, $\beta$-shifts, and transcendence, by Dong Pyo Chi and 1 other authors

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: Consider the minimal $\beta$-shift containing the shift space generated by given Sturmian word. In this paper we characterize such $\beta$ and investigate its combinatorial, dynamical and topological properties and prove that such $\beta$ are transcendental numbers.
