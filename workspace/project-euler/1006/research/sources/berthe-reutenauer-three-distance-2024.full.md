## Notes

1.

The word “symmetric” refers to the fact that the intervals are exchanged according to the central symmetry of the set \(\{1,2,\ldots ,\ell \}\), that is, the mapping \(h\mapsto \ell +1-h\).

2.

Two words are conjugate if they may be written *uw*and *wu*for some words *u*, *w*; for example, *acacbbc*and *cbbcaca*are conjugate.
