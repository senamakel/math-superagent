lothaire

**Algebraic Combinatorics on Words** |

This book covers developments on new topics in the domain of Combinatorics on Words. [Cambridge University Press][1]. A vailable since may, 2002.
ISBN: 0521812208

### Contents

1. ***[Contents and Preface][2]***
2. ***[Finite and Infinite Words][3]***( [Jean Berstel][4], [Dominique Perrin][5])
3. ***[Sturmian Words][6]***( [Jean Berstel][4], [Patrice S&eacute;&eacute;bold][7])
4. ***[Unavoidable Patterns][8]***( [Julien Cassaigne][9])
5. ***[Sesquipowers][10]***( [Aldo de Luca][11], [Stefano Varricchio][12])
6. ***[The Plactic Monoid][13]***( [Alain Lascoux][14], [Bernard Leclerc][15], [Jean-Yves Thibon][16])
7. ***[Codes][17]***( [V&eacute;ronique Bruy&egrave;re)][18]
8. ******[Numeration Systems][19] ( [Christiane Frougny][20])
9. ***[Periodicity][21]***( [Filippo Mignosi][22], [Antonio Restivo][23])
10. ***[Centralizers of Noncommutative Series and Polynomials][24]***( [Christophe Reutenauer][25])
11. ***[Transformations on words and q-Calculus][26]***( [Dominique Foata][27], [Guo-Niu Han][28], )
12. ******[Statistics on Permutations and Words][29] ( [Jacques D&eacute;sarm&eacute;nien)][30]
13. ***[Makanin's Algorithm][31]***( [Volker Diekert][32])
14. ***[Independent Systems of Equations][33]***( [Tero Harju][34], [Juhani Karhum&auml;ki][35], [Wojciech Plandowski][36])
15. ***[Bibliography and index][37]***(see also: ***[About the bibliography][38]***)
