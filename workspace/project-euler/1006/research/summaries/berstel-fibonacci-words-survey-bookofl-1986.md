## What the abstract itself establishes

"Fibonacci words have many amazing combinatorial properties. ... The aim of
this survey is [to] sketch some of the related factors (subwords) [and] also
describe basic arithmetic operations (i.e. normalization and addition) in the
[Fibonacci] number system."

That confirms the survey's two themes relevant to PE1006: the **factor/subword
structure** of Fibonacci words (the object Ψ sums over) and the **Fibonacci
number system** (the place-value reading behind interpreting a binary factor as
a decimal number). It does not let us cite a specific theorem without the
body.
