# Sturmian words, beta-shifts, and transcendence

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0308140

Pyo and Kwon provide the standard equivalences used here. A binary infinite word is Sturmian iff it is aperiodic and balanced (Theorem 3.1), and iff it is irrationally mechanical (Theorem 3.3). For fixed irrational slope alpha, all mechanical words of that slope have the same factor set (Proposition 3.1), and the orbit closure of a Sturmian word is the set of mechanical words of that slope (Proposition 3.4). Thus the rotation/floor-difference model is legitimate for the Fibonacci fixed point once its slope is identified as 1/phi^2. This source supports the theory, not the Project Euler numerical answer.
