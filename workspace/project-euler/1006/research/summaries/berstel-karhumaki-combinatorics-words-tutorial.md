# Berstel & Karhumäki — *Combinatorics on Words: a tutorial*

Source: https://www-igm.univ-mlv.fr/~berstel/Articles/2003TutorialCombinatoricsOnWords.pdf (URL as stored: univ-mlv tutorial)
Full text: [[berstel-karhumaki-combinatorics-words-tutorial.full]]

## What this source establishes

Background tutorial on combinatorics on words. Relevant to this run:

- **Fine–Wilf periodicity theorem and critical factorization theorem** — the
  standard periodicity toolkit; not directly used by the mechanical-word/
  floor-sum route, but the *period = Fibonacci number* property of the
  Fibonacci word's factors (Wikipedia) rests on this theory.
- **Morphisms, substitutions, non-periodicity of morphic words** — general
  background for "the Fibonacci word is morphic" (0→01, 1→0).
- No Sturmian-specific theorems beyond what the DLT'95 survey covers; no
  factor-complexity statement of its own that PE1006 needs beyond the k+1
  count already anchored to Perrin–Restivo / Berstel-DLT'95 / Sivasankar–Rama.

## What it implies for PE1006

Marginal. It validates the general framework (morphic words, periods) but adds
no load-bearing statement for the Ψ(k) computation. Keep as background; do not
cite it as the source for the k+1 theorem (use Perrin–Restivo Thm 1 /
DLT'95 Thm 2.1 / Sivasankar–Rama Prop 4).
