## What it would establish (per its references and the in-library coverage)

The article is an expository "Mathematical Gems" note on the Three-Distance (Three-Gap)
Theorem: the orbit {nα mod 1} partitions the circle into arcs of at most three lengths
(Steinhaus/Sós/Świerczkowski/Slater; van Ravenstein 1988). The references point at the
higher-dimensional extensions (Berthé–Kim 2018, Haynes–Marklof), interval exchanges, and
BWT–Sturmian words.
