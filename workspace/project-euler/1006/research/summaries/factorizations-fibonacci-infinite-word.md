Downloaded source is the arXiv **abstract landing page only** (title, abstract,
submission history, reference list) — no mathematical content, no theorems proved here. The
abstract: surveys factorizations of the Fibonacci infinite word using Fibonacci words and
related words, all derivable from elementary properties of the Fibonacci numbers.

## What it establishes / bears on PE1006
It confirms (by abstract statement) that the Fibonacci word admits many factorizations built
from Fibonacci words — consistent with the `fib-index` difficulty (the factor structure of the
Fibonacci word is organised around Fibonacci numbers / Zeckendorf representation). It provides
**no closed form and no enumeration rule** usable directly.

This file does not need re-reading for a theorem; it is a pointer. The relevant
factorization/standard-word structure is better sourced in the Berstel book and Perrin–Restivo
note. `status: background / pointer only`.
