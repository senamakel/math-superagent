# OEIS A276575 / A330064 — peripheral Beatty/counting entries — verdict

Source: https://oeis.org/A276575 (differences of A276573); https://oeis.org/A330064 (Beatty cosh x)

## A276575

First differences of A276573. The 2's/3's pattern superficially resembles a
Fibonacci word, but the sequence is built from A002828 (sums of squares),
unrelated to the golden-ratio factor structure. **Does not help.**

## A330064

Beatty sequence ⌊n·cosh x⌋ with x solving 1/x + sech(x) = 1; the constant is
1.61749989…, *close to but not equal* to φ² = 2.618. Its early terms (2,5,7,…)
match A001950 by coincidence but diverge (A330064 has 21,24 at positions
where A001950 has 23,26). This is a trap: do not use it as the upper Wythoff
sequence. **Does not help.**

## Verdict

Both **do not help**; retain only the negative finding (do not re-search).
A330064's near-match to A001950 is flagged specifically so nobody substitutes
it for the run's verified upper-Wythoff run-start index.
