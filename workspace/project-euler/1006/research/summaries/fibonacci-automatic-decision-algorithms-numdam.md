# Fibonacci-automatic decision algorithms

**Source:** https://www.numdam.org/item/ITA_2016__50_1_39_0.pdf  [[fibonacci-automatic-decision-algorithms-numdam.full]]

Mousavi–Schaeffer–Shallit prove a decision procedure for propositions over Fibonacci-automatic sequences using quantifiers, addition/subtraction, comparisons, and sequence indexing; they derive structural facts about the Fibonacci word (special factors, periods, repetitions, palindromes, etc.). This confirms that Fibonacci numeration gives effective logical descriptions of factor properties.

It does **not** prove that the base-10 weighted observable Ψ(k) is Fibonacci-automatic or recognizable, nor does it provide a finite transducer for decimal values or their squares. Consequently it does not close the PE1006 aggregation gap and should not be used to infer an O(log k) evaluator.
