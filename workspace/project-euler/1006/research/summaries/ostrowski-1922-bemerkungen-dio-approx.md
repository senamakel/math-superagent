Approximationen", Abhandlungen aus dem Mathematischen Seminar der Universität
Hamburg 1 (1922) 77–98. DOI 10.1007/BF02940581.

## Is the gap blocking?

No. The run's O(log) recursion is sourced, for its implementation, from the
universal-Euclidean / floor_sum treatments already on disk (oi-wiki 万能欧几里得,
fhq cnblogs, LOJ138, AtCoder math.hpp, plus Beck–Robins for the lattice-point
side). Ostrowski 1922 is the historical primary source for the
continued-fraction expansion of {nα} — a nice-to-have primary anchor, not
needed for any computation.
