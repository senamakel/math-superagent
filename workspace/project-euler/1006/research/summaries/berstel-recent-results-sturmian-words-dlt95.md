# Berstel — *Recent Results in Sturmian Words* (DLT'95 survey)

Source: http://www-igm.univ-mlv.fr/~berstel/Articles/1996SturmianDLTMagdeburg.pdf
Full text: [[berstel-recent-results-sturmian-words-dlt95.full]]

## What this source establishes

The definitive survey of Sturmian word theory. Key statements (verified in the
full text):

- **Three equivalent definitions (Theorem 2.1, Morse–Hedlund 1940 / Coven &
  Hedlund):** for an infinite binary word x, (i) x is Sturmian (P_x(n) = n+1
  for all n), (ii) x is balanced and not ultimately periodic, (iii) x = s_{α,ρ}
  or s′_{α,ρ} for irrational α ∈ (0,1) and real ρ. The mechanical words s_{α,ρ},
  s′_{α,ρ} are defined by a_n = a iff ⌊(n+1)α+ρ⌋ = ⌊nα+ρ⌋ (lower), etc.
- **Characteristic words:** c_α = s_{α,0}; a Sturmian word is characteristic
  iff both 0s and 1s are Sturmian (Prop 2.2's antecedent). Slope = density of
  the letter b in prefixes.
- **THE key sentence (Section 2, examples after Prop 2.3):**
  "The most famous characteristic word is the Fibonacci word f = abaababaabaab…
  generated by the morphism a↦ab, b↦a. **Its slope is 1/τ²**, where τ is the
  golden ratio."
  Reading (a,b) as (0,1): f = 0100101001001… = the problem's word S, of slope
  1/φ² ≈ 0.382. **This independently confirms the slope correction** (a second
  primary source, alongside Perrin–Restivo's "slope 2/(3+√5)").
- **Cutting sequences (Prop 2.3):** c_α = S_{α/(1−α)} — the characteristic word
  is the cutting sequence of the line of slope α/(1−α). (For α = 1/φ²,
  α/(1−α) = 1/φ — THIS is where "slope 1/φ" in some sources comes from: the
  *cutting-line* slope, not the letter-density slope.)
- **Same slope ⇒ same factor set (Prop 3.2):** Sub(s) = Sub(t) iff s,t have the
  same slope; distinct slopes ⇒ finite intersection. Justifies any intercept
  choice in the mechanical model.
- **Right-special factors (Prop 3.3, Mignosi):** the right-special factors are
  the reversals of the prefixes of the characteristic word.
- **Standard words (Section 7):** standard pairs (u,v) closed under (u,v)↦(u,uv)
  and (vu,v); the Fibonacci words are the standard sequence for the directive
  sequence (1,1,1,…).
- **Beatty (Thm 4.1):** ⌊ns⌋ and ⌊ns′⌋ complementary iff 1/s + 1/s′ = 1. This is
  the floor-function backbone of the mechanical/Beatty representation.

## What it implies for PE1006

1. **Slope of the problem's word = 1/φ², confirmed by a second authoritative
   source** (DLT'95, same morphism-and-word as the problem). This pins the
   mechanical-word slope for the digit rule unambiguously: α = 1/φ²
   (rational approximant F(n−2)/F(n)).
2. Explains the "slope 1/φ" statements seen elsewhere: for the *cutting line*
   (Prop 2.3) the relevant ratio is α/(1−α) = 1/φ; and the *complement* word
   (rabbit sequence, digit-swapped) has ones-density 1/φ ≈ 0.618. Neither is
   the problem's word.
3. Same-slope ⇒ same-factors (Prop 3.2) is the theorem that lets directive 2's
   arbitrary arc-midpoint intercepts generate the full factor set.
