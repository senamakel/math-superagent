# Library refresh, cycle 5

Sources added/read:

- [Vukusic, Balanced rectangles over Sturmian words and minimal discrepancy intervals](../sources/vukusic-balanced-rectangles-sturmian-2026.full.md), arXiv:2602.12801. Theorem 2.1 characterises balanced Sturmian rectangles by Ostrowski representations; Theorem 3.1 equates rectangle balance with interval balance. It gives telescoping factor-weight identities, but no logarithmic exact algorithm for arbitrary positional-weight sums over all length-k Fibonacci factors and no joint-intercept aggregation for PE1006.
- [Praveen M and Rama, A Note On ℓ-Rauzy Graphs for the Infinite Fibonacci Word](../sources/rauzy-ell-graphs-fibonacci-2022.full.md), arXiv:2210.08629. Theorem 3 proves strong connectivity of the ℓ-Rauzy graph and records factor-location forms; it does not supply a weighted aggregate algorithm.
