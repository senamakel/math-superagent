# O'Bryant — On the Sum of the Heights of Sturmian Factors

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611365

For a binary Sturmian word W, let F_n(W) be its n+1 distinct length-n factors and h(w) the number of 1s in w. The paper proves
\[
\sum_{w\in F_n(W)}h(w)\equiv n\pmod 2
\]
for every n>0, independently of the slope and intercept. The proof reduces the parity to a counting function for characteristic words and fractional-part identities. This gives an independent parity oracle for implementations of PE1006 factor generation; it does not itself evaluate the decimal-weighted second moment Ψ.
