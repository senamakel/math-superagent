## What this source is / whether it helps

A344953 is the sequence of *positions of the words ending with 1* inside the
OEIS A341258 listing of finite Fibonacci-representation words. It is a
peripheral catalogue entry picked up during a lookup, **not** a source about
PE1006's Ψ(k): it concerns a *different* encoding (A341258's word ordering), not
the distinct length-k factors of the infinite Fibonacci word.

**Verdict: does not help.** It establishes nothing about (i) the factor set of
the infinite Fibonacci word, (ii) its decimal interpretation, or (iii) any sum
of squares. No claim block attaches to it. The Ψ(1..5) = 1,101,20302,… sequence
has **no OEIS match** (recorded in `library-build-status.md`), and this is
consistent: the run's sequence is not catalogued, and A344953 is not it.

Do not re-read or cite this source for the solution; retain only the negative
finding (Ψ is not an OEIS sequence) so nobody re-searches for a closed form
from the catalogue.
