# Library note: factors of Sturmian words

URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(91)90172-X

The paper studies finite factors of Sturmian words and supplies standard combinatorial context (Christoffel words, special factors, and factor sets). Its headline counting formula concerns factors occurring across all Sturmian words, so it is not itself the PE1006 fixed-word answer; it is retained as an authoritative adjacent reference.
