# Hartwig–Patricio — Euclid, geometric and corner sums

**Source:** http://elib.mi.sanu.ac.rs/files/journals/flmt/177/flmn177p4613-4636.pdf  [[patricio-hartwig-euclid-corner-sums.full]]

The paper generalizes Euclid's algorithm to binomials, geometric sums, and noncommutative corner sums, with product rules and recurrence formulas in associative rings. This is relevant background for representing weighted concatenations and geometric contributions by finite algebraic states.

It does not state the PE1006 factor parametrisation, the required floor-moment monoid, or the aggregation over k+1 Fibonacci factors. Thus it is not a direct method for Ψ(10^18), and no claim from it should replace the executable/explicit fhq or LOJ138 recursion.
