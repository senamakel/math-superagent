## What this source establishes

The encyclopedic tier for the problem's central object. With the exact same
definition as the problem — S₀ = "0", S₁ = "01", S_n = S_{n−1}S_{n−2} (the
Wikipedia article's definition §/Definition section) — it states:

- The infinite Fibonacci word S_∞ = 0100101001001010010100100101001001…
  (A003849), each S_n a prefix. **This is exactly the problem's word.**
- Closed form for individual digits: the n-th digit is
  2 + ⌊n·φ⌋ − ⌊(n+1)·φ⌋ with φ = (1+√5)/2 — a mechanical-word digit formula.
- The word has complexity n+1: n+1 distinct subwords of length n, and the
  length-3 example **001, 010, 100, 101** — matching the problem's Psi(3)
  example exactly.
- It is balanced, aperiodic, hence **Sturmian** with slope 1/φ (in the
  Wikipedia *density-of-ones* convention) and is the standard word generated by
  the directive sequence (1,1,1,…). It is a morphic word generated by
  0 → 01, 1 → 0.
- Its last two letters are alternately 01 and 10; suppressing the last two
  letters (or prefixing the complement) gives a palindrome — the palindromic
  density is 1/φ.
- Frequency of 1 is 1/φ² and of 0 is 1/φ (letters densities). So the
  *ones-density* of the problem's word is 1/φ² ≈ 0.382 (NOT 1/φ ≈ 0.618).

## What it implies for PE1006

1. Confirms the problem's S_n are prefixes of a single Sturmian word, and that
   the k+1 factor count and the length-3 factor set are the standard facts —
   no need to reprove them.
2. **Slope convention trap.** Wikipedia says "slope 1/φ" while Perrin–Restivo
   say the Fibonacci word is characteristic of slope 2/(3+√5) = 1/φ². These
   agree after accounting for convention: Wikipedia's "slope" is the
   *density of ones* in the *1↔0-swapped* (rabbit) word, or equivalently the
   slope of the *complement*; the problem's word has ones-density 1/φ². When
   implementing the mechanical-word digit rule for the problem's word, the
   slope parameter α in s_{α,ρ}(n) = ⌊(n+1)α+ρ⌋ − ⌊nα+ρ⌋ must be 1/φ²
   (rational approximant F(n−2)/F(n)), not 1/φ.
3. The digit formula 2 + ⌊nφ⌋ − ⌊(n+1)φ⌋ is a ready cross-check: it is the
   same word (up to index convention) as c_α with α = 1/φ².
