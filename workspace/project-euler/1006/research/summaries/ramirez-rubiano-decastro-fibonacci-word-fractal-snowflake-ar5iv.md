# Ramírez, Rubiano & De Castro — A generalization of the Fibonacci word fractal and the Fibonacci snowflake

Source: José L. Ramírez, Gustavo N. Rubiano, Rodrigo De Castro, "A
generalization of the Fibonacci word fractal and the Fibonacci snowflake",
Theoretical Computer Science 528 (2014) 40–56, arXiv:1212.1368.
Full text: `research/sources/ramirez-rubiano-decastro-fibonacci-word-fractal-snowflake-ar5iv.full.md`
(URL recorded in file: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1212.1368).

## What it establishes

- Introduces a family of infinite words **generalizing the Fibonacci word** and
  studies their combinatorial properties.
- Associates to this family a family of curves with fractal properties; the
  curves have as attractor the **Fibonacci word fractal**.
- Describes an infinite family of polyominoes (double squares) from the
  generalized Fibonacci words — the **Fibonacci snowflake** generalization —
  with geometric properties (perimeter, area).
- Propositions from Pirillo and Monnerot on the standard Fibonacci word fractal
  structure are stated as background.
