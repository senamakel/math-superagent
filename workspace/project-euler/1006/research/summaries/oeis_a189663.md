# OEIS A189663 — partial sums of A189661 (the c1(k) sequence of PE1006)

**Source:** https://oeis.org/A189663 (catalogue entry, terms + formulas).

## What the entry establishes

A189663, offset 1, begins 0,1,1,2,2,2,3,3,4,4,... and is the partial-sum
sequence. Its exact closed forms (all listed in the entry):

- a(n) = 2(n−1) − floor((n−1)·φ), φ = (1+√5)/2  (R. J. Mathar);
- **a(n) = ceil((n−1)/φ²)** (J. Shallit, 2018) — the clean form;
- recurrence a(n) = n − a(n−1) − a(n−a(n−1)) for n > 2 (Alkan);
- A189663(n) = A060144(n−1) + sign(abs(n−1)).

These are all exact integer statements (no hypotheses beyond φ the golden
ratio); the entry is a catalogue (evidence: computed terms + attributed
formulas), not a proof source.

## What it means for PE1006 (verified match)

The run's own brute verification found (research/notes/pattern-hunt-pe1006.md,
`c1_terms.txt`): the number of length-k factors of the Fibonacci word that
start with digit 1 is

    c1(k) = 1 + floor(k/φ²),

verified exactly k = 1..400 and matching A189663's terms. A189663 as
catalogued is the (n−1)-indexed version: its term at n = k+1 is
ceil(k/φ²) — equal to 1+floor(k/φ²) except at the measure-zero points where
k/φ² is an integer (k = F_{2j}, where φ²·F_{2j} = F_{2j+2} is integer). So
the catalogue entry confirms the run's c1(k) closed form (same slope
1/φ² = 1 − 1/φ, the problem's letter-1 density), and the cross-references
(A189661/A189662/A060144/A026356) are the Beatty/Sturmian companions.

corroboration, not a new result. Verdict: **peripheral but consistent** —
