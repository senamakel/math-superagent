# Rampersad & Wiebe, "Correlations of minimal forbidden factors of the Fibonacci word" (2023)

Downloaded source is the arXiv **abstract landing page only** — no mathematical content in
this file. The abstract: characterises pairwise *correlations* between the minimal forbidden
factors of the infinite Fibonacci word (Guibas–Odlyzko overlap-correlation method for counting
words avoiding a forbidden set).

## What it bears on PE1006
The *minimal forbidden factors* view is the complement of the factor set (a word is a factor
iff it contains no minimal forbidden factor), so it is a conceptually plausible route to
enumerating the k+1 factors. **But** this run's factor parameterization is already fully
served by the Perrin–Restivo consecutive-factor lex-order rule, which is more direct and is
proved in-source. The correlation-of-forbidden-factors machinery would be an over-build for a
route we already have; it is not needed to derive a Ψ(k) recurrence and it supplies no
sum-of-squares formula.
