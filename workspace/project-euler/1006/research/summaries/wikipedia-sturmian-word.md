## What it claims

In [mathematics][4], more specifically in [combinatorics on words][5], a **Sturmian word**(**Sturmian sequence**or **billiard sequence**[1]) is a certain kind of infinitely long [sequence of characters][6]. The concept is named after [Jacques Charles François Sturm][7].

Such a sequence can be generated by considering a game of [English billiards][8] on a square table. The struck ball will successively hit the vertical and horizontal edges labelled 0 and 1 generating a sequence of letters. [2] This sequence is a Sturmian word.
