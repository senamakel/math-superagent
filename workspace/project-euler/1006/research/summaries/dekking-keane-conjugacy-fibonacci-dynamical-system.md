## What the abstract asserts

The paper characterises the symbolic dynamical systems topologically
isomorphic to the Fibonacci dynamical system. It proves there are infinitely
many injective primitive substitutions whose substitution dynamical system
lies in the Fibonacci conjugacy class, and infinitely many such systems not
generated by any substitution (example: doubling the 0's in the infinite
Fibonacci word).
