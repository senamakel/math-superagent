# Richomme, Saari & Zamboni — *Standard factors of Sturmian words* (RAIRO-ITA 44, 2010, 159–174)

Source: https://www.numdam.org/item/ITA_2010__44_1_159_0.pdf
(full text: [[richomme-saari-zamboni-standard-factors-sturmian.full]])

## What this source establishes

A complete characterization of which **standard words** occur as factors of a
characteristic (hence any) Sturmian word, in three independent ways — via
morphisms, via standard prefixes, and via palindromes.

**Setup (Section 2).**
- Standard words: s = s_n built from s_{−1}=1, s_0=0, s_k = s_{k−1}^{d_k} s_{k−2}
  (directive sequence (d_1,…,d_n)); primitive, and for length ≥ 2 have suffix in
  {01, 10}.
- A Sturmian word is an infinite word with exactly n+1 factors per length n;
  equivalently (Lothaire ACW Thm 2.1.13) its factors coincide with those of a
  characteristic word c_α, c_α(n) = ⌊α(n+1)⌋ − ⌊αn⌋.
- **The Fibonacci word f is the characteristic word c_α with α = (3−√5)/2,
  directive sequence (1,1,1,…); it is the unique fixed point of
  φ: 0→01, 1→0.** ← third independent confirmation of the slope (with
  Perrin–Restivo and Berstel DLT'95).
- Lemma 2.1: a binary word is standard **iff** it is f(0) or f(1) for some
  morphism f in the monoid generated by L0: 0→0, 1→01 and L1: 0→10, 1→1.

**Theorem 3.1**: for α = [0; d_1+1, d_2, …], a finite standard word u is a factor
of c_α iff u = (L0^{d1}∘L1^{d2}∘…)(x) for some x in S(d_{2k+1}, d_{2k+2}, d_{2k+3})
or with E(0↔1) in the odd case — the S-sets are explicit five-part unions built
from 0,1,10 powers (lines 163-172 of the full text).

**Example 3.3 (the Fibonacci case — the one that matters here):**
S(1,1,1) = {0, 1, 10, 010, 101, 0010, 0010010}; since L0∘L1 = φ² and L0∘E = φ,
**the standard factors of the Fibonacci word are exactly
{φⁿ(1), φⁿ(10), φⁿ(101), φⁿ(0010010) | n ≥ 0}.**

## What it implies for PE1006

1. The k = F_n − 1 factor set (directive 1's domain) is the set of F_n
   truncated cyclic rotations of the standard word q_n. This paper gives the
   *which standard words actually occur as factors* story — the rotations that
   are themselves standard appear exactly in the Example 3.3 list, and the
   L0/L1/E machinery is how the standard-word structure behaves under the
   Fibonacci morphism.
2. Confirms the slope α = (3−√5)/2 three ways, independently of the steer
   directive's (F(n−1)/F(n)) which the run already refuted at k=3.
3. Standard words' suffix-in-{01,10} / primitivity facts are directly usable for
   the period/rotation bookkeeping in the autocorrelation identity.

## What it does NOT establish

- Nothing about the decimal reading or the square-sum.
- Nothing about the mechanical-word/floor-sum primitive or universal-Euclidean
  evaluation; the factor-set description is combinatorial (morphisms), not
  numeric.
