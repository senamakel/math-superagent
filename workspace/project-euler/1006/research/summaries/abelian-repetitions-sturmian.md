## What it establishes (per the abstract)

Fici, Langiu, Lecroq, Lefebvre, Mignosi, Prieur-Gaston study **abelian repetitions** in Sturmian words via a bijection between factors of a Sturmian word and subintervals of the unit segment.

- In any Sturmian word, limsup of the ratio (maximal exponent of an abelian repetition of period m)/m is ≥ √5, with **equality for the Fibonacci infinite word**.
- The longest prefix of the Fibonacci infinite word that is an abelian repetition of period F_j (j > 1) has length F_j(F_{j+1}+F_{j−1}+1) − 2 for j even, F_j(F_{j+1}+F_{j−1}) − 2 for j odd.
- For j ≥ 3, the finite Fibonacci word f_j has smallest abelian period F_n with n = ⌊j/2⌋ if j ≡ 0,1,2 mod 4, else n = 1+⌊j/2⌋.
