## Formula

- a(n) = (a(n-1) + a(n-2)) mod 100 for n>1, a(0) = 0, a(1) = 1.

## Comments

- a(n) = A105472(n)*10 + A003893(n);
- the sequence is periodic with period 300; all blocks of 60 successive terms contain 20 even and 40 odd numbers, see A003893.
