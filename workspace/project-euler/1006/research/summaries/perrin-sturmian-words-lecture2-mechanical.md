## What it claims

As an example of the use of rotations, consider a word
w = b0b1 · · · bm−1, with b0, b1, . . . letters. We want to know
whether w is a factor of some sα,ρ = a0a1 · · · , with a0, a1, . . .
letters. By (1), an+k = bi if and only if R n+i (ρ) ∈ Ibi , or
equivalently, if and only if R n(ρ) ∈ R −i (Ibi ). Thus, for n ≥ 0,

w = anan+1 · · · an+m−1 ⇐⇒ R n(ρ) ∈ Iw (2)

where Iw is the interval

Iw = Ib0 ∩ R −1(Ib1 ) ∩ · · · ∩ R −m+1(Ibm−1)

The interval Iw is non empty if and only if w is a factor of sα,ρ.
Observe that this property is independent of ρ, and thus words sα,ρ
and sα,ρ′ have the same set of factors.

Theorem (Morse, Hedlund, 1940)

Let s be an inﬁnite word. The following are equivalent :

1 s is Sturmian ;

2 s is balanced and aperiodic ;

3 s is irrational mechanical.
