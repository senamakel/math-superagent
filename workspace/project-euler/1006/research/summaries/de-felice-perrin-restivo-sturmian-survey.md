# Library note: Sturmian survey

URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2019.03.006

This source was found by Exa search and downloaded into the local research library. The downloaded DOI endpoint produced only a short bibliographic/landing-page conversion, so it is a lead and corroborating source rather than the sole evidence for detailed theorem wording. The workspace already contains fuller local notes for Morse–Hedlund, Berstel–Vuillon, and related Sturmian references.

Relevant result: Sturmian words are binary infinite words with exactly n+1 distinct factors of length n; the Fibonacci word is the canonical fixed point of the Fibonacci morphism.
