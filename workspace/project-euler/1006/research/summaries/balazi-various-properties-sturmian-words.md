# Baláži 2005 — Various Properties of Sturmian Words

URL: https://doi.org/10.14311/760

The paper gives the Hedlund–Morse equivalence: a binary infinite word is Sturmian iff it is balanced and aperiodic, iff it is a mechanical word generated by an irrational slope and intercept. It presents the Fibonacci word as the canonical example and explains its cutting-sequence/mechanical representation. This supports the rotation/intercept parameterization used in the PE1006 reduction, but supplies no weighted Ψ evaluator.
