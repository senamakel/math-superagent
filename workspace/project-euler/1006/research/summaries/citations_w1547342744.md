## Abstract

Let <p be the golden number; we prove that the Fibonacci infinit word contains no frac tional power with exponent greater than 2 + <p and we prove that for any real number e>0 the Fibonacci infinit word contains a frac tional power with exponent greater than 2 + cp -e.
