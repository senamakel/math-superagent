```claim
id: sturmian-unique-special-factor-per-length
status: asserted
statement: In the language of a Sturmian word u there is exactly one left-special and exactly one right-special factor of each length, and Sturmian words are characterized by this property. (Masáková & Pelantová, arXiv:0809.0603, Preliminaries, verbatim.)
hypotheses: u an infinite Sturmian (aperiodic minimal-complexity) binary word; factor = contiguous substring; special = both letter extensions occur.
holds: yes — the PE1006 Fibonacci word f (fixed point of 0→01,1→0) is Sturmian of slope 1/φ², so it has exactly one right-special length-k factor R_k for every k≥1.
source: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/0809.0603
```
