# Governing theory

The infinite limit of the Fibonacci concatenation is the Fibonacci (Sturmian) word, the characteristic mechanical word of slope `alpha = 1/phi^2 = (3-sqrt(5))/2`, where `phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2`. Perrin–Restivo's Sturmian characterization (local source: `research/sources/perrin-restivo-note-sturmian-words.full.md`, URL in that file) identifies Sturmian words with codings of irrational rotations/mechanical words. Sturmian factor complexity is `p(k)=k+1`: exactly `k+1` distinct contiguous length-k factors for every positive k. This is the theorem controlling the object set in Psi.

For efficient evaluation, use the mechanical representation. The `k+1` factors correspond to the `k+1` rotation intervals cut out by `{ -m alpha mod 1 : 0<=m<=k }`; choosing one intercept in each interval gives each factor exactly once. The decimal value of a factor is a geometrically weighted sum of mechanical digits. Telescoping expresses it as a linear combination of floor values `floor(x+j alpha)`. Therefore Psi is a weighted first and second moment of affine floor sums.

The required primitive is the Euclidean floor-sum algorithm (AtCoder-style floor_sum / universal-Euclidean monoid): recursively transform a rational affine floor sequence under Euclidean division, carrying count, geometric weight, weighted first moment, and weighted second moment. Segment composition is exact and constant-size, so the Fibonacci continued-fraction path gives O(log k) arithmetic operations rather than enumerating k factors. The local algorithm references are `research/sources/atcoder-math-hpp-v151.full.md` and `research/sources/oi-wiki-universal-euclidean-floor-sum.full.md`.

Hypotheses hold: alpha is irrational; the Fibonacci word is its characteristic mechanical word; gcd(10,101001001)=1, so inverse powers of 10 exist modulo the target modulus.
