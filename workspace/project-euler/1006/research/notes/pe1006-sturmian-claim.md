# Claim: Fibonacci Sturmian factor complexity

The infinite Fibonacci word fixed by 0→01, 1→0 is Sturmian. Therefore its factor set has exactly k+1 distinct contiguous factors of length k for every k≥1. Sturmian words also admit mechanical/irrational-rotation codings. These facts apply because the finite S_n converge prefixwise to that fixed point, so the union of finite subwords equals the infinite factor set.

Source: Dominique Perrin and Antonio Restivo, “A note on Sturmian words”, Theoretical Computer Science (2011), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2011.12.047. Local related source: research/sources/perrin-sturmian-words-lecture2-mechanical.full.md.
