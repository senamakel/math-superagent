## Sivasankar–Rama, Fibonacci factor locations
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04304; full text [[fibonacci-1d-2d-enumerate-locate-factors-ar5iv.full]]. Proposition 1/Lemma 2 state that, for F(n)≤k<F(n+1), the k+1 factors of length k in the rabbit Fibonacci word are prefixes of rotations of f_{n+1} at i∈{0,…,F(n)-1}∪{F(n+2)-k-1,…,F(n+1)-1}, equivalently special-conjugate prefixes. Digit complement transfers factor sets to PE1006. This is the missing citable contiguous-window theorem, but the exact q_n q_n absolute indices and convention translation remain solver checks; it gives no O(log) second-moment aggregation.

## OI-Wiki universal Euclidean algorithm
Source: https://oi.wiki/math/number-theory/euclidean/; full text [[oi-wiki-universal-euclidean-floor-sum.full]]. The monoid recursion for floor-sum operation strings performs Euclidean parameter steps and runs O(log max parameters), independent of n. It supports the structural floor-sum primitive but only the unweighted prototype; no Sturmian or PE1006 result.

## fhq geometric-weight universal Euclidean
Source: https://www.cnblogs.com/dixiao/p/15719155.html; full text [[universal-euclidean-geometric-weight-fhq.full]]. Gives a six-component monoid tracking counts, geometric sums, first and second floor moments under U/R concatenation, with O(log) Euclidean recursion. It is directly relevant to the telescoped mechanical-word expression for v(x)^2, subject to exact intercept aggregation and modulus/index validation. Competitive-programming source; independently corroborated by OI-Wiki/LOJ138, not a proof of PE1006 wiring.

## LOJ138 moment-array variant
Source: https://www.cnblogs.com/AThousandMoons/p/13129167.html; full text [[loj138-universal-euclidean-floor-moments.full]]. States a moment-array universal-Euclidean algorithm for sums of floor powers times index powers through total degree 10 in O(225 log max parameters), with binomial concatenation. Confirms fixed-degree moment closure, but does not establish the PE1006 intercept sum or joint boundary state.

## Frougny / Cobham obstruction
Source: https://www.irif.fr/~cf/publications/lucas.pdf; full text [[frougny-mult-dep-linear-numeration-2002-irif.full]]. Theorems on finite-automaton conversion between linear/Pisot numeration systems require multiplicative dependence; 10 and φ are independent. Thus a finite-automaton Zeckendorf-to-decimal digit-DP route is ruled out. This does not affect exact universal-Euclidean arithmetic.
