# Library build notes

The research cycle searched widely across Sturmian factor complexity, Fibonacci factor locations, mechanical-word floor representations, Christoffel conjugacy, and Ostrowski/Zeckendorf numeration. The most directly relevant leads are:

- Mohankumar, Sivasankar, Rama Raghavan, *Locating the Factors of the Fibonacci Infinite Word*, arXiv:2207.04304: exact first-occurrence/location framework for the k+1 factors.
- Fici, *Factorizations of the Fibonacci Infinite Word*, arXiv:1508.06754: Zeckendorf representation and Fibonacci-word block structure.
- Hieronymi–Terry, *Ostrowski numeration systems, addition and automata*, arXiv:1407.7000: exact Fibonacci/Ostrowski arithmetic and finite-state operations.
- Berthé et al., *A note on Sturmian words*, HAL hal-00828351: mechanical/Sturmian factor ordering and generation.
- Bugeaud–Reutenauer, *On the conjugates of Christoffel words*, arXiv:2202.05486: Ostrowski parametrisation of conjugacy classes.

These are leads until downloaded and read; no Project Euler answer or solution was searched for or stored.
