# Approach: weighted-language diagonal / bivariate rational GF extraction

```approach
id: pe1006-weighted-language-diagonal
idea: Bivariate rational generating function with a formal position variable z, and diagonal extraction: carry z for digit position so the weight polynomial P_w(z) = Σ_j digit_j z^j is substitution-recursive, with the decimal weight entering only once as the single modular evaluation z=10 at the end.
mechanism: The Fibonacci factor language is finite-state in a structural sense; this route never converts decimal weights through an automaton. Psi(k) becomes a coefficient/diagonal of a bivariate rational series, to be evaluated by Christol's theorem (algebraicity over F_p iff p-automaticity) plus p-kernel diagonal extraction and CRT over the prime powers of the modulus M.
status: refuted
precedent: https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2306.02640; https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.901; https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.1505.01379; cobham-bes-frougny-multiplicatively-independent-conversion; fibonacci-sturmian-complexity
killed-by: Christol's theorem says, precisely, that for a finite field F_q a univariate series sum a_n t^n is algebraic over F_q(t) iff (a_n) is q-automatic; its multivariate/p-kernel versions require a finite p-kernel. Furstenberg says diagonals of rational series are algebraic, and Rowland–Yassawi give automatic-congruence algorithms for suitable diagonals. None says the PE1006 second-moment language has a finite p-kernel or a fixed algebraic/rational system. The finite-state claim is false in the needed sense: Fibonacci/Sturmian structure is not a fixed finite automaton in ordinary decimal position, and the base-10 positional weights do not disappear merely because z is formal. The known multiplicatively-independent numeration obstruction (10 versus phi) blocks the proposed uniform conversion. No paper found applies this diagonal construction to Psi. Therefore the key closure claim is unsupported; the route would buy fast coefficient extraction only after proving finite p-kernel/algebraicity, which is exactly the missing hard step.
```

Assessment: The reformulation is a diagonal/Hadamard-product generating-function method. The theorem hypotheses are finite-field coefficient series plus algebraicity (or rational diagonal input); they are not established for the weighted Fibonacci-factor second moment. CRT does not create algebraicity or finite kernels. The candidate is a plausible encoding, but the claimed fixed-size system is not a consequence of Christol and has no located precedent for this problem.
