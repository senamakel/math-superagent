# PE1006 — Christoffel conjugacy / rotation-sum

```approach
idea: Decompose the k+1 Fibonacci factors into cyclic-rotation (conjugacy) classes, each class the set of rotations of one Christoffel/standard word, and evaluate Psi(k) as a sum over classes using the exact rotation law on decimal values. Named mathematics: Christoffel words and their conjugacy classes (Bugeaud–Reutenauer: a word is a conjugate of a Christoffel word iff all its rotations are factors of a Sturmian word — the library claim conjugate-christoffel-factor-sturmian), the rotation algebra of base-10 values, and the leading-1 census c1(k) = 1 + floor(k/phi^2) (the library's own A189663 claim).
mechanism: For a length-k word w with value v, its left rotation w' has value v' = 10 v - d*(10^k - 1) where d in {0,1} is the most-significant digit of w (the digit that wraps to the units place). So within one conjugacy class of size t, the values are v_i = 10^i v_0 - (10^i-1) applied bitwise along the digit that leads, and the SUM OF SQUARES over the class is a closed form in (v_0, the class's leading-bit sequence, 10^k). The k+1 factors of a Sturmian word of length k are exactly the factors, and the library already holds the count of those starting with 1 (c1(k)); if the classes' leading-bit patterns are controlled by the three-distance/Cristoffel slope structure, Psi(k) reduces to a few class-sums with no per-word floor sum. This is a genuinely combinatorial representation — group the words, note how a rotation moves the value — rather than a mechanical/floor-sum evaluation.
status: refuted
killed-by: The grouping is REAL but does not have the shape the candidate assumes, and it does not bypass the floor-sum. (1) The conjugacy structure is NOT "k+1 rotations of one Christoffel word" — for a Sturmian word the length-k factors are exactly the k conjugates (cyclic rotations) of ONE Christoffel word PLUS exactly ONE singular word that is not conjugate to it. Verified in the literature: Avgustinovich–Frid–Puzynina, "Ergodic infinite permutations of minimal complexity", Eur. J. Combin. 2017 (doi:10.1016/j.ejc.2017.05.003): "the set of factors of s of a given length n consists of all n conjugates of the Christoffel word of that length, plus a single singular factor"; Bucci–De Luca–Zamboni, "Some characterisations of Sturmian words in terms of the lexicographic order", Fund. Inform. 2012 (doi:10.3233/fi-2012-665, Prop 2.5): factors not conjugate to a Christoffel word are the "singular words" xux; Bugeaud–Reutenauer, "On the conjugates of Christoffel words", DMTCS 2025 (doi:10.46298/dmtcs.15140): a word is a conjugate of a Christoffel word iff all its rotations are factors of a Sturmian word. So for length k (factors count k+1), there is exactly ONE full conjugacy class of size k (the k rotations of the Christoffel word) and one leftover singular factor — NOT a decomposition into "several classes of varying size, each a full rotation set". The candidate's mechanism ("within one conjugacy class of size t ... sum of squares is a closed form") would apply to the single Christoffel orbit of size k, but the singular word sits OUTSIDE any Christoffel conjugacy class and is exactly the object whose decimal value is not rotation-controlled. (2) Even TO the full Christoffel orbit, the claimed closed form is not a located literature result. The rotation law v' = 10v - d(10^k - 1) is trivially exact (it is just how a decimal digit wraps), so the per-orbit sum is a mechanical identity, not a theorem. The hard quantity is the orbit's leading-bit sequence — the ordered list of the most-significant digits as you rotate — and no source gives that as a closed three-gap form independent of the Sturmian/floor structure. The run's own exact data (pattern-hunt: V(R_k) noise-flat mod M, no catalogued form; no simple 0-prefix run rule, refuted in cycle3 §3) shows the auxiliary/leading-digit sequences are not closed-form simples. (3) The candidate itself flags the open factual question ("leading-bit pattern per orbit is likely three-gap/Sturmian") — the literature on rotations of Christoffel words (Borel–Reutenauer, "On Christoffel classes", RAIRO-ITA 2005, doi:10.1051/ita:2005038; the DMTCS paper's Ostrowski parametrisation of the conjugacy class) parametrises the WORD rotations but does not evaluate a DECIMAL sum over them. The decimal-value sum is precisely the mixed base-independent/Sturmian quantity that every route (floor-sum monoid, Ostrowski) ends up evaluating via the same floor primitive. Grouping into conjugacy classes re-labels the k+1 words but does not change the essential arithmetic — the singular factor plus the orbit's leading-bit sequence land the sum back on the Sturmian floor-sum object. It is not a cheaper independent route.
precedent: Grounding the structure — Bugeaud & Reutenauer, "On the conjugates of Christoffel words", DMTCS 2025, doi:10.46298/dmtcs.15140 (a word is a conjugate of a Christoffel word iff all its rotations are factors of a Sturmian word; Ostrowski parametrisation of the whole conjugacy class). Avgustinovich–Frid–Puzynina, Eur. J. Combin. 65 (2017) 124-145, doi:10.1016/j.ejc.2017.05.003 (length-n factors = n conjugates of the Christoffel word + exactly one singular factor). Bucci–De Luca–Zamboni, Fund. Inform. 2012, doi:10.3233/fi-2012-665 (Prop 2.5: singular words xux among the factors). Borel & Reutenauer, "On Christoffel classes", RAIRO-ITA 39 (2005), doi:10.1051/ita:2005038 (conjugacy classes of Christoffel/standard words). Carey–Clampitt, "Partitioned factors in Christoffel and Sturmian words", TCS 2018 (doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2018.06.019): Sturmian factors of length m = m+1 subintervals of a rotation, partitioned-factor varieties — the *counting* generalisation of the factor structure. Library claims: conjugate-christoffel-factor-sturmian; oeis-A189663-c1-leading-1-count (the leading-1 census the candidate wants to lean on). What is NOT sourced: any closed per-class decimal-sum (the payoff claimed) — searched, none exists.
first-step: If revisited, restrict to what is grounded: confirm empirically that for the Fibonacci word the k+1 factors = k conjugates of one Christoffel word + one singular word (this is the Avgustinovich–Frid–Puzynina structure, and it is worth verifying for the specific Fibonacci word at k<=400 against mech_psi/brute), and confirm the rotation law reproduces Psi(k) when combined with the exact values. But do NOT expect an O(log) closed form from it: the singular factor and the orbit's leading-bit sequence are the same Sturmian/floor object every route ends on. The payoff claimed (bypassing the floor-sum) is unsupported and is the reason to close it.
```

## Assessment

**Refuted as a route, though its structure is genuinely grounded.** The
literature gives exactly the conjugacy shape of the k+1 Sturmian factors — the
k rotations of one Christoffel word plus one singular word (Avgustinovich–Frid–
Puzynina; Bucci–De Luca–Zamboni; Bugeaud–Reutenauer). But the candidate's
mechanism assumed a decomposition into several full Christoffel orbits, which
is not what happens; and even over the single true Christoffel orbit, the
per-orbit decimal sum is not a located closed form — the leading-bit sequence
that would make it closed is exactly the Sturmian/floor object the committed
route already evaluates. It re-labels the sum rather than bypassing it, so it
is refuted as a cheap independent route, while its conjugacy structure remains
a correct (and useful for verification at small k) description of the factor
set.
