# Incidence Gram norm with three-gap complement

precedent: https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.math/0611365; https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2011.12.047; https://arxiv.org/1807.11273; claim: governing-sturmian; claim: rotation-arc-factor-frequencies
killed-by: The Gram identity is exact but only a repackaging of the pair-correlation/floor-sum problem. Sturmian balance bounds letter counts in equal-length factors, and the three-gap theorem classifies rotation gaps, but neither theorem says that the weighted quadratic form has a closed three-gap evaluation. In the off-diagonal term, each C(i,j) remains a rotation-arc/lattice-point count depending on the lag and boundary; summing it with decimal weights is the same floor-moment primitive already used by the universal-Euclidean route. No source found applying a Gram/three-gap identity to PE1006 or eliminating those boundary terms.
