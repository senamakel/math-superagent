```approach
idea: Replace the directive-9 affine decimal window recurrence by a Fibonacci-block transducer on boundary states. For a block B and fixed k, represent the binary word block by its (i) length, (ii) first/last k-bit contexts, (iii) decimal value, and (iv) weighted window statistics. Concatenation is an exact finite-state monoid; the Fibonacci words satisfy Q_{n+1}=Q_n Q_{n-1}, so summaries are generated by logarithmically many Fibonacci doublings/concatenations rather than by k+1 intercepts.
mechanism: Decimal windows crossing a concatenation boundary are the only non-additive terms. Their number is at most k-1, but do not enumerate them: store the boundary context as a symbolic binary polynomial in X, evaluated at X=10 modulo M. The rolling update is affine, and second moments are carried by a 4x4 upper-triangular matrix. For the special target interval, the Sivasankar–Rama/standard-word position theorem gives the final k+1 windows as one suffix interval of Q_n Q_n. Build the summary of Q_n recursively while simultaneously maintaining the two boundary contexts needed by that interval. The candidate O(log) claim is conditional on proving that the crossing-window correction itself admits the same two-context summary; the executable harness below falsifies this if a context of length k is insufficient.
status: refuted
first-step: Implement an exact bounded transducer that stores complete boundary contexts (oracle) and a compressed candidate that stores only four affine moments plus O(1) boundary symbols. Compare their summaries for every k<=400 and Fibonacci level n; first mismatch is the smallest obstruction to the claimed constant-state collapse.
precedent: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.13977 (Sivasankar–Rama, Fibonacci factor positions); https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2018.04.003 (Frid, Ostrowski/Sturmian prefix decompositions); https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2210.08629 (Praveen–Rama, Fibonacci Rauzy graphs); https://doi.org/10.1051/ita/2016010 (Fibonacci-automatic transducers and addition); claim: fibonacci-position-theorem-contiguous-windows; claim: fibonacci-word-contiguous-factors-position-theorem; claim: chuan-fibonacci-words-rotation-structure.

Thus the structural ingredients are grounded but the decisive O(log k), k-independent boundary algebra is unsupported; the candidate is refuted as a solution route rather than as a formal impossibility theorem.
