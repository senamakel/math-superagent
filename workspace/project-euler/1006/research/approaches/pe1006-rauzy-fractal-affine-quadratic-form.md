# PE1006 — Rauzy fractal / affine quadratic form (speculative)

```approach
idea: Represent each length-k factor of the Fibonacci word by a point in the Rauzy fractal (the compact self-affine attractor tiling associated with the substitution and its incidence matrix), so the decimal value of the factor is a coordinate projection and Psi(k) is the sum of squares of these products. If the sum-of-squares form obeys a self-affine (substitution) recursion, Psi(10^18) is a matrix-power evaluation of that form — a genuinely geometric engine with no floor sums, no windows, no extension recurrences. Named mathematics: Rauzy fractal of a substitution (Arnoux–Rauzy, the compact attractor with self-similar affine tiling for the Fibonacci substitution; three examples: the interval, the Sierpinski-like planar fractal for the 3-letter Arnoux–Rauzy case), the incidence/substitution matrix and its powers, quadratic forms closed under a linear substitution.
mechanism: The Fibonacci substitution 0->01, 1->0 has incidence matrix [[1,1],[1,0]] = Fibonacci. Its "fractal" is degenerate to the interval (the substitution is 2-letter unimodular Pisot, whose Rauzy fractal is a self-similar interval with the three-gap structure); each factor's digit string determines its location, and the base-10 value is v = sum_j digit_j 10^{k-1-j}, a projection against the weight vector 10^*. The sum over the k+1 factors of v^2 is a quadratic form on the (k+1)-dimensional indicator vectors, and self-similarity of the factor set under the morphism may close it to a matrix-power form. This is speculative: unlike the closed substitution-morphism-moment SET approach (which failed on variable image length at the factor-set level), this works at the level of the fractal's affine self-similarity rather than trying to push a whole factor set to one length.
status: refuted

The Rauzy-fractal embedding of a Pisot substitution represents factors by projections against the SUBSTITUTION's eigenvector/incidence coordinates (powers of the Pisot conjugate), not against an arbitrary base-10 weight vector; mixing in decimal weights is exactly the kind of cross-base mixing the Cobham/Frougny non-recognisability theorem forbids (10 and phi multiplicatively independent Pisot numbers — library claim cobham-bes-frougny-multiplicatively-independent-conversion, Frougny RAIRO-ITA 2002, doi:10.1051/ita:2002015). The speculative lever (self-affine closed quadratic form -> matrix power) has no object to act on in this 2-letter instance.

## Assessment

**Refuted — the candidate's own "speculative" flag is confirmed by two
independent structural facts.** (1) The 2-letter Fibonacci substitution's Rauzy
"fractal" is degenerate to an interval (the genuine self-affine fractal
requires a degree-≥3 Pisot eigenvalue, as in the Tribonacci/Arnoux–Rauzy
case), so there is no nontrivial self-affine attractor whose affine
self-similarity could close a quadratic form under a matrix power. (2) Even in
a genuine fractal, the decimal weights 10^{k-1-j} are multiplicatively
independent from the substitution's Pisot geometry (10 vs φ), so the decimal
value is not a coordinate of the Rauzy fractal and need not be
substitution-closed — the same Cobham/Frougny obstruction that refutes the
Zeckendorf digit-DP. No source applies a Rauzy-fractal quadratic form to a sum
of decimal factor values. Structurally closed.
