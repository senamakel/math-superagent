## Precedent pass (against the literature)

### Grounded premise (not the issue)
- **f is Fibonacci-automatic.** The Zeckendorf-parity reading of the Fibonacci
  word is a library claim (`fibonacci-zeckendorf-parity-characterization`, Fici,
  "Factorizations of the Fibonacci infinite word", arXiv:1508.06754 §2) and is
  literature-reinforced here: Du, Mousavi, Schaeffer & Shallit (arXiv:1406.0670,
  RAIRO-ITA 2018) generate f by a 2-state DFAO over Zeckendorf representations
  with a 17-state addition automaton; Kundnani, Virdi, Kant & Alam (2025,
  "Minimal Automaton and Asymptotics for the Parity of Zeckendorf Digit Sums")
  give a minimal 4-state DFAO and the run-length recurrence; "Ostrowski-automatic
  sequences: Theory and applications" (TCS 2021) puts the Fibonacci word in the
  general class.
- **Digit-DP / enumeration by automata is real** for properties that are
  functions of one representation (Shallit, "Enumeration and Automatic
  Sequences", PUMA 2015; the Walnut framework of the DMSS papers). This is why
  the premise is genuinely attractive — but the DP always runs over the SAME
  numeration in which the object is automatic.

### The obstruction (the decisive gap, found, not absence)
- The candidate's mechanism needs the map `position r ↦ v(r) mod M`, where
  `v(r) = Σ_j 10^{k−1−j} f(r+j)`, to be Zeckendorf-regular so a digit-DP over
  Zeckendorf digits can evaluate the range sum. The windows' decimal values are
  computed with **base-10 weights at Zeckendorf-digit positions** — a function
  of two numeration systems at once.
- Cobham's theorem (integer bases) and its Pisot generalization (Bès; Frougny)
  say exactly when two linear numeration systems can be converted or jointly
  recognised by finite automata: **iff the bases are multiplicatively dependent**.
  10 and φ are multiplicatively independent, so Zeckendorf ↔ decimal conversion
  is not automaton-realizable (Frougny, RAIRO-ITA 2002, doi:10.1051/ita:2002015;
  Berend–Frougny 1994; Frougny 1992). The value map mixes exactly that
  conversion.
- Size consequence: even staying inside Zeckendorf, computing f(r+j) for all
  j<k via the addition automaton yields one shift-automaton per j, i.e. O(k log k)
  states for v(r); there is no poly-size automaton in log k for the window value.
- The converse also fails: the Fibonacci (Sturmian, irrational slope) word is
  not k-automatic for any integer base k, so no decimal-base digit-DP exists
  either.

### Verdict
**Refuted on evidence** — the mechanism's core claim (a Zeckendorf digit-DP
carrying base-10 window values) contradicts the Cobham–Bès–Frougny
non-recognisability theorem for multiplicatively independent numeration bases.
The candidate itself flagged this as its open weakness (flag (b)); the
literature closes it in the negative. The automaticity of f is real and sourced,
but it buys a digit-DP only for Zeckendorf-native quantities, which Ψ's decimal
readings are not.
