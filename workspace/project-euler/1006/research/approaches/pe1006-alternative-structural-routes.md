```approach
idea: Use the Zeckendorf/ Ostrowski transducer, but not as base-10 digit-DP. Treat a Fibonacci block as a straight-line program over the two noncommuting operators L_0,L_1 on the affine quadratic state of a decimal window. Evaluate the requested suffix-window language by binary powering of the SLP, with boundary contexts represented as formal Laurent polynomials and reduced modulo X^k-1 only after pairing with the two boundary offsets.
mechanism: Fibonacci factor positions are Zeckendorf-recognisable, while the decimal window value is a homomorphism from binary words to Z/MZ under concatenation. The obstruction in the rejected route was conversion between Zeckendorf positions and base-10 digits; this route never converts positions to decimal. It composes word blocks first, and applies the decimal homomorphism to block summaries. The unresolved lemma is finite-rank closure of the boundary-crossing quadratic form under L_0,L_1.
status: refuted
first-step: Generate the exact block summary for Q_n=(length, decimal value, all suffix/prefix contexts, window-square sum) and fit the smallest rank of the crossing quadratic form from n=3..20. Reject immediately if rank grows with k; otherwise write the rank-r concatenation law and recurse to n≈90.
precedent: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2018.04.003 (Frid, Sturmian numeration and prefix decompositions); https://doi.org/10.1051/ita/2016010 (Mousavi–Schaeffer–Shallit, Fibonacci-automatic decision procedures and Fibonacci addition); https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2210.08629 (Praveen–Rama, Fibonacci Rauzy graphs); https://hal.science/hal-01121887/document (Berthé et al., return words in tree sets); claim: ostrowski-prefix-decomposition-characteristic; claim: fibonacci-zeckendorf-parity-characterization.
killed-by: The literature supports Ostrowski representations, finite-state addition, Fibonacci factor/Rauzy graphs, and return-word decompositions, but no theorem gives a fixed-dimensional summary for arbitrary decimal-weighted boundary-crossing quadratic forms. The exact summary necessarily includes prefixes/suffixes of length k, so its state dimension is not bounded independently of k; the proposed finite-rank closure is the missing assertion, not a consequence of the cited transducer or return-word theorems. No paper found applying this construction to Psi or proving its O(log k) evaluation. This is a literature-supported failure of the decisive payoff, not a proof that no such identity could ever exist.
```
