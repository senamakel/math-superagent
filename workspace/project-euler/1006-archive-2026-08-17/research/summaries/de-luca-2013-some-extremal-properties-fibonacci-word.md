# de Luca, "Some extremal properties of the Fibonacci word" (IJAC 23, 2013)

<!-- source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1209.3927 | arXiv 1209.3927 -->

Establishes that the Fibonacci word is extremal among characteristic Sturmian words for three
palindromic-prefix quantities.

## What it establishes (relevant to PE1006)
- Palindromization ψ, central words, Christoffel words: a proper Christoffel word is aψ(v)b
  (Thm 3.3); |ψ(v)| relates to Fibonacci numbers F_{n+1}-2 (Lemma 4.2).
- Continuant formulas for the slope of a Christoffel word (Thm 5.1, 5.3, 5.4).
- Characterization theorems of the Fibonacci word among characteristic Sturmian words by
  maximality of palindromic-prefix length/period/b-count (Thms 4.4, 4.7, 4.11, Cor 4.12).

## What it implies for this problem
**Marginal.** It is about the Fibonacci word being *extremal*, which is not what PE1006 needs:
the problem needs the *factor set* and a way to sum squares, not extremality of the
Fibonacci word among Sturmian words. It does provide, in passing, the central-word/Christoffel
apparatus (ψ, standard sequences, continuants) already covered by the Berstel book, and the
Fibonacci-number length identity |ψ(v(n))| = F_{n+1}-2 useful for interval boundaries, but it
gives no closed form for Ψ(k) and no factor-enumeration rule. Treat as background/reference,
not load-bearing. `status: background`.
