# Title: When waiting moves you in scoring combinatorial games

Authors: [Urban Larsson][2], [Richard J. Nowakowski][3], [Carlos P. Santos][4]

View a PDF of the paper titled When waiting moves you in scoring combinatorial games, by Urban Larsson and 1 other authors

[View PDF][5]

Abstract: Combinatorial Scoring games, with the property `extra pass moves for a player does no harm', are characterized. The characterization involves an order embedding of Conway's Normal-play games. Also, we give a theorem for comparing games with scores (numbers) which extends Ettinger's work on dicot Scoring games.
