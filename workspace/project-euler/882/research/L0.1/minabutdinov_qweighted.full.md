**Full text of arXiv:1801.03120** (Limiting curves for the dyadic odometer and
the generalized Trollope–Delange formula). Generalizes the classical
Trollope–Delange formula to the weighted sum-of-binary-digits `s_q`; deviations
of partial sums converge to Takagi–Landsberg curves `Ta`, `a=1/(2q)`, with the
classical Trollope–Delange fluctuation the `q=1/2` case. Evidence that weighted
digit sums (our k·-weighted A,B) keep the closed-form periodic-fluctuation
structure.
