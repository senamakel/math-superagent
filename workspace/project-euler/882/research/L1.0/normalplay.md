# Normal play convention — the win condition

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_play_convention

## What it establishes
- **Normal play**: the last player able to move wins; equivalently, a player who cannot move loses. Contrast with misère.

## Why it applies here
- The problem states "the player that is unable to move loses" — exactly normal play. Under normal play with the value A-B>0 (Left ahead), Left/One wins, which the problem confirms ("Dr. Zero can never win" without skips).

## Not settled
- The skip/pass mechanism is not part of standard normal-play CGT (normal play has no passes); analysis of the pass is separate (see zugzwang and loopy notes).
