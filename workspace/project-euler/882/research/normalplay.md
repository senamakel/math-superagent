Method of determining the winner

A **normal play****convention**in a game is the method of determining the winner that is generally regarded as standard. For example:

- Preventing the other player from being able to move
- Being the first player to achieve a target
- Holding the highest value hand
- Taking the most [card tricks][1]

In [combinatorial game theory][2], the normal play convention of an [impartial game][3] is that the last player able to move is the winner. [1]

By contrast " [misère games][4] " involve upsetting the convention and declaring a winner the individual who would normally be considered the loser.
