**Full text of arXiv:1003.0150** (Multidimensional Divide-and-Conquer and
Weighted Digital Sums). Establishes, via Mellin-transform analysis, that
**weighted** binary digital sums (`S_M(n)=Σ t^M b_t 2^t` and the variant
`W_M(n)=Σ t^M 2^{i_t}`, averages `TS_M`, `TW_M`) have exact closed forms of a
main-term plus 1-periodic-fluctuation type — periodic functions `A_{d,m}`,
`G_M`, `G_{M,d}` given by absolutely convergent Fourier series. This is the
polylog-computability warrant for the run's k·-weighted A(n), B(n).
