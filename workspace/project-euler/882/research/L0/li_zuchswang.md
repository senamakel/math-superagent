# Li, "Sums of Zuchswang games" (1976) — NOT obtained

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(76)90046-7
Shuo-Yen R. Li, J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 21 (1976) 52–67. This is the *original*
zugzwang-game paper (games where moving is disadvantageous) that our skip/zugzwang
structure belongs to. It could **not** be downloaded: the DOI resolves to a redirect
stub and the ScienceDirect PDF is 403/paywalled (Elsevier, subscription-gated).

**Recorded so the attempt is not repeated.** The zugzwang-game theory this paper
originated is instead available — with proofs — in Siegel's survey "Coping with
cycles" (MSRI Games of No Chance 3, 2009): `research/L1/siegel_zugzwang.md` and its
full text `research/L0/siegel_zugzwang.full.md`. Li's Theorem (loopy zugzwang games
= `x & y`, dyadic `x ≤ y`) is stated and proved there.
