# Seal — L1.0: the polylog arithmetic engine and its CGT frame

This batch of ten notes is sealed once. Together they establish the
**counting model of the bit-deletion game** and the **polylog arithmetic
engine** that lets S(n) be evaluated without iterating to n=10⁵. Folds that
synthesise them: [[counting-arithmetic]] and [[game-reduction-and-pass]] (both
in this L2.0 folder).

## The game reduces to counting (CGT frame)
The board "k copies of k, k=1..n" is a **disjunctive sum** of one subgame per
number ([[cgt]], [[disjsum]]). Model each number with a 1-bits, b 0-bits as
G(a,b)={G(a-1,b)|G(a,b-1)}, G(0,0)=0; inductively **G(a,b)=a−b**, so the
whole board's no-skip value is the single integer **A−B** (A=total 1-bits,
B=total 0-bits) — the structural fact in [[disjsum]] (derived) and [[cgt]]
(framework). Normal play, unable-to-move loses ⇒ A−B>0 leaves Dr. One always
winning without skips ([[normalplay]]). The **skip** is a self-loop in the
game graph (a loopy/stopper game, whence a finite S(n) fixpoint, [[loopy]]);
pass theory shows a pass can change structure unpredictably, so **S(n) ≠ A−B**
comes from the (A,B) minimax DP, not any CGT closed form ([[mfl_pass]]).

## The arithmetic engine is polylog
A(n)=Σ k·popcount(k): [[bitcount]] (A000788) gives O(log n) recurrences for
the unweighted summatory popcount. B(n)=Σ k·zerocount(k): [[a083652]]
(A083652, exact O(1) summatory bit length) plus [[zerocount]]'s identity
A059015 = A083652 − A000788 supply the zero side. The k·-weighting preserves
the closed-form main-term + 1-periodic-Takagi-fluctuation structure: sourced
prima- rily from [[flajolet_weighted_digitalsums]] (Cheung–Flajolet–Golin–Lee
2010, arXiv:1003.0150) and [[minabutdinov_qweighted]] (ArXiv:1801.03120,
generalised Trollope–Delange for weighted digit sums). [[normalplay]] shares
the win-condition frame. All converge on: **A(n) and B(n) are computable in
O(poly log n)**, letting the DP run at the stated bound.

## Caveat carried forward
The (A,B) form is the *approximate* surrogate (leading-1/drop-0 shortcut);
dyadic-Simplicity-rule refinement lives in [[simplicity_rule_dyadic]] (L0.2)
→ [[game-reduction-and-pass]]. Given S(2)=2, S(5)=17, S(10)=64 are reproduced
by it, but exactness of A−B is a derived surrogate, not yet the real-game rule.
