## What the abstract announces

A different problem: in [0,n]^3 partitioned into unit cubes, some cubes
obstructing lines of sight, what is the maximum number of obstructing cubes
simultaneously visible from an observer cube? Construction with Ω(n^{8/3})
visible obstructing cubes (generalized to Ω(n^{d−1/d}) in dimension d > 3),
and a Fourier-analytic O(n^{d−1/d} log n) upper bound in a reduced setting.

## Why it does not help

This is an extremal *blocking-configuration* problem in 3D (and higher),
with visibility of cubes from a cube — not lattice-point visibility from the
origin in a bounded 2D region, which is what PE 351 counts. No 2D counting
formula, no density claim usable here, no totient connection. Nothing
