## What the abstract announces (kept for reference)

Quasiprime matrices + CRT-based computational search for hidden forests
(fully invisible n×n blocks) closer to the origin than the classical CRT
method achieves: a 4×4 hidden forest at ≈ 1.84×10^8 units (classical CRT
gave > 3×10^18); conjecture that every hidden forest is found by the
CRT-algorithm on a quasiprime matrix. It also recalls the 6/π² visible
proportion.
