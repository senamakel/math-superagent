## What this source establishes

Pólya (1918, per the authors' citation of Pólya–Szegő): *how thick must the
trunks of the trees in a regularly spaced circular orchard grow if they are to
block completely the view from the center?* The paper studies a more general
orchard model — any compact, convex domain — and finds an expression for the
minimal trunk radius. Examples: rhombus-shaped and circular orchards. It also
gives estimates for the minimal radius in a 3-dimensional orchard.

## Why it is in this library

Names the classical orchard-visibility family that PE 351's hexagonal orchard
belongs to (trees at lattice points; occlusion of the view from the centre).
The problem is the **blocking-radius threshold** version: given tree size, can
you see out? PE 351 is the **counting** version: how many points are blocked?
The two are different questions; this source carries no counting formula and no
totient connection.
