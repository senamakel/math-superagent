# Euclid's Orchard

---

[image: DOWNLOAD Mathematica Notebook] [Download Wolfram Notebook][1]

[image: EuclidsOrchard]

An array of "trees" of unit height located at integer-coordinate points in a [point lattice][2]. When viewed from a corner along the line [image: y=x] in normal perspective, a [quadrant][3] of Euclid's orchard turns into the modified [Dirichlet function][4] (Gosper).
