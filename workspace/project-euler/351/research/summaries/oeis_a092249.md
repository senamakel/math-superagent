# A092249 — summatory totient without the leading zero (raw OEIS lookup, now summarised)

<!-- source: https://oeis.org/A092249 -->

**Definition.** a(n) = position of the integer n in the standard diagonal
enumeration of the positive rationals; by the entry's own comment,
**a(n) = A002088(n+1)'s tail = A002088 without the leading zero**, i.e.
a(n) = Φ(n) = Σ_{k=1..n} φ(k) for n ≥ 1.

**Terms.** 1, 2, 4, 6, 10, 12, 18, 22, 28, 32, 42, … (A002088 = 0, 1, 2, 4, 6,
10, 12, 18, … shifted by one index: A092249(n) = A002088(n)).

## What it establishes for this problem

Nothing new: it is the summatory totient under a second name, already held in
this library as OEIS A002088 (`summatory-totient-counts-visible-pairs` claim,
`research/summaries/oeis-A002088-summatory-totient.md`). The rationals-diagonal
interpretation (numerator+denominator coprime pairs) is exactly the
coprime-pairs counting used in the sector argument for H(n) = 6(C(n+1,2) −
Φ(n)).

## Hypotheses

n ≥ 1 integer. Holds here.

## What it does not settle

No closed form beyond the definition; no orchard geometry. Confirms only that
the run's Φ values are the standard sequence.

## Claims

None here — the summatory-totient claim lives under
`research/summaries/oeis-A002088-summatory-totient.md`.
