# A092249 — Positions of the integers in the standard diagonal enumeration of the rationals

<!-- source: https://oeis.org/A092249 -->

**Definition.** a(n) is the position of the integer n in the standard Cantor
diagonal enumeration of the positive rationals (integers in the first column,
diagonals moving up-right).

## What this source establishes

- **a(n) = A002088(n+1) = Φ(n+1)** — R. J. Mathar's comment: "A002088
  without the leading zero". Since A002088 = 0,1,2,4,6,10,12,… and this
  sequence is 1,2,4,6,10,12,…, the identity is a(n) = Φ(n+1) for n ≥ 0,
  equivalently **Φ(n) = A092249(n−1)** for n ≥ 1.
- The combinatorial model: in the diagonal enumeration of rationals the
  integer k/1 sits at position Φ(k−1)+1 — the k-th diagonal contributes
  φ(k) new fractions, so the integers occur exactly at the cumulative
  totient positions.
