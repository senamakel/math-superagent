## What the abstract announces

Visibility along polynomial curves y = q(a_n x^n + … + a_1 x), q ∈ ℚ_{>0},
generalizing straight-line (b = 1) and monomial (y = a x^b, density 1/(b+1))
visibility: a polynomial-gcd criterion giving a lower bound on visible pairs
in N²; conditions for a point to be the next visible point on such a curve;
an exact inclusion–exclusion double-sum formula for pairs ≤ N visible w.r.t.
the polynomial family; open questions on gap distributions.

## Caveat

The abstract says "the classical notion, where the density equals 1/2" —
that is not the standard lattice-point visibility density 6/π² (Sylvester
1883; every other source in this library). Without the body we cannot tell
what counting convention produces 1/2; do not use this abstract for any
density claim.

## Why it does not help

PE 351 is straight-line visibility in a bounded hexagon, i.e. the degree-1
