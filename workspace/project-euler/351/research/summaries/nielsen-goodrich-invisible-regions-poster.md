## What this source establishes

The poster (advisors: aBa Mbirika; UWEC) — the earliest public form of the Goodrich–Mbirika–Nielsen line — states two theorems:

- **Theorem (density).** The fraction of pairs (x,y) ∈ Z² with gcd(x,y) = 1 is ζ(2)⁻¹ = 6/π² ≈ 0.607927; hence ~40% of Z² is invisible from the origin. (Same result as the later paper's Prop 2.4, stated without the full Euler-product proof.)
- **Theorem (hidden forests).** For every n ∈ N there exist disjoint sets A₁, A₂, each n consecutive natural numbers, with gcd(a₁,a₂) > 1 for all aᵢ ∈ Aᵢ; hence an n×n hidden forest exists for each n. (Constructive via CRT on prime matrices; the 2014 seed of Theorem 3.4 of the 2021 Involve paper.)

**Future research** (as printed): find the closest H⁴(x,y) and H²(x,y,z); determine the minimal prime factors required for an arbitrary Hⁿ(x,y); conjecture that permuting the same n×n prime matrix yields the closest hidden forests.
