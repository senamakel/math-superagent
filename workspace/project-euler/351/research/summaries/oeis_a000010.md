## Key formulas (from the raw lookup)

- φ(n) = n·∏_{p|n}(1 − 1/p).
- **Σ_{d|n} φ(d) = n** (the Gauss divisor-sum identity).
- **φ(n) = Σ_{d|n} μ(d)·(n/d)** (φ = μ ∗ id, the Möbius transform of the
  natural numbers).
- Dirichlet series Σ φ(n)/n^s = ζ(s−1)/ζ(s); φ multiplicative with
  φ(p^e) = (p−1)p^{e−1}.
- limsup n/(φ(n)·log log n) = e^γ (Landau, 1909).
