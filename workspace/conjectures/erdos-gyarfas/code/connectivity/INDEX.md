| `verify_connectivity.py` | Glues Markström/Petersen lobes at a single cut vertex (all-single-edge k=3) and asserts the glued cycle set equals the union of the lobe cycle sets, machine-verifying the (a)/(c)/(e) ingredients of the cut-vertex structure lemma; also refutes "δ≥3⇒2-connected" via two K4s+bridge and validates the Markström edge list against graph6. Ran cleanly after fixing oracle unpack (`mine,_`→`_,mine`); output in code/out/connectivity/verify_connectivity.log — union claim holds for both lobes. |
| `verify_connectivity_k2.py` | Machine-verifies the cut-vertex lobe cycle-set lemma for k=2 split types: glues two lobes sharing only a cut vertex v and asserts cycle_lengths(G) == union of the two lobe cycle sets. Covers type (1,2) (Markström 1-edge + Petersen 2-edge, d(v)=3) and type (2,2) (two prism lobes, 2 distinct v-edges each, d(v)=4), and shows in (2,2) that the length-(ℓ1+ℓ2+2) closed walk through both lobes repeats v (3 times) so it is excluded from the simple-cycle set. Every case asserts equality via TWO independent enumerations — lib.cycle_oracle.all_simple_cycles and networkx.simple_cycles — and both agree on all cases. Output in code/out/connectivity/verify_connectivity_k2.log: all classes PASS. Correctness basis: a simple cycle through v needs two distinct v-edges into the SAME lobe (going into two lobes repeats v), so no simple cycle crosses lobes. |

Standalone hand-argument, not a program run; do not cite as computation. |
