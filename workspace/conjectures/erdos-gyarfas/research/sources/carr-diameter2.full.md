# Title: Cycles of Length 4 or 8 in Graphs with Diameter 2 and Minimum Degree at Least 3

Authors: [Avery Carr][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled Cycles of Length 4 or 8 in Graphs with Diameter 2 and Minimum Degree at Least 3, by Avery Carr

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: In this short note it is shown that every graph of diameter 2 and minimum degree at least 3 contains a cycle of length 4 or 8. This result contributes to the study of the Erdős-Gyárfás Conjecture by confirming it for the class of diameter-2 graphs.
