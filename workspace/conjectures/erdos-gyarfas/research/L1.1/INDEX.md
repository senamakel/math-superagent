| `carr-diameter2.md` | Digest of Carr, "Cycles of Length 4 or 8 in Graphs with Diameter 2 and Minimum Degree at Least 3" (arXiv:2508.19302, PDF conversion). Theorem 1.1: every diam-2 delta>=3 graph contains a cycle of length 4 or 8, confirming EG for the diameter-2 class. Fragment digests the claim-by-claim proof ruling out 4-free and 8-free diameter-2 delta>=3 graphs (a string of Claims 1.x, 2.x about forbidden 4-cycles). Full text at research/L0.1/carr-diameter2.full.md. |
| `gao-huo-liu-ma-unified.md` | Digest of Gao, Huo, Liu & Ma, unified cycle-length paper (arXiv:1904.08126). One unified approach proves Thomassen, Dean's conjecture (cycle of length 0 mod k, k>=3), Bondy–Vince tight (k cycles of consecutive lengths), and chromatic >= k+2 => k consecutive-length cycles. Theorem 1.3: min degree k+1 => k admissible cycles (1-AP or 2-AP lengths), giving at delta=3 (k=2) the sharpest unconditional cycle-length guarantee at the conjecture's degree. Full text at L0.0/gao-huo-liu-ma-unified.full.md. |
| `gao-ma-bondy-vince.md` | Digest of Gao & Ma, "Cycles of consecutive lengths in a graph" (Bondy–Vince line, arXiv:1902.05701, PDF conversion). Confirms Bondy–Vince's conjecture: for every nonnegative k, all but finitely many graphs with at most k vertices of degree less than three contain two cycles whose lengths differ by one or two (the k<=2 case, an earlier Erdős conjecture, proved by Bondy–Vince). The k=0 case gives delta>=3 => two cycles of consecutive length. Full text at research/L0.1/gao-ma-bondy-vince.full.md. |

| `liu-montgomery-odd-cycle.md` | Digest of Liu & Montgomery, "Odd cycles in graphs and a conjecture of Erdős and Hajnal" (arXiv:2010.15802, PDF conversion). Bearing on EG: Theorem 1.1 / Corollary 1.3 — an average-degree condition suffices for a power-of-2 cycle (large average degree forces every even length in a long interval [log^8 l, l], so 2-power cycles appear); this disproves the stronger belief that every min degree r can avoid 2-power cycles. Also proves the odd-cycle-reciprocal-sum theorem, the chromatic version, and a Thomassen conjecture on equally-subdivided K_k subdivisions. Full text at research/L0.1/liu-montgomery-odd-cycle.full.md. |
| `luo-ma-zhao-dean.md` | Digest of Luo, Ma & Zhao, Dean's conjecture paper (arXiv:2601.13552, PDF conversion). Resolves Dean's conjecture for k>=6: every graph with min degree >=k contains cycles of length r (mod k) for every even r, unless every end-block is in {K_{k+1}, K_{k,k}} union H_k (which fail only for length =2 mod k); also k admissible cycles for k>=7. Introduces the trigonal and tetragonal sparse outer-planar graph framework for path/cycle lengths — transferable machinery for the EG attack, though the k>=6 regime is above delta=3. Full text at research/L0.0/luo-ma-zhao-dean.full.md. |
| `markstrom-extremal-cycles.md` | Digest of Markström, "Extremal graphs for some problems on cycles in graphs" (converted PDF of cycex.pdf). The computational-experiments paper behind the EG verification bound: exhaustive computer search over cubic graphs for extremal cycle counts and for graphs without cycles of length 4 and 8 (relating to EG), plus the smallest 3-connected non-hamiltonian cubic class-I graphs. Fragment covers the many/maximum-cycles results (psi(k), generalized Petersen graph conjecture) and the extremal search; the EG-relevant search details are in the full text at research/L0.1/markstrom-extremal-cycles.full.md. |
| `pirzada-2power-unicyclic-cubic.md` | Digest of Pirzada et al., "On 2-power unicyclic cubic graphs" (Electron. J. Graph Theory Appl. 10(1), 2022, 10.5614/ejgta.2022.10.1.24, HTML conversion). Shows there is an infinite family of cubic graphs containing only one 2-power cycle (2-power unicyclic cubic graphs), and observes the sole 2-power cycle in a cubic graph cannot be removed — implying no counterexample exists in that form. Fragment is an abstract only. Full text at research/L0.1/pirzada-2power-unicyclic-cubic.full.md. |
