# Potočnik–Vidali, "Cubic vertex-transitive graphs of girth six" — summary

See `research/summaries/potocnik-vidali-cubic-vertex-transitive-girth6.md` for the
summary of what this source establishes and its bearing on the Erdős–Gyárfás run.

**One-line:** complete classification of finite simple cubic vertex-transitive
graphs of girth 6 — with the sole exception of the Desargues graph (GP(10,3), 20
vertices) they are skeletons of hexagonal torus tilings, truncations of
arc-transitive triangulations of closed hyperbolic surfaces, or truncations of
6-regular graphs with respect to an arc-transitive dihedral scheme (Discrete
Math. 345 (2022) 112734; arXiv:2005.01635).
