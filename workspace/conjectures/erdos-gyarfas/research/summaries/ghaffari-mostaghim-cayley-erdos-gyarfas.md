# Ghaffari & Mostaghim, "Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture for some families of Cayley graphs", Aequationes Math. 92 (2018) 1–6

[[research/sources/ghaffari-mostaghim-cayley-erdos-gyarfas.full.md]] · source URL: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00010-017-0518-3

## What it establishes

Proves the Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture for Cayley graphs on:
- generalized quaternion groups,
- dihedral groups,
- semidihedral groups, and
- groups of order p³ (p prime).

This is one more *settled restricted class* — the conjecture holds for these Cayley graphs when their (Cayley) minimum degree is ≥3. It joins the library's list of settled classes (planar claw-free, P8/P10/P13-free, cubic claw-free, 3-connected cubic planar, diameter-2 graphs, Cayley on these groups).
