## What it claims

Abstract: In this short note it is shown that every graph of diameter 2 and minimum degree at least 3 contains a cycle of length 4 or 8. This result contributes to the study of the Erdős-Gyárfás Conjecture by confirming it for the class of diameter-2 graphs.
