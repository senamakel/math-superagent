# Exoo — Live "Graphs Without Cycles of Specified Lengths" catalog

Source: http://isu.indstate.edu/ge/COMBIN/CYCLES/index.html (live, fetched this run)
Full text: `research/sources/exoo-cycles-catalog.md` (+ per-record pages under `research/sources/exoo-cycles-catalog-*.md`, mostly headers)

## What it is

Geoffrey Exoo's ongoing catalog of small trivalent (cubic) graphs avoiding specified
cycle lengths, motivated by the Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture. The index page (fetched
2026-07) lists:

- N4610: a smallest trivalent graph with no 4, 6 or 10 cycles
- N4832: a trivalent graph of order 32 with no 4, 8 or 32 cycles
- G24a: a smallest trivalent graph with no 4 or 8 cycles (one of four; Automorphism
  group order 3 — this is the Markström family's planar member is arguably among these)
- G24b: another with no 4 and 8 cycles
- N46: the (unique) smallest trivalent graph with no 4 or 6 cycles
- N468: a smallest trivalent graph with no 4, 6 or 8 cycles
- **"The smallest graph I know of with no 4, 8 or 16 cycles has 78 vertices."**
- **"The smallest with no 4, 8, 16 or 32 cycles has 540 vertices."** (→ this is the current
  smallest-known for m≤5; Exoo's 2014 paper gave a 450-vertex construction)

## Cross-reference

- The index links the 78-vertex example to the [arXiv:1403.5636] G78 — consistent.
- This catalog is referenced by Markström's cubicavoid catalog
  (abel.math.umu.se/~klasm/Data/cubicavoid.html) as "Exoo has also found several other
  graphs avoiding certain cycle lengths".

## Status

Primary source (author's own catalog), live. Numbers are "smallest known to me", not
proved-minimal except where Markström's exhaustive search is cited (n≤52 for
C4,C8,C16-free 3-connected cubic). Recorded so the run quotes "540 (smallest known,
catalog)" not "450 (construction)" when giving the m≤5 frontier, and "78 (smallest
known, catalog; Markström proves no 3-connected cubic < 52)" for m≤4.
