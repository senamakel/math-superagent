## What it claims

Three graphs related to the Erdős-Gyárfás Conjecture are presented. The graphs are derived from the Buckyball, the Petersen graph, and the Tutte-Coxeter graph. The first graph is a partial answer to a question posed by Heckman and Krakovski [1] in their recent work on the planar version of the conjecture. The other two graphs appear to be the smallest known cubic graphs with no 2 m 2^{m} -cycles for m ≤ 4 m\leq 4 and for m ≤ 5 m\leq 5.
