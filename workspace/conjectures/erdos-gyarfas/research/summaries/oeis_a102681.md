## Formula

- From _Hieronymus Fischer_, Jun 10 2012: (Start)
- a(n) = Sum_{j=1..m+1} (floor(n/10^j + 1/5) - floor(n/10^j)), where m = floor(log_10(n)).
- G.f.: g(x) = (1/(1-x))*Sum_{j>=0} (x^(8*10^j) - x^(10*10^j))/(1 - x^10^(j+1)). (End)

## Recurrence and program

- p:=proc(n) local b,ct,j: b:=convert(n,base,10): ct:=0: for j from 1 to nops(b) do if b[j]>=8 then ct:=ct+1 else ct:=ct fi od: ct: end: seq(p(n),n=0..120); # _Emeric Deutsch_, Feb 23 2005
