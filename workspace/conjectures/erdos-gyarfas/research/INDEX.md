| `L1.1/gao-huo-liu-ma-unified.md` | Digest of Gao, Huo, Liu & Ma, unified cycle-length paper (arXiv:1904.08126, PDF conversion). Core cycle-length machinery: one unified approach proves Thomassen (min degree k+1 => all even lengths mod k; 2-connected non-bipartite => all lengths mod k), Dean's conjecture for k-connected graphs (cycle of length 0 mod k, k>=3), Bondy–Vince tight (3-connected non-bipartite min degree k+1 => k cycles of consecutive lengths), and chromatic number >= k+2 => k consecutive-length cycles. Theorem 1.3: min degree k+1 => k admissible cycles (1-AP or 2-AP lengths), giving at delta=3 (k=2) the sharpest unconditional cycle-length guarantee at the conjecture's degree. Full text at research/L0.0/gao-huo-liu-ma-unified.full.md. |
