| `L1.0/sudakov-verstraete-sparse.md` | Digest of Sudakov & Verstraëte, "Cycle lengths in sparse graphs" (arXiv:0707.2117, abs/HTML conversion). Part 1 proves Erdős's conjecture that the set C(G) of cycle lengths has size Omega(d^floor((g-1)/2)) for average degree d and girth g (tight via d-regular Moore graphs), plus odd-cycle and H-free versions.
