# Title: A constant lower bound for the union-closed sets conjecture

Authors: [Justin Gilmer][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled A constant lower bound for the union-closed sets conjecture, by Justin Gilmer

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: We show that for any union-closed family $\mathcal{F} \subseteq 2^{[n]}, \mathcal{F} \neq \{\emptyset\}$, there exists an $i \in [n]$ which is contained in a $0.01$ fraction of the sets in $\mathcal{F}$. This is the first known constant lower bound, and improves upon the $\Omega(\log_2(|\mathcal{F}|)^{-1})$ bounds of Knill and Wójick. Our result follows from an information theoretic strengthening of the conjecture. Specifically, we show that if $A, B$ are independent samples from a distribution over subsets of $[n]$ such that $Pr[i \in A] < 0.01$ for all $i$ and $H(A) > 0$, then $H(A \cup B) > H(A)$.
