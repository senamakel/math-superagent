# Title: The Union-Closed Sets Conjecture for Small Families

Authors: [Jens Maßberg][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled The Union-Closed Sets Conjecture for Small Families, by Jens Ma{\ss}berg

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: We prove that the union-closed sets conjecture is true for separating union-closed families $\mathcal{A}$ with
$|\mathcal{A}| \leq 2\left(m+\frac{m}{\log_2(m)-\log_2\log_2(m)}\right)$ where $m$ denotes the number of elements in $\mathcal{A}$.
