# Characterizing 3-Sets in Union-Closed Families

- Jonad Pulaj (Davidson College)

*Experimental Mathematics* (2021). DOI: 10.1080/10586458.2021.1927254

## Result

Uses the polyhedral interpretation of Poonen's Theorem and exact rational integer
programming to classify families of 3-sets (3-element members) on ground sets of
size $n \le 9$, and thereby proves the **3-sets conjecture of Morris**: the
smallest number of distinct 3-sets (whose union is an $n$-set) that ensure
Frankl's conjecture holds for every union-closed family containing them is
$\lfloor n/2 \rfloor + 1$ for all $n \ge 4$.

## Note

This paper is the definitive treatment of the "3-set question" flagged in
GOAL.md and problem.md. It is published in Experimental Mathematics and has no
free full text on arXiv; this record fixes the bibliographic reference. The
*algorithmic* content is available in the free sources held alongside it:

- Morris, "FC-families, and improved bounds for Frankl's Conjecture" (arXiv:math/0702348) — proves the conjecture for families containing three 3-subsets of a 5-set, four 3-subsets of a 6-set, or eight 4-subsets of a 6-set; exact small FC values.
- Pulaj, "Cutting Planes for Families Implying Frankl's Conjecture" (arXiv:1702.05947) — the computable Poonen-weight algorithm; resolves open questions, counterexample to a 2006 Morris conjecture.
- Pulaj & Wood, "Local Configurations in Union-Closed Families" (arXiv:2301.01331) — new values of FC(k,n), automorphisms, generalization of Poonen.

These three free sources, together with this bibliographic record, cover the
computationally attackable FC/3-set line.
