# Frankl's Conjecture is True for Modular Lattices

Tetsuya Abe, Bumpei Nakano. _Graphs and Combinatorics_ 14 (1998), 305–311.
doi:10.1007/pl00021180
Note: only abstract obtainable (Springer paywall); full proof not captured.

## What is confirmed (abstract level)

The paper proves: **every finite modular lattice `L` with `|L| ≥ 2` contains a
join-irreducible element `x ∈ L` such that at most `|L|/2` elements `y ∈ L`
satisfy `y ≥ x`.** Modular lattices include distributive lattices, so this
subsumes Poonen's distributive case. It is a special case of Reinhold's later
lower-semimodular result.
