# Nived — "A Study On The Graph Formulation Of Union Closed Sets Conjecture" (arXiv:2409.02221)

> Digest points to the full summary; the landing page has only the abstract. Read
> the real content at [[nived-graph-formulation-2024.full.html]] (summary) and the
> full text at `research/sources/nived-graph-formulation-2024.full.html.full.md`.

**What it establishes (one line):** a decomposition theorem — a bipartite graph
decomposed into two edge-disjoint subgraphs whose common vertices are on one side,
stable and 2-layered, has maximal-stable-set count equal to the product of the
pieces, and rare vertices of the pieces stay rare — proving Frankl's conjecture
(graph form) for a new class of bipartite graphs. Extends the settled graph classes
beyond Bruhn et al.'s four; does not settle the bipartite case or move the constant.
