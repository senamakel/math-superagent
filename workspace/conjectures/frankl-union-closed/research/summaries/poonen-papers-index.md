# Poonen papers index — summary

**Source:** <https://math.mit.edu/~poonen/papers/> (Poonen's own publication index).
**Full text:** `research/sources/poonen-papers-index.full.md`.

## What this source establishes

Poonen's public index of his own papers — a bibliographic catalogue, not a
mathematical primary source. Its value to this library is negative evidence:

- **The 1992 JCTA paper "Union-closed families" (doi:10.1016/0097-3165(92)90068-6)
  does NOT appear** in Poonen's own catalogue. The index hosts his errata
  (`poonen-errata-union-closed-correction.full.md`, already on disk) and later
  lattice work, but not the 1992 paper and no free copy of it. This confirms,
  from the author's own site, the audit's conclusion that no free full text of
  the origin paper exists on the open web (ScienceDirect is paywalled).
- The index also confirms Poonen's broader lattice-theoretic output
  (Congruences on lattices, etc.), none of which bears on Frankl's conjecture
  beyond the errata already held.

## What it implies for this problem

The origin of the **lattice formulation** (UC ⟺ in every finite lattice |L|≥2 a
join-irreducible lies under ≤ |L|/2 elements) is therefore covered in this
library only through:

- Poonen's errata (corrections to the 1992 paper),
- the Bruhn–Schaudt survey's restatement of the formulations,
- the Morris / Marić–Živković–Vučković restatements of Poonen's FC
  characterization and his n≤7 / |F|≤28 verification.

Every formulation in `problem.md`'s "equivalent statements" section is
source-covered by the held items; the 1992 full text itself is a known,
recorded gap with no free copy.
