# Nived — "A Study On The Graph Formulation Of Union Closed Sets Conjecture" (arXiv:2409.02221, 2024)

**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02221 (full: https://arxiv.org/html/2409.02221)
**Full text:** [[nived-graph-formulation-2024.full.html]] (the `.full.md` copy is the

**Author:** Nived J M (M.Sc, IIT Hyderabad). v5 dated 19 Jul 2026.

## What it is

A graph-formulation paper extending the Bruhn–Charbit–Schaudt–Telle 2015
framework. Recall the graph form of Frankl's (UCC): *in a bipartite graph with at
least one edge, each bipartition class contains a "rare" vertex, one lying in at
most half the maximal stable sets.* This paper proves the UCC condition for a new,
broader class of bipartite graphs by a maximal-stable-set **decomposition**
argument.

## Main content

### Section 2 — connecting the two formulations (generalised incidence)
A vertex `x ∈ X` of a bipartite graph `G(X,Y)` is **rare** iff `x` is **abundant**
in the union-closed family `⟨F^X⟩` generated by the incidence family
`F^X = {N(y) : y ∈ Y}` (Proposition 2.1). This is the same correspondence as
Bruhn et al. but stated via the **generating** family's incidence graph, which the
author notes is the more general statement.

### Section 3 — the new graph results
- **Prop 3.1.** For a disconnected bipartite `G`, a vertex is rare iff it is rare
  in its own connected component; `w_G = ∏ w_{components}` (maximal-stable-set
  counts multiply across components).
- **Theorem 3.2 (structural core).** Let `{H, I}` be a decomposition of `G`
  (edge-disjoint subgraphs whose union is `G`). If the vertices of
  `V(H) ∩ V(I)` form a stable set and are all **2-layered** — every neighbour of
  such a vertex has a *distinct* pendant (degree-1) neighbour — then the number
  of maximal stable sets of `G` is the *product* of those of `H` and `I`.
- **Theorem 3.3 (the UCC consequence).** Let `G` be bipartite, `{H,I}` a
  decomposition whose common vertices lie in one bipartition class and are all
  2-layered. Then every rare vertex of `H` or `I` remains rare in `G`. **This
  proves the UCC condition for any such decomposable bipartite graph whose pieces
  individually satisfy it** — a genuinely new class, via pendant-vertex structure.
- Several propositions give the set-version of these statements (3.8, 3.10,
  3.12, 3.14), translating the graph results back to the union-closed-family
  language.

## Why it matters for this run

- ROOT.md flags the **bipartite graph case as the heart of the open problem**
  (only chordal bipartite, subcubic bipartite, series-parallel, and
  bipartitioned-circular-interval graphs were settled in Bruhn et al.). This paper
  adds a distinct new family: bipartite graphs decomposable into pieces with the
  common set on one side, stable, and 2-layered. The "2-layered / pendant-neighbour"
  condition is a named structural hypothesis the run could test, extend, or seek a
  counterexample to (drop 2-layeredness and the product formula can fail).
- The decomposition technique — `w_G(x) = w_P(x)·w_Q` (Prop 3.1) and the
  product formula — is a clean, checkable method. The run's oracle could, on small
  bipartite graphs, verify the correspondence Prop 2.1 (rare ⟺ abundant) which is
  cheap and independent.

## Status

Sourced (arXiv preprint, not journal-verified; v5 2026). Proves UCC for a new
restricted graph class by decomposition — a step toward the open bipartite case,
**not** a settlement of it and **not** a constant improvement. Theorems are
asserted-by-source; the elementary counting lemmas (Prop 3.1, Prop 2.1) could be
oracle-checked on small graphs if the run extends the checker to bipartite
maximal-stable-set incidence, a cheap future cross-check.
