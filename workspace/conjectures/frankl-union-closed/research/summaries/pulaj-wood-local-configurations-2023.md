# Pulaj & Wood, "Local configurations in union-closed families" (arXiv:2301.01331, v2 Oct 2024)

**Full text:** [[pulaj-wood-local-configurations-2023.full]]

New FC(k,n) values and bounds; answers Vaughan's 2002 symmetry question in the positive; proves an Ellis–Ivan–Leader question; gives a generalization of Poonen's theorem.
