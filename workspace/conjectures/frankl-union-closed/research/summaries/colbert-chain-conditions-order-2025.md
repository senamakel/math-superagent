## What this confirms (no change to the record)

- The result is now **peer-reviewed in Order (2026)**: UC holds for finite *and* infinite union-closed families whose chains of sets have size at most three; for all nontrivial topological spaces satisfying the descending chain condition on open sets; with proofs independent of the cardinality of the family/universe. Also gives families satisfying the conclusion without being union-closed.
- **Confirms the infinite failure**: UC is known false in the infinite setting — this is what motivates the chain-condition recovery.
