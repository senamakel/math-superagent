# Morris, "FC-families, and improved bounds for Frankl's conjecture" (arXiv:math/0702348, 2007)

**Full text:** [[morris-fc-families-2007.full]]

The FC-family engine. Proves UC for families containing three 3-subsets of a 5-set, four 3-subsets of a 6-set, or eight 4-subsets of a 6-set. Gives exact small FC values. Poses the 3-set conjecture.

```claim
id: morris-thm1
statement: A subfamily of P(5) is a proper FC(5)-family iff generated (up to permutation) by one of six listed systems; in particular FC(3,5)=3 and FC(4,5)=5.
hypotheses: union-closed containing family; Poonen's Theorem applies
holds-here: yes
status: proved (computational case analysis)
bearing: base of the FC(k,n) table; FC(3,5)=3 is the first "3-sets force it" threshold.
anchor: research/sources/morris-fc-families-2007.full.md
```

```claim
id: morris-thm2
statement: FC(3,6)=4 and 7 ≤ FC(4,6) ≤ 8.
hypotheses: n=6
holds-here: yes
status: proved (the exact values; 4-sets gap later closed to FC(4,6)=7 by Marić–Živković–Vučković)
anchor: research/sources/morris-fc-families-2007.full.md
```

```claim
id: morris-thm3
statement: Frankl's conjecture holds for all union-closed families whose largest set has at most 9 elements (n≤9), and for |F|≤36.
hypotheses: union-closed
holds-here: yes (superseded by n≤11 Bošnjak–Marković; kept for the FC machinery)
status: proved
anchor: research/sources/morris-fc-families-2007.full.md
```

```claim
id: morris-3set-conj
statement: FC(3,n) = ⌊n/2⌋ + 1 for all n ≥ 4.
hypotheses: n ≥ 4
holds-here: yes
status: asserted (conjecture; PROVED later by Pulaj 2021)
bearing: the "3-set question" in GOAL.md; its answer (⌊n/2⌋+1) is what Pulaj proved.
anchor: research/sources/morris-fc-families-2007.full.md
answers: 3-set-question
```

**Bearing for this run:** the FC(k,n) machinery is the computational line of attack in GOAL.md item 3-set. Morris's Conjecture 1 was proved by Pulaj; his Conjecture 3 (about non-FC staying non-FC under a move) got a counterexample in Pulaj 2017.

```claim
id: morris-conj3
statement: Suppose a union-closed family B has minimal generating family S, and B' is generated by (S\{B})∪{B∪{i}} for some set B∈S. If B is not FC then B' is not FC either.
hypotheses: union-closed B with minimal generator S, B∈S
holds-here: yes
status: asserted (Morris's Conjecture 3; REFUTED by Pulaj 2017 pulaj-morris-counterexample)
bearing: a known-dead-end structural shortcut about how non-FC-ness behaves under a set-enlargement move; Pulaj's counterexample closes it, so the run should not rely on it.
anchor: research/sources/morris-fc-families-2007.full.md
contradicts: pulaj-morris-counterexample
```

**Does not settle:** FC(4,6) exact value (closed later), FC(k,n) for general k,n, UC itself.
