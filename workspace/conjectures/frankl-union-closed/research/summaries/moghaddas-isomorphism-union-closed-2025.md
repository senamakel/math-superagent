**What it establishes (one line):** isomorphisms of pure union-closed families are
exactly those induced by ground-element bijections (Theorem 4.1), so the lattice
representation is a faithful encoding — and since redundant elements are abundant,
any counterexample may be assumed pure. Structural/foundational; does not move the
bound or settle a new class.
