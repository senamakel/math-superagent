Summary of what the paper establishes (full proof read, recorded in the
sibling note):
- **Theorem 1.3(A)**: `1` join-irreducible, or join of two join-irreducibles
  ⟹ Frankl's conjecture; Corollary: **breadth ≤ 2 lattices** satisfy it.
- **Theorem 1.3(B)**: upper semimodular with `|J(L)\A(L)| ≤ 3` ⟹ Frankl's.
- **Lemma 2.3/Cor 2.4**: re-proves Czédli–Schmidt's large-semimodular result.
- **Open**: general upper semimodular lattices.
