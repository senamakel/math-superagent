# Bhasin — "A Cubical Perspective on Complements of Union-Closed Families of Sets" (arXiv:2409.17050)

**What it establishes (one line):** for a simply-rooted family F ⊆ 2^[n] (complement of a union-closed family) with ∅ ∈ F, the associated cubical set X(F) is acyclic, giving the Euler-Poincaré identity Σ_k (−1)^k |C_k(F)| = 1. Topological/adjacent-problem treatment — this is the "Bhasin cubical homology" precedent the run's approach notes cited but never held; it is a global constraint, not a proof of Frankl's conjecture and not a constant improvement.
