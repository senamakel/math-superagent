## Formula

- a(0) = 1, a(n) = 2^(n-1).
- G.f.: (1 - x) / (1 - 2*x) = 1 / (1 - x / (1 - x)). - _Michael Somos_, Apr 18 2012
- E.g.f.: cosh(z)*exp(z) = (exp(2*z) + 1)/2.
- a(0) = 1 and for n>0, a(n) = sum of all previous terms.
- a(n) = Sum_{k=0..n} binomial(n, 2*k). - _Paul Barry_, Feb 25 2003
- a(n) = Sum_{k=0..n} binomial(n,k)*(1+(-1)^k)/2. - _Paul Barry_, May 27 2003
- a(n) = floor((1+2^n)/2). - Toby Bartels (toby+sloane(AT)math.ucr.edu), Aug 27 2003
- G.f.: Sum_{i>=0} x^i/(1-x)^i. - _Jon Perry_, Jul 10 2004

- a(n) = Sum_{k=0..floor(n/2)} A055830(n-k, k). - _Philippe Deléham_, Oct 22 2006
