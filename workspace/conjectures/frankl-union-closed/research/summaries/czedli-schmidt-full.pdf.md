Summary of what the paper establishes (full proof read, recorded in the
sibling note):
- **Theorem 1**: every finite *large* semimodular lattice
  (`|L| > 5·2^(m−3)`, `m=|J(L)|`) satisfies Frankl's conjecture; proof shows
  `|J(L)\A(L)| ≤ 1` then constructs an injection `↑a₁ → L\↑a₁`.
- **Theorem 2**: every finite *semimodular planar* lattice satisfies Frankl's
  conjecture; if `|L| ≥ 4` and `1` is join-reducible, two distinct
  join-irreducibles witness it.
- **Open**: whether all upper semimodular lattices satisfy the conjecture.

No separate claim blocks here — the claims `czedli-schmidt-large-semimodular`,
