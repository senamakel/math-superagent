## Theorem (from the arXiv full text)

For any nonempty union-closed `F ⊆ 2^[n]`, some `i ∈ [n]` is contained in at least `(3−√5)/2 ≈ 0.38` of the sets of `F`. One case is an explicit one-variable inequality checked by computer (with a numerical appendix to 10^-3, made rigorous by interval arithmetic). The inequality was conjectured by Gilmer.
