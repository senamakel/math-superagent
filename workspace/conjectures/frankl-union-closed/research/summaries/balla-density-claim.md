# Balla density bound — structured claim

```claim
id: balla-density
statement: For a union-closed family F with n = |∪F| ≥ 16, some element
  appears in at least sqrt((log₂ n)/n) * (|F|/2) sets of F. Equivalently the
  minimum density of union-closed families is asymptotically (log₂ n)/(2n)
  (Wójcik's conjecture, proved by Balla).
hypotheses: F finite union-closed, |∪F| = n ≥ 16.
holds-here: true
status: sourced
bearing: the density/large-family regime bound; one of the pre-Gilmer
  combinatorial bounds (via Reimer). Weakest exactly where the entropy line
  is strong; it is the regime the entropy method does not cover.
anchor: Balla, arXiv:1106.0369 (2011), Corollary 2 and Theorem 3
```
