```approach
idea: Second-moment / covariance method on the co-occurrence matrix — beyond the marginal densities that cap every entropy route. The iid and dependent-coupling certificates (AHS/Gilmer, Yu, Cambie, capped at ~0.382) are all *first-moment* arguments: they use only the single-coordinate densities p_x = d_x/m. The new object is the second moment: the co-occurrence counts c_xy = #{A ∈ F : x,y ∈ A}, i.e. the upper-triangle of the symmetric matrix C = M·Mᵀ (M the n×m incidence matrix), plus the pair identities Q = Σ_{x<y} c_xy = Σ_{A∈F} C(|A|,2) and Σ_{x} C(d_x,2). Union-closure forces c_xy to be bounded in terms of (d_x, d_y).

mechanism: Suppose a minimal counterexample: every d_x ≤ (m−1)/2. Write S = Σ_x d_x = Σ_A |A| and Q = Σ_{x<y} c_xy = Σ_A C(|A|,2). Two exact algebraic controls push against each other. (Lower bound from Jensen on Σ_x C(d_x,2) ≥ n·C(S/n,2).) (Upper bound from union-closure: closure bounds how concentrated the co-occurrence can be, forcing c_xy ≤ some concave f(d_x,d_y).) If the d_x are all below half, the forced c_xy are too small to support the Jensen lower bound. Endgame identity: Σ_x C(d_x,2) − Σ_A C(|A|,2) = the "overlap surplus"; if every d_x < m/2 forces this false, that is the theorem.

status: refuted

killed-by: two published results independently refute the proposed second-moment/overlap mechanism, and the core identity is already the known Reimer double-counting.

(1) THE STRICT FORCING CLAIM IS FALSE. David Ellis, "Union-Closed Families with Small Average Overlap Densities", Electron. J. Combin. 29(1):#P1.23 (2022), doi:10.37236/10121, constructs union-closed families whose average overlap density AOD(F) = E_{x,A}[1{x∈A}/|A|] is as small as Θ((log log|F|)/(log|F|)) for infinitely many n. AOD is a second-moment/overlap quantity, and it was conjectured (Polymath) that a universal lower bound on it would imply the union-closed conjecture; Ellis refutes that universal bound. So the specific mechanism "union-closure forces the overlap surplus large enough to hit an abundant element via Jensen" is not just unproved — it is false for a natural second-moment quantity. AOD is average abundance weighted by 1/|A| (not the bare |A∩B| overlap this candidate sums), so this refutes the *family* of second-moment-Jensen routes rather than the literal one-line identity; the literal identity (below) is already classical.

(2) THE IDENTITY IS ALREADY KNOWN — REIMER AND DOUBLE-COUNTING. The endgame identity Σ_x C(d_x,2) − Σ_A C(|A|,2) is second-moment double-counting of the same kind as the first-moment identity Σ_u |A_u| = Σ_A |A| (Bruhn–Schaudt survey, eq. 4, the "heart of the averaging method"), and the size-statistics on union-closed families are Reimer's theorem: average set size ≥ (1/2)log₂|F| (Reimer, "An Average Set Size Theorem", Combin. Probab. Comput. 12 (2003) 89–93), with the sharp minimum-average-size determination by Balla, Bollobás, Eccles, "Union-closed families of sets", JCTA 2013 (confirming Czédli–Maróti–Schmidt 2009's conjecture), giving the m ≥ 2^m−2^(m/2) large-family case. The "overlap surplus" is not a novel algebraic object; it is the pair version of the averaging method, and Raz's construction (Raz, EJC 2017, doi:10.37236/6989; + Lu–Raz arXiv:2405.10639) shows average-size-based conditions (Reimer's conditions) do NOT force the abundant element. Shan/Shi-Chao Wu, "Further analysis on the second frequency of union-closed set families" (arXiv:2412.03863) and Das–Wu "Frequent elements in union-closed set families" (arXiv:2412.03862) already attack second-frequency by LP over incidence patterns (exactly the c_xy object) and reach Nagel's 2-good bound |F|/(2^1+1)=|F|/3 — not |F|/2. So the second-moment machinery is already worked, and it stops at |F|/3, not |F|/2.

precedent: https://doi.org/10.37236/10121 (Ellis 2022, small average overlap densities — refutes the overlap-forces-abundance route), https://doi.org/10.37236/6989 (Raz 2017, Reimer's conditions do not force abundance), https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10639 (Lu–Raz), Reimer 2003 average-set-size (CPC 12(2) 89–93), Balla–Bollobás–Eccles JCTA 2013 (sharp min average size), arXiv:2412.03863 (Shi-Chao Wu second frequency), arXiv:2412.03862 (Das–Wu), Bruhn–Schaudt survey arXiv:1309.3297 eq. 4.

first-step: Not worth running as a route to abundance — the overlap/co-occurrence machinery is published and stops at |F|/3 (Nagel) / Θ(loglog/log) AOD (Ellis). The exact algebraic identity can still be verified computationally on n≤5 (it must hold as an integer identity), but it is Reimer's double-counting and cannot prove |F|/2.
```
