Define the convex family S of probability measures supported on F (a simplex), and the submanifold M of measures under which some coordinate has density exactly 1/2. Consider the KL-divergence projection of the empirical measure μ = Unif(F) onto M. BY STRICT CONVEXITY OF KL AND THE CSISZÁR–KEMPERMAN / CSISZÁR–ŠUJAN "Pythagorean" identity, if the projection is attained in the interior of one coordinate's half-abundance, complementary slackness forces a genuine coordinate with density ≥ 1/2 — i.e. UC follows from "the KL projection onto the 1/2-boundary always exits through a coordinate, never through a mixture."

Union-closure enters because the simplex over F is exactly the set of measures whose log-partition / moment structure is closed under the "OR of two members is in F" contract — convex duality (Lagrange dual / complementary slackness) turns "closest measure with all coordinates < 1/2" into a dual certificate that some coordinate's dual multiplier is positive, i.e. that coordinate's density is forced to 1/2. Distinct from the closed convex-weighting line (Murota M♮-concavity of a SET function, refuted as vacuous feasibility): here the convexity is on MEASURES/distributions and the certificate is the information-projection optimizer, not a set-function weight, and the named theorems (strict convexity of KL, Pythagorean projection) are specific and checkable. The left-submanifold structure is exact and even the interior/corner behaviour is decidable on small n.

status: proposed
