```approach
idea: The exact coordinate recursion via "charged" families and the multiset/rank generating polynomial — a coordinate-removal recurrence that counts abundance directly. Fix an element x. Partition F into the three pieces F_0 (sets avoiding x), F_x (sets containing x), F_x' = {A∖x : A ∈ F_x}. Union-closure of F is equivalent to: F_0 is union-closed, F_x' is union-closed, and F_0 ∪ F_x' is union-closed (the interaction constraint). Track the rank generating polynomial P_F(t), not just counts.

mechanism: x is abundant iff |F_x| ≥ |F_0|. Inductively F_0 and F_x' each have an abundant element inside [n]∖{x}; the question is whether the same y is abundant in both, in which case |F_y| = |(F_0)_y| + |(F_x')_y| + (interaction correction) is forced large. Hunting the falsifier: try to build a UC family where every y needs a negative correction. Bouchard's ucn-1 ⟹ ucn (arXiv:2310 generalised UC) shows the induction direction is live.

status: refuted

killed-by: the exact 3-way coordinate split is already the named "recursive decomposition" of the union-closed-family (Moore co-family) lattice in the literature, and it could not prove the conjecture there.

(1) THE REFORMULATION IS NOT NEW. Pierre Colomb, Alexis Irlande, Olivier Raynaud, Yoan Renaud, "A new generic class of Frankl's families" (2013) and "Recursive decomposition and bounds of the lattice of Moore co-families" (Annals of Math. and AI 67 (2013) 109–122, doi:10.1007/s10472-013-9345-y) decompose the set M_n of union-closed families (containing ∅) by the last element n−1 into M_sup (families all of whose sets contain n−1) and M_inf (the complementary union-closed family on U_{n−1}), with an f-map M_sup_n → M_{n−1} capturing exactly the "compatible"/interaction structure, and two bijections g1, g2 splitting M_sup into the with/without-singleton parts — precisely F_0, F_x, F_x' and the interaction constraint this candidate proposes. Poonen's "Union-closed families" (JCTA 59 (1992) 253–268) already proves UC for |∪F| ≤ 7 and |F| ≤ 28 by a coordinate-decomposition + subfamily-sufficiency theorem, which is the same recursion family.

(2) THE INDUCTION DOES NOT CLOSE. Colomb et al.'s entire paper is the attempt to prove Frankl's conjecture by structural induction on exactly this decomposition, and they only obtain a *proper class* of Frankl families (those arising from recursive combinations satisfying a "without-singleton" constraint), explicitly stating the full conjecture is not reached ("does not yet allow us to demonstrate by structural induction that the conjecture is true"). So the mechanism as posed — inductive abundance transfer across the split with interaction — is the known and already-attempted route, and it stops short. Bouchard's UC_{n−1} ⟹ UC_n (claim bouchard-ucn-minus1-to-ucn) confirms the induction direction is real but is itself a conditional/equivalence reduction, not a closing argument, and no source shows the interaction correction is controllable in the direction the candidate needs (the first-step itself admits the correction "must be negative" to get all-d_y<m/2, i.e. the mechanism is exactly what must fail, and no control for it is found).

(3) NEGATIVE CONTROL HOLDS but does not rescue: the 3-way split is sound and the interaction constraint is the genuine closure-use control, but soundness of the split is precisely what makes it the known decomposition, and it does not buy the abundance bound the literature already could not derive from it.

precedent: https://doi.org/10.15765/e.v1i1.184 (Colomb–Irlande–Raynaud–Renaud 2013, "A new generic class of Frankl's families" — the exact recursive decomposition), https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10472-013-9345-y (same authors, Moore co-families recursion, AMAI 67), Poonen JCTA 59 (1992) 253–268 (coordinate decomposition, |∪F|≤7 and |F|≤28 cases), claim bouchard-ucn-minus1-to-ucn (arXiv:2310 generalised UC, induction direction), Bruhn–Schaudt survey arXiv:1309.3297 (why simple induction fails: deleting basis sets need not decrease max element frequency, extremal families poorly understood).

first-step: The coordinate split itself is sound and worth keeping as a *representation* for exact abundance computation, but as a route to the conjecture it is the published decomposition that already stopped short. Do not re-derive it as if new. Any residual value is only in the specific "negative-correction obstruction" question, which none of the cited work explicitly addresses — say plainly so; but the reformulation being previously attempted with the same interaction mechanism means the burden is on showing what is genuinely new, and none is identified here.
```
