=== LESSON ===

(`python3 score.py candidates/<id>.py`, ONE module-path argument), never by
the old five-positional-float form. The earlier CALIBRATION invoked the
five-argument form, which is why a scorer that the searcher could not call
still passed calibration. The module-path form is what c0009/c0000/c0001 now
use; Yu's 0.38234 still certifies (SCORE: 0.3823435642), so the fix did not
break calibration.
