# Title: Universal quadratic forms and indecomposables in number fields: A survey

Authors: [Vítězslav Kala][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled Universal quadratic forms and indecomposables in number fields: A survey, by V\'it\v{e}zslav Kala

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: We give an overview of universal quadratic forms and lattices, focusing on the recent developments over the rings of integers in totally real number fields. In particular, we discuss indecomposable algebraic integers as one of the main tools.
