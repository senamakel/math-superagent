## What it claims

Abstract: We survey literature on all known families and examples of Dehn invariant zero tetrahedra. We also contribute two previously unknown families of Dehn invariant zero tetrahedra. Following a suggestion of Dill and Habegger, we show that there are finitely many Dehn invariant zero tetrahedra whose dihedral angles have a five-dimensional span over $\mathbb{Q}$, a first step towards classifying all Dehn invariant zero tetrahedra.
