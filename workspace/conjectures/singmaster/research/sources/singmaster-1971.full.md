## What is reliably known (from secondary attestation)

- Definition: N(a) = number of times a occurs as C(x,y). N(1)=∞; N(a)<∞ for a>1.
- Proposition: N(a) = O(log a). Proof: let b be first with C(2b,b)>a; monotonicity
  forces i<b or j<b; hence N(a) ≤ 2b ≤ 2 + 2·log₂ a.
- Conjecture: N(a) = O(1).
- "Added in proof": M(8)=3003, only N(a)≥8 with a<2^23; six N=6 values below
  2^23: 120, 210, 1540, 7140, 11628, 24310.
