# Title: A kit for linear forms in three logarithms

Authors: [Maurice Mignotte][3], [Paul Voutier][4] (with an appendix by Michel Laurent)

View a PDF of the paper titled A kit for linear forms in three logarithms, by Maurice Mignotte and 1 other authors

[View PDF][5] [HTML (experimental)][6]

Abstract: We provide a technique to obtain explicit bounds for problems that can be reduced to linear forms in three complex logarithms of algebraic numbers. This technique can produce bounds significantly better than general results on lower bounds for linear forms in logarithms. We give worked examples to demonstrate both the use of our technique and the improvements it provides. Publicly shared code is also available.
