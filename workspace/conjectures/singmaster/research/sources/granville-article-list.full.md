# Andrew Granville: Articles, reprints and preprints

This page gives access to most of my reprints and preprints. I have sorted the papers by subject area on this page, though here is an (unlinked) [list of publications][1].

Below is a list of general subject areas. Clicking on the most appropriate subject area will take you to my papers in that area. Clicking on the name of a paper will give you a postscript version of the paper. The TeX file is sometimes available.

##### (Warning: There may be some copyright problems if you use any published paper for anything other than your own scholarly use, so beware.)

Last updated: November 2000.
