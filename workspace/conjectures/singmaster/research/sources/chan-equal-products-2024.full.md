Abstract

In this article, we are interested in whether a product of three consecutive integers a(a +

some gaps between them, namely b ≫ a(log a)1/6

such as a2(a2 + l) dividing b2(b2 + l) for some ﬁxed integer l. Our method is based on eﬀective
Liouville-Baker-Feldman theorem.
