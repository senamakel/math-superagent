## Abstract

Tian’s conjecture states that for any fixed distinct prime numbers p 1 , … , p m , the Diophantine equation n + 1 2 = p 1 α 1 · p 2 α 2 · · · p m α m in positive integers n , α 1 , … , α m has at most m solutions. In this paper, we develop a computational method to verify some special cases of this conjecture. We also give an alternative proof using the classical Zsigmondy theorem. For m = 2 and 3, a sharp absolute upper bound for the number of solutions is given.
