# Title: Invariants of plane curve singularities and Newton diagrams

Authors: [Pierrette Cassou-Noguès][2], [Arkadiusz Płoski][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled Invariants of plane curve singularities and Newton diagrams, by Pierrette Cassou-Nogu\`es and Arkadiusz P{\l}oski

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: We present an intersection-theoretical approach to the invariants of plane curve singularities $\mu$, $\delta$, $r$ related by the Milnor formula $2\delta=\mu+r-1$. Using Newton transformations we give formulae for $\mu$, $\delta$, $r$ which imply planar versions of well-known theorems on nondegenerate singularities.
