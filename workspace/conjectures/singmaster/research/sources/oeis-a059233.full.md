Number of rows in which n appears in Pascal's triangle [A007318][4].

OFFSET

2,5

COMMENTS

Central binomial coefficients c = [A000984][12] (n) > 1 appear once in the middle column C(2n, n), and thereafter in one or more later rows to the left as C(r,k) and to the right as C(r, r-k), k < r/2; the last time in row r = c = C(c,1) = C(c,c-1). For these, a(n) = ( [A003016][13] (n)+1)/2. For all other numbers n > 1, a(n) = [A003016][13] (n)/2. - [M. F. Hasler][14], Mar 01 2023

FORMULA

a( [A180058][22] (n)) = n and a(m) < n for m < [A180058][22] (n); a( [A182237][23] (n)) = 2; a( [A098565][24] (n)) = 3. - [Reinhard Zumkeller][25], Dec 24 2012

a(n) = ceiling( [A003016][13] (n)/2). - [M. F. Hasler][14], Mar 01 2023

EXAMPLE

6 appears in both row 4 and row 6 in Pascal's triangle, therefore a(6) = 2.
