## Formula

- a(n) = (1/10)*((5+sqrt(5))*((7-3*sqrt(5))/2)^n + ((5-sqrt(5))*((7+3*sqrt(5))/2)^n)).
- a(n) = sqrt(1 - 2*F(2n+1)*F(2n+2) + 5*(F(2n+1)*F(2n+2))^2), where F = A000045.
- a(n) = sqrt(1 - 2*A081016(n) + 5*A081016(n)^2).
- a(n) = A033891(n-1), n>0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Feb 08 2010
- a(n) = (Lucas(4*n) - Fibonacci(4*n))/2, where Lucas = A000032. - _Gary Detlefs_, Nov 28 2010
- G.f.: (1 - 5*x)/(1 - 7*x + x^2). - _Bruno Berselli_, Mar 29 2016
- a(n) = Fibonacci(4*n-1). - _G. C. Greubel_, Jul 15 2019
- a(n) = (a(n-1)^2 + 9)/a(n-2). - _Klaus Purath_, Aug 30 2020
- For n > 0, a(n) = (A033889(n-1) + A033889(n))/3 = (Fibonacci(4*n-3) + Fibonacci(4*n+1))/3. - _Klaus Purath_, Nov 26 2025
- E.g.f.: exp(7*x/2)*(5*cosh(3*sqrt(5)*x/2) - sqrt(5)*sinh(3*sqrt(5)*x/2))/5. - _Stefano Spezia_, Jan 24 2026

## Recurrence and program

- with(combinat):F:= n-> fibonacci(n):L:=n-> 2*F(n+1)-F(n):
- seq(1/2*(L(4*n)-F(4*n)), n=0..20); # _Gary Detlefs_, Nov 28 2010

## Comments

- (x, y) = (a(n), a(n+1)) are solutions of ((x + y)^2)/(x*y - 1) = 9. The other solutions are in A033889.
