## What is confirmed on disk (verbatim)

- The glossary entry for the binomial coefficient symbol:
  `(~)  n choose k; the binomial coefficient n!/k!(n-k)!` — cross-referenced
  to problem entries **B31, B33, C10, D3**.
- The book is the 2nd (1994) edition; problem **B31** is the "Binomial
  coefficients" section (contents list shows "B31. Binomial coefficients" at
  p. 84 in the 1994 numbering; the later 3rd edition lists it at p. 129 with
  B32 Grimm, B33 Largest divisor of a binomial coefficient, B34).

## What this fixes

- The problem-collection entry for Singmaster's question ("How often does an
  integer occur as a binomial coefficient?") is **B31**, not D14. D14 is "a_i +
  b_j made squares", a different problem; earlier frontier text and the search
  chain conflated the two. Any future citation of "Guy's problem D14" about
  Singmaster is wrong; cite **B31**.
- The library now holds the canonical problem-collection volume (albeit
  partially): the B31/B32/B33/B34 cluster is the standard encyclopedic
  framing of the binomial-coefficient unsolved problems, of which Singmaster's
  conjecture (B31) is the flagship.
