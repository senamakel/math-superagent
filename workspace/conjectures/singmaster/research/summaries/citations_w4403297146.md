# Citation graph — Repeated Binomial Coefficients and Fibonacci Numbers

**David Singmaster** (1975). The Fibonacci Quarterly. Cited 20 times.

## Abstract

In this note, I show that there are infinitely many solutions to the equation given by n = F2i+2F2i+3 - 1, k = F2iF2i+3 - 1, where Fn is the nth Fibonacci number, beginning with F0 = 0. This gives infinitely many binomial coefficients occurring at least 6 times. The method and results of a computer search for repeated binomial coefficients, up to 248:, will be given.
