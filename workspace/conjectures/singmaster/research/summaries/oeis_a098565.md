# OEIS A098565 / A003015 — the six- and eight-fold multiplicity sequences

## Finding

`oeis_lookup` on `[120, 210, 1540, 7140, 11628, 24310, 3003]` returned no match.
That is expected and informative: **the list mixes two different catalogued
sequences.**

- The six `N(a)=6` values `120, 210, 1540, 7140, 11628, 24310` plus the giant
  Fibonacci-family member `61218182743304701891431482520` are **OEIS A098565**:
  "Numbers that appear as binomial coefficients exactly 6 times" (each has
  `A059233 = 3`, i.e. three nontrivial pairs incl. mirrors).
- The `8`-fold record `3003` is a **separate** entry (3003 appears exactly 8
  times). Cross-linked via A003015 (numbers that appear at least n times) and
  A098564.

## Consequence

There is **no closed form** catalogued for either sequence beyond the known
structural identity. The structure is not an OEIS recurrenced sequence but the
**Fibonacci/Pell family** (Singmaster 1975 / Lind 1968 / Tovey 1985): the infinite
solutions of `C(n+1,k+1) = C(n,k+2)` with `n = F_{2j+2}F_{2j+3}-1`,
`k = F_{2j}F_{2j+3}-1`. So a lookup will never provide the closed form; it has to
come from the Pell equation `u^2 - 5v^2 = -4`.

This is recorded so nobody re-runs this lookup. Source: OEIS A098565 (via search),
concordant with the library's MRSTT Remark 1.4 / Singmaster 1975.
Evidence class: sourced.
