# OEIS A182237 — numbers occurring exactly in 2 rows of Pascal's triangle

Source: https://oeis.org/A182237 (Zumkeller, Dec 2012; Noe 2013). Full text:
`research/sources/oeis-a182237.full.md`.

## What it records

A182237 = {a : a059233(a) = 2} — numbers appearing in exactly two rows (the
generic case: one interior occurrence which by mirror symmetry occupies its
two positions on two distinct rows... precisely: a = C(n,k) with k < n/2
appears in row n twice by symmetry, and in row a once trivially). First terms:
6, 10, 15, 20, 21, 28, 35, 36, 45, 55, 56, 66, 70, 78, 84, 91, ...

## Bearing

Catalogue record of the multiplicity-2 row-count class; no new bound. Relevant
only as the generic-case complement to A098565 (row-count 3) and A180058
(least per class). No claim needed beyond the sourced record; included because
the Erdős Problem 849 "External data" cluster lists it and A059233's formula
uses the same datestamp (Zumkeller 2012-12-24, co-authored with A180058).
