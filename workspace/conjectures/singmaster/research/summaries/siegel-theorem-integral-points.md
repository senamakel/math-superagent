## What it claims

The theorem was first proved in 1929 by [Carl Ludwig Siegel][4] and was the first major result on [Diophantine equations][5] that depended only on the genus and not any special algebraic form of the equations. For *g*> 1 it was superseded by [Faltings' theorem][6] in 1983.
