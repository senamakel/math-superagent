## What it is

A **slide deck** (probably Kane 2004 talk material), NOT a primary theorem paper. It
recaps the same material as Kane 2007 (`research/summaries/kane-combinations2.md`,
the definitive primary text): the `O((log t)(log₃t)/(log₂t)³)` bound, the method
(view `n` as convex function of `m`, count lattice points, use derivative bounds +
Rolle/interpolation), case splits by `α = log n/log m`, and the lcm `B` count.

## Bearing

**Does not establish anything the run lacks.** All numerical and structural claims
it sketches are stated more rigorously and with a full proof in the Kane 2007 full
text, which this run holds and has digested. The slide deck adds no new theorem,
no constant, and no bound not already in Kane 2007. **Verdict**: redundant
secondary exposition; do not cite it in place of the paper.
