# OEIS A138496 — Where record values occur in A003016

Source: https://oeis.org/A138496 (converted from HTML, held at
`research/sources/oeis-a138496.full.md`)

## What the sequence is

A003016(n) = number of occurrences of *n* as an entry in rows ≤ n of Pascal's
triangle (i.e. how many times the integer *n* appears). A138496 lists the
arguments *n* at which a **new record** of A003016 is set:

```
0, 1, 10, 120, 3003
```

i.e. the record multiplicity of A003016 is achieved successively at
n = 0, 1, 10, 120, 3003.

## What it corroborates (this run's witnesses)

- **3003 is the record** for A003016 — the highest known multiplicity, N(3003)=8
  under both-mirrors-and-trivial counting. Consistent with
  `code/out/witnesses.json` and the primary Singmaster FQ 1975 statement.
- **120 is a record** — the first of the N=6 set {120, 210, 1540, 7140, 11628,
  24310} (the other five ≤ 2^48 do not pass 120's record, since the record moves
  from 120 straight to 3003).
- **10** is the record before 120 (10 = C(5,2) = C(6,1)? — the small-multiplicity
  regime).

## Author comment (M. F. Hasler, Feb 2023)

The numbers appearing 3 times are the central binomial coefficients (A000984)
except 2 (which appears once); all C(n,k) with 1<k<n/2 appear at least 4 times
(triangular numbers appear via multiplicity). Numbers appearing 5+ times are
rare — listed in A003015, with subsequence A098565 of those appearing exactly 6
times. Some are C(n,k) triangular-and-central; some are of the form
C(n+1,k)=C(n,k+1), and "a subsequence of these has n and k given in terms of
Fibonacci numbers" — i.e. the infinite Singmaster/Lind/Tovey family that the run
already holds verified.
