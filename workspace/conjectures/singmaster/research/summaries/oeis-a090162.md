# OEIS A090162 — values of the Singmaster/Lind infinite family (catalogue record)

Source: https://oeis.org/A090162 (Weisstein 2003, Lang 2003; comments by
Pfoertner, Hasler). Full text: `research/sources/oeis-a090162.full.md`.

## What it records

a(n) = C( F(2n)*F(2n+1), F(2n-1)*F(2n) - 1 ), n >= 1. Terms:
**1, 3003, 61218182743304701891431482520**, next ≈ 3.537·10^204 (b-file has 5
terms). These are values known to occur **at least six times** in Pascal's
triangle — the catalogue record of the same infinite N(a) >= 6 family this run
derives from Singmaster FQ 1975 (`fibonacci-n6-family`,
`infinite-family-6`).

Check against the run's computed members (recorded values; a fresh exact
recomputation is a compute-role task):
- a(2) = C(F(4)F(5), F(3)F(4)-1) = C(15,5) = **3003** (the N=8 witness);
- a(3) = C(F(6)F(7), F(5)F(6)-1) = C(104,39) = **61218182743304701891431482520**
  (the run's second family member, from `code/family_seq/family_sequences.py`).

## Independent reference it carries

The entry cites A. I. Shirshov, "On the equation C(n,m) = C(n+1, m-1)",
chapter 10 in *Kvant Selecta: Algebra and Analysis I* (AMS 1999) 83–86 — a
third, independent treatment of the exact family equation (beside the held
Lind 1968 and Singmaster 1975 primaries). Not yet in the library.
