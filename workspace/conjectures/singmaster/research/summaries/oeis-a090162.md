A090162

Values of binomial(Fibonacci(2k)*Fibonacci(2k+1),Fibonacci(2k-1)*Fibonacci(2k)-1).

7

1, 3003, 61218182743304701891431482520

( [list][4]; [graph][5]; [refs][6]; [listen][7]; [history][8]; [text][9]; [internal format][10])

OFFSET

1,2

COMMENTS

These numbers are known to occur at least six times in Pascal's triangle.

The next term is approximately 3.537 * 10^204 and is in the b-file.

The numbers of digits in a(n), n >= 1, are given in [A100022][11].

FORMULA

a(n) = binomial( [A089508][18] (n), [A081016][19] (n-1)).

a(n) = binomial( [A089508][18] (n)+1, [A081016][19] (n-1)-1).

a(n) = Gamma(x)/(Gamma(y)*Gamma(1+x-y)) with x = [A206351][20] (n+1) and y = [A081016][19] (n-1). - [Peter Luschny][21], Jul 15 2017

MAPLE

a := proc(n) local a, b, s, p; s:= 1+sqrt(5); p:=16^n;

a := 4-2*p*s^(-4*n-1)+(s+2)*s^(4*n-1)/p:

b := 1+p*((s-2)^(1-4*n)/2-s^(-1-4*n)*(2+s)):

GAMMA(a/5)/(GAMMA(b/5)*GAMMA(1+(a-b)/5)) end:

digits := [1, 4, 29, 205, 1412]: A := n -> round(evalf(a(n), digits[n]+10)):

A(4); # [Peter Luschny][21], Jul 15 2017
