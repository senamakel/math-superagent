## How to close a row

- **Literature gap**: give the request_research `need` a fetchable source URL (seen in a search result, in FRONTIER.md, or in a held document — never invented), download it under research/sources/ with a descriptive name, summarize it with a claim block, then strike the row here.
- **Compute/proof gap**: hand it to the owning role (tool_builder/symbolic_math/theorem_prover); the row closes when a capture or a proof note lands in code/out/ or research/notes/.
- **Blocked**: mark with the blocker and the reason the blocker was confirmed, so nobody retries pointlessly.
