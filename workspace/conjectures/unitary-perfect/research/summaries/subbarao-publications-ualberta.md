# M.V. Subbarao — complete publication list (U. Alberta)

Full text: `research/sources/subbarao-publications-ualberta.full.md`.

## What it is

The author's own publication list at the University of Alberta
(`https://www.math.ualberta.ca/~subbarao/publications.html`), ~140 entries
spanning 1942–2007, each with exact venue/volume/pages and, for nearly every
item, a direct PDF link under `documents/<name>.pdf`. This is a **primary
bibliographic anchor**: it confirms shelf data for the papers this run cites
from secondary sources and gives working PDF URLs.

## Entries bearing directly on this problem

1. **Subbarao, *Are there an infinity of unitary perfect (perface) numbers?*,
   Amer. Math. Monthly 77 (1970) 389–390** — link `documents/1970Infinity.pdf`.
   This is the note that (per three Wall papers, all held) eliminated
   `a = 8, 9, 10` for a sixth unitary perfect number and hence established the
   run's `a ≥ 11` claim (`subbarao1970-a-ge-11`, still `asserted`).
   **The ualberta PDF is a JSTOR cover page only** (stable URL
   `https://www.jstor.org/stable/2316150`) — the body is not in this library;
   do not cite this file as the note's text.
2. **Subbarao et al., *On unitary perfect numbers*, Delta 3 (1972) 22–26** —
   link `documents/Subbarao_Cook_Newberry_Weber1972.pdf`. The Delta note (seed
   compression; the run's FRONTIER-cited chain item). The PDF has **no text
   layer** (scanned); OCR would be needed.
3. **Sitaramaiah & Subbarao, *On the equation σ*(σ*(n)) = 2n*, Utilitas
   Mathematica 53 (1998) 101–124** — link `documents/Sitaramaiah_Subbarao1998.pdf`.
   An adjacent non-UPN equation (iterated unitary sigma); not yet fetched, not
   load-bearing.
4. **Subbarao & Warren, *Unitary perfect numbers*, Canad. Math. Bull. 9 (1966)
   147–153** — held in full (three copies in `research/sources/`).
5. Sitaramaiah & Subbarao, *On unitary multiperfect numbers*, Nieuw Archief
   Wisk. 16 (1998) 57–61 — adjacent multiperfect class; not load-bearing.
