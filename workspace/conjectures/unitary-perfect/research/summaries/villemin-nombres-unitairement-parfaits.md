# Villemin, *Nombres unitairement parfaits* (pop-science page)

Full text: [[villemin-nombres-unitairement-parfaits.full]].

French popular page: definition of unitary divisor and unitary perfect number;
worked examples 60 and 90; the list of the five known UPNs with the fifth's
factorization `2^18 · 3 · 5^4 · 7 · 11 · 13 · 19 · 37 · 79 · 109 · 157 · 313`,
"discovered by C. R. Wall in 1966[!]"; the finiteness conjecture; links to
Wikipedia/MathWorld/OEIS.

**Value.** None beyond the encyclopedic tier already held (OEIS A002827 internal
format has the same factorization with correct attribution Wall 1975). The
"1966" for the fifth UPN is an error on the page (Wall's fifth was published
1975; the abstract listed it 1969 — see `research/summaries/oeis-a002827-unitary-perfect.md`).
The five-witness factorization matches the run's oracle exactly.
Do not cite for the discovery date.
