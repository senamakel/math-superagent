who  do  "hard  sums".  Even  inside  the  profession  we  dassify  ouselves  as
either  theorists  or  problem  solvers.  Mathematics  is  kept  alive,  much  more
than  by  the  activities  of  either  dass,  by  the  appearance  of  a  succession
of  unsolved  problems,  both  from  within  mathematics  itself  and  from  the
increasing  number  of  disciplines  where  it  is  applied.  Mathematics  often
owes  more  to  those  who  ask  questions  than  to  those  who  answer  them.
