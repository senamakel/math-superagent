# Unitary Perfect Number

---

[image: DOWNLOAD Mathematica Notebook] [Download Wolfram Notebook][1]

A unitary perfect number is a number [image: n] which is the sum of its [unitary divisors][2] with the exception of [image: n] itself. There are no [odd][3] unitary perfect numbers, and it has been conjectured that there are only a [finite][4] number of [even][5] ones. The first few are 6, 60, 90, 87360, 146361946186458562560000, ... (OEIS [A002827][6]).
