# Oracle: self-contained sound upper-bound oracle (upper_ok) in

# Search space: radius r enumerates the complete product of all

#   or free bits > 2^20 (bit boundary), or wall clock, or a stable
#   fixpoint from which no further radius grows.

## Interpretation
  A nonzero survivor count at a radius PROVES the seed extends to
  that radius under the sound criterion (complete enumeration).
  A zero would be the first genuine local obstruction.  A stable
  fixpoint that keeps adding no vertices means the seed extends
  locally to every radius (no local obstruction, ever).
  This is a LOCAL statement: it neither proves nor disproves the
  global existence of srg(99,14,1,2).
