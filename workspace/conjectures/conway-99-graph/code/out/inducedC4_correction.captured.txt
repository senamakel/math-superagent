=== induced-C4 correction (pattern_finder, exact) ===
Report 10 Finding 2 claimed induced C4 = C(mu,2)*#nonedges (= #nonedges in mu=2).
That is WRONG by a factor of 2: each induced C4 has TWO nonedge pairs (opposite
vertex pairs), so summing C(mu,2) over the #nonedges nonedges double-counts.

Correct identity (proved from c7 + double-counting):
   induced C4 = (1/2) * C(mu,2) * #nonedges   for every srg(v,k,1,2).

Proof: (a) c7 (proved in report 10, derived from lambda=1): common neighbours
of a nonedge pair are pairwise nonadjacent. (b) each (nonedge, nonadjacent-cn-
pair) gives one induced C4 (the cn pair + the nonedge's two endpoints). (c) each
induced C4 is produced by exactly 2 of its nonedges. Hence induced C4 =
(1/2)*sum over nonedges of C(mu,2) = (1/2)*C(mu,2)*#nonedges.  [EXACT, PROOF]
