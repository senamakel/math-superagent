# A218293 / A219272 — Standard Young tableaux (distinct parts) — NOTE: not related to the 99-graph family

**Source**: https://oeis.org/A218293, https://oeis.org/A219272.

A218293 = number of standard Young tableaux with shapes corresponding to
partitions into distinct parts; A219272 = the same, with largest part ≤ k
(triangle read by columns).

## Why it is here and what it does NOT establish

These OEIS rows were pulled in an automated lookup. **They are unrelated to the
srg(99,14,1,2) family.** The only intersection is a *coincidence*: the diagonal /
column entries of A218293/A219272 include the values **1, 3, 4, 10, 31** at small
positions, which are the same integers that appear as `u ∈ {1,3,4,10,31}` in the
run's divisor-63 characterization of the srg(v,k,1,2) family (claim
`divisor63-multiplicity-integrality`, k = u²+u+2). That is a numerical
coincidence, NOT a mathematical connection: the SYT sequences count tableaux, and
no theorem links them to srg parameter feasibility. Do not build on it.

The family's own vertex counts (v ∈ {9,99,243,6273,494019}) are NOT a catalogued
OEIS sequence — recorded in `research/notes/oeis-miss-family-vertex-counts.md`.

## Status
`catalogued` (read from OEIS, not derived); **does not help** this problem — filed
so nobody re-reads it expecting a connection.
