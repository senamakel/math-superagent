# Cesarz & Woldar, "On the automorphism group of a putative Conway 99-graph" (arXiv:2308.02978)

## Established (full statement, from the abstract; consistent across both)

Let Γ be a Conway 99-graph = srg(99,14,1,2), G = Aut(Γ).
- (Makhnev–Minakova 2004) |G| divides 2·3³·7·11; if |G| even then |G| | 42.
- **New here:** if 7 | |G| then G ≅ Z₇; consequently if 2 | |G| then |G| | 6,
  i.e. G ∈ {Z₂, Z₆, S₃}.
- **No order-14 automorphism.**

## Status nuance (the sole difference between versions)
The arXiv v1/v2's elimination of the Frobenius group Frob(21) (order 21) is
**computer-assisted**; the published ALCO paper reports the 2| and 7| claims as
**computer-free**. Flagged so the run quotes the computer-free version when it
matters.

## Implication
Directly answers GOAL's "which automorphism orders are excluded, by whom".
Combined with Crnković–Maksimović 2020 (no Z₆, S₃, Z₉, E₉) the automorphism
group of any (99,14,1,2) is very small — likely trivial — which kills
symmetry-assuming construction searches.
