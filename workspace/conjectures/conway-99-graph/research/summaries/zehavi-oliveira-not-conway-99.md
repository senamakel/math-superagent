# Zehavi & Oliveira, "Not Conway's 99-Graph Problem" (arXiv:1707.08047)

**Availability:** Only the arXiv abstract landing page is in the library (`research/sources/zehavi-oliveira-not-conway-99.full.md`); the paper body was not downloaded. This note records what the abstract states and what it implies — and, importantly, what it does NOT do.

## What the abstract establishes

Quotes Conway's definition of the 99-graph problem and gives an **alternative interpretation** of it, humorously named "not Conway's 99-graph problem", which is then solved completely. The alternative is a variant, not the actual 99-graph question.

## Implication here — boundary work, not a route

This paper does **not** solve or approach Conway's problem; it solves a differently-worded variant. Its value here is as a negative boundary: it shows how slight rewording of Conway's property produces a solvable (and different) problem, which is a caution that any reformulation must be checked against the exact srg(99,14,1,2) parameters. It does not help establish existence or nonexistence of (99,14,1,2). Not a route.

## Does not settle

Anything about the existence of srg(99,14,1,2). The precise definition of the variant it solves is not in the library (abstract only), so even the boundary caution cannot be made exact from this file.
