## The one thing to keep here

The classification (Thm 8.4) assigns (99,14,1,2) to the **exception bucket**
(E_m, m=4, since λ_min = −4) — **not** to a position the theorem forces to have
H₁ = 0. So the phrase "the classification forces H₁(99) = 0" (round-33 /
n3-positive-global.md) is stronger than the source. The gate's refuted-on-arrival
verdict rests on the computed controls (H₁ ≠ 0 on both rook(3)=4 and
bvls=1540), which is correct.
