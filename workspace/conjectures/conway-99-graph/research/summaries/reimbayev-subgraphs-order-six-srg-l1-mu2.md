## What the abstract establishes

For the family of strongly regular graphs with λ=1, μ=2, establishes **all
62 induced subgraphs of order six** (and the order ≤5 lists), each an explicit
closed form in (n,k) plus a single parameter n_3. The abstract itself gives
none of the formulas.
