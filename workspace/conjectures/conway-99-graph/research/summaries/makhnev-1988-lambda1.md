**Theorem 1** (Makhnev 1988): a λ=1 SRG satisfying condition (*) [pairs of
triangles joined by ≥2 edges are joined by exactly 3 = Reimbayev's n_3=0] is

builds an srg(33,12,1,6) subobject from a triangle's closure plus its 60
exterior points, contradicting Theorem 1 (μ=6>3).
