## What the abstract establishes

Work in the general family of strongly regular graphs with λ=1, μ=2 (the family
containing (9,4), (99,14), (243,22), (6273,112), (494019,994)). It establishes a
**lower bound for the number of hexagons (6-cycles)** in such a graph, and by
doing so shows a connection between the existence of srg(99,14,1,2) and the
number of its hexagons. The abstract states no explicit numeric bound.

## Verdict
Use the body summary (`reimbayev-hexagon-bound-body.md`): the bound is
`n12 >= (1/12)n k(k-2)(2k^2-21k+53)`, attained iff n3=0, and the hexagon
identity is verified exactly on both controls by this run. This landing page
adds nothing further.
