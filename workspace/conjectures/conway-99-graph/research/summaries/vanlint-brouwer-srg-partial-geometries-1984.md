## What the source is (from metadata and search, not from the download)

*Enumeration and Design* (D.M. Jackson & S.A. Vanstone eds.), Academic Press (1984),
pp. 85–122. The survey chapter that relates strongly regular graphs to partial geometries
and partial linear spaces — the exact geometric framing this problem lives in (a
