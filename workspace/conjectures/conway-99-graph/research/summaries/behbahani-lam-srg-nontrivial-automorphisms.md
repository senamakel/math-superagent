## What it is

Majid Behbahani, Clement Lam, "Strongly regular graphs with non-trivial
automorphisms," Discrete Math. 311 (2011) 132-144, doi 10.1016/j.disc.2010.10.005.
Full text NOT in library (paywalled, no open arXiv). This run's only safe arXiv
guess resolved to a physics paper, so this record exists to stop anyone
re-downloading the wrong id.

## Why it matters

Introduced the **orbit-matrix** method for SRGs with a prime-order automorphism
group — the foundation for the (99,14,1,2) automorphism constraints that
Crnković–Maksimović and Cesarz–Woldar build on. The actual automorphism
constraints for the 99-graph are in-library in those two papers.
