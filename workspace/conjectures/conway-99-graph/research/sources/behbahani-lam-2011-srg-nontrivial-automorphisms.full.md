# Behbahani & Lam (2011), "Strongly regular graphs with non-trivial automorphisms"

**Correct identity:**
- Majid Behbahani, Clement Lam, "Strongly regular graphs with non-trivial
  automorphisms," *Discrete Mathematics* **311** (2011) no. 2-3, pp. 132-144.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.disc.2010.10.005  |  MR 2739917

## What this paper establishes, as verified from the papers that cite it

The primary copy of this paper could not be placed in the library this run
(no open arXiv version; the DOI resolves to a paywalled Springer page). The
following findings about what it establishes are verified from secondary
primary sources already in this library, chiefly Crnković–Maksimović (2020)
and Cesarz–Woldar (2025):

1. Behbahani and Lam introduced the **orbit-matrix** method for strongly
   regular graphs admitting an automorphism group of *prime order*, plus an
   algorithm to reconstruct the SRG from an orbit matrix. (Confirmed by
   Crnković–Maksimović 2020, abstract.)
2. They conjectured/studied which SRGs can have non-trivial automorphisms, and
   framed the question of SRGs with prescribed automorphism — the method that
   the later (99,14,1,2) automorphism results build on. (Confirmed by Cesarz–
   Woldar 2025, which lists Behbahani–Lam among the "predecessors" whose
   results it re-proves computer-free.)

## Where the real content lives in this library

The *directly relevant* automorphism results for the 99-graph are covered by
primary sources this run DID place:
- **Cesarz–Woldar 2025** (`automorph-putative-conway-99-graph.full.md`):
  computer-free proofs that 2 | |G| ⟹ |G| | 6, and 7 | |G| ⟹ G ≅ Z₇.
- **Crnković–Maksimović 2020** (`crnkovic-maksimovic-composite-automorphism.full.md`):
  no SRG(99,14,1,2) with automorphism group of order 6 or 9 (Z₆, S₃, Z₉, E₉);
  order of any such group is 2^a·3^b with b ∈ {0,1}.
- **Makhnev–Minakova 2004** (cited throughout; paywalled, not yet placed):
  |G| divides 2·3³·7·11.
