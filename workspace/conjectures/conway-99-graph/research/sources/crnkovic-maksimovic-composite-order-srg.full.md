## The real paper

Dean Crnković, Marija Maksimović, "Construction of strongly regular graphs
having an automorphism group of composite order," *Contributions to Discrete
Mathematics* 15(1) (2020) 22-41. DOI 10.55016/ojs/cdm.v15i1.62323.

**Verified findings directly relevant to the 99-graph (from the abstract, in
library at `crnkovic-maksimovic-composite-automorphism.full.md`):**

- Generalises Behbahani–Lam orbit matrices to automorphism groups of composite
  order.
- **Theorem 7.2: There is no SRG(99,14,1,2) having an automorphism group of
  order six.**
- **Theorem 7.3: If an SRG(99,14,1,2) exists, the order of its full
  automorphism group is 2^a·3^b with b ∈ {0,1}; an order-3 automorphism has no
  fixed points; and there is no such graph with automorphism group Z₉ or E₉.**
- Together these rule out automorphism groups isomorphic to Z₆, S₃, Z₉, E₉.
