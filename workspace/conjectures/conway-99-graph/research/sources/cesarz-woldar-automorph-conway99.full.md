# Title: On the automorphism group of a putative Conway 99-graph

Authors: [Patrick G. Cesarz][2], [Andrew J. Woldar][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled On the automorphism group of a putative Conway 99-graph, by Patrick G. Cesarz and 1 other authors

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: Let $\Gamma$ be a {Conway 99-graph}, that is, a strongly regular graph with parameters $(99,14,1,2)$. In Makhnev and Minakova (On automorphisms of strongly regular graphs with parameters $\lambda =1$, $\mu= 2$, Discrete Math.\ Appl.\ 14 (2) (2004) 201-210), the authors prove that the automorphism group $G$ of $\Gamma$ must have order dividing $2\cdot 3^3\cdot 7\cdot 11$. They further show that if $|G|$ is divisible by $2$ then $|G|$ must divide $42$. In the present paper, we refine these results by proving that divisibility by $7$ implies $G \cong\mathbb Z_7$. As a consequence, divisibility by $2$ implies $|G|$ divides $6$, \ie $G$ is isomorphic to one of $\mathbb Z_2, \mathbb Z_6, S_3$.
