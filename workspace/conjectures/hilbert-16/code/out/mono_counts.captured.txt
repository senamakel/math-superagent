## Honest analysis (all exact over the terms supplied)

- L_d is homogeneous of degree h = d-2 in the 5 parameters (A,C,D,E,F). The
  number of monomials of general degree-h homogeneous polynomial in 5
  variables is dim = C(h+4,4).  a_d/dim:
      4/15≈0.267, 30/70≈0.429, 97/210≈0.462, 236/495≈0.477, 485/1001≈0.484,
      890/1820≈0.489.
  i.e. the obstruction fills ~half of the monomial space and the fraction is
  creeping toward ~1/2. This is an OBSERVED trend over 6 terms, not a theorem;
  no derivation.
- analyze_sequence: differences do not become constant within 5 levels → not a
  low-degree polynomial; leading ratios → 1.8..7.5, consistent with slow
  polynomial-in-degree growth, not exponential.
- find_linear_recurrence over these 6 terms returned a *rational-coefficient*
  order-3 fit (a(n) = 51814/10257 a(n-1) - 95987/10257 a(n-2) + 68576/10257
  a(n-3)). This is exactly the documented false-positive trap (see
  research/findings/sequence-tool-validation-sk.md): rational-coefficient fits
  on a handful of terms of a large-coefficient sequence do not survive. NOT
  reported as a regularity.
- OEIS: [4,30,97,236,485,890] → no match (recorded, so nobody searches again).

## Falsifier / what a bigger run would settle

A larger run (d=16,18,20) would test whether a_d/dim → 1/2 and whether some
low-order recurrence ever holds. d16 costs ~25-30 min (each degree is ~4x the
previous wall time). No derivation path to such a recurrence exists a priori:
a_d is just the sparsity of a coefficient of the recurrence, so a clean closed
form is NOT expected. Do not spend the run chasing it.

## Bottom line

The missing CONTEXT.md numbers (axis 8/10/12 monomial counts, and 14) are now
computed and recorded here: 97, 236, 485, 890. The sequence shows no clean
structural regularity over the terms supplied; the only apparent trend (~half
the monomial space, fraction approaching 1/2) is a weak 6-term observation and
is recorded as such, not as a claim.
