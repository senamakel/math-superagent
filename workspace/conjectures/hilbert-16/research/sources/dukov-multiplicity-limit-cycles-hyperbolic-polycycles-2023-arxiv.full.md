# Title: Multiplicity of limit cycles that appear after perturbations of hyperbolic polycycles

Authors: [Andrei Dukov][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled Multiplicity of limit cycles that appear after perturbations of hyperbolic polycycles, by Andrei Dukov

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: We consider the multiplicity of limit cycles that appear when a hyperbolic polycycle is perturbed. We prove, in particular, that if such unfolding happens in generic finite-parameter families, the multiplicity of every new limit cycle does not exceed the number of separatrix connections in the polycycle.
