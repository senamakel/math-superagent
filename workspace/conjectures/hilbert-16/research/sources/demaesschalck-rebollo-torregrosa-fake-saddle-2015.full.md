[Cyclicity of a fake saddle inside the quadratic vector fields][23]
[De Maesschalck, Peter][24] [25] (Hasselt University (Belgium))
[Torregrosa, Joan][26] [27] (Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona. Departament de Matemàtiques)
[Rebollo Perdomo, Salomon][28] [29] (Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona. Centre de Recerca per a l'Educació Científica i Matemàtica)

Data: | 2015 |

Resum: | This paper concerns the study of small-amplitude limit cycles that appear in the phase portrait near an unfolded fake saddle singularity. This degenerate singularity is also known as a impassable grain. The normal form of the unperturbed vector field is like a degenerate flow box. Near the singularity,the phase portrait consists of parallel fibers, all of which but one have no singular points, and at the singular fiber, there is one node. We study different techniques in order to show that the cyclicity is bigger or equal than two when the normal form is quadratic.  |
