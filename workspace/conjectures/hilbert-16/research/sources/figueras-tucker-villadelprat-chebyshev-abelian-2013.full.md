[Figueras, Jordi-Lluís][24] (Uppsala University. Department of Mathematics)
[Tucker, Warwick][25] (Uppsala University. Department of Mathematics)
[Villadelprat Yagüe, Jordi][26] [27] (Universitat Rovira i Virgili. Departament d'Enginyeria Informàtica i Matemàtiques)

Data: | 2013 |

Resum: | We develop techniques for the verification of the Chebyshev property of Abelian integrals. These techniques are a combination of theoretical results, analysis of asymptotic behavior of Wronskians, and rigorous computations based on interval arithmetic. We apply this approach to tackle a conjecture formulated by Dumortier and Roussarie in [Birth of canard cycles, Discrete Contin. Dyn. Syst. 2 (2009), 723-781], which we are able to prove for q < 2.  |
