# Title: Limit cycles of planar vector fields: Hilbert's 16th problem and o-minimality

Authors: [Patrick Speissegger][2]

View a PDF of the paper titled Limit cycles of planar vector fields: Hilbert's 16th problem and o-minimality, by Patrick Speissegger

[View PDF][3] [HTML (experimental)][4]

Abstract: I discuss some recent work linking certain aspects of the second part of Hilbert's 16th problem to the theory of \hbox{o-minimality}.
These notes are adapted from a lecture I gave in the Jour fixe seminar series at the Zukunfts\-kolleg of Universität Konstanz in June 2017.
