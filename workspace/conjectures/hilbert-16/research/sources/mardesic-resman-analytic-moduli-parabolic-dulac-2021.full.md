**Abstract:**This paper gives moduli of analytic classification for parabolic *Dulac*germs (that is, *almost regular*germs). Dulac germs appear as first return maps of hyperbolic polycycles. Their moduli are given by a sequence of *Écalle–Voronin*-type germs of analytic diffeomorphisms. The result is stated in a broader class of *parabolic generalized Dulac germs*having power-logarithmic asymptotic expansions.
Bibliography: 23 titles.

**Keywords:**Dulac germ, analytic invariants, Écalle–Voronin moduli, Gevrey summability, transseries.
