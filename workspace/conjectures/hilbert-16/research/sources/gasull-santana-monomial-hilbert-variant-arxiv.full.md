# Title: On a variant of Hilbert's 16th problem

Authors: [Armengol Gasull][3], [Paulo Santana][4]

View a PDF of the paper titled On a variant of Hilbert's 16th problem, by Armengol Gasull and Paulo Santana

[View PDF][5] [HTML (experimental)][6]

Abstract: We study the number of limit cycles that a planar polynomial vector field can have as a function of its number $m$ of monomials. We prove that the number of limit cycles increases at least quadratically with $m$ and we provide good lower bounds for $m\leqslant10$.
