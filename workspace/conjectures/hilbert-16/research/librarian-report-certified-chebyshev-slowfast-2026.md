## Sources added this cycle

1. **Figueras–Tucker–Villadelprat, "Computer-assisted techniques for the
   verification of the Chebyshev property of Abelian integrals", JDE 254 (2013)
   2647–3663, DOI 10.1016/j.jde.2013.01.036** — FULL POSTPRINT held
   (`figueras-tucker-villadelprat-chebyshev-abelian-2013-postprint.full.md`,
   UAB DDD postprint FigTucVil2012.pdf). The certified-verification instrument
   the run's sharp-Abelian approaches cite but did not hold. Theorem A proves
   the Dumortier–Roussarie birth-of-canard-cycles conjecture for q ≤ 2 via
   interval-arithmetic Wronskian nonvanishing in a transcendental setting.
   Claim `h16-ftv2013-chebyshev-abelian-ca`.
2. **Huzak–De Maesschalck–Dumortier, "Primary birth of canard cycles in
   slow-fast codimension 3 elliptic bifurcations", CPAA 13(6) (2014) 2641–2673,
   DOI 10.3934/cpaa.2014.13.2641** — FULL PUBLISHED PDF held
   (`huzak-demaesschalck-dumortier-primary-birth-canard-2014.full.md`, UHasselt
   open access). Open companion of the paywalled 2013 JDE slow-fast codim-3
   paper. Theorem 4.3: cyclicity exactly 1 for −2<B̄₂<2 (B̄₂≠0) and at ±2, 0
   for 2<|B̄₂|≤B₀₂; the transition-map/Lie-derivative zero-counting instrument.
   Claim `h16-hdd2014-canard-birth-slowfast-codim3`.
3. **Gavrilov–Iliev, "Bifurcations of Limit Cycles From Infinity in Quadratic
   Systems", Canad. J. Math. 54 (2002) 1038–1064, DOI 10.4153/CJM-2002-038-6**
   — RECORD level only (`gavrilov-iliev-bifurcations-infinity-quadratic-cjm-2002.full.md`,
   Cambridge Core): abstract + references + 7 Crossref citers. Full text NOT
   obtainable (Cambridge paywalled; author PDF
   math.univ-toulouse.fr/~gavrilov/publications/32.pdf malformed for the text
   extractor; no arXiv version; direct Cambridge PDF filename 404). Dead ends
   recorded. Claim `h16-gavrilov-iliev-2002-infinity-quadratic` (asserted-by-
   source at abstract level only). It is the origin of the infinity-graphics/
   Puiseux-Abelian technology the held 2025 hyperelliptic-Chebyshev sources
   extend.
