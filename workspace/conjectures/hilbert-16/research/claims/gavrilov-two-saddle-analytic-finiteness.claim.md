# Gavrilov 2011: finite cyclicity of two-saddle cycles

```claim
id: gavrilov-two-saddle-analytic-finiteness
statement: Every heteroclinic two-saddle cycle (a k=2 saddle graphic with two nondegenerate saddles joined by two separatrix connections) occurring in an analytic finite-parameter family X_λ of analytic planar vector fields, λ ∈ (ℝ^N,0), has finite cyclicity: the number of limit cycles of X_λ tending to the cycle as λ→0 is uniformly bounded in λ. Proved by complex continuation of the two Dulac maps, reduction of the displacement ψ_λ = 𝒟_λ¹ − 𝒟_λ² zero count to fixed points of separatrix holonomy maps, and Gabrielov's theorem; the argument-principle/Petrov-trick route does not use Dulac asymptotic expansions.
hypotheses: X_λ analytic family of analytic planar vector fields; Γ₂ a two-saddle heteroclinic cycle (hyperbolic ratios α₁,α₂ > 0, α₁α₂ ≥ 1); finite parameter dimension N; the cyclicity is per-family near λ=0, not a coefficient-uniform bound over all polynomial fields.
holds-here: yes — for the restricted hyperbolic two-saddle class only; NOT for the open DRR rows I¹₆b, H³₁₃, DI₂b, H³₁₄, which pass through triple nilpotent points at infinity (non-hyperbolic). Relevant as a displacement-counting template that avoids full asymptotic expansions, and as an independent confirmation that analyticity of the true return map (not its formal expansion) is the load-bearing hypothesis.
status: asserted-by-source
falsifier: an analytic family with a two-saddle cycle of unbounded local cyclicity, or a demonstration that the argument-principle/domain step is invalid.
sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.0857 (full HTML held)
anchors: research/sources/primary-gavrilov-two-saddle-cycles-2011-arxiv.html.full.md (Theorem 4, §5; §6 identity (18) holonomy fixed-point equation; §1 method vs Dulac expansions)
answers: the argument-principle counting template for the displacement of composed Dulac maps (slow-divergence/ECT gap), without needing full transseries
```
