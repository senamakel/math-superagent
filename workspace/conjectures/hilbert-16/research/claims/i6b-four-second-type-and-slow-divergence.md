# I^1_6b: four second-type Dulac maps and slow-divergence/ECT hypotheses

```claim
id: i6b-four-second-type-full-graphic-not-covered
statement: No held source supplies a finite-cyclicity theorem for the full quadratic graphic I^1_6b. Roussarie–Rousseau 2015 proves finite cyclicity only for its boundary limit-periodic set (Theorem 1.1 / Theorem 3.3). The same paper explicitly says that some non-boundary I^1_6b limit-periodic sets involve four second-type Dulac maps and cannot be reduced to one equation; in the center case they require a two-equation problem in (r_1,rho_1,r_2,rho_2), with r_i rho_i=nu_i. It says these remaining graphics were intended for future work.
hypotheses: Quadratic vector fields; I^1_6b is the triple point at infinity graphic in the center/integrable family, corresponding in RR's parameterization to 1/2 < B < 1; the statement concerns the full graphic, i.e. every blown-up limit-periodic set, not merely the boundary stratum.
holds-here: yes
status: asserted-by-source
falsifier: A primary paper proving finite cyclicity for every blown-up I^1_6b limit-periodic set, or explicitly giving a uniform zero bound for the four-second-type-Dulac two-equation displacement, would falsify the claim.
```
