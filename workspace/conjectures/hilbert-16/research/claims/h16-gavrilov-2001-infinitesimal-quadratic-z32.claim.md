# Gavrilov 2001 — infinitesimal 16th in the quadratic case: Z(3,2)=2

```claim
id: h16-gavrilov-2001-infinitesimal-quadratic-z32
statement: Let H(x,y) be a real cubic polynomial with four distinct critical values and X_H = H_y ∂/∂x − H_x ∂/∂y its Hamiltonian field. There is a neighborhood U of X_H in the space of all quadratic plane vector fields such that any X ∈ U has at most two limit cycles. Equivalently, the Abelian integral d²/dh² I_{αβγ}(h) has at most two zeros in the complex domain C \ [1/6, ∞) when X_H has one saddle and one center, at most four otherwise; exactly one zero for ([α:β:γ],a,b) ∈ U.
```
