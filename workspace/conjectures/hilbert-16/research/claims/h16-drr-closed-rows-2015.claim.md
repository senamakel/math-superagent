# DRR closed rows through 2015: the 88-graphics count

```claim
id: h16-drr-closed-rows-2015
statement: As of 2015, at least 88 of the 121 DRR graphics have had finite cyclicity proved inside quadratic systems. The 88th and 89th rows are the two graphics through a triple nilpotent point of saddle type, (I^1_12) and (I^1_13), closed by Rousseau–Shan–Zhu 2015 (arXiv:1502.00689); RSZ's own text says proving (I^1_12) and (I^1_13) "will bring the number of graphics of the program for which finite cyclicity is proved to 88".
hypotheses: quadratic systems; DRR program inventory of 121 graphics; the count 88 is the authors' own running total as of RSZ 2015, before the Roussarie–Rousseau 2015 closure of (I^1_14) (which makes the run's arithmetic 89).
holds-here: yes
status: asserted-by-source
evidence: sourced-held — RSZ 2015 full text (arXiv:1502.00689) states the 88 count verbatim; Roussarie–Rousseau 2015 (arXiv:1506.07104) closes (I^1_14) as Theorem 1.2, making 89 by this run's arithmetic (the authors do not state a new total).
falsifier: A primary source giving a different closed count as of 2015, or showing one of the 88 rows was not actually closed by the cited paper, would falsify this exact count.
sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00689 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07104
anchors: research/sources/primary-rousseau-shan-zhu-nilpotent-saddle-graphics-2015-v1.full.md lines 73-74 ("will bring the number of graphics of the program for which finite cyclicity is proved to 88"); research/sources/primary-roussarie-rousseau-2015-center-graphics.full.md lines 17-77 (Theorem 1.2 for (I^1_14))
note: "89" is this run's arithmetic (88 + I^1_14), not a number any single source states. The raw DRR 1994 catalogue is not held, so the names of the remaining un-closed rows are enumerated in the companion claims h16-drr-open-rows and drr-rr-boundary-only-for-3-graphics.
follows-from: h16-drr-121-graphics
answers:
```

## Why this claim block exists

The `drr-status` thread and several backward/goal files rest on `h16-drr-closed-rows-2015`,
but no claim block with this id was on disk. This block records the sourced 88 count with
its verbatim anchor, and the arithmetic caveat that 89 is this run's sum, not a source's.
