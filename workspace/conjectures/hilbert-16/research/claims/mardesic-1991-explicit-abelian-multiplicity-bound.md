## Why this claim block exists

Frontier entry W1981047244 resolved to this paper. The abstract (held) establishes
the theorem structure: explicit C(n) for the multiplicity of zeros of generic
Abelian integrals, with consequences for cyclicity of regular cycles (C(n)),
centres (C0(n)) and homoclinic loops (C1(n)). The explicit value of C(n) is not
held; Novikov–Yakovenko 1999 confirm it is polynomial in the degree. This is a
distinct object from the BNY double-exponential bound (which is uniform over ALL
Hamiltonians, not just generic ones) and from the BD linear-in-m bound.
