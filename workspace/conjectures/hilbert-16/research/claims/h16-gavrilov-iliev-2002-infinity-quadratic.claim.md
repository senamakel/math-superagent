# Claim — Gavrilov–Iliev 2002, bifurcations of limit cycles from infinity in quadratic systems

```claim
id: h16-gavrilov-iliev-2002-infinity-quadratic
statement: In one-parameter unfoldings of quadratic planar differential systems having a degenerate critical point at infinity, there are three types of quadratic systems with an elliptic critical point at infinity that bifurcate limit cycles around it. Via a degenerate transformation the system becomes a small perturbation of a reversible system with a center; the displacement function expands in a Puiseux series in the small parameter, coefficients expressed as Abelian integrals. In four cases (including the elliptic Bogdanov–Takens case and the isochronous center S3) the space of bifurcation functions has the Chebyshev property: every function has fewer real zeros than the dimension of the space. Proved by constructing the bifurcation diagram of zeros of Abelian integrals in a complex domain.
hypotheses: quadratic planar systems with a degenerate (elliptic) critical point at infinity; one-parameter unfoldings; limit cycles bifurcating from infinity; Chebyshev property: #zeros < dim for the bifurcation-function space.
```
