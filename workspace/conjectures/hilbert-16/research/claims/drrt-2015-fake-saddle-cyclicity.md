# DMRT 2015 — fake-saddle cyclicity in quadratic fields

[[demaesschalck-rebollo-torregrosa-fake-saddle-2015-postprint.full]]

```claim
id: drrt-2015-fake-saddle-cyclicity-lower-bound
statement: De Maesschalck–Rebollo-Perdomo–Torregrosa (JDE 258(2):588–620, 2015) prove that the cyclicity of a quadratic fake saddle (impassable grain: singular point with zero linear part, degenerate flow-box normal form X0: {ẋ=0, ẏ=x²+y²}) is at least 2 when the normal form is quadratic: small-amplitude limit cycles appear in configurations (2:0) and (1:1), via Hopf, Bogdanov–Takens, slow-fast/canard, homoclinic and heteroclinic mechanisms. The paper does NOT establish an upper bound for the general family: "Determining an upper bound for the number of limit cycles turned out to be too difficult" (line 63); the only at-most-two result is for the symmetric-restricted family (2) {ẋ=ax²+bxy+µ, ẏ=x²+y²−1} in configuration (1:1).
hypotheses: smooth unfoldings of the quadratic fake saddle; the symmetric at-most-2 additionally restricts µ2=µ3=0 and configuration (1:1).
holds-here: yes — the lower bound and the absence of a general upper bound are the precise state of the fake-saddle cyclicity question; any use of fake-saddle cyclicity must cite this as a lower-bound result, not a ≤2 theorem.
status: asserted-by-source — verified verbatim against the held postprint (abstract line 23 "cyclicity is bigger or equal than two"; lines 62–64 "up to two small amplitude limit cycles" / "upper bound ... too difficult"; line 118 symmetric-family at-most-2).
falsifier: a primary source proving a general upper bound on the cyclicity of the unrestricted quadratic fake saddle family, or a construction of three small-amplitude cycles near it.
```
