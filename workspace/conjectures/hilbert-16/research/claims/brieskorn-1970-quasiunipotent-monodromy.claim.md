# Brieskorn 1970 — quasi-unipotent monodromy (Gauss–Manin)

```claim
id: brieskorn-1970-quasiunipotent-monodromy
statement: For an isolated hypersurface singularity, there is an algebraic description of the local Picard–Lefschetz monodromy: a regular singular ordinary linear differential operator (the Gauss–Manin connection) whose monodromy coincides with the Picard–Lefschetz monodromy; consequently the eigenvalues of the monodromy are roots of unity (quasi-unipotence).
hypotheses: isolated hypersurface singularity; local Milnor fibration; the algebraic construction of the regular singular operator.
holds-here: yes — this is the analytic input the Abelian-integral bound of BNY 2010 consumes (Abelian integrals are horizontal sections of the regular Gauss–Manin connection with quasi-unipotent monodromy).
status: asserted-by-source
evidence: sourced-held — Brieskorn, "Die Monodromie der isolierten Singularitäten von Hyperflächen", Manuscripta Math. 2 (1970) 103–161, full text at research/sources/brieskorn-1970-monodromie-singularitaten-hyperflachen.full.md (abstract, §0 Resultate, the regular-singular-operator construction, roots-of-unity application). Cross-check: BNY 2010 full text attributes quasi-unipotence to Bri 70/Cle 69.
falsifier: An isolated hypersurface singularity whose local monodromy has an eigenvalue that is not a root of unity (would contradict the theorem, not just this reading of it).
```
