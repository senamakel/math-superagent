# Roussarie's finite-cyclicity conjecture holds for the NRH_d class (o-minimality route)

```claim
id: h16-ominimality-route-roussarie
statement: The o-minimality route toward Roussarie's finite-cyclicity conjecture is established for the NRH_d class — planar analytic vector fields whose limit periodic sets have only non-resonant hyperbolic singularities. Kaiser–Rolin–Speissegger 2009 (arXiv:math/0612745) construct a model-complete, o-minimal, polynomially bounded expansion ℝ_𝒬 of the real field (definable all convergent generalized power series with natural support) in which the transition map of any planar analytic vector field at an isolated non-resonant hyperbolic singularity is definable. Since finite compositions of definable functions are definable in the same o-minimal structure, and definable functions have finitely many isolated zeros (the o-minimality zero property), this gives finite cyclicity for the NRH_d class. The route is conjectural for non-hyperbolic/nilpotent/degenerate singularities, which is where the open DRR graphics lie.
hypotheses: planar analytic vector fields; limit periodic sets with only non-resonant hyperbolic singularities (NRH_d class); definability in a fixed o-minimal expansion of the real field; the o-minimality zero property for definable unary functions.
holds-here: yes (for the NRH_d class)
status: asserted-by-source
evidence: sourced-held — Kaiser–Rolin–Speissegger 2009 primary full text (research/sources/kaiser-rolin-speissegger-transition-maps-ominimal.full.md): abstract "We construct a model complete and o-minimal expansion ℝ_𝒬 of the field of real numbers such that, for any planar analytic vector field ξ and any isolated, non-resonant hyperbolic singularity p of ξ, a transition map for ξ at p is definable in ℝ_𝒬"; Speissegger's survey (research/sources/speissegger-hilbert16-ominimality-body.full.md); BIRS 2007 report §3.4 (research/sources/birs-workshop-h16-2007-report.full.md lines 231-246) records the o-minimality route and Mourtada/Schäfke discussions.
falsifier: A source showing that non-resonant hyperbolic transition maps are NOT all definable in one fixed o-minimal structure, or that the o-minimality zero property fails for the definable return-map compositions, would falsify the NRH_d closure. The route's extension to nilpotent/degenerate singularities is open and is exactly where the remaining DRR graphics sit.
sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0612745 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0604181 (Speissegger survey)
anchors: research/sources/kaiser-rolin-speissegger-transition-maps-ominimal.full.md lines 3-44; research/sources/birs-workshop-h16-2007-report.full.md lines 231-246
note: This is the o-minimality route behind the parametric-o-minimality approach. It settles the NRH_d subfamily only; the open DRR graphics (I^1_6b, H^3_13, DI_2b full; H^3_14; 11 degenerate) are precisely where non-hyperbolic/nilpotent/degenerate singularities make the definability argument fail.
follows-from: h16-drr-121-graphics
```
