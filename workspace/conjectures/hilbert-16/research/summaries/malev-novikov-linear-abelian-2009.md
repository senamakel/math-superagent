## What it claims

Abstract: We prove a linear in $°\omega$ upper bound on the number of real zeros of the Abelian integral $I(t)=\int_{\delta(t)}\omega$, where $\delta(t)\subset\R^2$ is the real oval $x^2y(1-x-y)=t$ and $\omega$ is a one-form with polynomial coefficients.
