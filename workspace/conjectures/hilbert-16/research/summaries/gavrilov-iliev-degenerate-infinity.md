## What it establishes

- Studies **bifurcations of limit cycles from a degenerate critical point at infinity** in one-parameter unfoldings of planar quadratic systems.
- Identifies **three types** of quadratic systems with an elliptic critical point that bifurcates from infinity together with eventual limit cycles around it.
- Key reduction: by a **degenerate transformation + rescaling**, such a system becomes a **small quadratic perturbation of a reversible quadratic system with a center**. The bifurcating limit cycles surround a finite focus (origin).
- The **displacement function is expanded in a Puiseux series** in the small parameter; **coefficients are expressed via Abelian integrals** (on curves of genus 0 or 1).
- In four detailed cases (including the **elliptic Bogdanov–Takens** case and the **isochronous center S3**), the vector space of bifurcation functions has the **Chebyshev property**: each function has **strictly fewer real zeros than the dimension** of the space → explicit non-oscillation/cyclicity bounds on cycles bifurcating from infinity.
- Method: constructs the **bifurcation diagram of zeros of the Abelian integrals in the complex domain** to prove the Chebyshev property.
