# Local reference set — Hilbert 16.2

Built 2026-08-18. The target is the uniform bound on isolated periodic orbits of planar polynomial vector fields; the mathematical object for local bifurcation is the displacement/return map, not a phase portrait.

## Primary full texts held

1. **Bamón, “Quadratic vector fields in the plane have a finite number of limit cycles,” Publ. Math. IHÉS (1986).** Full converted text: `research/sources/primary-bamon-quadratic-finiteness.pdf.full.md`; URL: https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1007/BF02699193.pdf. This is a primary quadratic finiteness treatment, proving finiteness for quadratic fields through graph classification and analytic/hyperbolic-graph arguments.

2. **Ilyashenko, *Finiteness Theorems for Limit Cycles* (1991).** The locally downloaded item is the AMS book record/contents, not the paywalled book text: `research/sources/primary-ilyashenko-finiteness-book.full.md`; URL: https://bookstore.ams.org/MMONO/94. It documents the monodromy/Poincaré-map, complex-domain, resolution, normal-form and superexact-asymptotic framework. Use the held Ilyashenko 2002 full survey for accessible mathematical text.

3. **Kaloshin, *Around Hilbert–Arnold Problem* (2001 lecture notes).** Full HTML: `research/sources/primary-kaloshin-elementary-polycycles.html.full.md`; URL: https://arxiv.org/html/math/0111053. Covers elementary polycycle cyclicity, the weak local Hilbert–Arnold problem, resolution/blow-up, and an independent finiteness route.

4. **Rousseau–Shan–Zhu, “Finite cyclicity of some graphics through a nilpotent point of saddle type inside quadratic systems” (2015).** Full HTML already held: `research/sources/rouseau-shan-zhu-nilpotent-saddle-graphics.html.full.md`; URL: https://arxiv.org/html/1502.00689. Proves finite cyclicity of (I¹₁₂), (I¹₁₃), using blow-up, two Dulac-map types, and generalized derivation–division.

5. **Roussarie–Rousseau, “Finite cyclicity of some center graphics through a nilpotent point inside quadratic systems” (2015).** Full HTML already held: `research/sources/rousseau-roussarie-center-graphics-nilpotent.full.md`; URL: https://arxiv.org/html/1506.07104. Proves (I¹₁₄) and boundary limit-periodic sets for (I¹₆b), (H³₁₃), (DI₂b), with the Bautin trick; (H³₁₄) remains the difficult exception in that source.

6. **Bautin, “On the number of limit cycles appearing with variation of the coefficients from an equilibrium state of the type of a focus or a center” (1952).** Full text: `research/sources/bautin-1952-full.pdf.full.md`; URL: https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=sm&paperid=5421&what=fullt&option_lang=eng. Primary source for local cyclicity M(2)=3 and the focus/center coefficient expansion.

7. **Binyamini–Novikov–Yakovenko, “On the Number of Zeros of Abelian Integrals” (2010).** Full HTML: `research/sources/binyamini-novikov-yakovenko-abelian-integrals.html.full.md`; URL: https://arxiv.org/html/0808.2952v3. Constructive double-exponential zero bound for the infinitesimal/tangential H16; it does not solve the full nonlinear problem.

8. **Kaiser–Rolin–Speissegger, “Transition maps at non-resonant hyperbolic singularities are o-minimal” (2009).** Full HTML: `research/sources/kaiser-rolin-speissegger-transition-maps-ominimal.full.md`; URL: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/math/0612745. Constructs a polynomially bounded o-minimal structure defining transition maps at isolated non-resonant hyperbolic singularities; this settles the corresponding restricted class, not nilpotent/degenerate graphics.

9. **Galias–Tucker, “The Songling system has exactly four limit cycles” (2022).** Full source: `research/sources/galias-tucker-songling-four-cycles.full.md`; URL: http://www.zet.agh.edu.pl/~galias/ps/amc2022.pdf. Rigorous adaptive-precision interval arithmetic certifies exactly four cycles in Shi’s quadratic example; this is the local library model for a certified return-map oracle.

## Already held companion sources

The library also contains DRR’s 1994 record and extensive companions: Ilyashenko’s 2002 survey; Dumortier–Guzmán–Rousseau 2002; Dumortier–Ilyashenko–Rousseau 2002; Dumortier–Rousseau 2009; Zhu–Rousseau 2002/2004/2005; Rousseau–Shan–Zhu 2015; Roussarie–Rousseau 2008/2015; Huzak 2018; Marín–Villadelprat 2025; Binyamini–Dor refinements; Novikov–Yakovenko Picard–Fuchs modules; Gavrilov 1999; Gautier–Gavrilov–Iliev 2009; Liénard slow–fast papers; and Yeung’s 2024–25 critique of the non-hyperbolic Dulac proof.

## Evidence and boundaries

Full texts are primary evidence where available; landing pages and surveys are orientation only. The DRR 121-graphic reduction is locally documented, but there is no established consolidated current graphic-by-graphic ledger. H(2)<∞ and H(2)=4 remain unproved here. Individual analytic finiteness is recorded with the Yeung 2024–25 proof-completeness caveat. Elementary/non-resonant hyperbolic and Abelian-integral restricted classes are settled under their stated hypotheses; nilpotent and degenerate graphics remain the central obstruction.
