## Abstract

In this paper we make essential steps in proving the finitecyclicity of degenerate graphics in quadratic systems, having a lineof singular points in the finite plane. In particular we considerthe graphics $(DF_{1 a})$, $(DF_{2 a})$ of the program of [8]to prove the finiteness part of Hilbert's 16th problem for quadraticvector fields. We make a complete treatment except for one veryspecific problem that we clearly identify.
