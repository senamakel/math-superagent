# Romanovski–Żołądek 2000 — center variety of polynomial differential systems

Full text: [[bautin-center-variety-primary.full]] (arXiv:math/0009061; published
as "The center variety of polynomial differential systems", J. Symbolic Comput.
35 (2003) 423–443).

## What the source establishes (held full text)

**Subject:** the **center variety** — the set of coefficient values for which a
polynomial differential system has a center at a given singular point — of polynomial
systems (with focus quantities as generators). The paper develops an algorithm for the
**symmetry component** of the center variety (systems with an axis of symmetry in the
real plane): a general algorithm to find this irreducible subvariety and compute its
dimension.

**The Bautin connection (load-bearing for this run):** the methods "provide a simple
way to compute the radical of the ideal generated by the focus quantities and,
therefore, to estimate the cyclicity of a center in the case when the ideal is
radical. In particular, we use our methods to get a simple proof of the famous Bautin
theorem on the cyclicity of the quadratic system." This is the algebraic-modern
restatement of Bautin's M(2)=3: the Bautin ideal of the quadratic focus is radical in
the relevant chart, the first obstruction is at degree 4, and the cyclicity bound is
read off the ideal structure.
