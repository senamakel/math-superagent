# Hyperelliptic Abelian-integral special families: 2025 primary records

## Sun–Dai
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11425-023-2334-8.
The paper studies I(h)=∮_{Γ_h}(α+βx)y dx on compact ovals of y²+P₅(x)=h, with a specified normal form P₅ depending on complex-conjugate parameters u,v. Earlier work gives parameter regions where at least two isolated zeros occur; the paper addresses whether two is also an upper bound, using a degree-reduction/simplification method. The held digest does not expose the complete theorem hypotheses, so this remains a lead rather than an established claim.

## An–Dai–Hu
Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12346-025-01330-x.
The abstract states that three classes of complete hyperelliptic first-kind integrals are Chebyshev, with exact zero bound one, using a criterion function and symbolic computation. The class definitions are not yet extracted from the full source; retain as asserted-by-source until read.

These are restricted Abelian-integral results, not results on the full displacement functions of arbitrary polynomial vector fields.
