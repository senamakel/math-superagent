## What the abstract establishes

A proof of a **known specific case of Dulac's theorem** in the style of
Ilyashenko; from it, a quasi-analyticity result for some return maps of
polycycles, and a Structural Theorem for the formal asymptotics of such a
polycycle. Subject: math.DS; MSC 34E99. (The precise case is not named in the
abstract.)

supports Statement 2.4 of the gap paper (every equilibrium orbitally equivalent
to {ẋ=x², ẏ=−y}). It is **not** a repair of the whole contested proof.
