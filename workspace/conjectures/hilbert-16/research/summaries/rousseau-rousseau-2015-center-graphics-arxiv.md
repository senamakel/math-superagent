# Roussarie–Rousseau 2015: center graphics through a nilpotent point

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07104 (arXiv:1506.07104; submitted 2015-06-23).

## Established by the source
The paper studies quadratic vector fields near graphics through a triple nilpotent saddle/elliptic point surrounding a center. A blow-up of the family produces a singular 3-dimensional foliation; finite cyclicity is then studied for the resulting limit-periodic sets, including boundary sets. It proves finite cyclicity of the graphic \((I^1_{14})\). It also proves finite cyclicity of the boundary limit-periodic sets in \((I^1_{6b})\), \((H^3_{13})\), and \((DI_{2b})\), while the abstract explicitly says these are the relevant cases among the triple nilpotent-at-infinity center graphics.

## Relevance and limits
This is a primary source for several closures/partial closures in the DRR quadratic program and for the analytic blow-up/displacement-map method. It does **not** prove the full quadratic uniform bound, and boundary finite cyclicity is not equivalent to finite cyclicity of every associated full graphic. Claims here remain asserted-by-source unless independently formalised or certified.
