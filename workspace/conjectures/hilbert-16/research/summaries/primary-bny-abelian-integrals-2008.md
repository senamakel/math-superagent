## What it claims

Abstract. We prove that the number of limit cycles generated by a
small non-conservative perturbation of a Hamiltonian polynomial vector
ﬁeld on the plane, is bounded by a double exponential of the degree of the
ﬁelds. This solves the long-standing inﬁnitesimal Hilbert 16th problem.
The proof uses only the fact that Abelian integrals of a given de-
gree are horizontal sections of a a regular ﬂat meromorphic connection
deﬁned over Q (Gauss-Manin connection) with a quasiunipotent mon-
odromy group.
