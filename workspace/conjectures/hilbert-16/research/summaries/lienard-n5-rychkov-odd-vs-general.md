# Liénard n=5: Rychkov (odd-only, =2) vs the open general case

*Digest of the refinement recorded in `research/notes/lienard-n5-rychkov-odd-vs-general.md`;
claim `h16-lienard-n5-rychkov-odd-vs-general`.*

**Rychkov 1975** (Differ. Uravn. 11:390–391, "The maximum number of limit
cycles of the system ẋ = y − Σᵢ aᵢx^{2i+1}, ẏ = −x is equal to two") settles the
**odd-polynomial** degree-5 classical Liénard system: at most 2 limit cycles.

**This does NOT settle the general degree-5 Liénard system** (F may mix even
and odd terms). The general degree-5 case — where the Lins–Neto–de Melo–Pugh
conjecture predicts at most ⌊(5−1)/2⌋ = 2 — remains **open** as of the held
Llibre–Zhang 2017 survey ([32] cites Rychkov; the survey's own open problem is
the maximum for n ≥ 5).

**Why the distinction matters:** a degree-5 Liénard lower-bound construction
(or a claimed sharpness result) must specify which class it concerns. The
odd-only class is beaten; the general class is not.

**Evidence:** Rychkov's paper not held (Russian, 1975); the statement is
second-hand, but consistent across five independent citations found this pass
(Llibre–Zhang 2017 [32]; Llibre–Valls 2013; Giacomini–Neukirch 1998; Odani
1996; Gasull–Giacomini–Grau 2018). The survey itself is held full text.

**Falsifier:** a primary/survey source settling the general degree-5 case to a
specific value, or a primary Rychkov text showing he treated the general class.
