# Bamón 1986, "Quadratic vector fields in the plane have a finite number of limit cycles" — record page

Source: `research/sources/bamon-quadratic-finite-limit-cycles-pdf.full.md` [[bamon-quadratic-finite-limit-cycles-pdf.full]] — IHÉS record page (PMIHES 64, 111–142, doi:10.1007/BF02699193).

## What this page establishes

Bibliographic record for Bamón's theorem (received 1985-09-16, published
1986-01-07): each **individual quadratic vector field** has finitely many limit
cycles. The page carries the full bibliography of the paper, which includes the
key antecedents:

- Dulac 1923 (Bull. SMF 51, 45–188) — the original flawed proof.
- Chicone–Shafer 1983 (Trans. AMS 278, 585–612) — separatrix and limit cycles
  of quadratic systems and Dulac's theorem (the compact-region finiteness prior).
- Ilyashenko 1984/85 (Funct. Anal. Appl. 18(3)) — limit cycles of polynomial
  fields with nondegenerate singular points (the hyperbolic-vertex result Bamón
  builds on).
- Petrovskii–Landis 1955–59 (AMS Transl. 2, 14/16) — the retracted H(2)=3
  attempt.
- Coppel 1966 survey, Dumortier 1977 singularities.

## What it implies here

The title alone establishes the claim (`h16-bamon-quadratic-finiteness`) — this
is individual finiteness for n = 2, NOT the uniform H(2) bound. The bibliography
confirms Bamón's dependence on Ilyashenko's non-accumulation result for
hyperbolic saddles (so Bamón's proof inherits the modern Dulac machinery, not
Dulac's). Note: this is the *record page*; the claim's substance is captured in
[[bamon-quadratic-finite-limit-cycles]].

Evidence class: record page. Hypotheses: n = 2, individual field. Falsifier: a
single quadratic field with infinitely many limit cycles (note the parallel
Yeung gap claim — if Ilyashenko's finiteness proof has a gap, Bamón's is
affected only insofar as it relies on Ilyashenko 1984/85's hyperbolic-vertex
result, which Yeung explicitly excludes from the gap: hyperbolic polycycles are
fully understood).
