## What the source establishes

**Object.** Hilbert quasi-regular algebras QRH^{k,.} of germs at 0 of real-analytic
functions on a semi-algebraic open set U, acted on by an irreducible derivation χ
(Hilbert derivation) with Sing(χ) ⊂ (B_k, 0), B_k = {∏x_j = 0} invariant under
the flow. Quasi-analyticity in the x-coordinates and elementary
Écalle–Khovanskii asymptotic structure are part of QRH.

**Théorème IVC1 (main).** QRH^{k,.} is locally χ-finie: the integral projection
π_χ restricted to fibers of elements of QRH has finite degree, and the
differential ideals I_{χ,f} are Noetherian (or locally Noetherian). Double
inclusion: (θ^n)π_χ*(J_{χ,f,γ}) ⊂ I_{χ,f} ⊂ π_χ*(J_{χ,f,γ}), where
θ = ∏x_j generates the ideal of B_k and n = m_χ(f) is the multiplicity, linked
to an algebraic multiplicity ma_χ(f). Reduced singularities of χ after
desingularisation: χ_ℓ = ρ∂/∂ρ − Σ_{j=1}^ℓ s_j u_j ∂/∂u_j (ℓ = 0..k−1).

**Théorème 0 (application to Hilbert's 16th).** For an analytic q-parameter
unfolding X_ν of a real monodromic hyperbolic polycycle X_0 (eigenvalue ratio
−1 at each singularity, "uniquement pour simplifier la présentation"), there
exist integers **N and L** and neighborhoods Γ_k ⊂ U ⊂ U_0, V ⊂ (ℝ^q,0) such
that (i) for all ν ∈ V the number of limit cycles of X_ν in U is bounded by N,
and (ii) the multiplicity of each is bounded by L. Hence **no accumulation of
limit cycles on hyperbolic polycycles in compact analytic families on S²**,
including polycycles that are themselves accumulations of cycles.

**Method.** Preparation of the perturbed return map p_ν of the polycycle in a
finite subdivision; finiteness properties of p_ν (number of fixed points and
their multiplicity) are given by those of a finite jet which is a fewnomial;
Khovanskii elimination applies to these jets. Generic cases were earlier done by
Mourtada [M], Ilyashenko–Yakovenko and Kaloshin with semihyperbolic
singularities; the difficulty in [I-Y2] was relating generic conditions to the
geometry of the polycycle.
