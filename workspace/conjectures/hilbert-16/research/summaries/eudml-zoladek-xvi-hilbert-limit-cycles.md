# Żołądek 1995 — historical survey of Hilbert 16.2

Source: http://eudml.org/doc/251301

Żołądek surveys the second part of Hilbert's sixteenth problem: number and position of limit cycles of planar polynomial vector fields. It records the historical framing and partial progress, while not giving a general uniform bound. The paper is useful as a period survey and bibliography, not as evidence that the unrestricted problem is solved.

**Evidence class:** asserted-by-source (survey). **Falsifier:** a later primary source showing the cited survey actually proves a uniform bound for all polynomial fields.
