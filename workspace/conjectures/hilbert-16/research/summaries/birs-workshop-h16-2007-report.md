# BIRS 07w5021 report: "Mathematical developments around Hilbert's 16th problem"

<!-- source: https://www.birs.ca/workshops/2007/07w5021/report07w5021.pdf | Banff International Research Station workshop report, 2007 -->

**State of the field and the DRR reduction, circa 2007.** Workshop report
(organiser: Ilyashenko et al.) surveying the two main approaches to H16.2.

## What it establishes

- The **DOSED method / strange attractors** and the **Poincaré–Bendixson /
  Abelian-integral (Hilbert–Arnold)** approaches as the surrounding research
  programmes.
- Confirms the **Roussarie compactification reduction**: compactifying both
  phase space (Poincaré sphere) and coefficient space, limit cycles can only
  accumulate on **limit periodic sets**, so a uniform bound on H(2) follows (by
  the Roussarie reduction) from proving each such set has finite cyclicity —
  the DRR 1994 program framing, whose target list is **121 graphics**.
- Reports the tools: Varchenko–Khovanskii finiteness for Abelian integrals;
  Picard–Fuchs systems bounding zero distributions over two-dimensional cycles;
  Chebyshev properties; normal forms; blow-ups. Describes state of the art for
  the "elementary" (hyperbolic) polycycle finiteness and the difficulty posed by
  non-elementary (nilpotent, semi-hyperbolic, degenerate) polycycles.
