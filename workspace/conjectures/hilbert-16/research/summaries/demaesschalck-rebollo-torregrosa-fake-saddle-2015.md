# DMRT 2015, "Cyclicity of a fake saddle inside the quadratic vector fields" — UAB record page

Source: `research/sources/demaesschalck-rebollo-torregrosa-fake-saddle-2015.full.md` [[demaesschalck-rebollo-torregrosa-fake-saddle-2015.full]] — UAB DDD record page (record 145330) for the JDE 258(2):588–620 paper.

## What this page establishes

Record page (metadata + abstract) for De Maesschalck–Rebollo-Perdomo–Torregrosa
2015. The abstract states the object: small-amplitude limit cycles near an
**unfolded fake saddle** (a.k.a. impassable grain), whose unperturbed normal form
is a **degenerate flow box** — parallel fibers, all non-singular but one, and one
node on the singular fiber; the paper studies techniques showing **cyclicity ≥ 2
when the normal form is quadratic**. Grant metadata confirms the funding
(MTM2008-03437, MTM2013-40998-P, FWO G093910).

## What it implies here

The substantive content is in the **postprint** held separately and digested:
