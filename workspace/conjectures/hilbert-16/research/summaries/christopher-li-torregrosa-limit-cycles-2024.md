## What it implies for this run

- The second edition (2024) **both** covers the infinitesimal/tangential H16
  for n=2 (the weak problem) and gives, in its final chapter, **a unified proof
  of the weak H16 for n=2**. This is a book-form statement that "the number of
  zeros of Abelian integrals (limit cycles born in first-order perturbation of
  a Hamiltonian quadratic field) for n=2" is settled*.* CONTEXT.md already
  records the general tangential bound (BNY double-exponential; Binyamini–Dor
  linear-in-deg-ω); this chapter is the book-form n=2-in-particular
  treatment. **It does not establish H(2)<∞ — the weak problem is only the
  first-order linearization** — and that distinction is exactly problem.md's
  warning.
- The book is a **reference/citation spine** (its chapter titles are the
  canonical sub-topics), not a source of new results this pass. Its value here
  is as a map of the instrument toolbox (Abelian integrals, Chebyshev systems
  via `gasull-lazaro-torregrosa` / `grau-manosas-villadelprat`, Darboux/Liou
  villian integrability) and as the bibliographic anchor for the weak-problem
  status.
- **No claim block is warranted** — it asserts nothing false or new; it is a
  survey/textbook TOC. Recording it as a source that "does not help" would be
  wrong (it confirms the weak-H16-n=2 book treatment exists), but nothing
  load-bearing is drawn from it.
