## What it establishes

- **Framework:** restates the DRR program (1994): for the quadratic family
  (1.2) there are **125 graphics** to be proved to have finite cyclicity (note
  the 125-vs-121 discrepancy with DRR's own count — the thesis counts a
  variant); most had been proved by 2013, with ~40 challenging cases left at
  thesis time.
- **Main result:** proves finite cyclicity of **four families of HH-graphics**
  through a triple nilpotent singularity of saddle or elliptic type, using
  normal-form theory, global blow-up, Dulac maps on the blown-up sphere, and
  the generalized derivation–division method (Roussarie). The four families are
  read by the abstract as (Ji₂), (Ji₃), (!Jb), (!I₁b) — OCR-corrupted; the
  full text is needed to pin the true labels.
- Theorems in the digest: 2.1.1 (convex RH-graphic through triple nilpotent
  saddle), 2.1.2 (HR-graphic through triple nilpotent elliptic point), 3.4.1
  ((!{₂}) finite cyclicity), 4.1.1 ((Ji₃) finite cyclicity inside quadratic
  systems), 5.2.1 ((!Jb) finite cyclicity), 6.1.1 ((!I₁b) finite cyclicity).
  Cyclicity bounds 1 per limit periodic set recorded for some (Prop 4.2.1:
  Cycl ≤ 1 for i = 1..10; Prop 6.2.1: Cycl ≤ 1 for i = 1..12).
