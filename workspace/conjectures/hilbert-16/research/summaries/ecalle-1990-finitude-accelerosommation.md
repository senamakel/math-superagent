## What the source establishes (held content only)

**This is the first Écalle-side primary record in the library** — previously
the only trace of the Écalle side was the Chicone 1993 book review of the
Ilyashenko monograph. The held reference list IS the spine of Écalle's proof:

- The *fonctions résurgentes* trilogy: Pub. Math. Orsay 1981 (T.1), 1981 (T.2),
  1985 (T.3).
- His own "Preuve de la conjecture de Dulac et accéléro-sommation de
  l'application de retour" (submitted to Travaux en Cours) and companion
  "Calcul accélératoire" / "Calcul compensatoire et linéarisation
  quasianalytique des champs de vecteurs locaux".
- **Écalle–Martinet–Moussu–Ramis, "Non-accumulation des cycles-limites",
  C.R.A.S. t.304 série I n°14 (1987): (I) pp. 375–378, (II) pp. 431–434** — the
  CRAS announcement of the Dulac proof. **This is the identified open-format
  target most likely to carry the Écalle-side proof concisely.**
- Seidenberg's desingularization (Amer. J. Math 1968); Françoise–Pugh 1986
  (JDE 65, "Keeping track of limit cycles"); Moussu Bourbaki 655 (1985);
  Yoccoz Bourbaki 690 (1987); Ilyashenko 1984/1987/1991; Petrovskii–Landis
  (1960 trans.); Bamon An. Acad. Bras. 57 (1985) (Dulac for quadratic); Mandelbrojt.

**Keywords on the record** ("machine-added", not author's): resurgence
emanée ("emanating resurgence"), point singuliers, comportement asymptotique,
nous allons — consistent with the accelero-summation method: the return map is
analysed through its resurgence (Borel-transform structure) rather than a
formal power series, which is the analyticity-entering step that Ilyashenko's
almost-regular / monomial-ordered route parallels.
