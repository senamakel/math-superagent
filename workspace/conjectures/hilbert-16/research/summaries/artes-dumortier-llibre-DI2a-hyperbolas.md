## What it establishes

- Introduces the notions of **infinity strip** and **strip of hyperbolas** as organizing centers of limit cycles in planar polynomial systems.
- Studies a specific strip of hyperbolas occurring in quadratic systems and deals with the **cyclicity of the degenerate graphic `DI₂a`** from the Dumortier–Roussarie–Rousseau (DRR) program (JDE 110 (1994) 86–133), toward the finiteness part of H16 for quadratic systems.
- **Methods:** geometric singular perturbation theory (GSPT / slow-fast), the **Bautin ideal**, and **Darboux integrability**.
