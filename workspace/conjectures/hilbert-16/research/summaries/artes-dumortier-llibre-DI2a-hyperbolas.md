## What it establishes

- Introduces the notions of **infinity strip** and **strip of hyperbolas** as organizing centers of limit cycles in planar polynomial systems.
- Studies a specific strip of hyperbolas occurring in quadratic systems and deals with the **cyclicity of the degenerate graphic `DI₂a`** from the Dumortier–Roussarie–Rousseau (DRR) program (JDE 110 (1994) 86–133), toward the finiteness part of H16 for quadratic systems.
- **Methods:** geometric singular perturbation theory (GSPT / slow-fast), the **Bautin ideal**, and **Darboux integrability**.

## Why it matters to this run (degenerate-graphics attack)

- `DI₂a` is one of the **degenerate DRR graphics**. The run's live direction (steering directive) is to attack one of the **eleven open degenerate graphics** (only DF1a, DF2a closed per Shan 2013). **`DI₂a` is itself one of those eleven open graphics** — ADL is a partial-result treatment (infinity-strip / strip-of-hyperbolas analysis via GSPT + Bautin ideal + Darboux integrability), not a closure. It is thus a **legitimate attack target**, and its partial analysis is the methodological starting point.
- It cites the preprint **Dumortier–Rousseau, "Study of the cyclicity of some degenerate graphics inside quadratic systems"** (later CPAA 8 (2009) 1133–1157, doi:10.3934/cpaa.2009.8.1133) — the DF1a/DF2a closure paper, whose full text this library holds only by citation. That CPAA paper is the single most valuable missing primary source for the degenerate-graphics attack.
- Related: a paper on slow–fast cycles with a unique generic turning point (canard cycles when the slow dynamics is singular at the turning point, with a saddle-node bifurcation through the turning point) shows the **slow divergence integral is no longer the right tool but its derivative with respect to the layer variable is**; used to treat open problems in this paper and in Dumortier–Rousseau 2009. This is exactly the kind of slow-divergence-integral machinery GOAL.md names as an instrument.
