## Abstract

In this paper we introduce new methods to prove the finite cyclicity of some\ngraphics through a triple nilpotent point of saddle or elliptic type\nsurrounding a center. After applying a blow-up of the family, yielding a\nsingular 3-dimensional foliation, this amounts to proving the finite cyclicity\nof a family of limit periodic sets of the foliation. The boundary limit\nperiodic sets of these families were the most challenging, but the new methods\nare quite general for treating such graphics. We apply these techniques to\nprove the finite cyclicity of the graphic $(I_{14}^1)$, which is part of the\nprogram started in 1994 by Dumortier, Roussarie and Rousseau (and called DRR\nprogram) to show that there exists a uniform upper bound for the number of\nlimit cycles of a planar quadratic vector field. We also prove the finite\ncyclicity of the boundary limit periodic sets in all graphics but one through a\ntriple nilpotent point at infinity of saddle, elliptic or degenerate type (with\na line of zeros) and surrounding a center, namely the graphics $(I_{6b}^1)$,\n$(H_{13}^3)$, and $(DI_{2b})$.\n
