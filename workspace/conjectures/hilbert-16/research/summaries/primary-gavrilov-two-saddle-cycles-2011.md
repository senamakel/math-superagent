> Gavrilov proves finite cyclicity of every heteroclinic two-saddle cycle occurring in an analytic finite-parameter family of analytic planar vector fields. The method uses complex-analytic continuation and the argument principle/Petrov trick rather than treating a formal Dulac asymptotic expansion as if it determined the return map. This is a restricted analytic-family result, not a solution of H16.2: it does not provide a coefficient-uniform bound for all polynomial fields or cover arbitrary nilpotent/degenerate graphics. Its analyticity hypothesis is explicit and therefore passes the smooth-test requirement for this restricted class.
>
> ```claim

> statement: Every heteroclinic two-saddle cycle in an analytic finite-parameter family of analytic planar vector fields has finite cyclicity.

> holds-here: only for this restricted class; not full H16.2.
