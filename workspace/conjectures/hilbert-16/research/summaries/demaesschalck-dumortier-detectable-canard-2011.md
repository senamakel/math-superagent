# De Maesschalck & Dumortier, "Detectable canard cycles with singular slow dynamics of any order at the turning point", DCDS 29(1):109–140 (2011)

**Source URL:** https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2011.29.109 (AIMS open-access page; abstract + references held; full text not captured this cycle)

**Evidence class: sourced-text (abstract + reference list).** The AIMS page exposes the abstract and full bibliography; the PDF body is behind the journal's reader and was not downloaded.

## What it establishes

- Studies limit cycles near a canard limit periodic set of **FSTS-type** (Fast branch + attracting Slow branch + Turning point + repelling Slow branch) in planar slow–fast systems.
- Extends the **slow divergence integral** technique to the case where the slow dynamics has **singularities of any finite order accumulating at the turning point** — the case where the slow divergence integral itself becomes unbounded.
- Derives bounds on the number of limit cycles near the FSTS-l.p.s. by examining **appropriate derivations of the slow divergence integral** rather than the integral itself.
- Relation to the 2008 companion (De Maesschalck–Dumortier, Proc. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh Sect. A 138:265–299, DOI 10.1017/S0308210506000199): the 2008 paper establishes that with singular slow dynamics the slow divergence integral alone is insufficient and its **derivative with respect to the layer variable** carries the information; the 2011 paper generalises the treatment to singularities of any finite order.

## Why it matters to this run (degenerate-graphics attack)

- This is the **instrument chain** for the open degenerate DRR graphics: DF1a/DF2a were closed by exactly this slow-fast/slow-divergence machinery (DR 2009 for DF1a with the P* gap; Huzak 2018 for DF2a via family blow-up + slow divergence), and the ADL 2009 partial treatment of (DI2a) uses the same GSPT + Bautin-ideal + Darboux-integrability toolkit.
