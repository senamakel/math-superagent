# Torregrosa 2024 — cubic local cyclicity

[[torregrosa-cubic-high-local-cyclicity-2024.full]]

Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s40863-024-00486-9.

The paper gives two explicit one-parameter families of real cubic planar vector fields with twelve small-amplitude limit cycles at exceptional parameter values, via analytic return/displacement expansions and Lyapunov quantities. This is a local cyclicity/lower-bound construction, not a global bound for cubic fields or H16.2.

Implication: corroborates the lower-bound landscape and is a validation target for exact focal-value/Lyapunov machinery; it does not alter the open status of H(2) or establish H(3) optimality.
