# Palma-Márquez–Yeung 2024 — Maximum Modulus dichotomy for Dulac classes

Full text: [[palma-marquez-yeung-maximum-modulus-dulac.full]]. arXiv:2410.07532.
Note: the stored file is the arXiv **landing page** (abstract + metadata); the
proofs are not in the held text. Claim below is sourced at abstract level only.

## What the source establishes

Abstract-level, verbatim: studies large classes of real-valued analytic functions
that emerge in Dulac's problem. Building on the authors' Maximum Modulus-type
result, for any function in these classes: **either it has isolated zeros or it
coincides with the identity** (dichotomy). Application: **non-accumulation of
limit cycles holds around a specific class of "superreal" polycycles**.

## What it implies here

This is a **partial repair of the Yeung 2024 gap claim** for a restricted class of
polycycles: it supplies a quasi-analyticity-type dichotomy (isolated zeros vs
identity) for specific classes admitting Stokes phenomena, which is exactly the
kind of step Dulac got wrong and Ilyashenko's proof of is contested. It does NOT
prove the full Dulac theorem; the class ("superreal" polycycles) is specific.
