# Abstract-page duplicates and landing pages — do not re-read

This note records sources whose held files are **arXiv abstract pages or journal
record pages**, not full texts. Each is a genuine acquisition record but contains
no mathematics beyond the abstract. Their claims are carried by the full-text
sources already digested; these files add nothing new. Kept so nobody re-opens
them expecting content.

## citations_w*.md — citation-graph lookups, not evidence

The `citations_w*.md` files under research/summaries are **citation-graph
lookups** (who-cites-whom tables for Ilyashenko 2002 etc.). As their own header
states: "Every work above is a lead and none of them is evidence: the row says a
paper exists and how often it is cited, which is not what it establishes."
They are pointers to the frontier, not claim-bearing sources. The `frontier`
ledger derives from them. Do not treat a high citation count as authority.
