## Abstract

We give an effective method for controlling the maximum number oflimit cycles of some planar polynomial systems. It is based on asuitable choice of a Dulac function and the application of thewell-known Bendixson-Dulac Criterion for multiple connected regions.The key point is a new approach to control the sign of the functionsinvolved in the criterion. The method is applied to several examples.
