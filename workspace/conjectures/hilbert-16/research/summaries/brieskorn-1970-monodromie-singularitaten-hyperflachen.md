# Brieskorn, "Die Monodromie der isolierten Singularitäten von Hyperflächen" (1970)

<!-- source: https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/brieskorn7.pdf | converted from PDF; full text at research/sources/brieskorn-1970-monodromie-singularitaten-hyperflachen.full.md -->

## What it establishes

Abstract (translated): Milnor recently introduced the local Picard–Lefschetz
monodromy of an isolated singularity of a hypersurface, an action on a certain
cohomology group defined in topological terms. This paper gives an **algebraic
description of the monodromy**: it constructs, by algebraic methods, a **regular
singular ordinary linear differential operator** whose monodromy coincides with
the Picard–Lefschetz monodromy. As an application it proves that **the
eigenvalues of the monodromy are roots of unity**. The treatment is close in
spirit to Grothendieck's theory of the Gauss–Manin connection.

Structure: §0 Resultate; §1 the de Rham lemmas and local cohomology setup;
§2 the regular singular differential operator; §3–5 construction and the
quasi-unipotence conclusion.

  Abelian-integral thread's central bound rests on: BNY 2010 ("On the number of

  system is quasiunipotent was proved by Brieskorn Bri 70 and Clemens Cle 69 by
