# Darboux-type center conditions for families of planar polynomial vector fields

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22558 (2026 preprint; full text at `research/sources/current-center-cyclicity-2026.full.md`).

The paper studies center-focus conditions and Lyapunov constants for homogeneous and non-homogeneous planar polynomial vector-field families of degree n≥2. Its method gives an enclosure of the Bautin ideal generated by Lyapunov constants, and consequently generic cyclicity upper bounds for the local center-focus problem. This is a local restricted result, not a global Hilbert-16.2 bound. Evidence class: asserted-by-source/unrefereed preprint. Falsifier: a failure of the stated genericity hypotheses or an explicit family violating the claimed ideal enclosure or cyclicity estimate.
