## Why the record still matters

Dulac 1923 is the paper whose claimed proof of individual finiteness contained
the gap Ilyashenko found (early 1980s): an asymptotic expansion of the return
map treated as if it determined the map — the exact shape of **Test 1 (the
smooth test)** in problem.md. The library's analysis of *where Dulac erred* is
carried by the held Ilyashenko 2002 Centennial History, the Ilyashenko 2016
digest of the revised proof, and the Yeung 2024 critique (all held). This record
is the citation anchor: Dulac 1923 = DOI 10.24033/bsmf.1031, Bull. SMF 51,
45–188. The Zbl review is 49.0304.01.
