# Binyamini–Dor: uniform Petrov–Khovanskii bound

Source: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1108.1846 (arXiv:1108.1846; published version DOI 10.1088/0951-7715/25/6/1931).

## Established by the source
For a real bivariate polynomial Hamiltonian H, a polynomial 1-form ω, and a continuous family of real ovals δ_t on H=t, the Abelian integral is I_{H,ω}(t)=∫_{δ_t}ω. The paper states the uniform zero-count problem in terms of n=deg H and m=deg ω. It records the Petrov–Khovanskii result as existential and linear in m, and the Binyamini–Novikov–Yakovenko result as explicit but doubly exponential. Its Theorem 5 gives the explicit uniform estimate
\[
\mathcal N(n,m)\le \exp_+^2(n^2)m+\exp_+^5(n^2),
\]
with exp(x)=2^x and exp_+(x)=exp(O_+(x)); the theorem is linear in m and explicit in n.

## Relevance and limits
The theorem applies to the infinitesimal/tangential Hilbert problem for Abelian integrals, not to arbitrary displacement functions of polynomial vector fields. It therefore supplies a restricted, Lean-friendly zero-count target but does not imply H(n)<∞. The analytic input is encoded through Gauss–Manin/Q-system methods, so it is not merely a formal asymptotic expansion.

## Falsifier
A source giving a stronger general explicit estimate under the same hypotheses, or showing that the displayed theorem has been superseded or misstated, would change this record.
