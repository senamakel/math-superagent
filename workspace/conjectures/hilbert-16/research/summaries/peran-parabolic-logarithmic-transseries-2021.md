> Peran, “Normal forms of parabolic logarithmic transseries” (2021).
>
> The paper gives formal normal forms for tangent-to-identity logarithmic transseries using fixed-point/Picard constructions. Its relevance is structural: semihyperbolic Dulac-return asymptotics may involve powers and iterated logarithms, so finite Taylor data or a short power-log germ is not generally an adequate model. This is background for the H^3_14 gap, not a theorem about that specific quadratic graphic. Evidence: asserted-by-source; falsifier: an explicit H^3_14 endpoint calculation proving the relevant return germ belongs to a finite ordinary power-log class with no iterated-log terms.
