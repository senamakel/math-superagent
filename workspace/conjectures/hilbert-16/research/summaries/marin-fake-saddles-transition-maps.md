# Marín 2026 — Fake saddles and their transition maps

[[marin-fake-saddles-transition-maps.full]]

Source: https://ddd.uab.cat/pub/artpub/2026/327411/p11901.pdf; DOI 10.14232/ejqtde.2026.1.5.

Marín characterizes generic fake saddles (zero linear part, nonzero quadratic jet) in a smooth parameter family. In the normal form with invariants a,b,c and d=4(1-c)-(a-b)^2, the generic fake-saddle condition is d>0 (with a separate c=1,a=b case, subject to an excluded degeneracy). For d>0, the transition map across the singular fiber has a parameter-uniform first term e^{gamma_±(mu)}y plus a remainder flat in the unfolding variables; gamma_± is given by the stated principal-value integral and explicit sqrt(d) term. A worked monodromic family has zero local cyclicity at its center, using uniform flat-remainder division.

This is relevant machinery for degenerate quadratic graphics and supports the need for uniform analytic/flat control. It does not derive the H^3_14 second-type endpoint map, close I^1_6b, or prove H16.2. The paper also supplies a counterexample correcting a necessity claim in Coll–Gasull–Prohens 2025.
