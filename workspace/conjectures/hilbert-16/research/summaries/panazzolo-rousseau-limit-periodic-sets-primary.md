# Panazzolo–Rousseau, *Topological classification of limit periodic sets*

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.04965

Primary result: for polynomial planar families, every limit periodic set compactifies to a connected semialgebraic subset of the sphere of dimension 0 or 1 up to homeomorphism; conversely every nonempty closed semialgebraic connected set of dimension 0 or 1 (with connected compactification) occurs as a limit periodic set. Proposition 1.2 classifies the compactified candidate as a singular point, periodic orbit, polycycle, or degenerate limit cycle. The paper supplies the structural topological underpinning for Roussarie/DRR reduction, but does not prove finite cyclicity or H(2)<∞. Evidence: asserted-by-source (primary paper read); falsifier: a counterexample polynomial family or a stronger claimed classification failing one of the stated hypotheses.
