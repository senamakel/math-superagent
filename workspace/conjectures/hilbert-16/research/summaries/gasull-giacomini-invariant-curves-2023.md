# Gasull–Giacomini 2023 — invariant algebraic curves

[[gasull-giacomini-invariant-curves-2023.full]]

Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12346-023-00746-7.

The paper proves restricted nonexistence/counting results when planar polynomial systems possess invariant algebraic or generalized invariant curves. Applications include at-most-one results for several quadratic families (including systems with an invariant parabola or one algebraic cycle), effective parameter/sign conditions, and a finiteness bound depending on degrees when two invariant curves are present. These hypotheses define proper subfamilies and do not yield a uniform upper bound for all quadratic vector fields.

Implication: useful for a restricted Dulac/invariant-curve certificate oracle and negative controls, but no progress on the DRR open graphics or H16.2 globally.
