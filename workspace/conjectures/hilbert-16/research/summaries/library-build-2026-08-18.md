# Local reference-set build: Hilbert 16.2

Date: 2026-08-18

This note records the primary/scholarly material now available under `research/sources/`. URLs are preserved in each source's summary or source header.

## Core problem and status

- **Dumortier–Roussarie–Rousseau (1994)**, *Hilbert's 16th problem for quadratic vector fields*, JDE 110, 86–133. Held record: [`drr-1994-record`](../sources/drr-1994-record-held-verbatim.md is represented by the existing DRR record/full capture). URL: http://hdl.handle.net/1942/3763. It gives the quadratic compactification/list of graphics and the finite-cyclicity reduction framework; the local capture is bibliographic/abstract-level, while the DRR companion corpus supplies the mathematics.
- **Ilyashenko (2002)**, *Centennial history of Hilbert's 16th problem*, Bull. AMS 39, 301–354. Full source: [`ilyashenko-centennial-history-hilbert-16.full.md`](../sources/ilyashenko-centennial-history-hilbert-16.full.md). URL retained in source metadata/mirror. It states individual polynomial-field finiteness (Écalle 1992/Ilyashenko 1991), the elementary-polycycle definitions and bounds, and the tangential problem.
- **Rousseau–Zhu (2002/2004)**, pp-graphics through nilpotent elliptic singularities. Full source: [`rousseau-zhu-pp-graphics-nilpotent-elliptic.full.md`](../sources/rousseau-zhu-pp-graphics-nilpotent-elliptic.full.md). DOI/search URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-0396(02)00106-5 (the held file also preserves the YorkSpace/preprint URL). It records the 121-graphic reduction and closes the pp-graphics in the stated elliptic nilpotent class.

## Elementary-polycycle theory

- **Ilyashenko–Yakovenko (2000)**, *Hilbert's 16th problem for dynamical systems*, full source [`ilyashenko-yakovenko-elementary-polycycles-2000.full.md`](../sources/ilyashenko-yakovenko-elementary-polycycles-2000.full.md). URL: https://export.arxiv.org/pdf/math/0010174v1.pdf. It supplies the generic finite-parameter/elementary-polycycle reduction and Khovanskii-style zero-count machinery.
- **Kaloshin (2000/2001)**, *The Hilbert 16-th problem and an estimate for cyclicity of an elementary polycycle*, source [`kaloshin-elementary-polycycle-2000.full.md`](../sources/kaloshin-elementary-polycycle-2000.full.md). URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0010174. The held conversion is primarily a record/abstract page; the full Ilyashenko–Yakovenko source and the Ilyashenko survey carry the theorem statement. The survey states E(k) ≤ 2^(25 k²).
- **Kaleda–Shchurov (2011)**, elementary polycycles, source [`kaleda-shchurov-elementary-polycycles-2011.full.md`](../sources/kaleda-shchurov-elementary-polycycles-2011.full.md). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S1061-0022-2011-01158-6. Held capture is a journal landing page, so exact theorem details remain to be checked from the paper itself.

## Degenerate/nilpotent quadratic graphics

- **Rousseau–Shan–Zhu (2015)**, *Finite cyclicity of some graphics through a nilpotent point of saddle type inside quadratic systems*, full source [`drr-nilpotent-saddle-graphics-2015-arxiv.full.md`](../sources/drr-nilpotent-saddle-graphics-2015-arxiv.full.md). URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00689. The source states finite cyclicity for I^1_12 and I^1_13 and gives the normal-form/blow-up/Dulac-map method.
- **Roussarie–Rousseau (2015)**, *Finite cyclicity of some center graphics through a nilpotent point inside quadratic systems*, full source [`rousseau-rousseau-2015-center-graphics-arxiv.full.md`](../sources/rousseau-rousseau-2015-center-graphics-arxiv.full.md). URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07104. It proves full finite cyclicity for I^1_14 and only boundary-set finite cyclicity for I^1_6b, H^3_13, DI_2b.
- **Dumortier–Guzmán–Rousseau (2002)**, source [`dumortier-guzman-rousseau-elementary-graphics-focus-center-2002.full.md`](../sources/dumortier-guzman-rousseau-elementary-graphics-focus-center-2002.full.md). URL: http://www.dms.umontreal.ca/~rousseac/DGR.pdf. It proves explicit finite bounds for seven elementary graphics.
- **Huzak (2018)**, DF2a cyclicity, source [`huzak-cyclicity-degenerate-df2a.full.md`](../sources/huzak-cyclicity-degenerate-df2a.full.md). DOI: https://doi.org/10.3934/cpaa.2018062. The held capture is abstract/record-level and is not evidence for transferring the theorem to I^1_6b.

## Analytic/displacement and Abelian-integral instruments

- **Binyamini–Novikov–Yakovenko (2010)**, *On the number of zeros of Abelian integrals*, Invent. Math. 181. Full source [`binyamini-novikov-yakovenko-abelian-integrals.html.full.md`](../sources/binyamini-novikov-yakovenko-abelian-integrals.html.full.md). URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.2952. Theorem 1 bounds cycles born from nonsingular Hamiltonian ovals by 2^(2^Poly(n)), exponent at most 61; Theorem 2 gives the corresponding explicit Abelian-integral zero bound. Remarks exclude separatrix polygons and integrable higher-variation cases.
- **Binyamini–Dor (2012)**, *A uniform version of the Petrovsky–Khovanskii theorem*, source [`binyamini-dor-nonlinearity-2012.full.md`](../sources/binyamini-dor-nonlinearity-2012.full.md). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/0951-7715/25/6/1931; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1846. Gives an explicit bound linear in deg(ω), with explicit dependence on deg(H), under the Abelian-integral hypotheses.
- **Grau–Manosas–Villadelprat (2008)**, Chebyshev criteria, source [`grau-manosas-villadelprat-chebyshev-2008-arxiv.full.md`](../sources/grau-manosas-villadelprat-chebyshev-2008-arxiv.full.md). URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.1140. Gives sufficient ECT/CT criteria for specified Abelian-integral families; not directly the full I^1_6b displacement.
- **Gavrilov (1999)**, *Abelian integrals related to Morse polynomials...*, source [`gavrilov-abelian-morse-hamiltonian-aif-1999.full.md`](../sources/gavrilov-abelian-morse-hamiltonian-aif-1999.full.md). URL: http://www.numdam.org/article/AIF_1999__49_2_611_0.pdf. Petrov-module freeness and quadratic-Hamiltonian applications.

## Status and caveats

The local library is sufficient for the initial phase criterion: `research/ROOT.md` states the minimal-counterexample structure (infinite cyclicity of a compactified limit-periodic set/graphic), the verification boundary, and at least three settled restricted classes. It does **not** prove H16.2, H(2)<∞, or H(2)=4. The exact post-2015 graphic-by-graphic ledger remains unresolved; the corpus documents a 121/125 convention discrepancy and partial-vs-full closures. Several held files are landing/record pages rather than complete proofs; those are labelled accordingly in their summaries.
