# An–Dai–Hu 2025 — special hyperelliptic Abelian integrals

[[an-dai-hu-chebyshev-hyperelliptic-2025.html.full]]

Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12346-025-01330-x.

The abstract states that three explicitly studied classes of complete hyperelliptic first-kind integrals have the Chebyshev property, hence at most one zero (and sharp bound one), using a criterion function and symbolic methods. The full class hypotheses were not extracted in this pass, so this remains asserted-by-source rather than a promoted general claim.

Implication: a possible Lean-friendly special-family Abelian-integral target, but it does not bound arbitrary Abelian integrals or displacement functions and does not affect H16.2 status until hypotheses are read and checked.
