# Gasull–Santana 2024 — structural facts about H(n)

[[gasull-santana-note-h16-2024-arxiv.html.full]]

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13465.

For H(n), defined using isolated periodic orbits of planar polynomial fields of degree n, Gasull–Santana state H(n+1) >= H(n)+1. They also state conditionally that if H(n) is finite, it is attained by a structurally stable field with hyperbolic cycles; if H(n)=infinity, arbitrarily large finite hyperbolic counts occur. The note records H(2)>=4, H(3)>=13, H(4)>=28 and known n^2 log n lower growth. It explicitly leaves H(2)<infinity open.

Implication: clarifies status and reduces any finite-maximizer search conditionally to hyperbolic examples, but supplies no upper bound and does not address DRR degeneracies.
