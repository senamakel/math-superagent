## What was added this cycle (5 primary full texts / records)

1. **Kaiser–Rolin–Speissegger 2009 (arXiv math/0612745), "Transition maps at
   non-resonant hyperbolic singularities are o-minimal"** — the primary source
   for the load-bearing Established claim `h16-ominimality-route-roussarie`,
   previously held only as a citation inside Speissegger's survey.
   - Full text: `research/sources/kaiser-rolin-speissegger-transition-maps-ominimal.full.md`
   - Result: constructs a model-complete, o-minimal, polynomially bounded
     expansion ℝ_𝒬 of the real field (defines all convergent generalized power
     series with natural support) in which the transition map of any planar
     analytic vector field at an isolated **non-resonant** hyperbolic
     singularity is definable. Consequence (Theorem [2] in Speissegger's
     survey): Roussarie's finite-cyclicity conjecture holds for the NRH_d
     class — the subfamily with only non-resonant hyperbolic singularities.
   - 11 new frontier citations added by the download.

2. **Han–Li–Li, "Limit cycles of planar polynomial vector fields",
   Scholarpedia 5(8):9648** — the canonical encyclopedic entry for H16.2.
   - Full text: `research/sources/han-li-li-scholarpedia-limit-cycles.full.md`
   - Confirms: H(2)≥4 (L. Chen & M. Wang 1979; S. Shi 1980), "widely
     conjectured H(2)=4"; **Petrovsky–Landis H(2)=3 proof erroneous**;
     Lins–de Melo–Pugh conjecture H(m,1)=[m/2] disproved with 4 limit cycles
     for degree m=6; H(1,1)=0, H(2,1)=1 (LMP 1977), H(1,2)=1 (Coppel 1988),
     H(2,2)=1 (Li 1986; Dumortier–Li).
   - 78 new frontier citations added.

3. **Yu–Zeng 2020, "Visualization of four limit cycles in near-integrable
   quadratic polynomial systems", IJBC 30(15)** — full open preprint from
   Western U (publish.uwo.ca/~pyu).
   - Full text: `research/sources/yu-zeng-four-limit-cycles-near-integrable-2020.full.md`
   - Gives the explicit Shi and near-integrable quadratic four-cycle systems
     that realise H(2)≥4, including the (3,1) distribution; the Shi equation
     explicit coefficients appear in the held text.
   - Caveat: this is a *visualization*/numerical paper, not a certificate —
     suitable as the construction record, not as a proof.
