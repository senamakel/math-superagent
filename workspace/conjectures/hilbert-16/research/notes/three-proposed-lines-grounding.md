# Literature grounding record

The three inventor proposals were researched and filed under `research/approaches/`.

- `dulac-positivstellensatz-region-atlas`: **grounded**, but restricted. This is Bendixson–Dulac exclusion combined with SOS/Positivstellensatz certificates. Gasull–Giacomini, DOI https://doi.org/10.3934/dcds.2010.27.217, gives constructive restricted polynomial Dulac bounds; August–Barahona, DOI https://doi.org/10.3934/jcd.2022017, gives SOS certificates for invariant sets and stable cycles. No universal H(2) application was found.
- `rotated-field-sturm-comparison`: **grounded**, but restricted. This is the rotated-vector-field/field-rotation parameter method with Wintner–Perko termination and Sturm-certified algebraic sign/root tests. Sources: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10958-005-0087-1, https://doi.org/10.1216/rmj-1984-14-3-619, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmaa.2015.03.015. The determinant sign and scalar-reduction hypotheses are essential; no application to an open DRR graphic was found.
- `schwarzian-return-map-cross-ratio`: **narrowed**, not general-grounded. Schwarzian/cross-ratio theory is established for restricted nonsmooth or unimodal maps: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022039696901771, https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.math/0011266, https://doi.org/10.3934/dcds.2009.24.1393. These hypotheses do not automatically hold for analytic Poincare diffeomorphisms, and no DRR application was found. It remains viable only for explicitly verified special subclasses.

Search evidence is distinguishable from proof: the cited papers support the named restricted methods, not a solution of Hilbert 16.2.
