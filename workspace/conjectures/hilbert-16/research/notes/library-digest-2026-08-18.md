## Core structural sources
- DRR 1994, as reported by Ilyashenko 2002 and RSZ/RR 2015: for quadratic fields, the compactification reduces uniform finiteness to finite cyclicity of 121 graphics. The exact row-by-row current ledger is not held, so no complete open-list claim is made.
- Rousseau–Rousseau 2015: I^1_14 is fully finite-cyclic; I^1_6b, H^3_13, DI_2b receive only boundary-stratum results. The full I^1_6b displacement can involve four second-type Dulac maps and a coupled two-equation problem; this is a concrete unresolved target.
- Zhu–Rousseau 2002, DIR 2002, DGR 2002, Roussarie–Rousseau 2008: restricted nilpotent, saddle-node, elementary and pp-type graphics are finite-cyclic under their stated normal-form/genericity hypotheses; these do not transfer automatically to the remaining degenerate graphics.
- Ilyashenko–Yakovenko and Kaloshin: elementary polycycles in generic finite-parameter families have finite cyclicity (Kaloshin gives an explicit exponential-shaped estimate). The elementary hypothesis is essential and excludes the nilpotent/degenerate obstruction.

## Analytic boundary
- Écalle/Ilyashenko sources support individual analytic-field finiteness via accelero-summation/quasianalytic methods, but the general proof is contested by Yeung 2024-25 for non-hyperbolic/semi-hyperbolic cases. Yeung is a challenge to proof completeness, not a disproof of the theorem. Bautin's/other quadratic pointwise finiteness is independently recorded. Any displacement proof must use analyticity/quasianalyticity/algebraicity; formal asymptotics alone fail the smooth test.

## Tangential route
- Binyamini–Novikov–Yakovenko: explicit doubly-exponential zero bound for Abelian integrals over nonsingular ovals, with degree dependence O^+(n^60) in the exponent as recorded. Binyamini–Dor improves dependence to linear in deg omega with exp^+(n^2) coefficients. These settle only first-order Hamiltonian/tangential H16, not the nonlinear return displacement or H(2).
- Novikov–Yakovenko and Gavrilov provide Petrov-module/Picard–Fuchs structure under their polynomial/Morse hypotheses; GMV gives Chebyshev criteria in selected families. They are instruments for restricted Abelian-integral counts, not a full H16 solution.

## Bounds and calibration
- Bautin: M(2)=3 local focus cyclicity. Galias–Tucker rigorously certify four global quadratic cycles, hence H(2)>=4. Held claims give H(3)>=13, H(4)>=28; M(3)>=11 is supported by the Zoladek/Tian–Yu analysis, with later library evidence of 12. Christopher–Lloyd/Han–Li give H(n) asymptotic lower growth at least order n^2 log n, ruling out quadratic upper bounds.
- Lu 2026 claims local uniform finite cyclicity for H^3_14 in a five-parameter unfolding, but is unrefereed and its analytic root/domain/zero-theorem remainder is unchecked. The workspace independently verifies only algebraic identities (Darboux cofactors, L4 obstruction, degree-6 P30 relation), not the theorem.
