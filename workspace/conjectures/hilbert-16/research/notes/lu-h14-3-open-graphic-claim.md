# Finding: the DRR graphic (H³₁₄) — claimed closed by Lu 2026

## The recorded open graphic

The held Roussarie–Rousseau "Finite cyclicity of some center graphics through a
nilpotent point inside quadratic systems" (arXiv:1506.07104, Moscow Math. J.
76(2) 2015) states:

> "We have a partial result for every graphic, but one (namely (H³₁₄)), through a
> triple point at infinity."

So **(H³₁₄)** — a graphic through a triple nilpotent point at infinity
(saddle/elliptic/degenerate type) surrounding a center — was explicitly left
open as of 2015. Theorem 1.1 there proves finite cyclicity of the *boundary*
limit periodic sets of (I¹₁₄), (I¹₆b), (H³₁₃), (DI₂b); Theorem 1.2 proves the
*full* finite cyclicity of (I¹₁₄) only.

## What has now been found

**H. Lu, "Local Uniform Finite Cyclicity of the H₁₄³ Semihyperbolic Hemicycle",
arXiv:2607.13785 (submitted 15 Jul 2026, v2 17 Jul 2026), 80 pp.**

The paper claims **local uniform finite cyclicity** of the H₁₄³ graphic in one
fixed annular collar, uniformly over the full five-parameter quotient unfolding.
It is explicitly identified with the Roussarie–Rousseau left-open case: the
paper says "the value B = 0 is precisely the labelled H₁₄³ case [of RR Theorem
3.1]" and that "Roussarie–Rousseau explicitly leave the B = 0 H₁₄³ case outside
their finite-cyclicity result."

## Provenance / evidence class: ASSERTED-BY-SOURCE, unrefereed

- Single-author arXiv preprint, **not peer-reviewed**, no journal publication
  found, dated 2026-07.
- 80 pages, computer-assisted finite derivations, with ancillary reproducibility
  materials (a `h14_3_reproducibility/` bundle: Bautin-recurrence and center-basis
  verification scripts, chart checks, a MANIFEST and environment lock).
- It is the same graphic (H³₁₄ = H₁₄³) recorded as open in our library, but this
  is a **claim**, not an established result — it needs independent verification
  and community/peer acceptance. The bound is existential (no explicit number).

## What this does NOT say

- It is *local uniform* finite cyclicity in one collar, over the parameter
  unfolding. It is NOT the full DRR-program "finite cyclicity inside quadratic
  systems" as defined for every graphic, though local uniform finite cyclicity
  of a graphic is the standard content of a "graphic has finite cyclicity" claim
  in the DRR listing. Whether the paper's collar + five-parameter result exactly
  matches the DRR definition of finite cyclicity of the limit periodic set (with
  the boundary sets at infinity too) must be checked against the DRR definition.
- Proving (H³₁₄) finitely cyclic is ONE graphic. The DRR program still needs all
  121; even at 88-by-2015 plus post-2015 closures, several dozen remain.
