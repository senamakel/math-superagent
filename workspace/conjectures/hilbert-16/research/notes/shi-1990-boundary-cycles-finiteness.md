# Shi Songling 1990 — finiteness of boundary cycles that are not limit cycles

*Scholar claim block, from the source acquired this cycle.*

Source: Shi Songling, "On the finiteness of certain boundary cycles for NTH degree polynomial vector fields", in Françoise–Roussarie (eds), Bifurcations of Planar Vector Fields, LNM 1455 (1990) 369–372, DOI 10.1007/BFb0085401.
Held: `research/sources/shi-songling-1990-boundary-cycles-lnm.full.md` (abstract + references; paywalled body).

The abstract states: "In this paper we give a precise definition of a boundary cycle for a polynomial vector field and prove the finiteness of boundary cycles which are not limit cycles for polynomial vector fields of degree n."

Context: this is the same Shi Songling who proved H(2) ≥ 4 (1980). It is a rare primary Shi source on a finiteness statement for degree-n polynomial fields — a distinct precursor to the Dulac/Écalle–Ilyashenko finiteness program, about "boundary cycles" (accumulation sets of periodic orbits) that are not themselves limit cycles.
