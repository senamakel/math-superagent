# Librarian cycle update — 2026-08-18

## Sources newly added or verified
- Li–Llibre–Zhang, *Abelian integrals of quadratic Hamiltonian vector fields with an invariant straight line* (1995), primary PDF and digest: `research/sources/li-abelian-invariant-line-1995-primary.full.md`. Theorem 6 states `N(2,2)=1` for quadratic perturbations of quadratic Hamiltonian fields with a center and invariant straight line.
- Malev–Novikov, *Linear estimate for the number of zeros of Abelian integrals* (arXiv:0903.5056), full ar5iv text: `research/sources/abelian-linear-bound-0903.5056.full.md`. For `H=x²y(1-x-y)`, real oval `t∈(0,1/64)`, and polynomial 1-form degree `n`, Theorem 1.1 gives at most `(7/4)n+9` isolated zeros; Theorem 1.2 gives `(7n+43)/4` limit cycles in the associated Pfaffian perturbation. This is a special-family linear bound, not full H16.2.
- Horozov–Iliev, *Perturbations of quadratic Hamiltonian systems with symmetry* (1996), Numdam full text: `research/sources/horozov-iliev-quadratic-central-symmetry-1996.full.md`. Theorem 1: generic cubic Hamiltonian with central symmetry, arbitrary quadratic perturbation, at most two limit cycles for sufficiently small epsilon.
- Gavrilov–Iliev, *Second-order analysis in polynomially perturbed reversible quadratic Hamiltonian systems* (2000), held full text: `research/sources/gavrilov-iliev-second-order-reversible-2000.full.md`. For degree-n perturbations of the specified reversible quadratic Hamiltonian, first nonzero Poincare–Pontryagin function gives exact upper bound `n−1`; if first vanishes and second does not, exact upper bound `2(n−1)`; for quadratic perturbations near the Hamiltonian field, at most two.
- Gavrilov–Iliev, *Perturbations of quadratic Hamiltonian two-saddle cycles* (arXiv:1306.2340), held as `research/sources/two-saddle-loop-cyclicity-2013.full.md`. Theorem 1 gives cyclicity at most 3 for a nondegenerate Hamiltonian two-saddle loop under arbitrary quadratic deformation; source records conjectured exact bound 2.
