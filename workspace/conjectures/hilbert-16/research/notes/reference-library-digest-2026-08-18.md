## Load-bearing source findings

- **DRR 1994; RSZ 2015; RR 2015.** Quadratic H(2) uniform finiteness reduces to finite cyclicity of 121 compactified graphics. RSZ reports 88/121 closed. RR fully closes I¹₁₄, but only boundary limit-periodic sets for I¹₆b, H¹³₃ and DI₂b. H³₁₄ had no partial result in RR 2015. A complete post-2015 row-by-row ledger is absent; Shan 2013 uses a conflicting 125 count. Implication: these are precise targets, not a proof of H(2).

- **Ilyashenko–Yakovenko 2000; Kaloshin.** Elementary polycycles in generic finite-parameter families have finite cyclicity, with explicit exponential-type estimates. Elementary/nonresonant hypotheses are essential; nilpotent/degenerate quadratic graphics are outside the theorem.

- **BNY 2010; Binyamini–Dor.** Abelian integrals over nonsingular compact ovals of polynomial Hamiltonians have explicit uniform zero bounds. BNY is doubly exponential in degree; Binyamini–Dor improves dependence to linear in deg(omega), with exp⁺(deg(H)^2) factors. This is tangential H16 only, not the nonlinear full displacement problem.

- **Bautin; Galias–Tucker; Li–Liu–Yang; Christopher–Lloyd/Han–Li; Torregrosa.** M(2)=3; H(2)>=4; H(3)>=13; H(4)>=28 in later constructions; M(3)>=11 is sourced; H(n) has n² log n lower growth. Hence quadratic-order global upper bounds are impossible. Torregrosa's high cubic local cyclicity makes a twelfth-cycle target stale.

- **Lu 2026.** Claims local uniform finite cyclicity for H³₁₄ in a five-parameter quadratic unfolding. Workspace exact captures independently verify the finite algebraic Bautin identities, but not analytic root uniqueness, Hadamard divisibility, domain completeness, or the final zero theorem. The preprint is unrefereed; it does not close H³₁₄ or H(2).

- **Ilyashenko/Écalle vs Yeung.** Classical individual-field finiteness is presented by the classical sources, but Yeung claims a gap in the non-hyperbolic Ilyashenko proof and community status is contested. This is a proof-status contradiction, not a disproof. Bamon separately supports individual quadratic finiteness.

## Sources that do not help directly

Landing/record pages and citation-graph files establish at most their abstracts and are not evidence for detailed theorem hypotheses. Huzak's DF₂a slow-divergence result cannot be transferred to the distinct I¹₆b four-Dulac displacement. Lu's algebraic identities cannot substitute for its analytic remainder argument. Pedregal's variational claim is unrefereed and fails the smooth-test audit because it does not identify the analytic return-map step; it is not usable evidence for H16.2.
