# RSZ 1998 — exact cyclicities for semi-hyperbolic graphics in quadratic systems

*Scholar claim block, from the source acquired this cycle.*

Source: Rousseau, Świrszcz, Żołądek, "Cyclicity of graphics with semi-hyperbolic points inside quadratic systems", J. Dynam. Control Systems 4 (1998) 149–189, DOI 10.1023/A:1022887001627.
Held: `research/sources/rousseau-swirszcz-zoladek-1998-semi-hyperbolic-graphics.full.md` (abstract + references; paywalled body).

The abstract states, verbatim: "the proof of the finite cyclicity of four elementary graphics among quadratic systems with two semi-hyperbolic points and surrounding a center, namely, the graphics (I²₅), (I¹₉), (H²₅) and (H¹₁₁)... We show that the cyclicity of each of the first three graphics is equal to 2 and that the cyclicity of the fourth one is equal to three. We improve the known results about finite cyclicity of the graphics (H¹₈), (H¹₁₀), (I²₄) by showing that their cyclicities are equal to 2."
