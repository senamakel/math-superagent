idea: The full displacement of an open non-hyperbolic DRR graphic (I^1_6b, H^3_13, DI_2b) is a sum of four second-type Dulac maps. The three refuted candidates each named a different container for that sum and each failed at the SAME place: not closed under the operations the displacement uses (addition, composition, specialization), and no zero theorem stable on the vanishing slow-divergence strata. The synthesis tests the one container research surfaced as proved-closed and never yet tried on an open graphic: the Rolin–Servi generalized quasianalytic algebra. Exhibit the full four-map displacement of one named graphic as an element of a finite-rank module over a Rolin–Servi quasianalytic algebra, then prove a specialization-stable zero bound on every slow-divergence stratum, including the identically-zero one.
mechanism: Rolin–Servi (PLMS 110 (2015) 773–825, doi:10.1112/plms/pdv010; Servi AIF 65 (2015), doi:10.5802/aif.2933): generalized quasianalytic algebras — including classes containing Dulac-type transition maps near hyperbolic non-resonant singularities and multisummable series — are o-minimal, hence closed under addition, composition, and specialization, with the definable zero property (finitely many isolated zeros, uniform on compact parameter boxes). Closure under addition is exactly what the ECT shortcut lacked (toy refutation (1,x)+(−1,−x)=0); specialization-stability is exactly what the ECT toy showed ECT lacks (Wronskian rank collapses at a=0); composition-closure is what the fewnomial/short-function line lacked (non-hyperbolic germs are transseries, not short); definability is what the o-minimality line lacked (L_trans is the open conjecture — Rolin–Servi is the proved sub-structure). Change of representation: instead of asking whether each passage is ECT/Pfaffian/short and then summing (fails), ask whether the SUMMED displacement lies in a quasianalytic algebra closed under the sum by construction; the zero theorem is the o-minimality zero property applied to the displacement as a single definable function. (1) The four second-type Dulac maps are individually multisummable (Ilyashenko Centennial History Thm 4.12; Écalle accelero-summation) — multisummable series are the input class of the Rolin–Servi algebras. (2) The displacement is a SUM of them, and the algebra is closed under addition. (3) The vanishing slow-divergence strata are parameter specializations, and o-minimal structures are stable under specialization. (4) Analyticity bites at the multisummability step (Test 1): a C^∞ field has no multisummable Dulac expansion, hence no algebra membership, hence no zero theorem — Dulac's error made precise.

Deliverable is the membership certificate, not a zero count: the summed displacement as an element of a finite-rank module over a proved-closed algebra is the missing hypothesis for every route this run has tried.
