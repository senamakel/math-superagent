**Adopted scope.** This is a restricted, displacement-centered result, not a claim about all quadratic fields or H(2). The theory is the rotated-vector-field (field-rotation parameter) principle, combined with Cherkas/Riccati reduction and exact Sturm comparison. A fixed-sign rotation determinant gives monotone motion of cycles with the parameter; Wintner-Perko controls possible termination. The proposed additional step is accepted only when an explicit scalar comparison theorem is written with all endpoint and nonsingularity hypotheses.

**Why this beat the alternatives.** It is the only candidate whose natural object is directly the parameterized nonlinear displacement and for which the literature supplies a matching monotonicity/termination framework. The Dulac/Positivstellensatz route is useful but is primarily a no-cycle exclusion certificate; it cannot address the cycle-supporting cells and substantially overlaps the already grounded restricted Dulac/SOS direction. Schwarzian/cross-ratio theory requires unimodal maps with critical points, whereas a regular analytic Poincare return map is normally an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism; its proposed fixed-point bound therefore lacks applicable hypotheses.

**Required attack before trusting it.** Verify the rotation determinant globally on the chosen box, verify that the Cherkas transformation is nonsingular, and prove rather than assume that the scalar comparison equation controls zeros of the nonlinear displacement. A sign change, endpoint singularity, or mismatch between auxiliary Sturm roots and displacement roots narrows the result and is recorded as a counterexample, not hidden.
