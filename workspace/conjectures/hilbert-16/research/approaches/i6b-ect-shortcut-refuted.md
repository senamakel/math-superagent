# Four-second-type common ECT shortcut — refuted as an inference

An exact symbolic toy probe composed four second-type passage expressions with logarithmic/iterated-log terms and computed derivation–division Wronskians. It obtained W3 identically zero (capture: `code/out/i6b_second_type_toy.captured.txt`); an independent probe also found W(1,x,(x-1)^2)=0. Therefore passage-wise Chebyshev-like behavior does not imply that the sum of four passages lies in a common finite-rank ECT family. This is not a counterexample to the actual I^1_6b dynamics.

RR 2015 supplies first-type formulas but explicitly does not establish the needed second-type endpoint germs. The remaining requirement is a uniform analytic/quasianalytic reduction of the complete four-map displacement, including vanishing slow-divergence strata and remainder control.
