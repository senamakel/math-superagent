```approach
idea: Treat the Bautin ideal and the zero locus of the displacement as an algebraic family over parameter space, and use generic flatness, Rees algebras, and effective Noetherianity to stratify parameter space by finite multiplicity without resolving individual Dulac maps.
mechanism: The coefficients of the analytic displacement define an infinite ideal in the coefficient ring. If one proves that, for the fixed-degree polynomial family, this ideal is the pullback of a finitely generated coherent ideal on a finite jet scheme after a finite sequence of algebraic blow-ups, then generic flatness gives a finite stratification on which the order of vanishing and the local intersection multiplicity are constant. The exceptional strata are handled recursively by the Rees valuation/initial-ideal data. Analyticity is used to identify the analytic displacement ideal with its convergent formal completion (or to supply a Weierstrass division theorem); uniformity comes from finite algebraic stratification, not from pointwise finiteness. The finite core is Gröbner bases, saturation, Hilbert functions, and resultant certificates suitable for Lean.
status: narrowed
survives: The established reformulation is Bautin-ideal / reduced-Bautin-depth theory for analytic Poincare maps near monodromic singularities. Noetherianity gives finite generation, and integral-closure stabilization gives cyclicity bounds when the hypotheses are met. Generic flatness and Rees algebras are legitimate algebraic tools for stratifying finite algebraic families, but they do not by themselves identify the infinite Dulac displacement ideal with a finite jet ideal. This surviving restriction (García's reduced Bautin depth for monodromic singularities) is the core of the adopted synthesis `reduced-bautin-depth-chen-coefficient-engine`.
killed-by: The hypotheses needed for the proposed global reduction are absent for the open DRR graphics: the full displacement is a composition of nonhyperbolic Dulac maps, not an analytic germ in an ordinary transversal coordinate at the singular boundary with polynomial coefficient functions. No theorem found asserting finite-jet pullback, coherent finite-jet representability, or effective generic-flatness stratification for these four-map Dulac compositions. Generic flatness applies after a finite-type morphism is already supplied; it cannot manufacture that finite-type model.
```
