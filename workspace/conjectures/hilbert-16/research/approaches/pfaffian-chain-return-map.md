```approach
idea: Bound zeros of the return/displacement map by Khovanskii's theory of Pfaffian functions: represent the parameterized return map on a regularized chart by a Pfaffian chain with uniform format and apply a Pfaffian zero bound.
mechanism: A Pfaffian chain is triangular: df_i is polynomial in the base variables and f_1,...,f_i. Khovanskii-type bounds give finitely many isolated nondegenerate common zeros of Pfaffian equations, with an explicit bound depending on chain order, degree, and equation degrees. For return maps, one must first establish a single chain covering all local/semilocal passages and the parameter domain, and handle degenerate zeros by a separate multiplicity argument.
status: narrowed
survives: The elementary/quasielementary restriction, where Kaloshin's E(k) ≤ 2^{25k²} (arXiv:math/0111053) is the grounded theorem. The open-graphic extension of this idea is carried by the adopted synthesis compensator-pfaffian-mourtada-moussu-synthesis, which supplies the missing chain via the Mourtada–Moussu bridge (Dulac map 1-Pfaffian iff analytically normalisable, DOI 10.24033/bsmf.2297).
precedent: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0111053 (Kaloshin 2001); https://doi.org/10.1090/s1061-0022-2011-01158-6 (Kaleda–Shchurov 2011); https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401079 (Zell 2004); https://doi.org/10.1090/bull/2002-39-03 (Ilyashenko 2002); claims: h16-kaloshin-indep-proof, drr-demr-1996-elementary-graphics, h16-drr-121-graphics
hypotheses: Kaloshin's precise cyclicity theorem applies to generic finite-parameter families and elementary/quasielementary polycycles; in the planar theorem all vertices must be elementary. Khovanskii's zero theorem applies to a fixed Pfaffian chain on a suitable domain and directly bounds isolated nondegenerate solutions. The current open quadratic graphics contain nilpotent/degenerate vertices and the full four-second-type I^1_6b displacement is not known to admit the required one-chain representation.
holds-here: The theorem and method hold for the elementary restricted class, not for the open DRR graphics as currently stated. Thus this is grounded as a restricted method, not as a solution of H(2).
what-it-buys: It gives a genuine finite-cyclicity theorem and explicit bound on elementary polycycles (Kaloshin records E(k) <= 2^(25 k^2)); it also gives a concrete diagnostic for an open graphic: prove a uniform Pfaffian-chain representation, including all parameter-dependent exponents and passages. Without that representation it buys no bound for the target.
falsifier: A published theorem or explicit construction showing that the full nonhyperbolic four-Dulac return map has a single uniform Pfaffian chain of bounded format, together with a multiplicity-sensitive zero theorem, would upgrade this from restricted precedent to a live H16 route. Conversely, a passage requiring non-Pfaffian parameter-dependent exponentiation or a chain format unbounded over the parameter stratum would kill the proposed extension.
```
