killed-by: Hill determinants encode linear stability/monodromy of an already-existing periodic orbit, not the nonlinear displacement zero set. The proposed correspondence between zeros of a spectral discriminant in an auxiliary spectral parameter and bifurcating limit cycles is not a theorem in the cited literature. Planar polynomial fields are not generally Lagrangian, and no uniform polynomial-degree growth estimate over all cycles is supplied. Thus the global trace-formula step is not merely unproved; the route lacks the required object-level identification.
