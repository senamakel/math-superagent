```approach
idea: Represent the return/displacement map after blow-up as a Chen series of iterated integrals in a finite-dimensional differential graded algebra, then use the shuffle algebra and a Lyndon-word basis to isolate the first nonzero holonomy coefficient.
mechanism: Along each analytic chart, variation of constants gives a path-ordered exponential; composing the four passages is multiplication in the shuffle Hopf algebra. The displacement is a coordinate of this holonomy. If the polynomial family forces a bounded alphabet of one-forms and a bounded word length after the relevant weighted blow-up, vanishing of the first coefficients becomes a finite shuffle-ideal membership problem, while a quasianalytic remainder theorem controls the full germ. This differs from the rejected short-germ/fewnomial route: it retains iterated logarithms and exponential scales as words in a noncommutative algebra rather than pretending they lie in a finite monomial module. Analyticity enters through convergence/quasianalyticity of the Chen expansion; uniformity would have to come from a degree-dependent bound on alphabet and word length.
status: narrowed
survives: Chen/Fliess/shuffle expansions are established for analytic ODE return maps and Abel equations as a COEFFICIENT ENGINE (exact word-by-word generation of return-map coefficients), even though the proposed finite word-length/finite shuffle-ideal determinacy theorem for the full nonhyperbolic four-Dulac graphic is not established. This surviving coefficient-engine role is absorbed into the adopted synthesis `reduced-bautin-depth-chen-coefficient-engine`, where it feeds García's reduced-Bautin-depth stabilization.
killed-by: No source found giving a bounded word length or finite shuffle-ideal determinacy for compositions of second-type Dulac maps at nilpotent/degenerate vertices. Iterated-integral expansions are generally infinite; finite generation of the Bautin ideal is Noetherian, but does not imply that a prescribed finite word truncation determines the analytic holonomy or a uniform zero bound.
precedent: https://doi.org/10.1155/2009/590856; https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08655; https://doi.org/10.1007/s10883-014-9227-6; https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bulsci.2019.02.001; claims: h16-mv-dulac-coefficient-formulas-2024, i6b-four-second-type-full-graphic-not-covered, rr2015-i6b-boundary-cyclicity
what-it-buys: An exact, Lean-friendly coefficient generator for restricted analytic/nilpotent return maps, and possibly center-ideal computations or finite-order obstructions. It does not currently buy uniform finite cyclicity for I^1_6b or the other open DRR graphics.
```
