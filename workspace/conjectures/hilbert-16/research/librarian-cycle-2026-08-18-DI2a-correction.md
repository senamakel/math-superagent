# Librarian cycle report — DI2a status correction and degenerate-graphics ledger

## The headline finding: `(DI2a)` is NOT closed — a held summary over-claimed

The library's summary `research/summaries/artes-dumortier-llibre-DI2a-hyperbolas.md`
recorded the DRR degenerate graphic `(DI₂a)` as a **closed** degenerate graphic
("methodological template, not an attack target"). This cycle established that
this was an over-claim, on the primary record:

1. **DR 2009 held full text, line 50** — Dumortier–Rousseau, "Study of the
   cyclicity of some degenerate graphics inside quadratic systems" (CPAA
   8:1133–1157), writing about the same programme, says verbatim:
   **"Partial results on the cyclicity of the graphic (DI2a) are ready to be
   presented as a preprint ([ADL])"** — i.e. the ADL paper is *partial results*,
   not a closure.
2. **Shan 2013 held full text, Table 1.1 + prose (lines 569, 619–623)** — counts
   only `(DF1a)` and `(DF2a)` among the 13 degenerate graphics as proved finitely
   cyclic; "the cyclicity of the rest 11 degenerate graphics are still open".
3. **A 2025 deep-research sweep of the DRR literature** — found no post-2015
   full closure of `(DI2a)` or `(DH5)`; only DF1a (DR 2009) and DF2a (Huzak
   2018) are closed among the degenerate graphics.

**Corrected ledger — the 13 degenerate graphics, named from DR 2009's own
enumeration (lines 114, 179, 250):**
- finite-plane line: `DF1a` ✓closed (DR 2009), `DF1b` open, `DF2a` ✓closed
  (Huzak 2018), `DF2b` open, `DH1` open, `DH2` open
- line at infinity: `DI1a` open, `DI1b` open, `DI2a` **open (ADL partial)**,
  `DI2b` open (RR 2015 closed only its *boundary* limit periodic sets), `DH3`
  open, `DH4` open
- two lines: `DH5` open — the hardest per DR 2009 (no analytic 5-parameter
  normal form; a natural one needs 7 parameters)

**Consequence for the run:** `(DI2a)` is a legitimate attack target, one of the
11 open degenerate graphics, with a partial-result starting point (ADL's
infinity-strip / strip-of-hyperbolas analysis via GSPT + Bautin ideal + Darboux
integrability). `(DH5)` is the hardest open degenerate row by DR 2009's own
account.
