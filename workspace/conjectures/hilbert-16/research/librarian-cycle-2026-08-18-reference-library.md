## Findings grounded in held material
- H(2) remains open; the DRR reduction is to 121 graphics, but no consolidated current graphic-by-graphic ledger is available.
- RSZ 2015 reports 88 closed. Roussarie–Rousseau 2015 fully closes I^1_14 and only boundary limit-periodic sets for I^1_6b, H^3_13, DI_2b; H^3_14 had no partial result in that source.
- Lu 2026 claims local uniform finite cyclicity for H^3_14 in a fixed collar and full five-parameter unfolding. The bound is existential and the preprint unrefereed; local algebraic identities are checked in this workspace, but the analytic proof remainder is not kernel-verified.
- Lower bounds repeatedly sourced: H(2) >= 4, H(3) >= 13, H(4) >= 28, and growth at least order n^2 log n. The quadratic formula H(n)=2(n−1)(4(n−1)−2) is refuted by Buzzi–Novaes against that lower-growth result.
- Elementary polycycles have explicit finite-cyclicity bounds (Kaloshin/Kaleda–Shchurov); nilpotent and degenerate graphics remain the main obstruction.
- BNY gives an explicit double-exponential Abelian-integral zero bound; Binyamini–Dor improves dependence to linear in deg omega with explicit deg-H dependence. These solve restricted/tangential problems only.
- Dulac individual-finiteness proofs are contested by Yeung and recent survey discussion; report the status as disputed, not unconditionally established.
