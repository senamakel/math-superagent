# Reference digest: usable knowledge (final)

Memory indexing was unavailable, so this file is the durable local record. Sources were checked against `problem.md` and `GOAL.md`; no claim of H(n)<∞ or H(2)=4 is justified.

## Load-bearing source findings

### DRR/Roussarie framework
DRR 1994 and its held companions formulate the quadratic target through finite cyclicity of a conventional 121-graphic catalogue; Shan reports 125, an unresolved counting convention. A failure of H(2)<∞ would manifest as an infinite-cyclicity limit-periodic graphic in the compactified family, with nilpotent/degenerate graphics the critical obstruction. RSZ 2015 closes named nilpotent saddle graphics I¹₁₂ and I¹₁₃. Roussarie–Rousseau 2015 closes I¹₁₄, but only boundary limit-periodic sets for I¹₆b, H³₁₃ and DI₂b. The full I¹₆b problem involves four coupled second-type Dulac maps and remains open.

### Elementary and saddle-node classes
Ilyashenko–Yakovenko/Kaloshin prove finite cyclicity for elementary polycycles in generic finite-parameter families; Kaloshin gives exponential-scale bounds and Kaleda–Shchurov gives a bound polynomial in parameter count when the number of elementary vertices is fixed. Nonzero eigenvalues/elementarity and genericity are essential; these theorems do not cover nilpotent/degenerate graphics. Dumortier–Ilyashenko–Rousseau 2002 supplies saddle-node normal forms and finite-cyclicity results for specified lips/ensemble graphics; its analytic normal-form/non-flatness input identifies where a smooth-only argument fails.

### Abelian-integral rung
Binyamini–Novikov–Yakovenko 2010 gives a constructive double-exponential zero bound for Abelian integrals over nonsingular compact ovals in small nonconservative Hamiltonian perturbations. Binyamini–Dor gives an explicit bound linear in deg(ω), with exp-plus dependence on deg(H). Novikov–Yakovenko, Gavrilov, and Grau–Manosas–Villadelprat provide Picard–Fuchs, Petrov-module, and Chebyshev tools. These apply to first-order/tangential Hamiltonian displacement, not arbitrary nonlinear Dulac-map compositions; GMV’s ECT hypotheses are not established for the full I¹₆b four-map displacement.

### Pointwise finiteness and analytic caveat
Écalle/Ilyashenko sources attribute individual analytic-field finiteness, while Yeung 2024–25 challenges completeness of Ilyashenko’s ordering-of-asymptotics step for semi-hyperbolic polycycles. This is a proof-status contradiction, not a disproof; Ecalle’s route and the hyperbolic case are not thereby refuted. Palma–Márquez–Yeung’s maximum-modulus dichotomy is only abstract-level evidence for a restricted “superreal” class, not a full repair.

### Lower bounds and calibrations
The library supports H(2)≥4, M(2)=3 (Bautin), H(3)≥13, H(4)≥28, M(3)≥11, and H(n) of at least n² log n scale. Slow–fast Liénard constructions show sharp-looking counts can fail: the Lins–de Melo–Pugh conjecture is true through degree 4, false for n≥6, and n=5 was open in the held 2017 survey. These are constraints on any upper-bound claim, not an upper-bound proof.

### Recent proposals
Lu 2026 claims local uniform finite cyclicity for H¹⁴₃ in a five-parameter unfolding. The workspace independently checked only finite algebraic/Bautin identities; root uniqueness, domain completeness, and the final uniform zero theorem remain unchecked, and the preprint is unrefereed. Pedregal claims a quartic H(n) bound and H(2)=4, but is unrefereed and fails the smooth/analyticity test prima facie; Buzzi–Novaes use the n²log n lower growth to refute a different closed-form proposal. Neither is a positive solution.

## Sources that do not directly help
Landing pages, bibliographic records, citation-only files, and mismatched captures establish provenance only. Panazzolo–Rousseau classifies limit-periodic sets but does not prove cyclicity. Abelian/Picard–Fuchs sources solve only the tangential problem. Pedregal/Buzzi–Novaes are critique evidence; Yeung is a proof-completeness challenge; Lu’s algebraic core does not establish its analytic theorem. Huzak’s held record supports only DF₂a (b=0), not I¹₆b. GMV cannot be transferred without a new exact Abelian representation and uniform ECT hypotheses. The stored `kaleda-shchurov-elementary-polycycles-2011-primary` capture is contaminated by an unrelated operad-spectrum paper; use the corrected elementary-polycycle source/summary instead.

## Contradictions flagged
1. RR 2015 did not fully close all four named center graphics: only I¹₁₄ is full; three have boundary-only results.
2. DRR/RSZ/RR use 121 graphics, Shan reports 125.
3. Older sources treat individual finiteness as settled; Yeung contests proof completeness in the semi-hyperbolic case.
4. H(2)=4 and H(n)<∞ remain unsupported.
5. The local source inventory has at least one contaminated/mislabeled capture; exact theorem claims must use the corrected source, not filename alone.

## Consequence for the investigation
The strongest precise usable target is not the whole conjecture but a full finite-cyclicity theorem for one remaining degenerate graphic, especially the four-Dulac I¹₆b displacement. Any successful route must state the normal forms and transition maps, retain uniform parameter-dependent remainders, and use analyticity/quasianalyticity or algebraicity at the zero-count step. Finite Taylor data, topology alone, pointwise finiteness, or a Hamiltonian Abelian-integral theorem cannot supply that missing uniform conclusion.
