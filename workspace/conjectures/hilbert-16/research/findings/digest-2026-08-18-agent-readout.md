# Research readout against H16.2 — 2026-08-18

## Sources that add usable evidence

- **Marín, Fake saddles and their transition maps (EJQTDE 2026, DOI 10.14232/ejqtde.2026.1.5).** Generic zero-linear-part fake saddles with nonzero quadratic jet and d=4(1-c)-(a-b)^2>0 admit a parameter-uniform first transition-map term e^{gamma_±}y plus a flat remainder; a worked center family has zero local cyclicity. Relevant to degenerate quadratic graphics and flat-class division, but no H^3_14 second-type endpoint map or H16.2 theorem. It corrects a necessity claim from Coll–Gasull–Prohens 2025.

- **Gasull–Giacomini 2023 (DOI 10.1007/s12346-023-00746-7).** Restricted invariant-algebraic-curve results: at most one hyperbolic cycle for quadratic systems with an invariant parabola; at most one in eight listed quadratic families with one algebraic cycle; effective bounds/sign certificates for two generalized invariant curves. Useful for restricted Dulac/invariant-curve oracles only, not a uniform quadratic bound.

- **Yang, arXiv:2512.19046 (2025, unrefereed).** Claims sharp annulus cyclicity n−1 for degree-n perturbations of a named cubic isochronous Hamiltonian family, using Abelian-integral generators/independence. Validation exemplar, not global evidence.

- **An–Dai–Hu 2025 and Sun–Dai 2025.** Narrow hyperelliptic Abelian-integral families: the digest reports Chebyshev/one-zero classes and a quintic family with at least two zeros while an upper bound two remains open. Exact hypotheses were not extracted, so do not promote beyond lead/asserted-by-source.

- **Villanueva–Tucker arXiv:2602.22558v2 (2026, unrefereed).** Claims finite Bautin-ideal enclosure for homogeneous-degree families from initial Lyapunov data plus an odd-degree obstruction. Aligns with local Bautin computations but is unverified and restricted.

## Confirmed negative/partial results

Roussarie–Rousseau 2015 proves only boundary limit-periodic-set cyclicity for I^1_6b, H^3_13, DI_2b; full graphics remain open. Rousseau–Shan–Zhu supplies first-type Dulac maps and explicitly says its proof uses only first-type maps, not the missing H^3_14/I^1_6b second-type endpoint germ. Exact toy runs refute closure of ECT under summing passage contributions and show rank loss at parameter boundaries; this is not a dynamical counterexample. Slow-divergence/ECT tools transfer only after a uniform four-map analytic reduction, noncancellation, and endpoint hypotheses.

Lu's verified Bautin identities do not imply a uniform zero bound: common analytic domains, exhaustive itineraries, Hadamard division, nonidentity, and uniform zero theorems remain independent hypotheses. Exact quadratic-focus ideal computations verify L8 not in <L4,L6> and L10/L12/L14 in <L4,L6,L8> through degree 14; d=16 is the first falsifier of all-degree generation. The radial exact oracle is validated only for its restricted class.
