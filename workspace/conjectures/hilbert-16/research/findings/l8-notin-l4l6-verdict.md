## Is it already known? Yes — completely, since Bautin 1952

`L8 ∉ ⟨L4,L6⟩` is exactly the "three generators genuinely needed / three
independent focus quantities" content of Bautin's own theorem — the same 1952
theorem (Mat. Sb. 30(72):181–196; AMS Transl. 100, 1954) that yields M(2)=3.
A weak focus of third order is precisely L1=L2=0, L3≠0; if L3 were in
⟨L1,L2⟩ the third-order classification would be vacuous. Modern restatements,
all located by search:

- Schlomiuk 1993, Trans. AMS 338:695–706, doi:10.2307/2154430 — the Bautin
  ideal of a quadratic system is generated by the first three focus quantities
  V1,V2,V3 (each defined modulo its predecessors).
- Jarrah–Laubenbacher–Romanovski, "The Sibirsky component of the center
  variety …", J. Symb. Comput., doi:10.1016/S0747717103000166 — verbatim: "for
  quadratic systems, B is generated by the first three focus quantities".
- Mahdi–Pessoa–Hauenstein 2017, J. Symb. Comput., doi:10.1016/j.jsc.2016.11.019
  — "the Bautin ideal B is generated by the first three focus quantities
  (Bautin, 1952)".
- Llibre–Schlomiuk 2004, Canad. J. Math. 56(2):310–343 — weak focus of third
  order, QW3 family.
- Romanovski–Shafer 2009, *The Center and Cyclicity Problems: A Computational
  Algebra Approach*, Birkhäuser — the standard textbook treatment.

The run's own arithmetic corroborates the literature: the Gröbner route
(code/out/membership.captured.txt, lex over ℚ) independently gives L8∉⟨L4,L6⟩
(16-monomial remainder), L6∉⟨L4⟩, and (Bautin-trick closure) L10,L12∈⟨L4,L6,L8⟩.

## Is the method standard?

Yes. Computing focal values by the formal-Lyapunov-function recurrence, then
deciding ideal membership by Gröbner reduction over ℚ, is Bautin's own method
and the textbook standard (Romanovski–Shafer 2009; Ferčec–Mahdi 2013,
doi:10.1016/j.matcom.2013.02.003). The only non-standard element is the
certificate *form* — replacing the Gröbner remainder with a single common-zero
evaluation (three rational evaluations a Lean kernel can close by `decide`).
That is a legitimate, stronger presentation (it puts L8 outside the *radical*
of ⟨L4,L6⟩) and a genuinely useful machine-certificate device, but it is a
proof-presentation choice, not new mathematics.
