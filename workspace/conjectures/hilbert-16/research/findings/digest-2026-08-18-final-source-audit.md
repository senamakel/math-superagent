# Final source audit against H16.2 (2026-08-18)

## Sources and implications

- **Dumortier–Guzmán–Roussarie 2002** proves finite cyclicity for seven elementary quadratic DRR graphics: H^3_4, H^3_5, H^3_6, I^2_14a, I^2_15a, I^2_15b, I^2_27. The record/abstract does not establish every numerical bound (the digest reports mostly 2, with an exceptional ratio-3 case). These rows are not the remaining nilpotent/degenerate obstruction and do not complete the 121-row inventory. Source: http://hdl.handle.net/1942/5292.

- **Bautin 1952** establishes local quadratic focus/center cyclicity M(2)=3 with a realizing example. This is local, distinct from global H(2), currently only H(2)>=4 with no accepted upper bound. It supports the exact focus-chart/Lyapunov oracle and Lean conditional theorem. Source: https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=sm&paperid=5421&what=fullt&option_lang=eng.

- **Panazzolo–Rousseau 2017** classify compactified polynomial limit-periodic sets as connected semialgebraic sphere subsets of dimension 0 or 1, with a converse realization theorem under stated hypotheses; candidates include singular points, periodic orbits, polycycles, and degenerate cycles. Structural support for DRR, no cyclicity bound. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.04965.

- **Marín 2026** gives, for generic fake saddles with nonzero quadratic jet and d=4(1-c)-(a-b)^2>0, a parameter-uniform first transition-map multiplier plus a flat remainder, and a worked center family with zero local cyclicity. This is a template for flat-class division, not the missing H^3_14 second-type endpoint map or a global result. Source: https://ddd.uab.cat/pub/artpub/2026/327411/p11901.pdf.

- **Gasull–Giacomini 2023** proves effective restricted cycle/nonexistence bounds under explicitly listed invariant algebraic/generalized curves, including at-most-one results in several quadratic families. It is useful for a restricted Dulac/invariant-curve oracle only, not for all quadratic fields. Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12346-023-00746-7.

- **Villanueva–Tucker 2026** claims (unrefereed) a finite Bautin-ideal enclosure for homogeneous degree-n families from first nontrivial Lyapunov data plus an odd-degree obstruction, with Darboux-center interpretation. Relevant to local ideal-membership experiments, but source-asserted and not H16.2 evidence. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22558.

- **Lu 2026 ancillary specification** confirms the workspace's boundary: recurrence/Bautin identities are only finite algebra; common domains, exhaustive itineraries, nonidentity, Hadamard division, and analytic zero theorems remain independently unverified. It therefore does not upgrade Lu's local-uniform-finiteness claim. Source: https://arxiv.org/src/2607.13785v2/anc/h14_3_reproducibility/specifications/bautin.md.
