# Fake-saddle / DRR degree-2 programme caveat verified verbatim in held source

## The question

The `fake-saddle-transition-maps` thread asks whether the uniform fake-saddle
transition-map expansion (Marín 2026, held) could certify finite cyclicity of an
open degenerate DRR graphic. The thread's own note carried a warning: DMRT 2015
"state the fake saddle has NO contribution to the DRR degree-2 programme" —
which, if correct, would temper any claim that fake-saddle cyclicity closes a
DRR row. The warning needed a primary-source anchor.

## The source and the exact statement

Held full text:
`research/sources/demaesschalck-rebollo-torregrosa-fake-saddle-2015-postprint.full.md`
(open-access UAB DDD postprint of JDE 258(2):588–620, 2015), lines 70–76:

> "Though this restriction immediately shows a relation between the Hilbert 16th
> problem in degree 2, the study of the singularity at X0 has in fact **no
> contribution in the degree-2 programme outlined by Dumortier, Roussarie and
> Rousseau [13]**: in that programme, homogeneous vector fields could be avoided
> using rescalings. Setting this remark aside, the study of the cyclicity of X0
> at the origin has a relevance on its own."

## What this settles

- The thread's blocking question — *does the fake-saddle machinery contribute to
  the DRR degree-2 programme?* — is answered **no**, directly from DMRT 2015's
  own text. The fake saddle (impassable grain) singularity at the origin does
  not appear in the DRR 121-graphic reduction, because homogeneous vector fields
  are avoided there by rescalings.
- The Marín 2026 uniform expansion remains a genuine instrument for
  degenerate-singularity transition maps, and DMRT 2015's cyclicity ≥ 2 for the
  quadratic fake saddle is a real result — but neither closes a DRR graphic.
- Any claim that fake-saddle cyclicity closes a DRR row is **refuted by this
  source**, not merely tempered. The thread's note was right.
