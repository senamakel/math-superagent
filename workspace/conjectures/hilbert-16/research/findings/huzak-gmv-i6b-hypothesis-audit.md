# Audit: Huzak DF₂a / GMV ECT and I¹₆b extension

## Scope

Objects audited: Huzak's quadratic degenerate graphic DF₂a; GMV's Abelian-integral ECT criterion; and the full I¹₆b four-Dulac displacement in the quadratic DRR blow-up.

## Findings

Huzak's held source supports only the graphic-specific result: DF₂a is finite-cyclic, for the b=0 member of the DF₁a family, by a family blow-up and slow-divergence/slow-fast analysis. The capture does not expose the detailed theorem hypotheses, but it does not mention I¹₆b or a four-Dulac displacement. Consequently no transfer is justified.

GMV's exact hypotheses are first-order Abelian integrals over ovals of a separated Hamiltonian H=Φ+Ψ, or H=A+B y^{2m}, with involutions, balance chains, CT conditions, and the required small-o (or s>m(n−2)) condition. Their dynamical conclusion concerns zeros of the first Melnikov coefficient of a small Hamiltonian perturbation. This is materially narrower than the I¹₆b task.

The precise obstruction is not merely that four maps are present. The full I¹₆b displacement is a parameter-dependent composition/difference of four endpoint Dulac passages and regular transitions. The held RR source gives first-type formulas for the cases it proves and explicitly says second-type maps are not needed there. It does not provide the four I¹₆b endpoint germs in a common finite-rank Abelian/ECT module. Nor is there a parameter-uniform CT/Wronskian certificate across the slow-divergence-zero strata; at such strata the leading rank may collapse and higher terms/remainders must be controlled. Thus GMV cannot bound the exact nonlinear displacement without a new derivation and a zero theorem stable under specialization.

No source found in the current library supports extending either Huzak 2018 or GMV directly to the full I¹₆b four-Dulac displacement.

## Evidence class

- Huzak closure: asserted-by-source; captured publication record gives the result and advertised method, but not the proof-level hypotheses.
- GMV criterion: sourced (full text held); non-coverage of I¹₆b is a checked applicability audit, not a theorem that no future reduction exists.
- Extension claim: unsupported / not established.

## Falsifiers

1. Huzak's complete proof explicitly treats I¹₆b or the same four-Dulac configuration.
2. A source derives the exact I¹₆b displacement in GMV's Abelian-integral form and verifies all balance, CT, small-o, and parameter-uniform hypotheses, including vanishing-stratum specialization.
