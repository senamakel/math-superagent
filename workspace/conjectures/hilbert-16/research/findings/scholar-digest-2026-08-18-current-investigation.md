# Scholar digest against current H16.2 investigation — 2026-08-18

## Sources actually establishing results

- **Dumortier–Guzmán–Roussarie 2002**, *Finite cyclicity of elementary graphics surrounding a focus or center in quadratic systems*, establishes finite cyclicity for seven named elementary quadratic graphics: H^3_4, H^3_5, H^3_6, I^2_14a, I^2_15a, I^2_15b, I^2_27. The abstract does not establish every numerical bound; the digest reports mostly 2, with one ratio-3 exception. This is a partial inventory, not a 121-row ledger. Source: http://hdl.handle.net/1942/5292.

- **Bautin 1952**, primary full text, establishes local quadratic focus/center cyclicity M(2)=3 with a realizing example. It is distinct from global H(2), currently only H(2)≥4 with no accepted upper bound. It supports the exact Lyapunov/Bautin oracle and Lean conditional theorem. Source: https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=sm&paperid=5421&what=fullt&option_lang=eng.

- **Panazzolo–Rousseau 2017** classify compactified polynomial limit-periodic sets as connected semialgebraic subsets of the sphere of dimension 0 or 1, under their hypotheses, with converse realization. This supports the DRR structural frame but gives no cyclicity bound. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.04965.

- **Gasull–Giacomini 2023** gives effective restricted cycle/nonexistence results under explicitly listed invariant algebraic or generalized invariant curves, including at-most-one results in several quadratic families. It supports a restricted Dulac/invariant-curve certificate oracle only. Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12346-023-00746-7.

- **Marín 2026** gives, for generic fake saddles with nonzero quadratic jet and d=4(1-c)-(a-b)^2>0, a parameter-uniform leading transition-map multiplier with flat remainder; a worked center family has zero local cyclicity. This is a template for degenerate transition maps, not the missing H^3_14 second-type endpoint germ. Source: https://ddd.uab.cat/pub/artpub/2026/327411/p11901.pdf.

- **Yang 2025** solves the weak problem for the restricted class of cubic isochronous Hamiltonian systems: maximum n−1 limit cycles, attained, under the stated isochronous-center normal form. It does not imply a global bound for all cubic fields. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19046v1.

- **Muciño-Rebollo 2025** gives explicit Abelian-integral zero bounds for primitive polynomial Hamiltonians with trivial global monodromy. This is a tangential/infinitesimal result for special Hamiltonian families, not full nonlinear Dulac displacement. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.15925.

- **Lu 2026 ancillary Bautin/domain specification** confirms that the finite recurrence/algebraic core is separate from common-domain construction, exhaustive stopped itineraries, center-ideal/Hadamard division, nonidentity, and uniform analytic zero theorems. These remain unverified; Lu's main theorem stays asserted/unrefereed. Source: https://arxiv.org/src/2607.13785v2/anc/h14_3_reproducibility/specifications/bautin.md.

## Sources not helpful for the present goal

Landing/abstract-only records establish provenance but not detailed hypotheses or proofs (notably the Villanueva–Tucker abstract copy and some Gasull–Santana pages). Panazzolo–Rousseau is structural, not a finite-cyclicity theorem. Abelian-integral sources (BNY/Binyamini–Dor/Novikov–Yakovenko/GMV and narrow 2025 families) do not control complete nonlinear Dulac displacement. An–Dai–Hu and Sun–Dai remain narrow leads because exact hypotheses were not extracted. The suggested Gasull–Santana arXiv identifier is contaminated by an unrelated astrophysics capture and must not be cited. Yu–Zeng is visualization/numerical evidence only.

## Contradictions / corrections

No new source overturns that H16.2 remains open. Sources contradict any assertion that RR 2015 fully closed I^1_6b, H^3_13, or DI_2b; that Lu 2026 is established; that H(2)=4 or H(2)<∞; or that Abelian zero bounds alone solve H16.2. Existing unresolved tensions remain 121 versus 125 DRR catalogue conventions and Yeung's challenge to completeness of the Ilyashenko proof.
