# Scholar digest — final source audit (2026-08-18)

## New usable findings

**Dumortier–Guzmán–Roussarie 2002**, *Finite cyclicity of elementary graphics surrounding a focus or center in quadratic systems*, establishes finite cyclicity for seven elementary DRR graphics: H^3_4, H^3_5, H^3_6, I^2_14a, I^2_15a, I^2_15b, I^2_27. The abstract gives the rows but not every numerical bound; the fuller digest records bounds mostly 2, with H^3_6 an exceptional ratio-3 case. Implication: these elementary rows are not candidates for the present open nilpotent/degenerate target, and the source supports only a partial inventory, not a 121-row ledger. Source: http://hdl.handle.net/1942/5292.

**Bautin 1952**, primary full text, establishes the local quadratic focus/center cyclicity M(2)=3 and gives a realizing example. It is explicitly local and must not be conflated with global H(2), for which the library records H(2)≥4 and no accepted upper bound. It supplies the exact canonical focus chart and Lyapunov-value machinery required by the Bautin oracle. Source: https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=sm&paperid=5421&what=fullt&option_lang=eng.

**Villanueva–Tucker 2026**, arXiv:2602.22558v2, claims (unrefereed) a finite enclosure of the Bautin ideal for homogeneous degree-n families by coefficients of the first nontrivial homogeneous Lyapunov term, with an extra odd-degree obstruction; it also identifies the resulting conditions with Darboux centers. This is relevant to the local Bautin/Lean direction and suggests decidable fixed-n ideal-membership tests, but it is not a proof of H16.2 and remains conditional/source-asserted.

**Lu 2026 ancillary Bautin specification** documents exact reproducibility predicates, scripts, and the human-proof remainder. It confirms the checked recurrence is only the finite algebraic component; center-domain, physical-exhaustiveness, nonidentity, analytic zero-theorem, and global-domain claims remain unexecuted/unverified. This supports, rather than upgrades, the standing status “Lu theorem asserted, not established.” Source: https://arxiv.org/src/2607.13785v2/anc/h14_3_reproducibility/specifications/bautin.md.

**Panazzolo–Rousseau** classifies compactified polynomial limit-periodic sets as connected semialgebraic subsets of sphere dimension 0 or 1, with converse realization under stated hypotheses. This supports the structural DRR frame but supplies no cyclicity bound.

The source set continues to contradict any claim that RR 2015 fully closed I^1_6b, H^3_13, or DI_2b; any claim that Lu 2026 is established; any claim H(2)=4 or H(2)<∞; and any claim that Abelian zero bounds alone solve full nonlinear displacement.
