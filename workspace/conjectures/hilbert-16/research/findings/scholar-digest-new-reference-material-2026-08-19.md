# Scholar digest — new reference material against H16.2

**Scope.** Read the new librarian reports, source summaries, relevant full-text-backed notes, current claims, tasks, threads, and CONTEXT. This is a source audit, not a solution of H16.2.

## Source-backed findings

### Mourtada 2009, arXiv:0912.1560v1
[[mourtada-0912.1560v1-algebres-quasi-regulieres-hilbert-pdf.full]] recovers the conclusion lost by the ar5iv conversion. For an analytic finite-parameter unfolding of a real monodromic hyperbolic polycycle (eigenvalue ratio −1 in the stated presentation), there are neighbourhoods and integers N,L such that every nearby field has at most N limit cycles in the neighbourhood and each has multiplicity at most L. The QRH theorem supplies local χ-finiteness/Noetherianity and the finite-jet/fewnomial mechanism. This is a local uniform theorem for a restricted hyperbolic-polycycle family, not H(2)<∞; it is relevant to Lu's all-hyperbolic words. The claim already exists in `research/claims/mourtada-2009-qrh-chi-finite.md`; the PDF closes only the earlier truncation uncertainty.

### DGR 2002
[[dumortier-guzman-rousseau-elementary-graphics-focus-center-2002.full]] proves explicit finite cyclicity for seven elementary quadratic graphics: H³₄,H³₅ ≤2; H³₆ ≤2 or ≤3 according as r(0)≠1 or r(0)=1; I²₂₇ ≤2; I²₁₄a and I²₁₅a finite; I²₁₅b ≤2. Hypotheses are the named quadratic graphic configurations and their stated rational/irrational saddle-ratio and full-unfolding conditions. These are elementary rows, not nilpotent/degenerate open rows, and do not resolve the 121/125 discrepancy. The source confirms the existing DGR finding; it adds no new global result.

### Luca–Dumortier–Caubergh–Roussarie 2009
[[luca-dumortier-caubergh-roussarie-alien-limit-cycles-2009.full]] establishes a cubic Hamiltonian 2-saddle-cycle unfolding with a limit cycle not controlled by a zero of the associated first Abelian integral: the second derivative of the transition map along the saddle connection contributes. Thus Abelian-integral zero counts alone cannot be promoted to a general nonlinear limit-cycle bound in degree ≥3. It does not invalidate the Abelian/tangential weakened problem and does not touch the quadratic DRR frame. This confirms the existing `h16-alien-limit-cycles-abelian-insufficiency` finding.

### Ilyashenko 1990 and 2016
The primary 1990 survey [[ilyashenko-1990-finiteness-theorems-rms-primary.full]] explicitly states pointwise finiteness for polynomial planar fields and the analytic closed-surface analogue, plus the elementary-polycycle neighbourhood theorem and identity/quasianalyticity mechanism. The 2016 digest [[ilyashenko-2016-digest-revised-proof-fulltext.full]] gives the revised architecture: almost-regular germs, sectorial normalisation, correspondence maps to/from degenerate elementary points, and finite compositions for monodromy. These sources support individual analytic/polynomial finiteness and explain where analyticity enters; they do not supply a parameter-uniform H16.2 bound. Yeung's proof-completeness challenge remains a live qualification, not a contradiction of the theorem's statement.

### Marín–Villadelprat 2025 separation function
[[separation-function-generalized-saddle-connections-qtds-2025.full]] generalises the separation/Melnikov derivative to semi-hyperbolic and nilpotent endpoints using a residue-type formula when the classical improper integral diverges. It applies to connections at infinity and a quadratic unbounded period annulus with a semi-hyperbolic hemicycle. This is a methodological tool for H³₁₄/hemicycle analysis, not a DRR closure or uniform H(2) theorem.

### Marín–Villadelprat, hyperbolic hemicycles (published 2025)
The paper is now confirmed as JDE 258 (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.jde.2025.113281. Its stated hemicycle cyclicity results remain as previously recorded; the publication-status upgrade does not change the DRR inventory.

### Andronov et al. 1971
The OCR source is a classical reference for local planar bifurcation notions (separatrix loops, saddle-node loops, hyperbolicity, structural stability), but the available digest is noisy and not a precise modern theorem source for the current H16.2 tasks. It supplies background only; no new load-bearing claim is taken from it.

### Mardešić 1991
Only the abstract/record is securely held. It states an explicit multiplicity bound for generic Abelian integrals and consequences for generic Hamiltonian regular cycles, centres, and homoclinic loops, but the numerical formula is not held because the PDF is bot-blocked. Treat as abstract-level asserted-by-source, not as a computable bound.
