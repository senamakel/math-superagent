# Research digest against H16.2 — 2026-08-18

## Sources that add usable evidence

- **Marín, “Fake saddles and their transition maps” (EJQTDE 2026, DOI 10.14232/ejqtde.2026.1.5)** — Full source held and read through its summary. For generic zero-linear-part fake saddles with nonzero quadratic jet and `d=4(1-c)-(a-b)^2>0`, it gives a parameter-uniform first transition-map term `e^{gamma_±}y` with a flat remainder, and a worked family with zero local cyclicity at a center. This is relevant to the degenerate D-families and to division in a flat class. It does **not** give the missing H^3_14 second-type endpoint map and does not solve H16.2. It also corrects a necessity claim in Coll–Gasull–Prohens 2025.

- **Gasull–Giacomini, “Number of Limit Cycles for Planar Systems with Invariant Algebraic Curves” (2023, DOI 10.1007/s12346-023-00746-7)** — Restricted algebraic-curve results only: at most one hyperbolic cycle for quadratic systems with an invariant parabola; at most one in eight listed quadratic families with one algebraic cycle; effective bounds/sign certificates for systems with two generalized invariant curves. Explicitly not a uniform quadratic result. Useful as a restricted nonexistence-certificate target, not as progress on the global goal.

- **Yang, “The cyclicity of period annulus of cubic isochronous Hamiltonian systems” (arXiv:2512.19046)** — Unrefereed primary preprint. In a named cubic isochronous Hamiltonian family, perturbations of degree `n` have sharp annulus cyclicity `n-1` (multiplicity counted), with an explicit Abelian-integral generator/independence analysis. This is a validation exemplar for the Abelian/Picard–Fuchs approach, not a global theorem.

- **An–Dai–Hu, 2025 (DOI 10.1007/s12346-025-01330-x)** — The digest claims three explicitly defined classes of complete hyperelliptic first-kind integrals are Chebyshev, hence have exact zero bound one. Narrow special-family result; the full theorem hypotheses should be read before promotion.

- **Sun–Dai, 2025 (DOI 10.1007/s11425-023-2334-8)** — For quintic hyperelliptic ovals and `I(h)=∮(alpha+beta x)y dx`, the source records a region with at least two isolated zeros and says the matching upper-bound-two problem remains open. It is a lower-bound/special-family lead, not a resolved sharp bound.

- **Villanueva–Tucker, arXiv:2602.22558v2 (2026)** — Unrefereed. Claims a finite enclosure of the Bautin ideal for homogeneous degree-n families by coefficients of the first nontrivial homogeneous Lyapunov term (plus one odd-degree obstruction), with a Darboux-center interpretation. This aligns with the local Bautin work but is not independently verified and is only local/restricted.
