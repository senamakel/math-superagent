## The certificate

Family (duplicated from `code/bautin/cofactor_certificate.py`, six coefficients):

    u' = -v + a1 u^2 + a2 u v + a3 v^2
    v' =  u + b1 u^2 + b2 u v + b3 v^2

`L_d` = focal value at degree `d`, recovered from the formal Lyapunov function
recurrence (rotation `rot(p)=-v p_u + u p_v`; `L_d` = coefficient of
`(u^2+v^2)^(d/2)` at even `d`). Homogeneous of degrees 2, 4, 6 respectively.

**Witness point:**
`(a1,a2,a3,b1,b2,b3) = (-2,-2,1,-1,-1,1)`

At that point, exact rational arithmetic:

    L4(witness) = 0
    L6(witness) = 0
    L8(witness) = 25/64 ≠ 0

Every element of the ideal `⟨L4, L6⟩` vanishes at every common zero of `L4` and
`L6`. So a single common zero at which `L8` does not vanish refutes
`L8 ∈ ⟨L4,L6⟩` — and proves the stronger statement that `L8` is outside the
RADICAL of `⟨L4,L6⟩`, hence outside the ideal. The certificate is three
rational evaluations, arithmetic the Lean kernel can decide.
