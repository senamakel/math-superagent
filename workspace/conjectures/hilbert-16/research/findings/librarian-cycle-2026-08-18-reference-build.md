## Verified/source-backed findings from readable triage

- Ilyashenko's 2002 history states the individual analytic/polynomial finiteness theorem, while posing uniform compact-family and polycycle finite-cyclicity questions separately; this supports the crucial distinction between pointwise finiteness and H16.2 uniformity. Source held: `research/sources/ilyashenko-centennial-history-hilbert-16.full.md`.
- Rousseau–Shan–Zhu 2015 proves finite cyclicity of the two graphics `(I^1_12)` and `(I^1_13)` through a triple nilpotent saddle inside quadratic systems, and reports 88 graphics closed in its count. Source held: `research/sources/rousseau-shan-zhu-2015-second-type-dulac-full.full.md` / related arXiv record.
- Roussarie–Rousseau 2015 proves full finite cyclicity for `(I^1_14)` and boundary finite cyclicity for `(I^1_6b)`, `(H^3_13)`, and `(DI_2b)`; it does not thereby close those latter full graphics. Source held: `research/sources/rousseau-rousseau-2015-center-graphics-arxiv.full.md`.
- Lu 2026 claims local uniform finite cyclicity for the full five-parameter unfolding of the `H^3_14` semihyperbolic hemicycle, with an existential bound. The library and context correctly mark this unrefereed claim as not established; only its finite algebraic core has local computational verification.
- Marín–Villadelprat 2025 is a relevant neighboring source for hyperbolic hemicycles; the search record says it gives exact small cyclicities in specified quadratic reversible classes, not a global H16.2 result.
- Gasull–Santana 2024 proves a structural-stability realization/monotonicity statement conditional on finiteness of H(n); it does not prove finiteness.
- Gasull–Santana 2025 studies invariant algebraic curves and transfers lower-bound information; it supplies no upper bound for H(n).
