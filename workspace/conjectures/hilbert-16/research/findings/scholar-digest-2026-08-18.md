## Binyamini–Novikov–Yakovenko
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.5056; `research/sources/binyamini-novikov-yakovenko-abelian-integrals-primary.full.md`.
For H=x²y(1−x−y), compact oval family δ(t), 0<t<1/64, and polynomial 1-form ω of degree n, the Abelian integral I(t)=∮δ(t)ω has at most 7n/4+9 isolated zeros. The associated specified nonconservative perturbation has at most (7n+43)/4 cycles converging to smooth cycles. This is restricted tangential/first-order evidence, not a bound for the nonlinear displacement near singular graphics.
