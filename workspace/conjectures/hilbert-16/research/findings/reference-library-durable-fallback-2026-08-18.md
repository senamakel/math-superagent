- Panazzolo–Rousseau/Belotto da Silva–Espín Buendía, arXiv:1702.04965: compactified polynomial limit-periodic sets are topologically equivalent to compact connected semialgebraic subsets of the sphere of dimension 0 or 1 with empty interior; types are singular points, periodic orbits, polycycles, or degenerate cycles. This locates a possible infinite-cyclicity counterexample but gives no bound.
- Roussarie–Rousseau 2015, arXiv:1506.07104: full finite cyclicity is proved for I^1_14. I^1_6b, H^3_13 and DI_2b receive only boundary limit-periodic-set results; additional blow-up limit sets and four second-type Dulac maps prevent treating those as closed. This contradicts any recollection that all four were settled.
- Ilyashenko–Yakovenko/Kaloshin, arXiv:math/0010174: generic finite-parameter families near elementary polycycles have finite cyclicity, with an exponential-scale estimate recorded as 2^(25 k^2). Elementary/nondegenerate hypotheses are essential; nilpotent/degenerate graphics are excluded.
- Binyamini–Novikov–Yakovenko 2010, arXiv:0808.2952, and Binyamini–Dor 2011, arXiv:1108.1846: explicit uniform zero bounds for Abelian integrals over nonsingular Hamiltonian ovals, via Gauss–Manin/Picard–Fuchs/Petrov–Khovanskii. General bounds are doubly exponential in Hamiltonian degree; dependence on deg(omega) is linear in the latter. They do not control arbitrary nonlinear Dulac compositions/full H16.2.
- Bautin 1952: local quadratic focus cyclicity M(2)=3, distinct from global H(2). Rigorous interval work supports H(2)>=4. Do not conflate M(2)=3 with H(2)=4.
- Yeung, arXiv:2402.12506 and 2025 paper: challenges completeness of Ilyashenko's non-hyperbolic/semi-hyperbolic pointwise finiteness proof via failure of closure/order in a logarithmic asymptotic class; does not disprove the theorem, and hyperbolic cases are not challenged. This conflicts only with an unqualified 'proof status settled' statement.
- Lu, arXiv:2607.13785: unrefereed claim of local uniform finite cyclicity for H^3_14 in a five-parameter unfolding. Workspace checks only finite algebraic/Bautin identities; analytic remainder, root uniqueness, domain completeness and uniform zero theorem remain unchecked. Asserted-by-source, not established.
- Pedregal 2021: unrefereed quartic bound/H(2)=4 claim lacks the analytic/quasianalytic return-map step and fails the smooth test. Buzzi–Novaes refute a separate quadratic formula using n^2 log n lower growth. Neither solves H16.2.
- Dumortier–Panazzolo–Roussarie slow–fast/Liénard work refutes sharp low-degree counting heuristics for degree >=6; Torregrosa 2024 gives twelve local cubic cycles. These are lower-bound/stress tests, not upper bounds.
