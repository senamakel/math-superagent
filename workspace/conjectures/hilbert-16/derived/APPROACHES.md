Derived from the files under `research/approaches/`, and rewritten whenever one of them is written. Do not edit this file; the next write re-derives it. One approach is one candidate reformulation: open `research/approaches/<name>.md` to work on it.

An approach is what comes *before* a thread. A thread already has a question and rests on claims; an approach is the idea that might become one, and it carries whether anybody has checked it against the literature yet. `precedent` empty means unchecked, which is not the same as nothing having been found.

Refuted and spent approaches are kept deliberately. Proposing again what this run already closed is the one failure the inventor exists to avoid, and the reason it closed is the only thing that prevents it.

Two stances carry a result rather than a verdict, and each requires its own line. `narrowed` failed in general and holds on the restriction in `survives`, which is live work and not a re-proposal. `reserved` did not fail at all — it waits for the condition in `revive-when`, and when that condition holds it should be proposed.

Thus the proposed finite-dimensionality and codimension-to-zero-count steps are false or unsupported.
- [[nevanlinna-argument-principle-displacement]] (refuted): No theorem found that gives a parameter-uniform Nevanlinna characteristic/growth bound for complexified return germs of the relevant non-hyperbolic polycycles; the proposed first-order polynomial ODE for displacement is not established, and existing complex zero-counting results require hypotheses (constructible orbits, algebraic trajectory/hypersurface intersections, or special polycycles) absent here.
- [[newton-polyhedral-displacement]] (refuted): The literature supports finite blow-up/desingularisation for analytic families, but does not establish the proposed finite-Newton-type uniform displacement theorem; Newton diagrams classify selected singularities and compute local return terms, not a general finite Weierstrass/zero-counting reduction for the open non-hyperbolic DRR graphics.
- [[noetherian-chain-khovanskii-rolle-zero-bound]] (refuted): Noetherian/LN zero theorems require an explicitly given finite algebraic differential system, a domain avoiding its singular locus (or the precise LN cell hypotheses), and bounded complexity. The open DRR return map has not been shown to be a Noetherian or Log-Noetherian function in a uniform parameterized chart; its Dulac transseries has singular, parameter-dependent exponents and iterated logarithmic/exponential structure. Calling it Noetherian is the missing theorem, not a finite Jacobian check.
- [[resurgent-borel-bridge-equation]] (refuted): The literature supports resurgence, transseries, sectorial extensions, and quasi-analyticity for Dulac germs, chiefly in the hyperbolic/parabolic Dulac setting, but no theorem was found establishing a finite, parameter-uniform bridge-equation rank or a zero-multiplicity bound for the open non-hyperbolic DRR graphics. The closest sources explicitly describe resurgence as part of the individual Dulac finiteness proof, not as a uniform H(2) cyclicity theorem. The proposed finite recurrence is therefore an unproved extra implication, not a literature-grounded reformulation. This is evidence-based…
- [[reversibility-quotient-ect]] (refuted): Reversibility simplifies center-case transitions and can make selected maps identities after section choices, but the literature does not establish that the four second-type Dulac maps for the full I^1_6b or DI_2b graphics descend to first-type passages, nor that the quotient displacement is an Abelian-integral ECT family. The required reduction is the central unproved assertion, so the proposed route cannot be grounded as stated.
- [[spectral-determinant-hill-operator]] (refuted): Hill determinants encode linear stability/monodromy of an already-existing periodic orbit, not the nonlinear displacement zero set. The proposed correspondence between zeros of a spectral discriminant in an auxiliary spectral parameter and bifurcating limit cycles is not a theorem in the cited literature. Planar polynomial fields are not generally Lagrangian, and no uniform polynomial-degree growth estimate over all cycles is supplied. Thus the global trace-formula step is not merely unproved; the route lacks the required object-level identification.
- [[tropical-discriminant-bkk-cyclicity]] (refuted): BKK counts isolated torus solutions of a specified square Laurent-polynomial system under Newton-polytope/nondegeneracy hypotheses. It does not bound connected components of an arbitrary semialgebraic set, and it does not turn analytic Dulac displacement maps into polynomial equations. The proposed semialgebraic description of Sigma_N and its defining supports are absent for the open DRR graphics; therefore the claimed reduction is the missing Hilbert-16 theorem, not an automatic BKK step.

## Narrowed, and what survived

Each of these failed in general and still holds on the restriction named. The general form is closed; the restriction is live work, and proposing it is not re-proposing what was refuted.

- [[bautin-scheme-flatness-discriminant]]: The established reformulation is Bautin-ideal / reduced-Bautin-depth theory for analytic Poincare maps near monodromic singularities. Noetherianity gives finite generation, and integral-closure stabilization gives cyclicity bounds when the hypotheses are met. Generic flatness and Rees algebras are legitimate algebraic tools for stratifying finite algebraic families, but they do not by themselves identify the infinite Dulac displacement ideal with a finite jet ideal. This surviving restriction (García's reduced Bautin depth for monodromic singularities) is the core of the adopted synthesis…
- [[chen-iterated-integrals-shuffle-displacement]]: Chen/Fliess/shuffle expansions are established for analytic ODE return maps and Abel equations as a COEFFICIENT ENGINE (exact word-by-word generation of return-map coefficients), even though the proposed finite word-length/finite shuffle-ideal determinacy theorem for the full nonhyperbolic four-Dulac graphic is not established. This surviving coefficient-engine role is absorbed into the adopted synthesis `reduced-bautin-depth-chen-coefficient-engine`, where it feeds García's reduced-Bautin-depth stabilization.
- [[elimination-displacement-taylor-ideal]]: local analytic return-map families with a common regular transversal and a finitely generated Bautin ideal; it does not supply a global bound for non-hyperbolic polycycles. The surviving restriction is carried into the adopted synthesis `bautin-elimination-regular-transversal-sigma-n`, which makes the missing uniform common chart a named, computable first step rather than an afterthought.
- [[iterated-abelian-gauss-manin-rank]]: Hamiltonian/near-Hamiltonian perturbations where the displacement variation is a finite-order Melnikov function represented by iterated Abelian integrals; it does not cover the full nonlinear displacement of the open DRR four-Dulac graphics. On this restriction it yields a finite differential system and zero-counting tools for a fixed variation order.
- [[pfaffian-chain-return-map]]: The elementary/quasielementary restriction, where Kaloshin's E(k) ≤ 2^{25k²} (arXiv:math/0111053) is the grounded theorem. The open-graphic extension of this idea is carried by the adopted synthesis compensator-pfaffian-mourtada-moussu-synthesis, which supplies the missing chain via the Mourtada–Moussu bridge (Dulac map 1-Pfaffian iff analytically normalisable, DOI 10.24033/bsmf.2297).
- [[schwarzian-return-map-cross-ratio]]: one-dimensional return maps with an explicitly proved critical point and global Schwarzian sign (piecewise-smooth, unimodal, or Abel-reduction classes), where Singer/Kozlovski-type theorems apply
