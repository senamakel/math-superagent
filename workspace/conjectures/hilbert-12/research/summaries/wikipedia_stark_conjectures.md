## What it claims

In [number theory][1], the **Stark conjectures**, introduced by [Stark][2] ( 1971, 1975, 1976, 1980) and later expanded by [Tate][3] ( 1984), give [conjectural][4] information about the [coefficient][5] of the leading term in the [Taylor expansion][6] of an [Artin L-function][7] associated with a [Galois extension][8]*K*/*k*of [algebraic number fields][9]. The conjectures generalize the [analytic class number formula][10] expressing the leading coefficient of the [Taylor series][11] for the [Dedekind zeta function][12] of a number field as the product of a [regulator][13] related to [S-units][14] of the field and a [rational number][15].

When *K*/*k*is an [abelian extension][16] and the [order of vanishing][17] of the L-function at *s*= 0 is one, Stark gave a refinement of his conjecture, predicting the existence of certain S-units, called **Stark units**, which generate abelian extensions of number fields.
