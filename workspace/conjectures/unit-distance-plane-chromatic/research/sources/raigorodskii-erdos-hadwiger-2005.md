# The Erdős–Hadwiger problem and chromatic numbers of finite geometric graphs — Raigorodskii 2005 survey

**Source:** A.M. Raigorodskii, "The Erdős–Hadwiger problem and the chromatic
numbers of finite geometric graphs", Sbornik: Mathematics 196:1 (2005)
doi:10.1070/sm2005v196n01abeh000874. Russian original: Матем. сб. 196:1 (2005).
**Full text: not on disk** — download_document fails on doi.org at the network
layer (known environment limit). Read via read_sources (server-side), summary
below from that triage.

## What the survey covers (per read_sources triage)

- Survey of the classical Erdős–Hadwiger problem — chromatic number of the
  unit-distance graph of Euclidean space — studied through its finite
  geometric-graph special case.
- New lower-bound **methods** for chromatic numbers of unit-distance graphs and
  related finite geometric graphs; approaches that generalize and improve
  previously known bounds, applied to broad classes of finite geometric graphs.
- The survey reviews the status of 4-colourability in unit-distance / finite
  geometric settings: techniques currently fail to prove 4-colourability in
  general, while restricted finite configurations force chromatic numbers above
  4.
- **Technique tier, not record constructions**: primarily a methods survey;
  not a report of new record 5-chromatic graphs.
