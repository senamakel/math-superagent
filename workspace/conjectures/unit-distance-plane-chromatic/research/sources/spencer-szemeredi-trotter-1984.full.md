## Established result (from the records of the paper and of works that state it — see summaries/spencer-szemeredi-trotter-1984-unit-distances.md)

- **Theorem:** the maximum number of unit distances among `n` points in the
  Euclidean plane is `O(n^{4/3})`.
- First proof of the `n^{4/3}` upper bound for the planar unit-distance problem.
- Method: an incidence bound for points and unit circles; the same growth rate
  was later re-derived (Clarkson–Edelsbrunner–Guibas–Sharir–Welzl, Székely via
  the crossing lemma, Pach–Tardos), none yielding an improvement. Best-known
  upper bound still `O(n^{4/3})` as of the 2025–2026 literature.
- Erdős's matching-constant lower bound `Ω(n^{1+c/log log n})` predates it
  (Erdős 1946).
