## Established content (from the records of the paper and of works citing it — see summaries/de-bruijn-erdos-1951-colour-problem.md)

1. **De Bruijn–Erdős theorem.** For any graph `G`, `χ(G) = sup { χ(H) : H` finite
   subgraph of `G }`. Equivalently: if every finite subgraph is `k`-colourable,
   so is the whole graph.
2. **Choice content.** The proof is a compactness argument; the countable case
   needs only König's lemma, the general case is equivalent to (and classically
   follows from) the Boolean prime ideal theorem / Tychonoff compactness. Not a
   theorem of ZF alone for uncountable graphs.
3. **Second half.** The paper also treats "a problem in the theory of relations"
   — colouring points of the plane with a family of forbidden mutual distances.
   This is the direct ancestor of the Hadwiger–Nelson question for `R²`.
