# The Sandwich Theorem — Lovász theta (duplicate, superseded)

**Primary source:** Donald E. Knuth, *The Sandwich Theorem*, The Electronic Journal of
Combinatorics, 1994. https://doi.org/10.37236/1193

**Exact statement:** For any finite simple graph G, `omega(G) <= theta(Gbar) <= chi(G)`,
where `theta` is the Lovász theta function (polynomial-time computable SDP quantity) and
`Gbar` the complement — hence `theta(Gbar)` is the vector chromatic number and a
polynomial-time lower bound on `chi(G)`.
