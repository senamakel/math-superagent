# A045945 — Hexagonal matchstick numbers: a(n) = 3*n*(3*n+1).

## What this establishes for this problem

This is the **exact edge count of the triangular / A2 hexagon patch**
`H_r = {(i,j) : |i|,|j|,|i+j| <= r}` of the unit-distance graph, verified by this
run's lattice census (code/lattice_census.py): e(H_r) = 3r(3r+1) for every r in
0..21 computed. 3r(3r+1) is the catalogued hexagonal-matchstick formula. The run
also derives it independently: the degree partition (6 corner vertices of degree
3, 6(r-1) side vertices of degree 4, the interior of degree 6) plus the handshake
gives 2e = 6·3 + 6(r-1)·4 + (3r(r+1)+1−6r)·6 = 6r(3r+1), so the edge count is
proved, not merely fitted. chi(H_r)=3 for r>=1 by complete test.

Closed form exact here: yes, and derived two independent ways (direct integer
count and handshake from degree partition). No falsifier among computed terms.

## Formula

- a(n) = a(n-1) + 6*(3*n-1) (with a(0)=0). - _Vincenzo Librandi_, Nov 18 2010
- G.f.: 6*x*(2+x)/(1-x)^3. - _Colin Barker_, Feb 12 2012
- a(n) = 6*A005449(n). - _R. J. Mathar_, Feb 13 2016
- a(n) = A059986(n) - A059986(n-1). - _Peter M. Chema_, Feb 26 2017
- a(n) = 6*(A000217(n) + A000290(n)). - _Peter M. Chema_, Mar 26 2017
- From _Amiram Eldar_, Jan 14 2021: (Start)
- Sum_{n>=1} 1/a(n) = 1 - Pi/(6*sqrt(3)) - log(3)/2.
- Sum_{n>=1} (-1)^(n+1)/a(n) = -1 + Pi/(3*sqrt(3)) + 2*log(2)/3. (End)
- From _Elmo R. Oliveira_, Dec 12 2024: (Start)
- E.g.f.: 3*exp(x)*x*(4 + 3*x).
- a(n) = 3*a(n-1) - 3*a(n-2) + a(n-3) for n >= 3. (End)

## Comments

- This may also be construed as the number of line segments illustrating the isometric projection of a cube of side length n. Moreover, a(n) equals the number of rods making a cube of side length n+1 minus the number of rods making a cube of side length n. See the illustration in the links and formula below.
