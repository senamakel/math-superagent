# Approach: locally-cyclic classification of the 5-critical kernel

```approach
idea: Prove the size bound by *classifying* the kernel class — the finite family every minimal 5-critical unit-distance graph must lie in (K4-free, K2,3-free, every neighbourhood a disjoint union of paths and one 6-cycle, min degree >= 4, 2-connected) — via the theory of locally-cyclic graphs and quotients of the triangular (A2) lattice, replacing the C_N kernel census that re-hit the 600s wall at N=12.
mechanism: The certified sharp-nbhd lemma gives N(v) max degree 2, i.e. a disjoint union of paths and at most one 6-cycle, with consecutive neighbours 60 degrees apart. A degree-4 or degree-5 vertex has N(v) = a path with a 120-or-180-degree gap; a degree-6 vertex has N(v) = C6. The structural reduction: a vertex whose neighbourhood is a *proper path* is reducible in a 5-critical graph (Kempe-chain / generalized-Brooks argument — four colours suffice to recolour its <= 5 path-neighbours and free the vertex), so a minimal counterexample has every vertex of degree 6 with N(v) = C6 — i.e. is *locally cyclic*. Locally cyclic graphs are classified: the connected locally cyclic graphs are quotients of the infinite triangular (A2) lattice, together with finitely many sporadic examples (the Perkel graph, 57 vertices, is the smallest non-quotient). A K4-free locally cyclic graph is thus a finite quotient of the A2 lattice, and its chromatic number is controlled by the quotient's winding/balanced-ness: balanced quotients inherit the lattice's 3-colouring, and the unbalanced (4-chromatic) quotients form an explicitly characterizable set. If every such quotient is 4-colourable, the size bound holds for *every* N at once, with no enumeration — and the 4-critical quotients form a finite explicit list, which is exactly the set of candidate bases for a 5-chromatic construction.
status: proposed
first-step: (1) State and machine-check the reducibility lemma: for each of the finitely many degree-4/5 path-neighbourhood types on the unit circle, verify by exact SAT that a 5-critical UDG cannot contain it (or find the counterexample that kills the reduction). (2) Compute chi exactly for small triangular-lattice quotients (toroidal grids with a twist parameter, built in the Eisenstein field Z[omega]) via the calibrated SAT oracle, and match the 4-chromatic ones against the balanced/unbalanced classification — confirming the classification rather than re-enumerating C_N.
speculative: the reducibility lemma is a conjecture to be proved, not established; the locally-cyclic quotient classification is standard (named: locally cyclic graphs, Perkel graph, triangular-lattice quotients, regular maps on the torus), but its application to the 4-colourability of the UDG kernel is unrecorded and is the contribution.
```
