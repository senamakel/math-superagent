## Settled

Bets this run has finished with, and what became of each.

- `diophantine-cycle-collision` — Sub-claim (b) falls by the Diophantine collision: a non-trivial m-cycle forces both (upper, Hercher Thm 16 + S <= m/x_min) (m+L)/m < delta + (3 log 2 / m) * S with S <= m/x_min and (lower, Zudilin mu=8.616) |delta - (m+L)/m| > c_0/m^mu, so x_min > (3 log 2 / c_0) * m^mu ~ C * m^8.616. The open gap G-min-element-lower is the missing lower arm that collides for all m; the finite exclusions (Hercher m<=91, Eliahou period) are its checked initial segment. The table m=92..200 makes the gap between Hercher's min-element bounds and the Diophantine threshold visible as a function of m.
  - because: The bridge identity 2^(m+L) = prod(3 + 1/n) is proved in Hercher 2022 (Thm 16); the irrationality measure mu<8.616 of log 3/log 2 is proved in Zudilin 2004 (Thm 3); every known cycle exclusion (Steiner, Simons-de Weger, Eliahou, Hercher) is a special case of this two-sided bound. The map mixes 2-adic division with 3-multiplication-plus-one, and log 3/log 2 is the only proved handle connecting the two primes; a cycle's shape is exactly a near-integer relation 2^(m+L) ~ 3^m.
  - refuted-by: A non-trivial m-cycle with x_min <= (3 log 2 / c_0) * m^mu for some m would break the collision (though the Diophantine arm then gives a huge explicit x_min bound instead — see the table). Also: if the Hercher min-element bounds for m <= 200 are already far below the Diophantine threshold at c_0 = 1 and the deficit grows with m, the collision approach as stated is the wrong shape. Either outcome is a number on disk, not a mood.
  - reason: Refuted by the Lean-arithmetic attack in agent-run-22 and the exact table in agent-run-21: Hercher's Corollary 24 table bounds K (number of odd members), not x_min; and combining bridge upper bound, Zudilin lower bound, and S <= m/x_min gives S > c0*m^(mu-1)/(3 log 2), hence x_min < (3 log 2/c0)*m^(2-mu), the opposite direction. The proposed collision lower arm cannot be inferred and is algebraically false over the cited hypotheses. The remaining viable cycle work must rederive the bridge/sign or use a genuine independent x_min bound.
  - refs: hercher-m92, zudilin-mu-8616, hercher-K-1p375e11, lagarias-W2
