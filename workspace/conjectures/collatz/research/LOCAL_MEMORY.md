# Library status and durable findings (memory service unavailable)

The Cognee memory service was unavailable during this cycle; the following findings are therefore stored locally and remain source-traceable.

- Lagarias overview: `research/sources/ams-lagarias-overview.full.md`, URL https://www.ams.org/bookstore/pspdf/mbk-78-prev.pdf. Classical C and accelerated T satisfy T=C on even inputs and T=C² on odd inputs; acceleration preserves the conjecture. The overview separates proofs, heuristics, and finite computation.
- Barina 2025: `research/sources/barina-2025-improved-verification-2p71.pdf.full.md`, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-025-07337-0. Reports all n<2^71 reach the trivial cycle via distributed CPU/GPU and 3^k-sieve computation; this is a finite asserted-by-source verification, not a proof.
- Tao: `research/sources/tao-almost-all-orbits.full.md`, URL https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/forum-of-mathematics-pi/article/almost-all-orbits-of-the-collatz-map-attain-almost-bounded-values/1008CC2DF91AF87F66D190C5E01C907F. For every f(N)→∞, Col_min(N)≤f(N) for logarithmic-density-one N; exceptional individual orbits are not ruled out.
- Hercher 2022: `research/sources/hercher-no-collatz-m-cycles.full.md`, URL https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.00406. No nontrivial accelerated m-cycle has m≤91 local minima; verification through 3·2^69 implies >1.375·10^11 odd members in any nontrivial cycle.
- Monks 2006: `research/sources/monks-2006-sufficiency-arithmetic-progressions.full.md`, URL https://monks.scranton.edu/files/pubs/SufficiencyRev4.pdf. Every nonconstant arithmetic progression is sufficient; the reduction applies also to divergent-orbit and nontrivial-cycle sub-conjectures.
- Angeltveit 2026: `research/sources/angeltveit-2026-improved-algorithm.full.md`, URL https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10466. Proposes recursive bitvector and sieve methods, but is not itself a completed frontier report beyond 2^71.
