# Laurore 2025 — claimed no-cycle argument (unverified)

[[laurore-2025-stopping-time-nontrivial-cycles.full]]

Source: https://doi.org/10.4236/apm.2025.156018

Laurore defines ordinary stopping time `σ` and coefficient stopping time `ω` for the accelerated map. The paper proves computationally a large finite range, then claims a strong-induction extension `σ(s)=ω(s)` for every positive `s`; Theorem 4.8 would then exclude every nontrivial cycle. The load-bearing residue-class induction has not been independently certified and the source is not corroborated by the stronger established cycle literature. Therefore this is an asserted claim, not a durable result for this investigation. It does not address divergent orbits even if correct.
