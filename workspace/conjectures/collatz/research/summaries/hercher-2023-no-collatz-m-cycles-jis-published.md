# Hercher — no small m-cycles

[[hercher-2023-no-collatz-m-cycles-jis-published.full]]

Source: https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL26/Hercher/hercher5.pdf

Hercher defines `m` as the number of local minima in a nontrivial accelerated Collatz cycle and proves no such cycle exists for `m≤91`. The proof combines inequalities, continued fractions, and the then-current verification range. A further conditional calculation shows that verifying starts through `1536*2^60 = 3*2^69` would force at least `1.375*10^11` odd members. This excludes a restricted cycle class only; it does not exclude all cycles or divergent trajectories.
