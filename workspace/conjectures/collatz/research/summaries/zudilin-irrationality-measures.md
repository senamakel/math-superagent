# Zudilin — irrationality measure for logarithms

[[zudilin-irrationality-measures.full]]

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0404523

Zudilin states (Theorem 3, following Rhin) that every nonzero `γ∈Q log 2 + Q log 3` has irrationality exponent `<8.616`. This applies to `log(3)/log(2)` because it is a ratio of the two logarithms, though translating the theorem into a precise approximation inequality requires the usual denominator normalization and an effective constant. This supplies a quantitative Diophantine input for cycle-length arguments, but by itself does not exclude cycles: parity-vector inequalities and all hypotheses of the chosen cycle theorem must also be established.
