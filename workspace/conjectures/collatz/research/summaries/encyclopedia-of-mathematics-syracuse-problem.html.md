# Encyclopedia of Mathematics — Syracuse problem

Source URL: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Syracuse_problem

The entry gives the standard positive-integer Collatz/Syracuse definition and the conjecture that every orbit reaches 1, with examples 1→4→2→1, 5→16→8→4→2→1, and 7’s orbit. It explicitly distinguishes the positive problem from variants on negative integers and records that the problem is unsolved. This is an encyclopedic statement source, not evidence for a new theorem.
