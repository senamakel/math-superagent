# Barina 2025 — finite verification below 2^71

[[barina-2025-improved-verification-2p71.pdf.full]]

Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-025-07337-0

Barina reports a distributed CPU/GPU computation verifying all starts `n<2^71` reach the trivial cycle. The method uses accelerated dynamics, `3^k` sieves, `2^40` work units, and large-scale parallel computation. The paper also reports a conditional cycle-period consequence `355,504,839,929`, relying on the cited Eliahou-type formula. This is finite numerical evidence, not a universal proof. It is the external input used by Ansari's recursive-sufficiency extrapolation.
