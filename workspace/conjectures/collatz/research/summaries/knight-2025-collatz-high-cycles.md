# Knight — high-cycle exclusion

[[knight-2025-collatz-high-cycles.full]]

Source: https://hal.science/hal-04261183/document

Knight uses the Böhm–Sontacchi parity-vector formula and Christoffel words. Theorem 5.4 excludes integer high cycles: integrality of the high cycle and its reversed-word companion would force `2^(k-1)/(2^k-3^x)` to be an integer, impossible by odd-divisor divisibility. The paper also gives an elementary exclusion of circuit cycles, using a lower bound on `2^k-3^x` plus prior finite bounds. These are restricted parity-vector classes only; non-extremal cycles and divergent orbits remain untouched. The claims are relevant structural targets for formalisation, but do not resolve Collatz.
