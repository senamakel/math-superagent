## Literature assessment

The established reformulation is the **Böhm–Sontacchi cycle equation** (also called the rational-cycle/parity-vector formula), and related work calls the modular approach **p-adic divisibility obstructions**. Brox explicitly expresses cycle existence through divisibility equations, while Simons–de Weger and Eliahou use continued fractions, computational Diophantine approximation, and verification bounds to exclude ranges of cycle shapes.

The necessary condition is sound: a cycle with total accelerated period L and m odd steps has a rational starting value with denominator (or equivalent denominator factor) 2^L−3^m, and integrality requires the corresponding numerator to be divisible by that denominator. However, LTE must be applied with its hypotheses checked; the familiar formula for v_p(a^n−b^n) concerns matched powers or special congruence conditions and cannot be substituted for arbitrary 2^L−3^m. Also, a finite SAT/CP-SAT UNSAT certificate proves only the finite encoded instance unless one proves that the encoding is equivalent to all gap patterns.

No source found proves the proposed m=92..100 finite classification. Aliabdali’s cited Conjecture 17.13 is explicitly a conjecture, so it supports the target’s relevance but not the proposed conclusion. The line is therefore grounded as a legitimate restricted cycle-exclusion program, with its load-bearing completeness step open.
