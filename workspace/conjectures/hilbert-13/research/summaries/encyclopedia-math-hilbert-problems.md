## What it claims

At the 1900 International Congress of Mathematicians in Paris, D. Hilbert presented a list of open problems. The published version [a18] contains 23 problems, though at the meeting Hilbert discussed but ten of them (problems 1, 2, 6, 7, 8, 13, 16, 19, 21, 22). For a translation, see [a19]. These 23 problems, together with short, mainly bibliographical comments, are briefly listed below, using the short title descriptions from [a19].

Three general references are [a1] (all 23 problems), [a9] (all problems except 1, 3, 16), [a24] (all problems except 4, 9, 14; with special emphasis on developments from 1975–1992).
