# A001653 — odd-indexed Pell numbers (catalogue)

<!-- source: https://oeis.org/A001653 -->

**Terms:** 1, 5, 29, 169, 985, 5741, 33461, 195025, 1136689, 6625109, …

**What A001653 is.** a(n) = A000129(2n−1): the **odd-indexed Pell numbers** P_{2n−1}
(Gessel's identification, in the OEIS entry). Recurrence a(n) = 6a(n−1) − a(n−2),
a(0)=1, a(1)=5; growth a(n) ~ (1/4)√2·(√2+1)^{2n+1} with ratio → 3+2√2 = (1+√2)².
Equivalently the hypotenuses Z of Pythagorean triples (X, X+1, Z), i.e. solutions of
2Z² = odd-square + 1; the Pell-pair identity used in `phi-suprema-are-pell-pairs`.

**Hypotheses / holds here.** Catalogue facts (status: catalogued, no derivation
reproduced here). They hold for this run's φ-record structure:
`f(P_k, P_{k−1}) = 1 − 1/P_{2k−1}²` with the record denominators' roots
P_{2k−1} = 5, 29, 169, 985, 5741, … being **exactly A001653(2..6)** — the run's own
computations (`code/out/pell_records_established.md`, checked to k=59) reproduce
these values and the 3+2√2 asymptotic from first principles without reading the
catalogue, so the catalogue confirms the run's numbers. The recurrence turns the
record-sequence evaluation into an O(1) recurrence rather than an enumeration.

**Does not settle.** Nothing about the additive Φ-triple conjecture; the
record-structure is about single-element size, not sums of two elements.
