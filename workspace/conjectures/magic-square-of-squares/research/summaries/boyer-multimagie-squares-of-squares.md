# Boyer, "Magic squares of squares" (multimagie.com survey) — [[boyer-multimagie-squares-of-squares.full]]

Survey/index page for Boyer's Mathematical Intelligencer article (Vol 27, N2, 2005, 52–64) and its supplement. Mostly **does not help** the 3×3 open problem directly: it is a recreational survey covering 4×4 (Euler, LE2), 5×5–7×7 constructions, multimagic and bimagic squares, cubes, and prime-square squares. It is not the source of the hard facts.

## What it does confirm (secondary)
- Problem D15 of Guy's *Unsolved Problems in Number Theory* (3rd ed. 2004), first raised by LaBar (1984), famous via Gardner's 1996 $100 challenge.
- The LS1 near-miss (7 of 8 sums = 21609, one = 38307) and Bremner's AB1 seven-square magic square `[373²,289²,565²; 360721,425²,23²; 205²,527²,222121]` (centre 425²) — matching this run's witness set.
- Robertson showed equivalence to problems on APs, Pythagorean triangles, congruent numbers and elliptic curves y²=x³−n²x (i.e. the elliptic reduction already captured from Bremner 1999).
- Points to the primary sources: Bremner 1999 & 2001 (Acta Arith.), Buell's magic-hourglass preprint, Sallows 1997, Rabern (Rose-Hulman 2003) — all already in the run's library or named.

**Conclusion:** useful only as a pointer survey and for confirming witness provenance; contains no structural statement about the 3×3 case beyond what the primary papers already establish. Do not cite it as the source of any hard fact; cite Bremner/Buell/Sallows.
