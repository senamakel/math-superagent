# Boyer, "Magic squares of squares" (multimagie.com) — survey page

[[boyer-multimagie-squares-of-squares]]

Index/landing page for Boyer's `The Mathematical Intelligencer` art
icle and its supplement, plus open problems with a €100 + champagne prize (awarded for
solving *easier variants*, not the full MSS).

## What it establishes
- Confirms the problem history (LaBar 1984, Gardner 1996) and that it is Guy's
  *Unsolved Problems in Number Theory* problem **D15** (3rd ed. 2004).
- Repeats LS1 (`S²=21609`, failing diagonal `38307`) and notes Robertson's equivalence to
  problems on arithmetic progressions, Pythagorean right triangles, congruent numbers and
  `y²=x³−n²x` — corroborating the run's recalled Robertson attribution.
- Links to the supplement and to Boyer's 4×4–7×7 squares of squares material.

## Implications / assessment
- **Does not help** beyond confirming history and the LS1/Robertson facts already carried in
