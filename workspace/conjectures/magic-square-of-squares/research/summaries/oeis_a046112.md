## What it actually establishes (replacing the template lookup)

**Closed form.** `x²+y² = r²` has `4·∏_{p≡1 mod 4}(2aᵢ+1)` lattice points
(`h = ∏pᵢ^{aᵢ}`), so `8n−4` points ⇔ `∏(2aᵢ+1) = 2n−1`. Up to offset this
is the SAME sequence as A006339 (as the cross-reference says):

  `A046112(n) = A006339(n) = min h with ∏_{p≡1 mod 4}(2aᵢ+1) = 2n−1.`

**Why this matters here.** Half the lattice points on `x²+y² = e²` (the
non-axis ones, paired by sign) correspond one-to-one to the run's four-AP
differences: each `d ∈ S(e) = {d>0 : e²±d both squares}` arises from exactly
one unordered positive pair `(x,y)`, `d = 2xy`. So `|S(e)| = (∏(2aᵢ+1)−1)/2`
is (lattice points on the circle of radius `e`)/8, rounded down by the axis
term — the same closed form the run's `ap_structure2.py` established and
verified against an independent enumeration for `e ≤ 1500`.

**Relationship to A088959/A088111 (already summarised).** Record lattice-point
radii = run's S3 record-holder `e`'s; record counts = run's S2 record values,
13 terms each, cross-checked in `research/summaries/oeis_a006339.md` and by
`code/oeis_verify.py`.
