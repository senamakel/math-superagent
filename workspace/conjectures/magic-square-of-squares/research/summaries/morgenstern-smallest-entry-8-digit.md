# Morgenstern, "Smallest entry in a 3x3 Magic Square of Squares", June 2007

[[morgenstern-smallest-entry-8-digit]]

26-page paper: a **complete, provable enumeration** of all 3-square APs with a fixed starting
value `L²`, proving **all 9 entries of a 3×3 MSS of distinct squares are ≥ squares of 8-digit
numbers**.

## Established (structural) statements

**Fixed-start AP generation.** Study `L², M_n², H_n²`, `L² = 2M_n²−H_n²`, `0<L<M_n<H_n`.

- **Forward recursion:** `M_{n+g}=3M_n+2H_n`, `H_{n+g}=3H_n+4M_n`; positive coefficients ⇒
  values grow fast (step values >2× per step).
- **5/7 Lemma:** `H_n > 7L ⇒ M_n < (5/7)H_n`.
- **Finite Generator Theorem:** generators are exactly representations with `L < H < 7L`
  (finite); `H=7L` is always a generator; every other representation is a forward-recursion
  image of a generator. Generator count `g = (2a+1)(2b+1)…` over prime powers `p^a q^b …` of L.
- **Non-redundancy + Relative Placement:** one forward step leaves the generator range, so no
  duplicate checks; interleaved sequences stay ordered, so all `H_n` come out increasing.

**Enumeration Termination Theorem.** The MSS search (three equal-start APs with steps
`a,b,a+b`) becomes `H_i² + H_j² = H_k² + L²` (`H_i<H_j<H_k`). Two lemmas (Changing Geometric
Progression, Corresponding Combination Rejection) imply: once combinations over `g`
consecutive `H_k` fail (too small by `< H_k²−L²`, too big by `> H_k²+L²`), **all future
combinations are doomed** — the enumeration for that `L` terminates as impossible. Worked
eliminations: L = 1, 41, 71, 49, 119 (e.g. L=1: each larger step exceeds twice the step
below, so no `(a,b,a+b)` triple; 1 can't be the smallest entry).

**Prime-factor reduction.** A prime `≡3 or 5 mod 8` is inert in `Z[√2]`; from the
factorization `L²=(M√2+H)(M√2−H)` it divides all three AP terms, hence can be divided out.
Only `8k+1, 8k+7` primes need be kept. (Consistent with the properties paper's Thm 6/7.)

## Result (claimed, computed)
- Complete enumeration proved: **all nine entries of any 3×3 MSS of distinct squares are ≥
  squares of 8-digit numbers** (each entry ≥ 10⁷²).
- Enumeration aborted after L up to 7 digits under the old method; new generator/
  pre-generator/composition methods (Part 3) accelerate further (search ideas in Part 4).

## Implications for this run
- Independent of the elliptic/K3 path, this is a real lower bound on all entries (8 digits,
  `≡1 mod 24`). Confirms "MSS numbers are large" with explicit method.
- The `(H_i²+H_j²=H_k²+L²)` shape is exactly the run's four-AP obstruction (three equal-start
  APs, steps `a,b,a+b`). This generator/termination machinery is the standard approach to that
  shape and should be cited by the four-AP approach file.
- The `8k+3/8k+5` prime elimination matches the extended-searches result and the properties
  paper. Any run sieve must not violate it.
