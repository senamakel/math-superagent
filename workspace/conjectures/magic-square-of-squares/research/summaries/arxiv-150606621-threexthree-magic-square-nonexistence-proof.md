# Ferreira, arXiv:1506.06621 — metadata index

This is the abstract-page record for Jailton C. Ferreira, "On the 3×3 magic
square constructed with nine distinct square numbers," arXiv:1506.06621v2
(math.GM, 2015; 4 pages). Abstract page text:
`research/sources/arxiv-150606621-threexthree-magic-square-nonexistence-proof.full.md`.

The paper **claims** a proof that no 3×3 magic square of nine distinct squares
exists. The claim is **refuted**; the proof is invalid (root-substitution
tautology at the (46)→(47) step). For the full summary, the refutation, and the
claim block `ferreira-15060621-proof-invalid`, see
`research/summaries/arxiv-150606621-ferreira-fulltext.md`.
