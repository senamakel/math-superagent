## What it actually establishes (replacing the template lookup)

**Closed form.** For `h = ∏ pᵢ^{aᵢ}`, the number of representations of `h²` as
`x² + y²` including the degenerate `(0,h)` is `(∏_{pᵢ≡1 mod 4}(2aᵢ+1) + 1)/2`
(primes `p ≡ 3 mod 4` and `2` contribute nothing; a minimal `h` uses only
primes `≡ 1 mod 4`). Hence (checking the OEIS definitions, which agree as
cross-references state):

  `A006339(n) = A046112(n) = min h with ∏_{p≡1 mod 4}(2aᵢ+1) = 2n−1.`

**Why this matters here.** The run's own quantity `|S(e)| = #{d>0 : e²±d
both squares} = (∏(2aᵢ+1) − 1)/2` (ap_structure2.py, verified against the
x-loop up to `e ≤ 1500`) is the SAME function: `e²` admits `|S(e)|+1` sums of
two squares including the axes, so `e` with `∏(2aᵢ+1) = 2k+1` sits at
`n = k+1` of this sequence. Record-holder `e`'s (`∏ = 1,3,5,9,15,27,45,63,81,
135,189,243,405`) are exactly the run's S3 list
`1,5,25,65,325,1105,5525,27625,32045,160225,801125,1185665,5928325`
(`code/out/pattern_seq_output.txt`) and the values are S2
`0,1,2,4,7,13,22,31,40,67,94,121,202` — **an inter-source program cross-check
between this run's exhaustive data and OEIS A088959/A088111** (13 terms each).
