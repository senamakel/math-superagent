## Established statements

**Six-square configurations.** Six square entries ⇔ intersection of three quadrics in P⁵.
Up to symmetry exactly sixteen configurations (I–XVI); **all sixteen admit infinitely many
magic squares with those six entries square** (one-variable parametrisations). A seventh
square means a rational point on a (generally high-genus) hyperelliptic `f(t)=☐`. Only the
single 7-square example (1) ever arose.

**Buell bound (cited).** No 7-square "magic hourglass" with central element `< 25·10²⁴`.

**Category II.** `a±b+c=(g±h)²` etc. ⇒ elliptic curve `E: T²=S(S²−4(1−3λ²+λ⁴)S+4(1−λ²)⁴)`
over `Q(λ)`, torsion Z/4Z, rank 1. Multiples of P give 6-square families (degree 10, 26, …);
a specialization gives degree-8 entries and rank 2 over `Q(µ)`.

**Category VII — 7-square search.** `±b+c=☐, ±(a−b)+c=☐, −a+c=☐, c=☐` reduces to an
elliptic curve F over `Q(λ)`, rank 2; remaining entries square become quartic conditions
(12),(13). Search `p+q+n(λ)+d(λ) ≤ 1000`: **the only 7-square solution (up to symmetry) is
λ=13, (p,q)=(9,2)** — Bremner's witness.

**Category III — the K3.** `±a+c=☐, ±b+c=☐, ±(a+b)+c=☐` becomes
`S: T²+U²=V²+W²=X²+Y², TU+VW+XY=0`, non-singular hence K3. Symmetry group order 384,
24 real + 8 complex conics, 32 lines. Elliptic fibration cubic model
`τ²=σ(σ+(3−λ²)²)(σ+(1−3λ²)²)`.

- `E_λ(C(λ)) ≅ Z×Z×Z/4Z×Z/2Z` (Lemma); rank 2, height matrix det 1/4.
- **Theorem:** 20 divisors Γ1…Γ14 + 6 lines generate `NS(S,C)`.
- **Corollary:** `NS(S,Q)` generated by the **twelve** Γ1…Γ12. → `rank 12 < 20 = complex
  Picard rank`: **not Q-maximal**, the 8-gap a Brauer–Manin/rationality argument needs.
- **Corollary:** **any rational curve on S has even degree.**
- Rational-curve census from `½deg²−4(Γ·Γ)` = sum of squares: degree 2 only trivial conics;
  **a degree-6 curve (Γ4+Γ5+Γ8+Γ9−Γ12, 96 symmetries)** exists whose "next square entry"
  condition gives only λ=±1 (trivial) — the degree-6 census is the one explicitly computed;
  a degree-10 curve and six degree-14 curves exist (impracticable beyond). The summary
  previously mis-stated this as "no rational curves of degrees 4 or 8": the source's actual
  census covers degrees 2, 6, 10, 14 explicitly. The claim ledger `k3-ns-rank-12-not-maximal`
  repeats the mis-statement and will be corrected from this note.

**Q(√3) family.** Over `Q(√3)` there is an **infinite family of 8-square magic squares**
(e.g. `(22+4√3)², (17−9√3)², …`, middle `2²·7·19 = 532`, i.e. the degree-4 grid of
Bremner 1999 transposed; note its centre is 532 = 133·2², NOT "133·22" — Bremner 1999's
OCR shows 2926, but exact row-sum arithmetic forces 532), via curves `Y²=X(X²+8X+4)`
(rank 1) and `Y²=X(X²+2X−2)` (rank 2). So 8-square MSS exist over the quadratic field,
matching "no rational 8-square example known".
