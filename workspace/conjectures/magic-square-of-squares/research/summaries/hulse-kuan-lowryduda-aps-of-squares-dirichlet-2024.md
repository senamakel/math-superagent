## What the abstract claims

- Studies a **two-variable Dirichlet series which counts primitive three-term
  arithmetic progressions of squares**.
- Proves this multiple Dirichlet series has **meromorphic continuation to C²**.
- Uses **Tauberian methods** to obtain asymptotic **counts of primitive
  three-term APs of squares**, and of rational points on x² + y² = 2.

## Bearing on this run

Three-term APs of squares are the fundamental building block of the MSS (the
four lines through the centre are four such APs sharing the middle term e²).
This paper gives analytic estimates for *how many* primitive APs of squares
exist — a count, not a structural obstruction. It does **not** address the
additive-dependence condition among the four differences u, v, u+v, u−v that
is the actual obstruction (a full MSS needs a quadruple of APs with differences
in that additive relation, sharing a middle term). So this is background on the
asymptotics of the AP-of-squares counting function, relevant to the
`four-ap-additive-triple` thread only as infrastructure: the counts say APs of
squares are abundant (as the run's |S(e)| analysis already established —
millions of centres admit four AP-differences), reinforcing that the scarcity
lies in the additive relation, not in AP existence.

No claim block: the substantive result is bounded by its abstract here; the run
already has the sharper |S(e)| structure (phi-universal-set) it needs.
