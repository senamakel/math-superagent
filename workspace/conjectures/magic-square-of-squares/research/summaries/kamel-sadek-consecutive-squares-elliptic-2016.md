The abstract states the main result: an infinite family of elliptic curves
`E_m: y² = a x³ + b x + c` over Q, each carrying a 5-term sequence of
consecutive squares of x-coordinates of rational points, those five points
linearly independent, so `rank E_m(Q) ≥ 5`. See the body summary for detail.
