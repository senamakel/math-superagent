**Definition.** Number of ways of expressing n as a sum of two square numbers (i.e. the count
`r₂`-type multiplicity for the Pythagorean decomposability), the sequence paired with A088959:
A088959 indexes by record, A088111 gives the associated counts.

## Relevance to this run
- These multiplicities count how many Pythagorean decompositions a candidate centre `e²`
  admits — exactly the data feeding Bremner's "two realised + two half-realised" AP structure
  and Brown's "needs at least 5 representations".
- No closed form in the entry; the counts come from the standard formula for the number of
  representations as a sum of two squares (divisors ≡1 mod 4 minus ≡3 mod 4, + Euler's
  identity). For `e²` the count is essentially the product over `(1 mod 4)`-prime exponents.

## Assessment
- Recreation-only value beyond confirming the multiplicity growth is slow (logarithmic in the
  divisor function). The counts do not give a usable closed form for locating a MSS centre;
  the real content is Brown's ≥5 bound + A088959's smallest candidates.
