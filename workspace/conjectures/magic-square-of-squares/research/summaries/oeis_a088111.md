# A088111 — number of sum-of-two-squares representations at the A088959 record-holders (catalogue)

<!-- source: https://oeis.org/A088111 -->

**Terms:** 0, 1, 2, 4, 7, 13, 22, 31, 40, 67, 94, 121, 202, 283, 364, 607, 850, 1093, 1822, 2551, 3280, …

**What A088111 is.** The sequence of record *values* of the number of ways a
square is a sum of two positive squares, i.e. the values `4·∏(2a+1) − 4` recorded
at the A088959 record-holders: a(n) = 4·∏_{p≡1 mod 4}(2a_p+1) − 4 where e is the
n-th A088959 term. Dividing by 4: 0, 1, 2, 4, 7, 13, 22, 31, 40, ... (the run's
hand-checked |S(e)| increments shifted by one: |S| = (∏(2a+1)−1)/2, and
(4∏−4)/4 = ∏−1 = 2·|S|.)

**Holds here.** yes — as a catalogue corroboration of `phi-universal-set`'s
multiplicative |S(e)| formula: the growth is exactly the product-of-(2a+1)
structure (slow, divisor-driven), not a closed form in e.

**Does not settle.** Nothing about additive Φ-triples; these are sizes of the
per-centre AP-difference set, not relations among differences.
