**Status: reference list captured; article body paywalled.** Springer served the abstract, keywords and the full reference list (28 items) but the article text requires a subscription. The full text is therefore NOT on disk; what is captured is the canonical bibliography of the subject, which alone is valuable because it pins the exact citations and page ranges.

## What is captured (the canonical reference list of the subject)

- **LaBar, Problem 270**, *College Mathematics J.* 15 (1984) 69 — the origin of the problem (as stated in problem.md).
- **Robertson, "Magic squares of squares"**, *Mathematics Magazine* 69 (1996) 289–293, DOI 10.2307/2690537 — the elliptic-reduction paper whose reduction Bremner reports.
- **Bremner, "On squares of squares"**, *Acta Arithmetica* 88 (1999) 289–297 and **"On squares of squares II"**, *Acta Arithmetica* 99 (2001) 289–308, DOI 10.4064/aa99-3-6.
- **Buell, "A search for a magic hourglass"**, preprint 1999/2004 — the `25×10²⁴` hourglass search (see caveats below and `buell-search-for-magic-hourglass-1999.md`).
- **Sallows, "The lost theorem"**, *Math. Intelligencer* 19 (1997) no. 4, 51–54, DOI 10.1007/BF03024415 — the "Parker square" LS1 (7 of 8 line sums).
- **Rabern, "Properties of magic squares of squares"**, Rose-Hulman Undergrad. Math. J. 4 (2003) N.1 — entry-parity/prime-factor restrictions.
- **Gardner, "The magic of 3×3"**, *Quantum* 6 (1996) no. 3, 24–26 (and no. 4, 60) — the $100 prize offer history.
- **Guy & Nowakowski, Monthly unsolved problems**, *Amer. Math. Monthly* 102 (1995) 921–926 etc.
- **Guy, Problem D15**, *Unsolved Problems in Number Theory* (3rd ed., 2004) 268–271 — the problem's catalogue number.
- **Schroeppel, HAKMEM Item 50** (1972) and the centre-cell-of-magic-3³ = 63 note — early computational folklore.
- **Lucas, "Sur un problème d'Euler relatif aux carrés magiques"**, *Nouvelle Correspondance Math.* 2 (1876) 97–101 — the 1876 family containing Sallows' LS1.
- **Boyer's own supplement** ("Supplement to the article", downloadable from multimagie.com, 2005) — already in this library as `boyer-notes-supplement-2005`.
- **Cain, "Gaussian Integers, Rings, Finite Fields, and the Magic Square of Squares"**, arXiv:1908.03236 — already in this library.
