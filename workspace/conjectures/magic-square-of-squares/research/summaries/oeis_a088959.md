**Independent cross-check with the run's own exhaustive data.** The first
**13 terms** of A088959 equal the run's record-holder `e`'s for `|S(e)|`
computed by exhaustive enumeration over `e ≤ 10⁷`
(`pattern_seq_output.txt`, `oeis_verify.py`): 1,5,25,65,325,1105,5525,27625,

catalogue the run's own enumeration reproduces to 13 terms.

**Why it matters here.** `e²` is the centre of a candidate MSS, and its
two-square representation count `|S(e)|+1` determines (via Brown's bound) how
many candidate AP differences the centre admits. The sequence shows the
necessary growth: `|S(e)| ≥ 4` requires `e ≥ 325` (the 5th term, `|S|=7`);
`|S| ≥ 12` requires `e ≥ 1105`. The record **growth is slow** (logarithmic in
the divisor function), so the mere multiplicity condition does not force huge
centres — the obstruction is elsewhere.

**Does not settle.** The sequence pins the centre-multiplicity *necessary*
condition only; it says nothing about whether the four AP differences
`u, v, u+v, u−v` (which must each be a different two-square-derived `d` of
the same centre) can be simultaneously realised. That is the four-AP
obstruction, which this catalogue does not touch.
