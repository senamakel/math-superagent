# OEIS A088959 — d-Pythagorean decomposable record holders

[[oeis_a088959]]

**Terms.** 1, 5, 25, 65, 325, 1105, 5525, 27625, 32045, 160225, 801125, 1185665, 5928325,
29641625, 48612265, 243061325, 1215306625, 2576450045, 12882250225, 64411251125, …

**Definition.** Lowest numbers which are d-Pythagorean decomposable, i.e. whose square is
expressible as a sum of two positive squares in more ways than for any smaller number. These
are also the integer radii of circles around the origin containing record numbers of lattice
points (integer points `(x,y)` with `x²+y² = r²`).

## Relevance to this run
- For the centre square `c = e²`, the run's reformulation is `c = x²+y²` with the AP
  difference `d = 2xy` realised by a Pythagorean pair. The **number of distinct ways to write
  `c` as a sum of two squares is exactly the multiplicity** feeding the two realised APs in a
  near-MSS. A055959 gives the smallest `r` with a record count.
- Brown (kmath417) Prop 1: a MSS needs `c` expressible as a sum of two squares in **at least
  5 distinct ways**. A088959 (equivalently A016032 column of record counts) tells the smallest
  such numbers, bounding where any MSS centre must lie.

## Assessment
- This is a catalogued sequence, `status: catalogued`, not a theorem. It agrees with Brown's
  multiplicity bound but does not prove it. No closed form given in the entry; the record
  counts come from the divisors-of-`r²`-with-`≡1 mod 4` structure.
