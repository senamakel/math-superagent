```approach
idea: Apply Chabauty-Coleman (p-adic integration) to the specific hyperelliptic curves that arise when demanding a seventh or eighth square entry in Bremner's 2001 classification of six-square configurations.
mechanism: Bremner (Acta Arithmetica 2001) showed that for each of the 16 configurations of six square entries, demanding a seventh square entry reduces to finding rational points on a hyperelliptic curve Y² = f(t) with f of generally high genus. For the particular configuration that yielded his 7-square magic square (1), the transition from 7 to 8 squares involves an additional such curve. If that curve has genus g ≥ 2 and the Mordell-Weil rank r of its Jacobian satisfies r < g, then Chabauty's theorem (1941) says the set of rational points is finite and Coleman's refinement (1985) makes it effectively computable via p-adic integration. One would: (a) extract the explicit curve for the 7→8 transition from Bremner's parametrisation, (b) compute its genus, (c) compute the rank of its Jacobian via 2-descent or by bounding it using the known structure of the elliptic surface, (d) if r < g, run Coleman integration at a suitable prime p to determine all rational points. If the only rational points correspond to the known 7-square configuration (or to degenerate cases), this proves no 8-square magic square exists in that configuration. Since Bremner classified all 16 configurations and parametrised them, covering each one would settle the 8-square sub-question mod finiteness of the remaining cases. This is the p-adic counterpart to the Brauer-Manin proposal — it works on the curves directly rather than on the K3 surface.
status: proposed
first-step: From Bremner II (2001), identify the explicit hyperelliptic curve for the 7-square configuration (1) when the non-squares are the two entries at positions corresponding to 360721 and 222121, and compute its genus. If genus ≥ 2, compute the rank of its Jacobian via a 2-descent (Magma or Sage).
```
