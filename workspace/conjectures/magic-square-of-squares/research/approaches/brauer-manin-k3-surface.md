# Approach: Brauer-Manin obstruction on the K3 surface

```approach
idea: Bremner II (2001) identifies the magic-square-of-squares problem with rational points on a K3 surface S over Q(λ) whose Néron-Severi group NS(S,Q) is generated by 12 explicit divisors, and proves every rational curve on S has even degree. The arithmetic of K3 surfaces has advanced considerably since 2001: the Brauer-Manin obstruction — the étale-cohomological pairing Br(S) × S(A_Q) → Q/Z — is now a standard tool for detecting failures of the Hasse principle on K3 surfaces. Compute Br(S)/Br(Q) for Bremner's surface and evaluate the Brauer-Manin pairing on the adelic points that correspond to the known local (congruence) solutions. If Br(S)/Br(Q) is nontrivial and the pairing is constant and nonzero on all adelic points, then no rational point exists and the MSS conjecture is proved.

mechanism: The "hinge" in CONTEXT.md is that MSS exist over extension fields (e.g., Q(√3,√133) of degree 4) but (conjecturally) not over Q. The Brauer-Manin obstruction is precisely the tool that can separate Q from its extensions: an element of Br(S) may vanish upon base change to a finite extension, so the obstruction disappears over that extension. This matches the known data perfectly. The computation has three steps: (1) Compute the geometric Brauer group Br(S_Qbar) using the known NS(S_Qbar) from Bremner II — for a K3 surface with NS of rank 12, the transcendental lattice has rank 10, and Br(S_Qbar) is controlled by the transcendental lattice. (2) Compute the algebraic part Br₁(S)/Br(Q) via the Galois action on NS, using the explicit divisor generators Γ₁,...,Γ₁₂. (3) Evaluate the Brauer-Manin pairing on the adelic points coming from the known local solubility (problem.md: solutions mod every prime power). If the image does not contain 0, then S(Q) is empty.

This is computationally demanding but conceptually clean: it answers "does the known local solubility come from a global point?" using the standard cohomological machinery for K3 surfaces. It does not require a descent or an infinite search.

status: proposed
first-step: Extract the explicit equations of the K3 surface S from Bremner II (2001), compute its Néron-Severi lattice with Galois action from the 12 generators Γ₁,...,Γ₁₂, and determine whether Br(S)/Br(Q) is nontrivial by computing H¹(Gal(Q̄/Q), NS(S_Qbar)) via the standard K3 Brauer-group sequence.
```
