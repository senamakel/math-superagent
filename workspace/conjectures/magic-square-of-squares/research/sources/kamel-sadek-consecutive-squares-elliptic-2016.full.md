# Title: On sequences of consecutive squares on elliptic curves

Authors: [Mohamed Kamel][2], [Mohammad Sadek][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled On sequences of consecutive squares on elliptic curves, by Mohamed Kamel and Mohammad Sadek

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: Let $C$ be an elliptic curve defined over $\mathbb Q$ by the equation $y^2=x^3+Ax+B$ where $A,B\in\mathbb Q$. A sequence of rational points $(x_i,y_i)\in C(\mathbb Q),\,i=1,2,\ldots,$ is said to form a sequence of consecutive squares on $C$ if the sequence of $x$-coordinates, $x_i,i=1,2,\ldots$, consists of consecutive squares. We produce an infinite family of elliptic curves $C$ with a $5$-term sequence of consecutive squares. Furthermore, this sequence consists of five independent rational points in $C(\mathbb Q)$. In particular, the rank $r$ of $C(\mathbb Q)$ satisfies $r\ge 5$.
