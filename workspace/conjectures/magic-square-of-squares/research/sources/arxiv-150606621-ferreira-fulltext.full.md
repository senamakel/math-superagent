# On the 3 × 3 3\times 3 magic square constructed with nine distinct square numbers

Jailton C. Ferreira

###### Abstract

A proof that there is no 3 × 3 3\times 3 magic square constructed with nine distinct square numbers is given.

## 1 Introduction

In 1984 Martin Labar [1] formulated the problem: Can a 3 × 3 3\times 3 magic square be constructed with nine distinct square numbers? The problem is found in the second edition of Guy’s Unsolved Problems in Number Theory [2] and became famous when Martin Gardner republished it in 1996 [3].
