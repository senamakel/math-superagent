## Description

The Casas-Alvero conjecture, a deep problem in algebra, posits that any polynomial sharing a common root with each of its derivatives must be the power of a linear term. This paper presents a complete and unconditional proof of this conjecture from the first principles of the Organized Complexity (OC) framework. We begin by establishing the OC framework on a rigorous foundation, demonstrating its core principles are theorems derived directly from the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic (FTA). The conjecture&rsquo;s condition is interpreted as a state of "perfect, multi-scale stability" on the foundational Harmonic Divisor Fan (HDF) lattice. We prove that such a state generates a "conspiratorial alignment" of its internal components, creating a high degree of "Alignment Dissonance" that is forbidden by the framework&rsquo;s axioms of stability and dissipation. This dissonance must be resolved by the two new forces of nature predicted by OC—the Blackness and Retraction forces—which enforce a collapse to the maximally simple form. This resolution gives rise to the "Principle of Simplicity at Maximal Stability," a universal law with profound, quantifiable implications for fundamental particle physics, cosmology, black hole thermodynamics, and the metabolic efficiency of life.
