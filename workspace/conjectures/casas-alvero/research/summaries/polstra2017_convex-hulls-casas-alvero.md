# Polstra, *Convex Hulls and the Casas-Alvero Conjecture for the Complex Plane* (2012)

Rose-Hulman Undergraduate Mathematics Journal 13(1), art. 2 (2012). Thomas
Polstra (Georgia State), faculty sponsor Florian Enescu. Full text held.

<!-- source: https://scholar.rose-hulman.edu/rhumj/vol13/iss1/2 -->

## What this source is

A peer-reviewed (undergraduate journal) paper giving an **equivalent
reformulation of CA over ℂ using the convex hull of the roots**, plus several
special cases proved.

## What it establishes

- **Reformulation**: If f shares a root with each of its n−1 derivatives, then
  every root of f is a vertex of the convex hull C_f **iff** f has a single
  root of multiplicity n. I.e. a counterexample must have a root in the
  interior of the convex hull (or on an edge) — a geometric constraint
  consistent with (weaker than) the Laterveer–Ounaïes Gauss–Lucas statement
  already held.
- **Real-rooted case**: an equivalent condition to CA for polynomials with
  only real roots, proved via Viète's relations and multiplicity patterns.
  This is a second independent treatment of the real-rooted case (cf.
  Yakubovich 2015).

## Bearing

- Gives the run a convex-geometric angle on the minimal-counterexample
  structure: any counterexample's roots are not all vertices of their convex
  hull. Complements the Gauss–Lucas hull result already in CLAIMS.md.
- Real-rooted case is now sourced twice (Polstra 2012, Yakubovich 2015).
