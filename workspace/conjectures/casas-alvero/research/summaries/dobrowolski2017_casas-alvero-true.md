## What this adds to the run

This is a third documented claim of a full CA proof that was withdrawn with a
specific, named error. It joins:
- **Battiston 2015** (arXiv:1511.04932) — withdrawn by author: "crucial error in its
  last page," point out to her by Joseph Schicho (held: battiston source).
- **Dobrowolski 2017** (this one) — withdrawn: irreparable error found by Jacob
  Tsimerman.
- **Ghosh 2025/26** (arXiv:2501.09272) — CLAIMED, unverified preprint, not withdrawn,
  not peer-reviewed, still under "major revisions".
- Fernández de las Heras 2013, Yakubovich 2015, Lu 2017 — other unverified claims.

Pattern (worth recording as a claim): every claimed complete proof of CA so far has
either been withdrawn with a named error by a referee-comparable expert, or remains
an unverified preprint. No claimed proof has become an accepted resolution. This
reinforces ROOT.md's working assumption that CA remains open.

## Evidence class

- Withdrawn status + withdrawal comment: **verified from the arXiv abs page (held)** —
  this is primary, the arXiv record itself.
- That the "irreparable error" is real / the conjecture still open: **asserted by the
  withdrawal, not independently re-derived here.**

## Why it matters / falsifier

The run's "has a proof been claimed / does one stand" answer should now cite this:
Dobrowolski (2017) claimed and withdrew; Tsimerman found the error. A falsifier to
watch: if later literature says Dobrowolski's claim was rehabilitated (it has not been
in any held source), this entry would need revisiting.
