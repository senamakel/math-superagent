## What it claims

This article is about certain properties of higher order polars of a germ of complex analytic curve \(C\), say defined near the origin of \(\mathbb{C}^2\) by a convergent power series \(f(x,y)\). Recall that the first \(x\)-polar of \(f\) is the germ \(P_x(f)\) defined by the analytic equation \(\partial f/ \partial y=0\). If the \(y\)-axis \(Y\) is not an irreducible component of \(C\) then \(\partial f/ \partial y\) is not identically zero, so \(P_x(f)\) is defined. Then it is not hard to see that \(Y\) is not an irreducible component of the first polar and the process may be iterated, getting the second polar \(P^{(2)}_x(f)\). Repeating we may define \(x\)-polars \(P^{(r)}_x(f)\) of any order \(r\). The main result obtained in this paper is of the following type.
