# Wikipedia, *Casas-Alvero conjecture* (accessed revision)

Full text: [[wikipedia_casas_alvero.full]]

Encyclopedic summary — the run's check on the **status question** (smallest open degree) and the statement of the char-p counterexamples. Not authoritative on its own, but corroborates the primary sources.

## What it establishes (corroborating)
- Formal statement over a char-0 field K: if f has a common factor with each derivative f^(i), i=1..d−1, then f must be a power of a linear polynomial. (Matches all primary sources.)
- **Analogue in non-zero characteristic**: CA is false; any inseparable f(X^p) without constant term satisfies the condition (all derivatives vanish); a separable counterexample is X^{p+1} − X^p. (Corroborates Graf-von-Bothmer & Schaub–Spivakovsky.)
- **Special cases**: holds in char 0 for degrees p^k and 2p^k (p prime, k≥1); for 3p^k (p≠2), 4p^k (p≠3,5,7), 5p^k (p≠2,3,7,11,131,193,599,3541,8009); similar for 6p^k and 7p^k. Established for **d=12**, making **d=20 the smallest open degree**.
- Lists references: Casas-Alvero 2001 origin; Diaz-Toca & Gonzalez-Vega 2006 (d≤7); GvB 2007; Draisma–de Jong 2011; Castryck–Laterveer–Ounaïes 2012 (d=12).
