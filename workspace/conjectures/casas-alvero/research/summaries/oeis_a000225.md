# A000225 — a(n) = 2^n − 1 (Mersenne numbers)

<!-- source: https://oeis.org/A000225 -->

**What it establishes for this problem.**

The verified scenario-count law (see [[oeis_a008277]]) gives: for degree d,
the number of scenarios of type t=1 (i.e. exactly 2 distinct recycled roots
used, normalised s_1=0) is **S2(d−1, 2) = 2^(d−2) − 1**, the Mersenne row
A000225 evaluated at n = d−2. This is the exact closed form behind the
fixed-type C-finite law (char polynomial (x−1)(x−2), recurrence a_n =
3a_{n−1} − 2a_{n−2}) verified through d=120 by scenario/attack_eigen_recurrence.py.

Hypotheses: none. Holds-here: yes — the type-1 scenario count for the full
(unfiltered) scenario space. Status: catalogued; the identification with the
scenario count is verified-computational.

For degree 20 the type-1 scenario count is 2^18 − 1 = 262143.
