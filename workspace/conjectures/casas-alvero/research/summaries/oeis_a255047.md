# A255047 — 1 together with the positive terms of A000225

<!-- source: https://oeis.org/A255047 -->

**What it establishes for this problem.**

A255047 = {1} ∪ {2^n − 1 : n ≥ 1} = {1, 1, 3, 7, 15, 31, …}. It is the
Mersenne row A000225 with a leading 1 prepended; the scenario-count law (see
[[oeis_a008277]]) uses the type-1 count S2(d−1,2) = 2^(d−2)−1, which is the
tail of this row. The prepended 1 matches the degenerate type-0 count (always
S2(d−1,1) = 1). Kept on file to identify the row; no independent content.

Status: catalogued; holds-here: yes.
