# Soham Ghosh receives Simons Dissertation Award

Submitted by Rose Choi on August 20, 2025 - 8:09 pm

[image: Soham Ghosh] [1]

UW Mathematics PhD student Soham Ghosh has been selected to be part of the first class of [Simons Dissertation Fellows][2] in mathematics. These fellowships provide research support to exceptional graduate students in mathematics. This award recognizes the excellence of Ghosh's research, which includes his recent [resolution][3] of the [Casas-Alvero conjecture][4], a problem posed by Eduardo Casas-Alvero about polynomials which have factors in common with their derivatives, and which has been open since 2001. Ghosh is co-advised by Max Lieblich and Farbod Shokrieh.
