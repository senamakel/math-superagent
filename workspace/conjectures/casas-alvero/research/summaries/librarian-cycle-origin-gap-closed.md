## What this cycle added

1. **Closed the one persistent primary-source gap: the origin paper.**
   Casas-Alvero, "Higher order polar germs" (J. Algebra 240(1):326–337), was
   documented blocked (Elsevier paywall; UB repository 404) across earlier
   cycles. This cycle obtained the deepest open statement of its content:
   the MaRDI portal item `Q5938612`, which carries A. Nobile's zbMATH review
   stating the paper's theorem. Held in full at
   `research/sources/casas-alvero2001_higher-order-polar-germs_mardi-record.full.md`,
   summarised at `research/summaries/casas-alvero2001_origin-polar-germs.md`.
   - Content captured: the higher-polar decomposition
     P_x^(r)(f)=D_1^(r)+…+D_u(r)^(r) of a plane branch, the
     intersection-number formulas, the Teissier polar-invariant ratios, and
     new proofs of the Dickenstein–Sessa and Merle formulas. This is the
     singularity-theory origin of the CA conjecture that `problem.md` and
     GOAL.md refer to.
   - Canonical identifiers now on record (zbl 0985.14012, OpenAlex
     W2003962780, DOI 10.1006/jabr.2000.8727) so no later role re-invents a
     citation for the origin paper. Note the portal records publication date
     2 June 2002 (the 2001 volume year, issue dated 1 June 2001) — a small
     precision on "around 2001".
