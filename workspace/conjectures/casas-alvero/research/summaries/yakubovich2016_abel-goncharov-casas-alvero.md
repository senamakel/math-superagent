## What the abstract establishes

- Derives **new properties of the Abel–Goncharov interpolation polynomials**, applied to necessary-and-sufficient conditions for a degree-n polynomial to be trivial, f(z) = a(z−b)^n.
- **Determinantal representation** of the Abel–Goncharov interpolation polynomials (a result of independent interest).
- New **Sz.-Nagy-type identities** for complex roots.
- A **generalization of the Schoenberg-conjectured analogue of Rolle's theorem** for polynomials with real and complex coefficients.
- Framed as progress toward the Casas-Alvero conjecture (2001).
- Keywords: Casas-Alvero problem; Abel–Goncharov interpolation polynomials. MSC: 26C05, 12D10, 41A05, 13F20.
- Funding: CMUP UID/MAT/00144/2013, FCT Portugal.
