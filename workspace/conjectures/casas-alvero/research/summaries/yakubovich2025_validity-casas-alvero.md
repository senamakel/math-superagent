# Yakubovich, *The validity of the Casas-Alvero conjecture* (2015, arXiv:1504.00274)

<!-- source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.00274v1 | PDF held in full -->

## What this source is

A 2015 preprint by Semyon Yakubovich claiming CA is solved affirmatively, via
Abel–Goncharov interpolation polynomials, Sz.-Nagy type identities for complex
roots, and generalizations of Schoenberg's analogue of Rolle's theorem.

## What it claims

- Abstract states outright: "an attractive problem conjectured in 2001 by
  E. Casas-Alvero is solved affirmatively in this note."
- Method: identifies a CA-satisfying f with the Abel–Goncharov interpolation
  polynomial f(z) = G_n(z, z_0, z_1, ..., z_{n-1}), derives determinant
  representations (incl. Levinson polynomials H_n), and root-location /
  unit-circle / disk-confinement constraints on the common roots z_i.
- Includes results on the **real-rooted case**: e.g. a real-rooted polynomial
  of degree n ≥ 2 is trivial if its (n−2)-nd derivative has a double root;
  and Schoenberg-analogue statements for real/complex coefficients — the
  "real-rooted case" `problem.md` mentions.

## Status

**Another claimed complete proof that did not become an accepted resolution.**
CA was still treated as open by every source after 2015 (Castryck et al. d=12
2018, Schaub–Spivakovsky 2023-2025, Wikipedia). It belongs in the same class
as Battiston (withdrawn), Fernández de las Heras (claimed, unpublished), and
now Ghosh (claimed, unverified): CA attracts claimed proofs; none has stood.

## Bearing on this run

- Supplies the **real-rooted case** and Abel–Goncharoff machinery — a
  restricted class `problem.md` lists but the library did not hold. The
  real-rooted results here are the strongest "restricted class settled" row
  the run has for real coefficients (with the caveat that this is a preprint).
- The determinant/resultant technology is adjacent to this run's
  elimination approach (see Laterveer–Ounaïes and Massri, who also use
  Abel–Gontcharoff polynomials).
