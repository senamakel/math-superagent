## What this source is

A short **peer-reviewed** note (4 pages, J. Commut. Algebra 17(2):199-202,
Summer 2025) by Daniel Schaub and Mark Spivakovsky. Received 7 Nov 2024,
accepted 12 Jan 2025. This is the published, refereed version of their earlier
arXiv note (arXiv:2312.08742), which this library already holds in full.

## What it establishes

**The resultant independence reformulation of CA.** Let
`f = x^d + a_1 x^{d-1} + ... + a_1 x ∈ K[a_1,...,a_{d-1}][x]` and let
`R_i = Res(f, H_i(f)) ∈ K[a_1,...,a_{d-1}]` for `i = 1,...,d-1` (H_i the i-th
Hasse–Schmidt derivative). Then:

> **The Casas–Alvero conjecture is equivalent to the independence of the
> resultants**: `ht(R_1, ..., R_{d-1}) = d-1` in `K[a_1,...,a_{d-1}]`.

This is the exact commutative-algebra / elimination statement this run's
method (GOAL's scheme-theoretic, Gröbner, height-of-ideal approach) is built
around, and it comes from a **refereed source**.

**Partial result toward that independence.** The paper proves:

> if `i ∈ {d-3, d-2, d-1}` then `R_i ∉ (R_1, ..., Ř_i, ..., R_{d-1})`

i.e. each of the three highest resultants is not in the ideal generated by the
others. This matches the earlier arXiv note's Theorem 5. It is a genuine but
narrow step: it shows independence of each high-index resultant from the rest,
not the full height-d-1 statement.

## Bearing on this run

- The height/independence reformulation is stated by a peer-reviewed source —
  the run can cite it rather than re-derive it. It is exactly the scheme-
  theoretic view GOAL.md calls for (the arithmetic Casas-Alvero scheme and
  its ideal in K[a_1,...,a_{d-1}]).
- It confirms the status boundary: this 2025 refereed paper does not resolve
  CA, and its publication independent of Ghosh's preprint gives no support to
  the Ghosh claim. CA remains open as of 2025-26.
- The `i ∈ {d-3,d-2,d-1}` membership is a candidate to stress-test / extend
  computationally: does the analogous non-membership hold for lower i, and is
  the full height-d-1 reachable by Gröbner over Q for small d?
