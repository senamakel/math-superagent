# Schaub & Spivakovsky, *A note on the CA conjecture* (arXiv:2312.08742, v7 2025)

Full text: [[schaub_spivakovsky_2023_note]]

A small partial result on the **independence** of the resultants — directly the run's "radical = (a_1,…,a_{d−1})" target. (Held text is the arXiv abstract page; the claim below is exactly what the abstract states.)

## Claim

```claim
id: ss-note-independence
statement: CA in degree d is equivalent to the height of the ideal (R_1,…,R_{d−1}) being
  d−1 in K[a_1,…,a_{d−1}] (independence of the resultants). A very partial result in this
  direction: for i ∈ {d−3, d−2, d−1}, R_i ∉ sqrt(R_1,…,\hat{R}_i,…,R_{d−1}). I.e., each of
  the top three resultants is not in the radical of the ideal generated by the others.
hypotheses: char 0, degree d
holds-here: yes (this is the independence direction of the CA-variety problem)
status: asserted-by-source (abstract; the paper is short and this is its whole content)
bearing: The full claim would be: a_i^N ∈ (R_1,…,R_{d−1}), equivalent to the variety
  being {0}. This note proves only that the top three R_i are each indispensable; it does
  not give independence of all R_j at once, nor membership of the a_i. A modest but real
  step the run's own ideal-membership computation might extend.
anchor: research/sources/schaub_spivakovsky_2023_note.full.md (abstract)
falsifies: finding R_{d−3} or R_{d−2} in the radical of the other resultants for some degree.
```

## What it does not settle
Only the top three indices; not the general independence, and not CA for any degree.
