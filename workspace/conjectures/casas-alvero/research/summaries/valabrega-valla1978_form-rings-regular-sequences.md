## What it establishes

The paper studies the **form ring** (associated graded ring) `G_A(a) = ⊕ a^n/a^{n+1}` of a noetherian ring `A` with respect to an ideal `a`, and for an ideal `b = (f_1,…,f_r)` the **form ideal** `b*` generated by the initial forms `f_i*` of the `f_i`. Key fact relating `b*` to generators: `b* = (f_1*,…,f_r*)` iff for all `n≥1`, `b ∩ a^n = Σ_j a^{n−p_j} f_j` where `p_j = ord(f_j)` (Theorem 1.1).

**The result that matters to this run (Theorem 2.3 + Corollary 2.4, and Remark 2.5 after it):**

```claim
id: valabrega-valla-initial-forms-regular-sequence
statement: Let A be noetherian and a, b=(f_1,…,f_r) ideals with a+b ≠ A. Then b* is generated by a regular sequence f_1*,…,f_r* in G_A(a) iff (i) the f_i form a regular sequence on A localised correctly, and (ii) b ∩ a^n = Σ_j a^{n-p_j} f_j for all n≥1 (weights p_j = ord_a(f_j)). If A is local and b* is generated by a G_A(a)-sequence, then b is generated by an A-sequence (Cor 2.4).
hypotheses: A noetherian (local for Cor 2.4); a, b ideals with a+b≠A; f_i* the initial forms in the associated graded with respect to a.
holds-here: yes (for the u-resultant setting, A = K[a_1..a_n] localised at the origin, a = m_0, b = I=(R_1..R_{n-1}))

A mismatch B ≠ ∏ ord is evidence gr is not CM (Cor 2.4 fails), NOT a counterexample to CA. The equality is strictly stronger than regularity, hence strictly stronger than CA.
anchor: research/sources/valabrega-valla1978_form-rings-regular-sequences.full.md (Thm 2.3, Cor 2.4)
```

**Caveat (Remark 2.5):** Initial forms forming a regular sequence does *not* by itself make the `f_i` an A-sequence, and vice versa — the two regular-sequence properties are not equivalent without the `b ∩ a^n` condition. This is exactly why `∏ ord_0(R_i) = e` is a strictly stronger certificate than CA, and why failing it does not fail CA.

## Why it matters here

The u-resultant approach (thread `uresultant-converge`) computes the exponent `B` of the pure power `Res_u = c·u^B` and compares it to `∏ ord_0(R_i)`. Valabrega–Valla Theorem 2.3 is the precise name of that comparison: equality holds iff the associated graded ring `gr_{m_0}(K[a]/(I))` is Cohen–Macaulay. The existing claims `ord0-resultant-weighted-order-proved-all-n` (ord_0(R_i) = n(n−i)) and `uresultant-multiplicity-trees-new` (length = n^(n−2)) already rest on it. Nothing in this source contradicts the run's durable memory; it supplies the theorem the equality was already citing.

## Not a CA-specific result

This is a general commutative-algebra theorem. It is held because it is the load-bearing hypothesis for the run's complete-intersection multiplicity certificate, not because it says anything about Casas-Alvero directly.
