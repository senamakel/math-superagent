The run's adopted root-difference-coloring approach (thread root-difference-coloring) is built on the Abel–Gontcharoff / root-difference factorization. Levinson 1944 is the primary reference for the defining properties (G_n^(r)(z_{r+1}) = 0) and the sharp bounds.
