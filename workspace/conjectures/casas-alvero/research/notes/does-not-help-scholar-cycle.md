# Does-not-help sources — digested once, will not help again

Which held sources do not advance the CASAS-ALVERO run, and why, so nobody reads them a second time expecting a result. (Related earlier note: `research/notes/does-not-help-2026.md`.)

## Lang 1990 — "Old and new conjectured Diophantine inequalities" (Bull AMS)

`research/summaries/lang1990_old-new-conjectured-diophantine-inequalities.md` is a raw auto-digest of height/elliptic-curve Néron-function inequalities (Prop 6.1–6.7: lower bounds for canonical heights, reduction mod p of elliptic curve points, p-integrality of multiples of rational points). **Does not help.** It is Diophantine-approximation material (Mordell–Lang/heights) with no connection to the CA coefficient scheme, its resultants, or multiplicity. Nothing in it bears on the char-p break, the bad-prime lists, or the Gauss–Lucas/Abel–Gontcharoff analysis. It was held as a possible § degree-growth source and is not. **Do not re-read.**

## EOM encyclopedia entries (resultant, Groebner basis, Newton diagram)

`eom_resultant`, `eom_groebner-basis`, `eom_newton-diagram` are generic definitions-level encyclopedia entries. They restate facts the run already has with proper citations and used daily (resultant = a_0^s ∏ g(α_i); Sylvester/determinantal form; Groebner basics; Newton polytope = convex hull of exponent vectors). **No bearing beyond confirming definitions; strictly weaker than the primary commutative-algebra sources already held** (CLO, Valabrega–Valla, Kostov). Keep as definition reference only; no claim blocks needed — there is no theorem here beyond textbook statements already sourced elsewhere. The weight-graded Newton-polytope fact the run uses (a_j weight j) is already recorded in `ord0-resultant-weighted-order-proved-all-n` with a proper source (de Frutos Marín 2013).

## Dzhaparidze–Janssen 1994 "Abel–Goncharov interpolation problem" (PDF)

`dzhaparidze_janssen_abel-goncharov-interpolation_1994` — the deeper analytical treatment of the Abel–Goncharoff interpolation problem. The specific bound the run relies on is already extracted and claimed (`macintyre-goncaroff-bounds`, from Macintyre 1949). The DJ volume is a much longer measure-theoretic exposition; its CA-relevant content is the same Abel–Goncharoff growth machinery already captured. **Does not add a new claim**; kept as the full-text reference behind the analytic toolchain.

## Adeniran–Yan "Generalized Goncarov polynomials ... partition lattices" (arXiv:1907.07814)

`adeniran_yan_goncarov-partition-lattices_2019` is a combinatorial-enumeration paper: it realizes generalized Goncarov polynomials as weight enumerators in partition lattices, in exponential families, and shows they enumerate enriched vector parking functions (delta-operator / binomial-type framework of Lorentz–Tringali–Yan). **Does not help CA.** The run's Abel–Goncharoff use is (a) the analytic growth bounds (Macintyre 1949, claim `macintyre-goncaroff-bounds`) and (b) the interpolation-uniqueness/ordering step (Yakubovich). This paper is the *enumerative parking-function* flavour of Goncarov polynomials, which has no bearing on the derivative-sharing zero structure, the CA coefficient scheme, or the char-p break. Do not re-read.
