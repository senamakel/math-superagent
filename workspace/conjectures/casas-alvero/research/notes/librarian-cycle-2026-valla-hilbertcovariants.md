1. **Valabrega & Valla 1978, "Form rings and regular sequences", Nagoya Math. J. 72 (1978) 93–101** —
   genuine full text now held at
   `research/sources/valabrega-valla1978_form-rings-regular-sequences.full.md`
   (460 lines; verified: title page + intro + Theorem 1.1 + §2 regular-sequence
   criteria; source DOI 10.1017/S0027763000018225, Cambridge Core PDF).
   - **This closes the load-bearing gap flagged in
     `research/notes/uresultant-multiplicity-literature.md`** ("Valabrega–Valla
     1978 ... not held"). Key statements now quotable from the held text:
     - Theorem 1.1: b* = (f_1*,…,f_r*) in G_A(a) iff a^n ∩ b = Σ a^{n−p_i} f_i for all n ≥ 1 (Artin–Rees-style equality).
     - Theorem 2.3: the initial forms are a G_A(a)-sequence iff the Artin–Rees-style equality holds and A_{i−1} : f_i ⊆ A_{i−1}.
     - Corollary 2.7 (local A): f_1*,…,f_r* a G_A(a)-sequence iff f_1,…,f_r an A-sequence and a^n ∩ b = Σ a^{n−p_i} f_i for all n.
   - This is the exact source of the "multiplicity of a complete intersection =
     product of initial orders" identity the run's `uresultant-multiplicity-trees-new`
     claim cites (Valabrega–Valla; equality iff associated graded is CM).

3. **Raicu, Sam, Weyman & Yang 2026, "Powers of binary forms and derived Hermite reciprocity" (arXiv:2602.15175)** —
   genuine full text at
   `research/sources/raicu-sam-weyman-yang2026_powers-binary-forms-hermite.full.md`
   (1573 lines; verified: Theorem 1.1: for d=ab, a,b≥2, the ideal I(X_(ab)) of
