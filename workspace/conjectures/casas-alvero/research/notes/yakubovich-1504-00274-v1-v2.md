# Yakubovich arXiv:1504.00274 — v1/v2 reconciliation (same paper, two download routes)

**Finding:** the library previously held
`research/sources/yakubovich2025_validity-casas-alvero.full.md` — a download of
the **v1** PDF of arXiv:1504.00274 (1 Apr 2015), summarised under the v1 title
"The validity of the Casas-Alvero conjecture". This cycle downloaded the
**v2** (14 Aug 2015), titled "Towards the Casas-Alvero conjecture", via the
University of Porto repository mirror (`repositorio-aberto.up.pt`,
bitstream/10216/90434/2/171243.pdf), into
`research/sources/towards-casas-alvero_porto.full.md`.

They are the **same paper, two versions**: same author (S. Yakubovich), same
arXiv id, same content core (Abel–Gontcharoff/Levinson polynomials, Sz.-Nagy
identities, real-rooted CA constraints).

## Why this matters

The run's earlier note `research/notes/casas-alvero-status.md` (line ~292)
listed "arXiv:1504.00274, 2015, preprint" as one name in the claimed-proof
family. A reader holding only the v1 filename would not recognise the v2
title, and vice versa. Both are now local, cross-linked, and the version
record is explicit.

## Authoritative version

Prefer the **v2** text for statements (it is the later revision, dated
14 Aug 2015, and is the version the published J. Classical Analysis record
and later citing papers refer to). Keep v1 for the version history.

## What the paper claims (v2, held here)

- Triviality criteria for real-rooted polynomials (Props 1–3; ≥5 / ≥6 distinct
  roots under real-root hypotheses).
- Unit-circle reduction of CA (Prop 4).
- Determinantal representation of the Levinson polynomials (upper-Hessenberg);
  sharper Goncharov-type bound for Abel–Gontcharov polynomials (Lemma 1, (7),
  (8)); Sz.-Nagy type identities; Schoenberg-analogue generalisation.

## Status

Claimed-proof-family preprint; not peer-reviewed for its full-CA implications;
the ≥5-distinct-roots real-rooted statement is corroborated by held primaries
(Laterveer–Ounaïes 2012 Prop 5 — no real-root hypothesis needed). Nothing here
changes CA's standing: **open**.
