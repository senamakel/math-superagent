# Battiston arXiv:1511.04932 — withdrawn, not a survey

<!-- source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04932 -->

Correction to `problem.md` and `GOAL.md`, which call this a "survey".

The paper "The Casas-Alvero conjecture" by Giulia Battiston (arXiv:1511.04932,
16 Nov 2015 v1) is a **claimed proof**, and it was **withdrawn by the author**
on 19 Nov 2015 (v2). The withdrawal note on the arXiv abstract page reads:

> "The paper has been withdrawn as it contains a crucial error in its last
> page, I am thankful to Joseph Schicho for pointing it out to me."

So this is not a survey of the subject, and its proof does not stand — a
specific documented instance of a claimed CA proof with a located gap (last
page, flagged by J. Schicho). It is an example for the "claimed proofs attract
and must be checked" thesis, in the same family as the 2013 "Three proofs"
preprint and the Ghosh 2025 preprint.

Library status: only the abstract page (which carries the withdrawal notice)
was retrievable; the full text of v1 was withdrawn from arXiv and no PDF
remains. Full text not obtainable.
