## The canonical statement it fixes (Problem 14**, lines 421–432)

> "Let P be a univariate polynomial over a field of characteristic zero. Assume
> that each (non constant) derivative shares a divisor with P. (a) Is P a monomial
> (ax+b)^k? (b) What could be the respective statement for multivariate polynomials?"

Attached note (validating several held claims from a primary problem-list source):
- proposed by Casas-Alvero; **common divisors may a priori be different for each
  derivative** (confirms the run's reading — each i has its own shared root);
- **prime degree (and several more cases) proved by Graf von Bothmer–Labs–Schicho–
  van de Woestijne 2007** (corroborates held claim gvb-2007 and ROOT.md class 1);
- **"for positive characteristic, there exist easy counterexamples"** (corroborates
  held claims charp-false / charp-witnesses);
- **"for fixed degree, it is easy to set up a system of equations which has a
  solution over K iff there is a counter-example"** (corroborates the scheme/resultant
  agenda of this run: exactly the R_i=0 ideal / arithmetic CA scheme formulation).
