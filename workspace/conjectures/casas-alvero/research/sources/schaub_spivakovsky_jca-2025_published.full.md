## Abstract

The Casas–Alvero conjecture predicts that every univariate polynomial f over a field K of characteristic zero having a common factor with each of its derivatives H i ( f) is a power of a linear polynomial. Let f = x d + a 1 x d − 1 + ⋯ + a 1 x ∈ K [a 1, … ⁡, a d − 1] [x] and let R i = R e s ( f, H i ( f)) ∈ K [a 1, … ⁡, a d − 1] be the resultant of f and H i ( f), i ∈ { 1, … ⁡, d − 1 }. The Casas–Alvero conjecture is equivalent to saying that R 1, … ⁡, R d − 1 are independent in the sense that ht ( R 1, … ⁡, R d − 1) = d − 1 in K [a 1, … ⁡, a d − 1]. We prove a partial result in this direction: if i ∈ { d − 3, d − 2, d − 1 } then R i ∉ ( R 1, … ⁡, R ˘ i, … ⁡, R d − 1).
