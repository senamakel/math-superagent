# Title: The Casas-Alvero conjecture

Authors: [Giulia Battiston][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled The Casas-Alvero conjecture, by Giulia Battiston

No PDF available, click to view other formats

Abstract: We present a proof of the Casas-Alvero conjecture, stating that if a complex polynomial has a root in common with each of its derivatives it must be a multiple of the power of some monomial.
