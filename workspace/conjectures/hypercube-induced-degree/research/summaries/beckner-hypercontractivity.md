# Beckner, "Inequalities in Fourier Analysis" (1975) — summary

Source: https://doi.org/10.2307/1970980 (read via read_sources; full note at
research/sources/beckner-hypercontractivity-1975.md)

**Establishes:** sharp hypercontractive (norm-contraction) inequalities for the
Boolean/Gaussian semigroups — ||T_ρ f||_q <= ||f||_p for ρ² <= (p−1)/(q−1) —
giving dimension-free bounds relating a Boolean function's L^p norms, its
Fourier–Walsh spectrum, and its influences / edge sensitivity.

**Why here:** the primary source of the hypercontractivity at the core of the KKL
maximum-influence bound, the deepest Fourier technique in the influence class.
Bounds averages (norms, influences, total sensitivity), not maximum internal
degree D(S) — confirming the obstruction for the whole Fourier/influence toolset.
