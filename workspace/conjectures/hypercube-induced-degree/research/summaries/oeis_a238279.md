## What this sequence is

A triangle T(n,k) = number of compositions of n into nonzero parts with k parts
followed by a different part; equivalently compositions of n with k+1 runs.
G.f. (1 + Σ_i x^i(1−y)/(1 + y x^i − x^i)) / (1 − Σ_i y x^i/(1 + y x^i − x^i)).
