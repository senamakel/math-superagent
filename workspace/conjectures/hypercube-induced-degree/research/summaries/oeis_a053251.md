# OEIS A053251 — coefficients of 3rd-order mock theta function psi(q)

Source: https://oeis.org/A053251 (catalogue, `status: catalogued`).

## What it establishes
psi(q) = sum_{n>=1} q^(n^2)/((1-q)(1-q^3)...(1-q^(2n-1))). Terms count
partitions of n into odd parts closed downward (if a part occurs so do all
smaller odd parts); a(n) ~ exp(pi sqrt(n/6))/(4 sqrt(n)) (Kotesovec).

## Verdict
**Does not help.** Partition-theoretic (a mock theta function); no connection to
hypercube internal max degree. a(1..5)=1,1,1,2,2, not f(1..5)=1,2,2,2,3. The
sqrt in its asymptotic is a partition/plane-partition growth rate, unrelated to
the cube's A_n^2=nI.
