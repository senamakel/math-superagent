# Friedgut, "Boolean Functions With Low Average Sensitivity Depend On Few Coordinates" (1998) — summary

Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/pl00009809 (read via read_sources; full note at
research/sources/friedgut-low-average-sensitivity-1998.md)

**Establishes:** a structural theorem on the influence side — if a Boolean
function f: {−1,1}^n → {−1,1} has small average sensitivity (total influence),
then most of its influence concentrates on few coordinates (it is close to a
function depending on few coordinates). Technique: Fourier analysis +
concentration.

**Why here:** a canonical *structural* result on the influence route, distinct
from the isoperimetric sources. It confirms that low total influence (average
edge boundary) forces few-coordinate structure — but the quantity it controls is
total dependence, not the maximum internal degree D(S) of the +1-vertex excess.

**Claim:** `friedgut-low-average-sensitivity` (asserted-by-source).
