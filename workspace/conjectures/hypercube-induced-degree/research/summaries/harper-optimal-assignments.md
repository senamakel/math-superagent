# Harper, "Optimal Assignments of Numbers to Vertices" (1964) — summary

Source: https://doi.org/10.1137/0112012 (read via read_sources; full note at
research/sources/harper-optimal-assignments-1964.md)

**Establishes:** the edge-isoperimetric theorem for the hypercube Q_N: for each
set size m, the edge boundary |∂_e(S)| is minimised by the initial segment I_m
of the binary (coordinate-wise) numbering — equivalently, the edge-minimal sets
are the binary-order initial segments (subcube-like / shadow-closed families).
Proof by compression/shifting.

**Why here:** canonical primary source of the cube edge-isoperimetric
inequality, the first of the four "classical techniques" problem.md names. It
bounds the outer edge boundary (an average/total quantity), not the maximum
internal degree D(S) — confirming the obstruction that isoperimetric profiles
cannot reach f(n).
