# A007895 — Number of terms in the Zeckendorf representation of n

Source: https://oeis.org/A007895 (OEIS catalogue lookup)

## What this sequence is

a(n) = the number of (non-consecutive, distinct) Fibonacci numbers in the
Zeckendorf representation of n. Equivalent to A000120(A003714(n)) and to the
number of 0s in the Wythoff representation. Growth is ~ log_φ(n) but highly
irregular (0,1,1,1,2,1,2,2,1,2,2,2,3,1,...).

## Verdict for this problem: does not help

- **Not the sequence f(n).** Terms on n=0..5 (0,1,1,1,2,1) do not match
  f(1..5)=1,2,2,2,3, and the object counted (Zeckendorf term-count) has no
  connection to hypercube internal degree D(S).
- It is catalogue noise fetched by an over-eager closed-form lookup. No
  hypothesis links it to S ⊆ {0,1}^n with |S|=2^{n-1}+1.
- Contradicts nothing in durable memory; it is orthogonal.
