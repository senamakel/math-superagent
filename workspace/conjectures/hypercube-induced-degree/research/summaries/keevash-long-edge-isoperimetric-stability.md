# Keevash–Long, "Stability of the cube edge isoperimetric inequality" (2017) — summary

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2017.11.005 (read via read_sources; full
note at research/sources/keevash-long-edge-isoperimetric-stability-2017.md)

**Establishes:** a quantitative stability theorem for the cube edge-isoperimetric
inequality: a set A ⊆ {0,1}^n whose edge boundary is within ε of the minimum for
its size is structurally close (distance → 0 as ε → 0) to a subcube or a small
perturbation of one. Technique: compression/monotonicity + averaging.

**Why here:** a genuine *technique* extension of the edge-isoperimetric theorem —
the edge-boundary companion to the vertex-stability Keevash–Long (1807.09618)
already in the library. Near-minimal edge boundary = low average internal
degree, not low max degree, so it confirms the obstruction while describing the
edge-near-minimal extremal shape (subcube-like).
