# OEIS A202453 — Fibonacci self-fusion matrix

Source: https://oeis.org/A202453 (catalogue, `status: catalogued`).

## What it establishes
A 2-D triangular array: F(n,k) = F(n)·F(k+1) if k even, F(n+1)·F(k) if k odd
(products of Fibonacci numbers) — the fusion of the lower/upper triangular
Fibonacci matrices P**Q. Entries of the inverse lie in {-3..3}.

## Verdict
**Does not help.** It is a 2-D Fibonacci matrix array, not a 1-D sequence, so it
cannot be indexed directly against the 1-D f(n) (the OEIS listing reads it by
antidiagonals). No connection to hypercube internal degree D(S). An OEIS lookup
mismatch.
