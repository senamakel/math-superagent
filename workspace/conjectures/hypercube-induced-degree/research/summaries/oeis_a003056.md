# OEIS A003056 — inverse triangular numbers; n appears n+1 times

Source: https://oeis.org/A003056 (catalogue, `status: catalogued`).

## What it establishes
Exact closed form a(n) = floor((sqrt(1+8n)-1)/2), the inverse of the triangular
numbers (largest k with k(k+1)/2 <= n). G.f. (Theta_2(0,x^{1/2}) - 2x^{1/8})/(2x^{1/8}(1-x)).

## Verdict
**Does not help — a sqrt-shaped red herring.** a(1..5) = 1,1,2,2,2, which does
not match f(1..5)=1,2,2,2,3. It looks relevant because it is ~sqrt(2n), but f(n)'s
sqrt growth comes from a genuinely different source: the quadratic/Hadamard
relation A_n^2 = n·I (a signed-adjacency identity), not from an inverse-triangular
index. The surface similarity is coincidence.
