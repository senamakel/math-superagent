# OEIS A002264 — floor(n/3), nonnegative integers repeated three times

Source: https://oeis.org/A002264 (catalogue, `status: catalogued`).

## What it establishes
Exact closed form a(n) = floor(n/3); g.f. x^3/((1-x)(1-x^3)). Only relevance
here is the art-gallery (Chvátal) interpretation: a(n) visibility points. It is
a linear-counting sequence, nothing to do with the hypercube.

## Verdict
**Does not help.** a(1..5) = 0,0,1,1,1, which does not match this run's exact
f(1..5) = 1,2,2,2,3 = ceil(sqrt(n)). It grows linearly; no hypothesis links it
to internal max degree D(S). Filed by an over-eager "find a closed form" lookup;
this is not the sequence f(n) is.
