Record: Mangerel (arXiv:2404.12117, IMRN 2024) proved |Σ_{n<N} λ(n)λ(N−n)| < N−1 for all sufficiently large N, answering the 2018 AIM Sarnak-conjecture question. Mangerel (arXiv:2412.17199) proved under GRH that every sign pattern (η1,η2) occurs ≫ N exp(−C(log log N)^6) times among n<N for prime N, in particular λ(a)=λ(b)=−1 solutions to a+b=N.

Bearing: these are analogues with the same additive equation but a multiplicative sign function in place of primality. They show the obstruction to binary Goldbach is not the additive equation itself but the primality predicate. Neither paper transfers to primes.
