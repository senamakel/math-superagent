Record: Goldston–Suriajaya (Nagoya Math. J. 250 (2023) 511–532) made Fujii's average Goldbach formula unconditional (previously RH-conditional). Pintz (arXiv:1804.05561) gave an explicit major-arc formula at level P_0 ≤ X^{4/9−ε}. Bhowmik–Grimmelt (to appear Anal. Math.) give a fully explicit major-arc formula in the exceptional-set problem. Brüdern–Kaczorowski–Perelli (TAMS 372 (2019) 6981–6999; arXiv:1712.00737, claim `bkp-cesaro-riesz-explicit-formula`) proved an explicit formula for the Cesàro–Riesz means of any order k>0 of Goldbach representation counts. (Note: the file `brudern-kaczorowski-perelli-...TAMS-2019.full.md` is a misnamed download of an unrelated withdrawn note; the BKP text is the `bkp-...-1712.00737.full.md` file.)

Bearing: average identities connect representation counts to zeros of ζ(s) and are tools for the R-density-delta / R-sharpen-delta rungs; none resolves the every-n statement.
