Record: Tsuda (Monatsh. Math. 206 (2025) 217–258) proved for almost all even N, primes p,p′ with N−p, N−p′ prime and |p−p′| < 0.8201·S(N)^(−1)(log N)^2. Akeno (arXiv:2508.02769) improved the multiplier to 0.76542 via the Bombieri–Davenport method, and showed a straightforward Maynard–Tao application cannot improve it further (though it yields bounded gaps with bounded error). Akeno (arXiv:2606.29559) gives level of distribution 1/6 for Goldbach primes.

Bearing: almost-all structural information about where Goldbach representations sit inside the representation set; does not resolve every-n Goldbach, but constrains how the exceptional set could be arranged if it were nonempty.
