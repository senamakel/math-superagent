## Not obtained (recorded gaps)

- **Chen 1973 original** (Scientia Sinica 16, 157–176): paywalled at SciEngine; the World Scientific Goldbach Conjecture reprint (Wang ed.) is the accessible form. Exact statement verified via two independent secondary sources.
- **Montgomery–Vaughan 1975 full text**: only a textless scan exists at matwbn.icm.edu.pl; content quoted in Pintz 1804.09084 §1.
- **Lu 2010 "Exceptional set of Goldbach numbers"** (J. Number Theory 130, 2359–2392): JNT paywall, no arXiv; the best *published* exceptional-set exponent E(X)<X^0.879 (δ=0.121) rests on abstract + corroboration from Pintz 2018 and Bhowmik–Halupczok (both on disk).
- **Salmensuu 2022 "The Goldbach conjecture with summands in arithmetic progressions"** (Q. J. Math.): paywalled, no free arXiv full text; a genuine restricted-class result, statement corroborated via Huixi Li 2023 survey (on disk) and search abstracts.
- **Oliveira e Silva et al. 2014 AMS PDF**: AMS rate-limits downloads (HTTP 429); the author's page and the paper's abstract document the result fully.
- **Granville corrigendum** (Funct. Approx. 38 (2008) 235–237): bibliographic record only, not downloaded.
- **Brüdern–Kaczorowski–Perelli TAMS 2019** — the file `brudern-kaczorowski-perelli-...TAMS-2019.full.md` is a misnamed download of Saha's withdrawn arXiv:1802.10562; the true BKP paper is at `bkp-explicit-formulae-averages-goldbach-representations-arxiv-1712.00737.full.md` (arXiv:1712.00737, abstract page on disk).
