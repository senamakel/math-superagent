**Statement, corroborated by secondary sources (Languasco 1995 PhD thesis via
hdl.handle.net/11577/122132, and the search-index excerpts):** for arbitrary
ε > 0, A > 0 and N^{7/48+ε} < H < N, the number of even integers in [N, N+H]
that are not sums of two primes satisfies

  E(N, H) ≪_A H·L^{-A},  L = log N.

This was the best unconditional short-interval exceptional-set bound at the
time (improving Perelli–Pintz's 7/36), later improved by Jia (7/78), Li (7/81),
and Jia (1/12) — see the Jia 1996 Acta Arith. 77 (1996) 207–287 excerpt in
search results. The method is Linnik's dispersion method combined with sieve
estimates.

Relevance: short-interval exceptional-set results are the local-density
counterpart of the global E(X) < X^{1−δ} line; they say nothing about the
location of a hypothetical minimal counterexample, but they bound how many
exceptions can cluster in a short interval.
