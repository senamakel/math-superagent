# Connection between Goldbach and Elliott–Halberstam

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-67996-5_17 (Huang and Li, 2021), full text at `research/sources/chen-parity-eh-goldbach-2021.full.md`.

## Verified claim
The binary Goldbach conjecture for all sufficiently large even integers follows from two distribution hypotheses: Elliott–Halberstam with level θ and a Möbius-twisted Elliott–Halberstam variant with level θ′, when θ+θ′>1. This is a conditional theorem, continuing Pan’s work.

## Relevance and limitation
The result identifies a precise route past the sieve parity obstruction, but neither hypothesis is known. Ordinary GRH is not the stated hypothesis and must not be substituted for it. The implication is stronger than an almost-all theorem only conditionally; it does not prove the conjecture unconditionally. Full text: `research/sources/chen-parity-eh-goldbach-2021.full.md`.
