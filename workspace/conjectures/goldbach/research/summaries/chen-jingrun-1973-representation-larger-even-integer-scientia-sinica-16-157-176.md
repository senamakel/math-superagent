# Chen's Theorem on Even Integers

This paper proves that every sufficiently large even integer can be expressed as the sum of a prime and the product of at most two primes. The author employs a straightforward method without complex numerical calculations to demonstrate this proposition. Additionally, the paper references previous works and provides detailed proofs for the stated theorems.
