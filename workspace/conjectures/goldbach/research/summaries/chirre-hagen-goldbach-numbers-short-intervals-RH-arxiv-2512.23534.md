## What it claims

Abstract: Assuming the Riemann Hypothesis, we prove that for all $x\geq 2$, there exists at least one even integer within the interval $(x, x+123\log^2x]$, that can be expressed as the sum of two primes. This result is an improvement over the recent work of Cully-Hugill and Dudek, who obtained the constant $9696$ instead of $123$.
