**J Chen** (1980). unpublished or preprint. Cited 40 times.

## Abstract

Let us call Goldbach-numbers the even integers which can be represented as a sum of two odd primes. Suppose that E(X) denotes the number of even integers not exceeding X, which are not Goldbach-numbers. In this paper, E(X) = O(X~(0.96)) is proved true.
