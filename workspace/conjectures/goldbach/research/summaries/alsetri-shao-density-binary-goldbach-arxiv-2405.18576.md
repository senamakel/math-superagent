# Density versions of the binary Goldbach problem

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18576 (Ali Alsetri and Xuancheng Shao, revised 19 Sep 2024; to appear in Acta Arith.)

## Verified claim
Let A be a subset of the primes whose relative density in every reduced residue class is at least δ>1/2. Then almost all even integers are sums of two elements of A. The threshold 1/2 is sharp: for every ε>0 there is a subset A of the primes with relative density at least 1−ε whose sumset misses a positive proportion of even integers.

## Relevance and limitation
This is a restricted-prime, almost-all theorem, not the ordinary binary Goldbach conjecture: taking A as all primes gives only an almost-all statement, while the theorem’s sharpness example shows that density alone cannot force an all-n conclusion for arbitrary subsets. The result is a clean structural warning against replacing prime distribution hypotheses by global density. Full text: `research/sources/alsetri-shao-density-binary-goldbach-arxiv-2405.18576.full.md`.
