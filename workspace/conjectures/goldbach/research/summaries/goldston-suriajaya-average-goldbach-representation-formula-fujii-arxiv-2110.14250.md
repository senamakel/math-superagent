# Goldston–Suriajaya (2023), Fujii average formula
[[goldston-suriajaya-average-goldbach-representation-formula-fujii-arxiv-2110.14250.full]]

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14250 (v2, 20 Dec 2022; Nagoya Math. J. 250 (2023) 511–532).

```claim
id: goldston-suriajaya-fujii-unconditional
statement: Fujii's formula for the average number of Goldbach representations, whose lower-order terms are a sum over zeros of ζ(s) plus a smaller error term, holds unconditionally (Fujii had assumed RH). Conditional applications follow under various conjectures on the zeros of ζ(s).
hypotheses: none beyond the average over representations; conditional applications assume zero conjectures.
holds-here: yes
status: asserted
bearing: An average-order identity for Goldbach representations tied to the zeros of ζ(s). It cannot resolve the binary conjecture (it averages over n), but it is the sharpest average formula of its kind and connects representation counts to the zero structure of ζ(s) — a tool for the R-density-delta rung's minor-arc analysis rather than a proof of every-n.
anchor: research/sources/goldston-suriajaya-average-goldbach-representation-formula-fujii-arxiv-2110.14250.full.md
```

This closes a gap the library had (the Fujii formula was previously only available under RH). The paper also draws applications conditional on zero conjectures such as the Gonek–Hejhal conjecture.
