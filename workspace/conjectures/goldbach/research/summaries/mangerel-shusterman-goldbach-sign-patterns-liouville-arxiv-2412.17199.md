# Mangerel (2024), Shusterman sign patterns
[[mangerel-shusterman-goldbach-sign-patterns-liouville-arxiv-2412.17199.full]]

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17199 (v1, 23 Dec 2024).

This is the companion note to `mangerel-goldbach-type-problem-liouville-function-arxiv-2404.12117.md`; its claim block is recorded there (`mangerel-shusterman-sign-patterns`), with full statement: under GRH, every sufficiently large even N has a,b with a+b=N and λ(a)=λ(b)=−1, and every sign pattern occurs ≫ N exp(−C(log log N)^6) times among n<N for prime N.

Key point for this run: the result is GRH-conditional and concerns the Liouville function, not primes. It is a sign-pattern analogue of binary Goldbach, useful as a benchmark for what the true Goldbach statement would need (all sign patterns occur with positive density), but it neither proves nor contradicts the conjecture. Method: Pierce expansion of n/N.
