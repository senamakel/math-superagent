## Description

***The Goldbach Conjecture states that every even integer ≥ 4 can be written as a sum of two prime numbers. It is known to be true for all even numbers up to 4 × 10 18 [1]. Using the new formulation of a set of even numbers as [9], and the fact that, an even number of this formulation can be partitioned into all pairs of odd numbers [10], we present a computational algorithm that confirms the Strong Goldbach Conjecture holds true for all even numbers not larger than 9 × 10 18.***
