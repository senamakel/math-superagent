# Tsuda (2025), small gaps between Goldbach primes
[[tsuda-small-gaps-primes-satisfy-goldbach-equation-monatshefte-2024.full]]

Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00605-024-02032-3 (Monatsh. Math. 206 (2025) 217–258).

```claim
id: tsuda-2025-small-gaps-goldbach-primes
statement: For almost all even N, there exist primes p,p′ with N−p, N−p′ prime and |p−p′| < 0.8201...·S(N)^(−1)(log N)^2, where S(N) is the singular series for Goldbach.
hypotheses: N even large; almost-all N.
holds-here: yes
status: asserted
bearing: The Bombieri–Davenport-method predecessor to Akeno's 0.76542 bound on small gaps between Goldbach primes. It quantifies how densely the Goldbach primes sit near each other inside the representation set for almost all N — an almost-all structural result, not an every-n proof. Superseded by Akeno 2025 for the constant.

```
