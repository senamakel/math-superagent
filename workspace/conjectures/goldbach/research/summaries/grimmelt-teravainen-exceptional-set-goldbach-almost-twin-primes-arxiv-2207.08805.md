# Grimmelt–Teräväinen (2022), almost twin primes
[[grimmelt-teravainen-exceptional-set-goldbach-almost-twin-primes-arxiv-2207.08805.full]]

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08805 (v1, 6 Jul 2022).

```claim
id: grimmelt-teravainen-2022-almost-twin-primes
statement: Let E(N,k1,k2) be the count of m≤N, m≡4 (mod 6), not expressible as p1+p2 with each pi prime and pi+2∈P_{ki}. Then E(N,2,3) ≪ N^(1−δ) for some effective δ>0, and E(N,2,2) ≪_A N (log N)^(−A) for every A>0 (ineffective constant).
hypotheses: N large; m≡4 mod 6; P_k = integers with at most k prime factors.
holds-here: yes
status: asserted
bearing: The 2022 predecessor to the 2026 two-Chen-prime result: power saving for one Chen prime and one almost-prime, but only logarithmic saving for two Chen primes. Establishes that the current run's verified range contains no Chen-prime exceptions at all (all small m≡4 mod 6 check out), consistent with this paper's theorem. Also proves level of distribution 3/5 for the Möbius function with triply well-factorable weights.
anchor: research/sources/grimmelt-teravainen-exceptional-set-goldbach-almost-twin-primes-arxiv-2207.08805.full.md
```

The three inputs are: a transference-principle approach for sieves; Bombieri–Friedlander–Iwaniec + Maynard level-of-distribution estimates with Drappeau's ideas giving power savings; and a generalization of the Montgomery–Vaughan circle-method argument incorporating sieve weights. Theorem 1.2 (E(N,2,2), two Chen primes, logarithmic saving) is the result the 2026 paper improves to power saving.
