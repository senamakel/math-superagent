# Kumchev–Tolev, *An invitation to additive prime number theory*

Source URL: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/math/0412220 (arXiv:math/0412220).

## Verified claims

- The survey states the binary Goldbach problem and distinguishes it from ternary Goldbach.
- Chen's theorem is stated in the standard form: every sufficiently large even integer is p+P₂, where p is prime and P₂ has at most two prime factors counted with multiplicity. The survey also gives a quantitative lower bound for the number of such representations in terms of the Goldbach singular series and n/(log n)^2.
- The survey explains that sieve methods reach this almost-prime conclusion rather than two primes; it is therefore a source for the historical near-miss, not a proof of binary Goldbach.
