# Chirre–Hagen (2025), Goldbach numbers in short intervals under RH
[[chirre-hagen-goldbach-numbers-short-intervals-RH-arxiv-2512.23534.html.full]]

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.23534v1 (Dec 2025).

**Canonical claim already filed** in `research/notes/claims-post2020-state-of-the-art.md` as `chirre-hagen-short-interval-RH-123`. This note is the per-source digest.

Theorem 1: assuming RH, for all x ≥ 2 there is at least one even integer in (x, x + 123·log²x] expressible as a sum of two primes. Improves Cully-Hugill–Dudek's constant 9696 to 123.

Method: explicit formula for ψ(x) (Theorem 2: explicit bound for J_ψ(x,δ)) plus Saffari–Vaughan averaging; the delicate step is passing from J_ψ(x,δ) to J_θ(x,δ) (primes in short intervals, Theorem 3). Works directly with zeros of ζ(s) under RH.

Bearing: a conditional (RH) restricted-class result on the *location* of Goldbach numbers — under RH, counterexamples provably cannot cluster: every short interval of length 123·log²x contains a Goldbach number. It is an existence statement in short intervals, not an every-n proof and not a density bound on E(X). Consistent with the ledger claim; abstract+outline on disk (full HTML text at the source link).
