# Pintz, *A new explicit formula in the additive theory of primes with applications II: The exceptional set in Goldbach's problem*

Source URL: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1804.09084 (arXiv:1804.09084).

## Verified claims

- Let E(X) count even integers n≤X that are not sums of two primes. The introduction records the historical unconditional progression: Čudakov–Estermann–van der Corput give E(X)≪_A X/(log X)^A; Vaughan gives E(X)≪X exp(-c√log X); Montgomery–Vaughan give E(X)≪_δ X^(1−δ) for an explicitly calculable δ>0; Chen–Liu obtain δ=0.05; Li obtains δ=0.079 and later E(X)≪X^0.914; Lu obtains E(X)≪X^0.879, all latter published-history claims as reported by Pintz.
- Pintz's paper is a preprint and develops an explicit-formula method intended to improve the exponent; its stated Theorem 1 and surrounding text must be used for any precise current preprint exponent. It is not evidence that binary Goldbach is true for every n.
- The source explicitly distinguishes an exceptional-set estimate from the conjecture: E(X)=0 is required for the full conjecture, whereas every power-saving bound still permits exceptions.

## Status and falsifier

These are asserted-by-source claims, not formalised proofs in this workspace. Falsifier: the full theorem text or a later correction changes the definition of E(X), the historical exponents, or the publication status.
