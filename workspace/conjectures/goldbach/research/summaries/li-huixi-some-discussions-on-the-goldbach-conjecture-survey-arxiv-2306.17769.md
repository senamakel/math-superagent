## What it claims

Abstract: According to some discussions based on syllogism, we present results on the binary Goldbach conjecture in three categories: results that are weaker than the Goldbach conjecture, sufficient conditions for the Goldbach conjecture, and results that are similar in nature to the Goldbach conjecture. Additionally, we explore the connections between the Goldbach conjecture and other well-known conjectures.
