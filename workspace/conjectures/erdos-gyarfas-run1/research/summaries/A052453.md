A052453

Number of nonisomorphic (3,n) cage graphs.

9

1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 18, 3, 1, 1

( [list][4]; [graph][5]; [refs][6]; [listen][7]; [history][8]; [text][9]; [internal format][10])

OFFSET

3,7

COMMENTS

A (3,n) cage graph is a 3-regular (or cubic, or trivalent) graph which has girth n, and has the fewest possible number of vertices. - [Harry Richman][11], Jan 14 2025

LINKS

[Table of n, a(n) for n=3..12.][12]

Andries E. Brouwer, [Cages][13]

Geoffrey Exoo, [Regular graphs of given degree and girth][14].

Geoffrey Exoo and Robert Jajcay, [Dynamic cage survey][15], Electr. J. Combin. (2008, 2011).

Gordon Royle, [Cages of higher valency][16]

Eric Weisstein's World of Mathematics, [Cage Graph][17] (claims too much)

Wikipedia, [Cage (graph theory)][18]

EXAMPLE

a(3) = 1 since the complete graph K_4 is the unique smallest cubic graph with girth 3.

a(5) = 1 since the Petersen graph is the unique smallest cubic graph with girth 5.

a(12) = 1 from the unique generalized hexagon of order 2.
