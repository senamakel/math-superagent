**J. A. Bondy, Andrew Vince** (1998). Journal of Graph Theory. Cited 33 times.

## Abstract

Several problems concerning the distribution of cycle lengths in a graph have been proposed by P. Erdös and colleagues. In this note two variations of the following such question are answered. In a simple graph where every vertex has degree at least three, must there exist two cycles whose lengths differ by one or two? © 1998 John Wiley & Sons, Inc. J Graph Theory 27: 11–15, 1998
