# Ghaffari & Mostaghim — E-G for some Cayley graphs

Source: Aequationes Math. (2018), arXiv/link.springer "Erdős–Gyárfás
conjecture for some families of Cayley graphs". Landing page / abstract held;
[[ghaffari-mostaghim-cayley.full]].

## Establishes

The conjecture holds for Cayley graphs on: **generalized quaternion groups,
dihedral groups, semidihedral groups, and groups of order p^3**.

## For this problem

A settled restricted class, but of limited structural value for a minimal
counterexample (Cayley graphs are highly symmetric and regular; a minimal CE
is predominantly but not exactly cubic and certainly not assumed vertex-
transitive). Records the class for completeness of ROOT.md.
