# A002218 — number of 2-connected (nonseparable) simple graphs

Source: OEIS A002218. Terms (n=1..): 0,1,1,3,10,56,468,7123,194066,...

## What this catalogue is for this run

This is the ground-truth count of **unlabeled 2-connected (nonseparable)
simple graphs on n vertices** (Robinson's convention includes K2, excludes
K1; for n>2 it equals the number of simple biconnected graphs).

It is the catalogue the run's 2-connected generator is checked against. The
ear-decomposition generator `code/lib/biconnected_gen_hash.py` reproduces
`1,3,10,56,468,7123` at n=3..8 (A002218), and `code/lib/canonical.py`'s
canonical-key counts cross-check against these. This is a catalogued
relationship the oracle uses: reproducing A002218 terms with the generator
(without reading the catalogue) is the validation of the 2-connected
enumeration.

## For this problem

2-connected graphs are the natural class for cycle-search arguments (every
cycle lies in a 2-connected subgraph; a minimal counterexample's blocks are
2-connected). The catalogue makes the generator's exactness checkable. Not
itself a result about powers of two — a counting tool for the enumeration
side, not a structural input.
