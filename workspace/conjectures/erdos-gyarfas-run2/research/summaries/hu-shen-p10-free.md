# Hu & Shen 2023 — P10-free graphs

Source: arXiv:2308.05675 "Erdős-Gyárfás Conjecture for P10-free Graphs".
Full text held as landing page / abstract only; [[hu-shen-p10-free.full]].

## Establishes

**Theorem:** every P10-free graph (no induced path on 10 vertices) with
minimum degree ≥ 3 contains a cycle of length 4 or 8. Hence the conjecture
holds for P10-free graphs. Published in Discrete Mathematics 2024 (per Hegde
et al.).

## For this problem

Settled restricted class (P10-free ⊇ P8-free ⊇ P13-free ⊇ P12-free... wait,
direction): P10-free is a *larger* class than P8-free (fewer forbidden
configurations), so this strictly extends Gao–Shan. Still conclusion bounded
(C4 or C8).
