# Salehi Nowbandegani et al. — claw-free graphs

Source: arXiv:1109.5398 "On the Erdős-Gyárfás conjecture in claw-free
graphs". Full text held as landing page / abstract only;
[[salehi-etal-clawfree.full]].

## Establishes

Hobbs asked whether E–G holds in claw-free graphs. The authors obtain results
on this question, in particular for **cubic claw-free** graphs. (Abstract
does not assert the full claw-free case; the settled claw-free result is due
elsewhere: Daniel–Shauger proved **planar claw-free**, Shauger proved
K_{1,m}-free — see West.)

## For this problem

Note the distinction the sources force: the *general* claw-free case is
NOT recorded here as settled; what is settled is **claw-free + planar**
(Daniel–Shauger) and **K_{1,m}-free** (Shauger). This is a partial, and it is
the weakest of the settled-class contributions for the structural attack.
