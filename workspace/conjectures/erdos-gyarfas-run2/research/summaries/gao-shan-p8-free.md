# Gao & Shan 2021 — P8-free graphs

Source: arXiv:2109.01277 "Erdős-Gyárfás Conjecture for P8-free graphs".
Full text held as landing page / abstract only; [[gao-shan-p8-free.full]].

## Establishes

**Theorem:** every P8-free graph (no induced path on 8 vertices) with minimum
degree ≥ 3 contains a cycle of length 4 or 8. Hence the conjecture holds for
P8-free graphs.
