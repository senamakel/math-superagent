# Citation graph — On q-power cycles in cubic graphs

**Julien Bensmail** (2016). Discussiones Mathematicae Graph Theory. Cited 3 times.

## Abstract

In the context of a conjecture of Erds and Gyrfs, we consider, for any q 2, the existence of q-power cycles (i.e., with length a power of q) in cubic graphs. We exhibit constructions showing that, for every q 3, there exist arbitrarily large cubic graphs with no q-power cycles. Concerning the remaining case q = 2 (which corresponds to the conjecture of Erds and Gyrfs), we show that there exist arbitrarily large cubic graphs whose all 2-power cycles have length 4 only, or 8 only.
