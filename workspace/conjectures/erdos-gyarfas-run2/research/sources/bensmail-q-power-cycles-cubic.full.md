# INVALID DOWNLOAD — do not cite as Bensmail's q-power paper

This file was downloaded from `https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05567` believing it
was Julien Bensmail's "On q-power cycles in cubic graphs". **It is not.** arXiv
1508.05567 resolves to an unrelated paper about dual-based approximation
algorithms for network connectivity problems (Minimum 2-Edge-Connected
Spanning Subgraph, Dual Power Assignment, etc.). No part of this document
concerns cubic graphs, Erdős–Gyárfás, or q-power cycles.

The correct result is Bensmail, *Discussiones Mathematicae Graph Theory*
37(1):211–220 (2016), doi:10.7151/dmgt.1926, which is paywalled. Its statement
is sourced from the DMGT abstract and corroborated by Couch–Daniel–Wright
(Australas. J. Combin. 79 (2021) 100-), Pirzada–Shah–Baskoro (EJGTA 2022), and
Madaras–Tamásová (Opuscula Math. 38 (2018)):

- For every q ≥ 3, arbitrarily large cubic graphs with **no** q-power cycle.
- For q = 2, arbitrarily large cubic graphs whose all 2-power cycles have
  length **4 only**, or **8 only**.
