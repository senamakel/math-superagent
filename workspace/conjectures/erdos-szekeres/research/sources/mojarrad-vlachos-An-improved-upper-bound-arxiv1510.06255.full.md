# Title: An improved upper bound for the Erdős-Szekeres conjecture

Authors: [Hossein Nassajian Mojarrad][3], [Georgios Vlachos][4]

View a PDF of the paper titled An improved upper bound for the Erd\H{o}s-Szekeres conjecture, by Hossein Nassajian Mojarrad and 1 other authors

[View PDF][5] [HTML (experimental)][6]

Abstract: Let $ES(n)$ denote the minimum natural number such that every set of $ES(n)$ points in general position in the plane contains $n$ points in convex position. In 1935, Erdős and Szekeres proved that $ES(n) \le {2n-4 \choose n-2}+1$. In 1961, they obtained the lower bound $2^{n-2}+1 \le ES(n)$, which they conjectured to be optimal. In this paper, we prove that $$ES(n) \le {2n-5 \choose n-2}-{2n-8 \choose n-3}+2 \approx \frac{7}{16} {2n-4 \choose n-2}.$$
