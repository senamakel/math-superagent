# Title: Erdős-Szekeres without induction

Authors: [Sergey Norin][2], [Yelena Yuditsky][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled Erd\H{o}s-Szekeres without induction, by Sergey Norin and Yelena Yuditsky

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: Let $ES(n)$ be the minimal integer such that any set of $ES(n)$ points in the plane in general position contains $n$ points in convex position. The problem of estimating $ES(n)$ was first formulated by Erdős and Szekeres, who proved that $ES(n) \leq \binom{2n-4}{n-2}+1$. The current best upper bound, $\lim\sup_{n \to \infty} \frac{ES(n)}{\binom{2n-5}{n-2}}\le \frac{29}{32}$, is due to Vlachos. We improve this to $$\lim\sup_{n \to \infty} \frac{ES(n)}{\binom{2n-5}{n-2}}\le \frac{7}{8}.$$
