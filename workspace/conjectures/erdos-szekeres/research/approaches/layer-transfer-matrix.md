REALIZATION-INDEPENDENCE (the crux that grounds it — corrects the earlier placement hazard):
The onion-layer partition is an ORDER-TYPE INVARIANT. A point p is on the convex hull of X iff p lies in the interior of no triangle of three other points; "p inside triangle(a,b,c)" is decided by the signs of the three orientation determinants det(b-a, c-a), det(c-b, p-b), det(a-c, p-c), i.e. by triple-orientation (chirotope) data. Hull membership therefore depends only on the order type, and peeling inductively makes every layer order-type-determined. Similarly, the DP transition "which points of layer i+1 lie in the forward cone spanned by the last two points of the current chain" is a radial-order question about one pivot point, which is also triple-orientation data (the circular sequence around a point is an invariant). Hence the WHOLE automaton — state space, layer partition, cone capacities, and the implied max total points — is a function of the order type alone, so a bound it yields is realization-independent and can carry a universal ES upper bound. What research correctly established is only that the layers are NOT the recursive binomial blocks; that refutes the Gale/block route (gale-transform-convex-layers), which needed layers = blocks, and is exactly the premise THIS route does not use.
