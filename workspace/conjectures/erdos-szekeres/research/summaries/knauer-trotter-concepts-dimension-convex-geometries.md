## What the held text actually establishes

Abstract only: a convex geometry (family P of subsets of a finite set X closed under
intersection and with the anti-exchange/accessibility axiom (3)) carries six dimension
parameters — VC-dimension, convex dimension (Edelman–Saks), Dushnik–Miller dimension, Boolean
dimension, local dimension, fractional dimension. The paper investigates, for each pair of the
six, whether there is an infinite class of convex geometries on which one parameter is bounded
and the other is not.
