**Bottom line for the run.** Horton (1983) constructs, for every $k$, a
$2^k$-point set $S_k = \{(i,\sum_j a_j c^{j-1})\}$, $c=2^k+1$, with no **empty**
convex 7-gon; hence $g(n)$ does not exist for $n\ge 7$ and $g(6)$ is open. This
is the empty-*side* analogue of the ES 1961 lower-bound construction (same
digit-coded, recursively self-similar staircase shape) and is structural context
for the [[extremal-structure]] thread — it does **not** bear on the ES(n)
convex-position conjecture and is kept out of Established per GOAL.md's ban on
adjacent results.
