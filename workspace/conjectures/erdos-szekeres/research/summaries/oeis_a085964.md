## Formula

- P(4) = Sum_{p prime} 1/p^4 = Sum_{n>=1} mobius(n)*log(zeta(4*n))/n
- Equals A086034 + A085993 + 1/16. - _R. J. Mathar_, Jul 22 2010
- Equals Sum_{k>=1} 1/A030514(k). - _Amiram Eldar_, Jul 27 2020
