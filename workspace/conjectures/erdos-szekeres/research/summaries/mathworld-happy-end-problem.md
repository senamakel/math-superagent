# MathWorld, "Happy End Problem"

Source: https://mathworld.wolfram.com/HappyEndProblem.html
Full text: [[mathworld-happy-end-problem.full]]

## What it establishes

An encyclopedic restatement of the happy-ending problem. Cross-checks the small values:
g(3)=3 (any 3 non-collinear points form a triangle), g(4)=5 (Klein: any 5 points contain a
convex quadrilateral; 4 do not — the 5th and 8th arrangements in the figure have no convex
quad). Describes the convex-hull case analysis (hull a quad/pentagon → trivial; hull a
triangle with 2 interior points → pick a side + 2 interior points).

## Value to the run

None beyond cross-checking ES(3)=3, ES(4)=5 against the primary source (Erdős–Szekeres 1935).
Not a source of any new theorem; trust the primary and survey for the actual mathematics.
