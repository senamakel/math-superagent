**Bottom line.** $h(6)=30$: every 30-point planar general-position set contains
an **empty** convex hexagon (a convex 6 points with no point inside). This is the
Erdős–Szekeres–Horton **empty-hexagon** problem — GOAL.md marks it **adjacent**,
NOT the ES(6)=17 convex-position result (which does not require emptiness). Keep
the two apart; the reusable value is the compact O(n⁴)-clause encoding and
search-space partitioning, not the number itself.
