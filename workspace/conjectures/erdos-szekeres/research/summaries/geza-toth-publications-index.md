# G. Tóth publication index (beacon to primary EPS/PDFs of ES-related papers)

<!-- source: http://www.cs.bme.hu/~geza/pub.html | full text: research/sources/geza-toth-publications-index.full.md -->
Author-hosted publication index of Géza Tóth (Rényi Institute). Held as a *beacon*:
it is not a result-bearing source itself, but it carries the author copies of several
primary papers the run's library otherwise has only via paywalled journals.

Notable items (item [link-index] → author PDF URL):
- **[24]** Károlyi & Tóth, "An Erdős-Szekeres-type problem in the plane", Periodica
  Math. Hungar. 39 (1999) 153–159 — defines f(k,n), smallest m so m points force n
  whose hull has ≥ k vertices; bounds c₁kn + 2^{c₂k}.
- **[66]** Károlyi & Tóth, "Erdős-Szekeres theorem for point sets with forbidden
  subconfigurations", DCG 48 (2012) 441–452. The page's linked PDF
  (`neohorton.pdf`) is **stale/404**; the full text was instead obtained from the
  Springer article page at
  `research/sources/karolyi-toth-2012-ES-forbidden-subconfigurations-springer.full.md`.
- **[15]** Pach & Tóth, "Erdős-Szekeres-type theorems for segments and non-crossing
  convex sets", Geom. Dedicata 81 (2000).
- The page carries many further ES-adjacent papers with live author PDFs.

Note: author-PDF URLs on this page are occasionally stale (as `neohorton.pdf` proved
for item [66]). A listed URL is a lead, not a verified fetch — verify any file's
content matches its intended paper before citing it, per the library's
MIS-DOWNLOAD lesson.
