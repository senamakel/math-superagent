The request asked for the full text or a faithful exposition of Balko–Valtr, "A SAT
attack on the Erdős–Szekeres conjecture", for the run's triple-orientation-variable
SAT encoder. That content is already in the library, in the open-access EuroComb 2015
proceedings version:

- **Held full text:** `research/sources/balko-valtr-A-SAT-attack-on-ES-ENDM2015.full.md`
  (314 lines), source URL https://eurocomb2015.w.uib.no/files/2015/08/endm1938.pdf
  (in the leading comment). This is the same authors, same title, same content family
  as the EJC 66 (2017) journal version whose DOI the request names; the EJC version
  remains paywalled but nothing in the request's need is absent from the held version.
- **What it establishes** (read directly from the held text):
  - Abstract: "every set of 2^{k−2}+1 points ... contains k points in convex
    position. In 2006, Peters and Szekeres introduced the following stronger
    conjecture: every red-blue coloring of the edges of the ordered complete 3-uniform
    hypergraph on 2^{k−2}+1 vertices contains an ordered k-vertex hypergraph consisting
    of a red and a blue monotone path that are vertex disjoint except for the common
    end-vertices. Applying the state of art SAT solver, we refute the conjecture of
    Peters and Szekeres. We also apply techniques of Erdős, Tuza, and Valtr to refine
    the Erdős–Szekeres conjecture in order to tackle it with SAT solvers."
  - So both things the request asks for — the exact statement of the Peters–Szekeres
    strengthened conjecture, and the statement that it is refuted by SAT — are present
    verbatim, with the encoding developed in the body of the held file.
