# Norin & Yuditsky 2016, "Erdős–Szekeres without induction", DCG 55(4)

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.03332
Full text: [[norin-yuditsky - Erdos-Szekeres without induction - DCG 2016 full.full]]

## What it establishes

```claim
id: norin-yuditsky-asymptotic
statement: limsup_{n->infty} ES(n)/C(2n-5, n-2) <= 7/8, improving Vlachos's 29/32.
hypotheses: planar, general position
holds-here: yes
status: proved
bearing: best asymptotic improvement of the binomial-form bound; a fraction of the cups-caps square. Confirms the binomial family can be pushed but not to 2^{n-2}.
anchor: research/sources/norin-yuditsky - Erdos-Szekeres without induction - DCG 2016 full.full.md
```

The name "without induction" refers to a non-inductive cup/cap estimate. The 7/8 constant is
far above the conjectured value (which is ~2^{-n}·C(2n-5,n-2)·(2^n/...)), so it is purely
asymptotic and does not bear on the exact conjecture; included to keep the upper-bound picture
complete in ROOT.md.
