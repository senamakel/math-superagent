## Formula

- a(n) = (2^n - 0^n)/2 + 1.
- a(n) = 3*a(n-1) - 2*a(n-2).
- a(2*n) = 2*a(2*n-1) - 1, n>0.
- Row sums of triangle A135225. - _Gary W. Adamson_, Nov 23 2007
- a(n) = A131577(n) + 1. - _Paul Curtz_, Aug 07 2008
- a(n) = 2*a(n-1) - 1 for n>1, a(0)=1, a(1)=2. - _Philippe Deléham_, Sep 25 2009
- E.g.f.: exp(x)*(1 + sinh(x)). - _Arkadiusz Wesolowski_, Aug 13 2012
- G.f.: G(0), where G(k)= 1 + 2^k*x/(1 - x/(x + 2^k*x/G(k+1) )); (continued fraction). - _Sergei N. Gladkovskii_, Jul 26 2013
- a(n) = 2^(n-1) +1 = A000051(n-1) for n>0. - _M. F. Hasler_, Sep 22 2013

- This sequence has a(0) = 1 and for all n > 0, a(n) = 2^(n-1)+1. Consequently 2*a(n) >= a(n+1) for all n > 0 and the sequence is complete. - _Frank M Jackson_, Jan 29 2012
