(claim `chirotope-extendibility-npcomplete`, status asserted-by-source).

**Bottom line.** Extending a boolean function on a subset of the triples of a
groundset to a full chirotope / pre-CC-system is **NP-complete** (implicit in
Knuth, made precise by Baier). Knuth's CC-system axioms (cyclic symmetry,
antisymmetry, nondegeneracy, interiority, transitivity) are the exact rank-3
axiomatization. This is the sharpest statement of *why* an abstract-chirotope
upper bound cannot be trusted for the geometric ES conjecture: even completing a
partial orientation to a *consistent abstract* chirotope is NP-hard, and the
abstract axioms (necessary, not sufficient) let a SAT solver wander into
non-realizable territory. Realizability (∃ℝ-complete) is a further, harder layer.
