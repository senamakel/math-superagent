## What it establishes

```claim
id: hmpt-bound
statement: ES(n) <= 2^{n + O(sqrt(n log n))}; improves Suk's error term.
hypotheses: n large (planar general position)
holds-here: yes
status: proved
bearing: currently best-known upper bound of the form 2^{n+...}. Still asymptotic; exact conjecture untouched.
anchor: research/sources/holmsen-mojarrad-pach-tardos - Two extensions of the Erdos-Szekeres problem.full.md
```

Also extends the result to convexity structures induced by pseudoline arrangements and convex
bodies (not this run's planar-point-set focus).

## Does not help

The improvement is a refinement of Suk's method (better dense-subset/cap-union analysis); it does
not change the structural obstruction. For this run it fixes the "current best upper bound" line in
ROOT.md and confirms the exact conjecture is the genuine open gap.
