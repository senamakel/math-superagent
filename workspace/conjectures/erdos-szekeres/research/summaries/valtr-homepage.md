# Pavel Valtr

Welcome to the home page of Pavel Valtr. I am a member of the [Department of Applied Mathematics][1] of the [Faculty of Mathematics and Physics][2] at the [Charles University][3] in Prague. My mathematical interests include discrete and computational geometry, classical combinatorics, probabilistic methods in combinatorics, Ramsey theory, and stochastic geometry.
