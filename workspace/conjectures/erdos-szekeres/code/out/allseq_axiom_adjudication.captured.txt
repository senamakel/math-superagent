VERDICT
==========================================================================
  The old allowable_encoder.py used TWO inconsistent code paths:
   - replay(): reverses MERGED RUNS of positions in multi-event groups
   - tally:    swaps each event's pair independently
  A tied group with disjoint side-by-side pairs [A,B,C,D] over (A,B),(C,D)
  must give [B,A,D,C]; run-reversing gives [D,C,B,A] and corrupts the
  running permutation, so later 'non-adjacent swap 11,13' reports are a
  corrupted-state artifact, NOT an axiom violation.
  The adjudicator's corrected per-pair replay (allseq_adjudicate.py 
  TEST 1) gives PAIR/PASS (= N(N-1)/2 adjacent, zero non-adjacent) at
  n=4..7, so the Goodman-Pollack axioms hold on es_construct.
