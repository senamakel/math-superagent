# Wikipedia — Inscribed square problem (status reference)

**Source:** Wikipedia, "Inscribed square problem," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inscribed_square_problem (captured at library build). Full text at [[research/sources/wikipedia-inscribed-square-problem.full.md]].

## What it establishes

The canonical encyclopedic status reference. The inscribed square problem (square peg problem, Toeplitz conjecture, 1911) is **unsolved** in general: "Does every plane simple closed curve contain all four vertices of some square?" True for convex curves, piecewise-smooth curves, and other special cases; the general case remains open. Early positive results: Arnold Emch (1913, convex + sufficiently smooth; 1916, larger class including polygons) and Lev Schnirelmann (1929, smooth).

Resolved cases listed: piecewise analytic curves, locally monotone curves (Stromquist), curves without special trapezoids (Matschke), curves in annuli, symmetric curves (Nielsen–Wright), Lipschitz graphs (Tao/Rifford), Jordan curves close to a C² Jordan curve.
