# Asano–Ike 2024 — HTML primary text

**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2412.21057

This primary HTML version states that a large class of continuous Jordan curves, including all rectifiable curves and Stromquist's locally monotone curves, inscribes every θ-rectangle for θ∈(0,π). The proof uses microlocal sheaf theory and continuous Legendrian lifts. Theorem 1.1 gives the lift hypothesis; Corollary 1.2/5.9 gives the rectifiable case, hence squares; Corollary 1.3/5.12 gives locally monotone curves. The general continuous Jordan-curve case remains outside the theorem. Full text: [[research/sources/asano-ike-2024-rectifiable-rectangular-peg-html.full.md]].
