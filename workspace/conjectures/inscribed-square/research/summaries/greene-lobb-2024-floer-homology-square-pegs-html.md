# Greene–Lobb 2024 — Floer homology and square pegs (HTML primary text)

**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2404.05179

The paper constructs Jordan Floer homology for rectangle inscriptions into real-analytic Jordan curves and uses spectral invariants to prove a rectifiable-curve result. Its main square consequence is: a rectifiable Jordan curve enclosing more than half the area of a circle with the same diameter inscribes a square. It explicitly identifies shrinkout as the obstruction to passing from smooth approximations to arbitrary continuous curves. This is an adjacent symplectic approach, not a proof of the general Toeplitz conjecture. Full text: [[research/sources/greene-lobb-2024-floer-homology-square-pegs-html.full.md]].

