## Abstract

Let*Q*be any rectangle and let*K*⊂ ℝ*d*(*d*≥ 2) be a continuum which is either symmetric across a hyperplane or symmetric through a point*z*∉*K*. We show that*K*contains the vertices of a rectangle similar to*Q*which exhibits the same symmetry as does*K*.
