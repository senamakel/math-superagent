## The standing bet

Work under this. `refuted-by` is the evidence that would end it — if the run has produced that evidence, say so and revise, because a thesis nothing can refute costs a run its whole budget.

- `legendrian-lift-frontier` — The sharp attackable frontier is the continuous Legendrian lift: every Jordan curve admitting one inscribes every θ-rectangle (Asano–Ike Thm 1.1), and rectifiable curves all admit one. The open question is whether a non-rectifiable curve admits one; if not, that class bounds the sheaf method.
  - because: ROOT.md and the weakened ladder: non-rectifiability is the only obstruction never switched off; the Legendrian lift is the exact hypothesis of the strongest positive theorem.
  - refuted-by: A source or checked construction showing (a) every Jordan curve admits a lift (settling the conjecture through the cited theorem), or (b) a non-rectifiable Jordan curve provably without a lift, or (c) retraction of Asano–Ike v3.
