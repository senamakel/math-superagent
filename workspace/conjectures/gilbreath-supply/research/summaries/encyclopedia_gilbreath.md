## What this establishes

Dictionary-level statement: for a sequence `(x_n)`, define the absolute-difference
sequence `δ^1_n = |x_{n+1} − x_n|` and iterate `δ^{k+1} = δ^1 δ^k`. Gilbreath (1958)
conjectured that applied to the primes, the first term in each iterated sequence is
always 1. Odlyzko verified this for primes ≤ 10^13.
