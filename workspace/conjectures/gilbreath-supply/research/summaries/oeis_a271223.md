# OEIS A271223 — 3-adic sqrt(−2) digits — does not bear on SUPPLY

This tracks the 3-adic constant sqrt(−2) via Lucas(3^n) digits; a base-3 /
Lucas-number curiosity, unrelated to the mod-2 fold of the prime gap-parity string
that SUPPLY is about. It does not help: no connection to the parity barrier,
positive mod-4 switch density, or wt(Φ_n h). Do not re-read for this problem.
