authors, same Monthly 2006 article), retrieved from the UBC mirror there and
from dms.umontreal.ca here. Both files are the full text of:

> Andrew Granville, Greg Martin, *Prime Number Races*, Amer. Math. Monthly
> 113 (2006) 1–33.

Read the summary and claim at:
`research/summaries/granville_martin_prime_races.md` (this is the one the
claim ledger anchors, and it is properly written).

Duplicate-detection lesson: `granville_martin_prime_races` and
`granville_martin_prime_number_races` are the SAME article under two
filenames. A later run searching "Prime Number Races" will hit both. Keep the
`_prime_races` file as canonical and treat this `_prime_number_races` file as
a duplicate of the identical UBC-hosted PDF (both converted from the same
paper).
