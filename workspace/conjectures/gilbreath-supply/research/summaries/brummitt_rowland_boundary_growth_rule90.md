## One-line bearing (from the body digest)

Inventory of one-dimensional 2-colour CA boundaries; for Rule 90 from a single
black cell the boundary length is exactly `ℓ(t) = 2t + 1` (linear support) while
the interior is the self-similar Pascal/Sierpinski pattern with `2^wt(n)` ON
cells (Sloane). This is the canonical citation for the fold's qualitative shape —
linear support width, 2-automatic interior density — but gives **no** per-seed,
input-dependent lower bound on `wt(Φ_n h)`, so it does not close request
`walsh-spectral-subset-b904`. Adjacent, not decisive.
