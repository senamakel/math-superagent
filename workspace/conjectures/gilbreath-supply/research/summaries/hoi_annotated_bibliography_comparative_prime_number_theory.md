# Hoi/Martin et al., annotated bibliography of comparative prime number theory

Source: Chi Hoi Yip, Greg Martin, et al. (12 authors), *An annotated
bibliography for comparative prime number theory*, arXiv:2309.08729,
accepted Expositiones Mathematicae (Dec 2024); supersedes Martin–Ng
"Comparative prime number theory: a survey" (arXiv:1202.3408).

## What it is

A 110-page annotated **bibliography** recording every publication on
comparative prime-number theory through mid-2024, with a unified notation for
the quantities studied and the hypotheses under which results hold. It is an
index/survey of the literature, not a family of new theorems.

## Why it does not bear directly on SUPPLY

It surveys Chebyshev bias / prime-number-race results (the equal-residue side
the run already knows is the "wrong" direction for the switch density) and the
hypotheses (GRH, LL, GUE) under which they are proved. Nothing in it bears on
the fold `Φ_n`, `wt(Φ_n h)`, or the mod-4 non-constant-pattern parity barrier
beyond what the primary sources already digested establish
(`granville_martin_prime_races`, `rubinstein_sarnak_chebyshev_bias`,
`lemke_oliver_soundararajan_bias`, `lau_residue_patterns`). It is a useful map
of the equal-residue/race literature if a later pass needs one, but it supplies
no arithmetic input on the gap-parity string and no fold result. Do not read
the large HTML expecting the switch-density barrier — that is in the primary
sources, which are digested and citable.
