## Formula

- Euler transform of [1, 1].
- a(n) = 1 + floor(n/2).
- G.f.: 1/((1-x)(1-x^2)).
- E.g.f.: ((3+2*x)*exp(x) + exp(-x))/4.
- a(n) = a(n-1) + a(n-2) - a(n-3) = -a(-3-n).
- a(0) = a(1) = 1 and a(n) = floor( (a(n-1) + a(n-2))/2 + 1 ).

## Comments

- The floor of the arithmetic mean of the first n+1 positive integers. - _Cino Hilliard_, Sep 06 2003
- Number of partitions of n into powers of 2 where no power is used more than three times, or 4th binary partition function (see A072170).
- Number of partitions of n in which the greatest part is at most 2. - _Robert G. Wilson v_, Jan 11 2002
- Number of partitions of n into at most 2 parts. - _Jon Perry_, Jun 16 2003
- Number of symmetric Dyck paths of semilength n+2 and having two peaks. E.g., a(6)=4 because we have UUUUUUU*DU*DDDDDDD, UUUUUU*DDUU*DDDDDD, UUUUU*DDDUUU*DDDDD and UUUU*DDDDUUUU*DDDD, where U=(1,1), D=(1,-1) and * indicates a peak. - _Emeric Deutsch_, Jan 12 2004
- Smallest positive integer whose harmonic mean with another positive integer is n (for n > 0). For example, a(6)=4 is already given (as 4 is the smallest positive integer such that the harmonic mean of 4 (with 12) is 6) - but the harmonic mean of 2 (with -6) is also 6 and 2 < 4, so the two positive integer restrictions need to be imposed to rule out both 2 and -6.
- Second outermost diagonal of Losanitsch's triangle (A034851). - _Alonso del Arte_, Mar 12 2006
- Arithmetic mean of n-th row of A080511. - _Amarnath Murthy_, Mar 20 2003
- a(n) is the number of ways to pay n euros (or dollars) with coins of one and two euros (respectively dollars). - _Richard Choulet_ and _Robert G. Wilson v_, Dec 31 2007
