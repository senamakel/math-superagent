## What the abstract asserts (for orientation only)

The MacWilliams identity relates the weight enumerator of a code to that of its
dual. Delsarte framed an equivalent relation at the level of association-scheme
eigenvalues (less practical). Functional transforms exist for specific
association schemes (rank metric, skew rank metric, Hermitian matrices). This
2024 paper unifies those into a consistent theory over "Krawtchouk association
schemes" using a b-algebra, and uses the resulting derivatives to compute
moments of the weight distribution of codes in these schemes.
