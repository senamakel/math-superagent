A bibliography index page of Odlyzko's papers on computational number theory.

venue) with download links, and none of the rows establishes anything by itself.
The Gilbreath-relevant primary source (Odlyzko 1993, "Iterated absolute values
of differences of consecutive primes") is already digested in full at
`research/summaries/odlyzko_iterated_abs_values_diff_primes.md`, which is the
canonical note and carries the real claims. This index page adds no claim this
