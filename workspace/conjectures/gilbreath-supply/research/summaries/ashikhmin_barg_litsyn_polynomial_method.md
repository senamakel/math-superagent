## What the abstract asserts (for orientation only)

The authors present a general framework for the "polynomial / Delsarte method"
in coding theory — the technique that accounts for structural results and
bounds on the size of extremal configurations (codes, designs) in metric
spaces — and show it yields new asymptotic bounds on code performance in
binary-input memoryless and Gaussian channels, improving Shannon-era (1959–67)
results.
