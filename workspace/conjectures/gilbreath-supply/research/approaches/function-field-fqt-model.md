  amplifies a weak (equidistribution-class) input and the integer obstruction

  there, that is a strong structural negative: even with the arithmetic granted,
  Φ does not force linear weight, and SUPPLY over Z is likely false in its

## Why this is not a closed door

None of the five closed doors is about arithmetic worlds; they are all
"h is complicated enough" hypotheses on the FIXED integer prime string. The
refuted analytic routes (dispersion, large sieve, Matomäki–Radziwill,
Rubinstein–Sarnak, prime-race-variance) all died for the SAME reason: the
needed correlation is index-domain, and no unconditional theorem reaches
index-domain prime correlations. The function-field world is the standard
device for making index-domain statements tractable — the primes are replaced
by an algebraically generated set (irreducibles) whose counting function has an
explicit closed form, so the index-domain correlations become computable sums.
This is a *model theorem* attack: settle the structural question (does Φ do
work?) in the model, then price the transfer. It is the function-field analogue
technique used throughout analytic number theory (Rudnick, Soundararajan,
function-field random matrix theory), applied here not to a value-domain
statistic but to the fold itself.

## Open question the literature must settle

Does the function-field Chebotarev statement control the *degree-ordered
consecutive* switch statistic (two irreducibles adjacent in the degree-then-lex
order), or only value-domain short intervals? This is the well-posedness check
in `falsifies` (b), and it is exactly what a research pass should price first.

## Decision (convergence pass)

**Adopted.** This is the only grounded candidate of the three, and the
convergence sharpens it into the run's single hypothesis test in its cleanest
form. The other two candidates died to structural findings, and their corpses
are the ammunition:

- `mod2m-lift-onepoint` established (structurally, world-independent): every
  fold cell is a product of character pairs at *distinct* indices, so
  `wt(Φ_n h)` is a count of ≥2-point objects — no one-point input can force it
  (GOAL priority 2 closed negatively; priority 5 corroborated).
- `newton-series-degree-dichotomy` was shown to be the ANF/Möbius basis in
  disguise — a change of name, not of ground.

**The synthesis that neither candidate named.** Combine the two dead ends:
the fold is a ≥2-point object, *and* the function field grants one-point and
macroscopic value-domain inputs but not the microscopic lex-consecutive pair.
The function-field model's job is therefore not "model the switch statistic"
(which re-encounters the same two-point barrier) but **to localize which
correlation order Φ actually needs**: run Φ on the lex-consecutive string and
on strings built from *macroscopic* (Chebotarev-controlled) structure, and
read off whether the weight tracks the microscopic adjacency or the
macroscopic window. If it tracks the macroscopic window, the integer transfer
reduces to a value-domain input strictly weaker than switch density — GOAL
priority 4. If it tracks the microscopic adjacency, we have located the parity
barrier inside Φ — GOAL priority 5.

**Scholze gate (hard check).** The new setting reproduces
`primes-not-eventually-periodic` (irreducibles are aperiodic under degree
order), `fold-rank-is-n-2-nullity-2-alternating` (rank is input-string
independent), and `linearisation-fold-weight` (the linearisation is pure
F_2-linear algebra, world-free). Gate passes.

**First concrete step (tool_builder, today).** Sieve monic irreducibles of
F_2[t] to degree D, build `h^{fq}` from the T-coefficient of the lex-consecutive
difference, run the existing string-agnostic oracle at n = 50..4000, and
compare against the four controls. This is one run, exact, and decisive: the
Thue–Morse control is the kill condition.
