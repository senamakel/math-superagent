# Research grounding: the inventor's three candidates

Took each of the three proposed lines to the literature and to the run's own
on-disk library. Per candidate: what the reformulation is called, the precise
theorems it relies on and whether their hypotheses hold here, who has applied
it, and what it buys. Each candidate's file under `research/approaches/` is
updated with `precedent` and a `status`.

## 1. `anf-mobius-reed-muller` — status: **grounded (identity) / payoff ungrounded**

**What it is called.** The submask-XOR map is the F₂ **Möbius transform**
(alias Zhegalkin / Reed–Muller transform). Its values at a fixed window are
exactly the **algebraic-normal-form (ANF) coefficients** of that Boolean
function: `a_d = ⊕_{x ⊆ d} g(x)` is the coefficient of the monomial `x_{i_1}…x_{i_k}`
at index `d`. This is a standard fact of Boolean-function theory —
see the Springer *Cryptography and Communications* 2023 article
(DOI 10.1007/s12095-023-00660-4): "the coefficient of a monomial ... is
computed by summing f over the vector space generated by the e_i ... this
method is sometimes called the Möbius transform"; and hal.science/hal-01178356
(RM/ANF/Möbius decomposition). A checker for the gap-parity window exists on
disk (`code/out/anf_dictionary_check.py`) but has NOT yet been executed (see
honesty note below).

**Consequence.** `ν₂(n) = wt(Φ_n h)` equals the number of nonzero ANF monomials
of the reversed prime-gap window. Correct, and it *predicts* the negative
controls (Thue–Morse and all-ones have sparse ANF, matching their sublinear
ν₂) rather than merely observing them.

**Honesty note on verification.** The on-disk checker `code/out/anf_dictionary_check.py`
(which asserts `T(n,d)==a_d` for n=3..40 and all-ones ANF support 1) is **written but
has NOT been executed** — neither the inventor nor this role holds an execution tool,
and `code/out/INDEX.md` flags it accordingly. The identification itself is a classical,
sourced fact of Boolean-function theory (the F₂ Möbius transform computes ANF
coefficients), and it reproduces the already-checked claim `supply-fold-submask-zeta-involution`
by one substitution `x = d−o`; the specific equality `T(n,d)==a_d` for the *gap-parity
window* is therefore a sourced/derived identity held at dictionary level, not a
machine-verified number. tool_builder must execute the checker before candidate 1's
payoff is rested on.

**What it does not buy.** This is a *relabelling*, not a new mechanism. It is
the same F₂ fold read through a coding-theory lens. The uncertainty principle
(Donoho–Stark, Tao–Meshulam, both on disk) gives `|supp f|·|supp f̂| ≥ |G|`
(or `|supp f|+|supp f̂| ≥ p+1` cyclically) — a *product* bound relating f to its
Fourier transform, giving neither factor, and neither factor is the ANF support.
And the literature lower-bounds ANF support / algebraic immunity only under
hypotheses that are exactly the five refuted "h is complicated enough" inputs.
The Reed–Muller weight spectrum itself is a hard open problem (Carlet), so the
reformulation hands the problem to a category that is not easier. It is worth
having (a correct dictionary, and it predicts the controls) but it is not by
itself a route to `ν₂ ≥ c·n`.

## 2. `kummer-2adic-lift` — status: **refuted** (killed-by: valuation cannot be pushed forward)

**What it is called.** The lift to the 2-adics, using the ultrametric law and
Kummer's carry-count for binomial valuations. The inputs are real and classical:
Kummer's theorem `v₂(C(d,i))` = number of base-2 carries adding `i` and `d−i`
(in Meštrović's survey on disk; Barat–Grabner, JLMS 64 (2001),
DOI 10.1112/S0024610701002630), and Granville's Zaphod–Beeblebrox mod-4
Pascal structure (AMM 1997 correction, DOI 10.1080/00029890.1997.11990728; and
the on-disk Bacher reduction with the Beeblebrox χ_B character).

**Why it fails.** The mechanism's first step — an *explicit valuation
propagation* from the initial gap valuations — is not realisable. Under the
ultrametric law `|a−b|₂ ≤ max(|a|₂,|b|₂)`, the strict (cancellation) case
`v₂(a)=v₂(b)` determines `v₂(a−b)` from the **2-adic residue of the ratio a/b**,
not from the two valuations. So valuation — the only coordinate the mechanism
proposes to carry — is insufficient; one needs the full 2-adic values of every
previous cell, i.e. exactly the data the F₂ fold discards and exactly what makes
the triangle hard (Odlyzko 1993 gives no valuation-from-valuations recursion).
The lift would re-derive `ν₂ = wt(Φ_n h)` through cell valuations with no new
tractable invariant. No source applies a Kummer fold to force ν₂ large from
gap-valuation data.

## 3. `mahler-2kernel-automaticity` — status: **refuted** (killed-by: the sparse-window ⇒ finite-2-kernel inference fails)

**What it is called.** Christol's theorem / Allouche–Shallit: a sequence whose
`k`-kernel (span of `n ↦ a(k^j n + r)`, all `j,r`) is finite is `k`-automatic.
This specific statement is real and on disk (Allouche–Shallit, *The ring of
k-regular sequences* I & II). And one half of the non-automaticity side is
genuinely established: the prime **indicator** `χ_P` (n ↦ 1_P(n) by integer
value) is not k-automatic for any k — Hartmanis–Shank 1968, Schützenberger 1968
(no infinite subset of primes is finite-automaton recognizable), Coons 2008
(arXiv:0810.3709).

**Why it fails.** The load-bearing inference (a) — "ν₂ small on a density-1 set
of n (sparse window ANF) forces the 2-kernel of the infinite string τ to be
finite" — is unsupported and false. The 2-kernel is a **rigid global condition**
on a single infinite string; small `ν₂(n)` gives only **local, n-specific**
window constraints on a density-1 set. The Möbius involution is a bijection on
each fixed window and does not transfer to a global finite-dimensionality
statement. So the "sidestep of the e_{2^m} witness" is not earned — the witness
is excluded only by asserting the conclusion (finite 2-kernel). A second,
independent flaw: candidate 3 steps from the prime **indicator**'s
non-automaticity to the gap-**parity-by-prime-index** string h's
non-automaticity; I searched "prime gap string automaticity" and "prime gap
sequence not automatic" and found **no** source proving h (the by-index
gap-parity string) non-automatic. Only the prime indicator is established. That
gap-string non-automaticity would need its own proof and is not sourced.

Candidates 2 and 3 are closed. Candidate 1 is grounded as a correct dictionary
but its payoff is open: does the ANF/spectral view, together with an arithmetic
input *strictly weaker than positive mod-4 switch density*, force
`ν₂ ≥ c·n`? The first-step (verify the primes' ANF support is spread at small n,
and whether a spectral/Walsh bound on the window — the `walsh-spectral-subset-b904`
