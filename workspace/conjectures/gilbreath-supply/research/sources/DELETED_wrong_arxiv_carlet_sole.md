# Carlet–Solé weight spectrum — correct source status

**Status: NOT in the library as a full text.** The paper Claude Carlet &
Patrick Solé, *The weight spectrum of two families of Reed-Muller codes*,
Discrete Mathematics (2023), DOI 10.1016/j.disc.2023.113568, is real and is the
source behind the `anf-mobius-reed-muller` refutation's citation
"RM weight spectra of `RM(m−3,m)` and `RM(m−4,m)` determined (Carlet–Solé 2023)".

It is **Elsevier-paywalled**; a guessed arXiv id (`2306.04731`) fetched an
unrelated quantum-physics paper and was rejected. No free arXiv mirror was
found in search. **Do not re-fetch this DOI.**

The result is nonetheless **reproduced for free** by:
- the survey `research/sources/abbe_shpilka_ye_reedmuller_survey.full.md`
  (arXiv:2002.03317, catalogs the known extreme-order spectra and flags the
  middle orders open), and
- `research/sources/lou_wang_weight_spectrum_RMm6.full.md` (arXiv:2406.03803,
  states that Carlet–Solé determined `RM(m−3,m)`, `RM(m−4,m)` and that Carlet
  did `RM(m−5,m)`).

So the *content* the refutation relies on is now sourced, even though the
primary's own PDF is absent (Wiley/Elsevier-type paywall).
