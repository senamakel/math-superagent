# DELETED — duplicate wrong arXiv fetch

The file `research/sources/carlet_sole_weight_spectrum_two_families.full.md`
and its digest `research/summaries/carlet_sole_weight_spectrum_two_families.md`
were created by guessing the arXiv identifier `2306.04731` for Claude Carlet &
Patrick Solé, *The weight spectrum of two families of Reed-Muller codes*
(Discrete Math, DOI 10.1016/j.disc.2023.113568). That identifier belongs to an
unrelated quantum-physics paper (*Free Fermion Distributions Are Hard to Learn*).

The correct paper is confirmed real but paywalled: the DOI
`10.1016/j.disc.2023.113568` points to it on ScienceDirect, and no free arXiv
mirror was found in search. The two wrong files were removed. Recorded in
`research/sources/DELETED_wrong_arxiv_carlet_sole.md`. Do not re-fetch this
DOI's content; the survey `abbe_shpilka_ye_reedmuller_survey.full.md` and the
open-archive Carlet `RM(m−5,m)` / Lou–Wang `RM(m−6,m)` papers carry the same
results for free.
