LESSON (the sanity check's point): the statement '(1/N)Σa_n is bounded below' is a Chebyshev/mean condition; it does NOT imply that the set where a_n is large has density approaching 1. Here the mean is exactly c yet P(a_n >= c) stays ~0.32 < 1 for every N. In the SUPPLY analogue (GOAL priority 1), the averaged form M(N)=mean(ν₂/n) rising to ~0.49 is a *bounded-mean* fact; turning it into 'ν₂(n) >= c·n for density-1 many n' is exactly the step that does NOT follow from the mean alone and needs a second moment / concentration argument.
