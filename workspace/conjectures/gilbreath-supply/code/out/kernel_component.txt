======================================================================
TASK B — kernel component of prime switch bit h
======================================================================
Phi_n: (n-2) x n, rows d in [2,n-1], entry C(d-1, j-(n-d)) mod 2.
Known ker = span(even-alt, odd-alt), so the 4 collapse vectors are 0, even-alt, odd-alt, all-ones.

   n rank null  ker=span?  wt(h)/n   dmin/n  attaining v
--------------------------------------------------------
   8    6    2       True   0.6250   0.1250  even-alt
  16   14    2       True   0.6875   0.1875  even-alt
  32   30    2       True   0.6250   0.3750  all-ones
  64   62    2       True   0.6875   0.3125  all-ones
 128  126    2       True   0.6484   0.3516  all-ones

Interpretation: dmin/n = min over collapse directions of the
distance from the prime h to that direction. wt(h)/n = switch
density. A small dmin/n would mean h is close to a direction
whose fold image has weight 0 (nu2 = 0); a dmin/n near 1/2
would mean h is far from every collapse direction.
NOTE: dmin over the kernel is essentially the distance to the
nearest parity-alternating string; with this script's own
measured wt(h)/n in 0.6250..0.6875 (n=8..128, see table), dmin/n
is expected ~ |wt(h)/n - 0.5| away from 0, not near 0.
