# Lagarias, "Ternary expansions of powers of 2" — arXiv landing page (abstract only)

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0512006 (arXiv:math/0512006v4, 11 Jul 2008); published J. London Math. Soc. 79 (2009) 562–588.

This is ONLY the arXiv abstract/landing page — it contains the abstract and bibliographic metadata, not the theorems. The substantive note with the exact statements (Narkiewicz bound `N1(X) ≤ 1.62·X^α0`, `α0 = log_3 2 ≈ 0.63092`; Lagarias Thm 1.4 generalising to `Ñ_λ(X) ≤ 2X^α0` for every nonzero `λ ∈ Z_3`; the Hausdorff-dimension theorems 1.5/1.6; the middle-digit gap; Conjectures A, B, E) lives in `[[lagarias-2009-ternary-pdf]]` (`research/summaries/lagarias-2009-ternary-pdf.md`, full text at `research/sources/lagarias-2009-ternary-pdf.full.md`).

**Do not re-read this page for content.** Its only value here is the paper's identity and provenance. The abstract confirms the thrust: two dynamical formalisations (real `y → 2y`; 3-adic `λ → λ2^n`), an asymptotic upper bound on digit-2-omitting iterates, and Hausdorff dimension of intersections of translates of 3-adic Cantor sets.
