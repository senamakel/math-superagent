## What it claims

Abstract: This paper considers a class of closed p-adic sets obtained by graph-directed constructions analogous to those of Mauldin and Williams over the real numbers. These sets are characterized as those p-adic integers that have p-adic expansions describable by paths in the graph of a finite automaton, and we call then p-adic path set fractals. The class of such sets is shown to be closed under addition and multiplication by p-integral rational numbers, and also under set-valued addition (Minkowski sum). The Hausdorff dimensions of such sets are explicitly calculable.
