## The real Abram–Lagarias papers, and their correct identifiers

The relevant primary sources are:

1. **William C. Abram, Jeffrey C. Lagarias, "Intersections of multiplicative translates of 3-adic Cantor sets", J. Fractal Geom. 1 (2014), no. 4, 349–390.** (The search result gave the arXiv-free DOI 10.4171/jfg/11.) Content: intersecions `S = Σ ∩ (1/M_1)Σ ∩ … ∩ (1/M_n)Σ` of multiplicative translates of the 3-adic Cantor set (digits in {0,1}, omitting 2) are 3-adic path-set fractals with computable Hausdorff dimension `log_3 β` where β is a real algebraic integer; bounds the exceptional set `E(Z_3)` of points whose ×2 orbit meets the Cantor set infinitely often; `dim_H(E(Z_3)) ≤ 1/2`, conjectured `= 0`. Motivated by the ×2 dynamical system on Z_3.

2. **William C. Abram, Artem Bolshakov, Jeffrey C. Lagarias, "Intersections of Multiplicative Translates of 3-Adic Cantor Sets II: Two Infinite Families", Experimental Mathematics (2016), DOI 10.1080/10586458.2016.1205532.** Content: two infinite families of translates; the second gives the sharper bound **`dim_H(E(Z_3)) ≤ log_3 φ ≈ 0.438018`** where φ = golden ratio.
