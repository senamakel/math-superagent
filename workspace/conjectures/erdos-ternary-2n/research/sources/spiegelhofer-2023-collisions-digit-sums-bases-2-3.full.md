# Title: Collisions of digit sums in bases 2 and 3

Authors: [Lukas Spiegelhofer][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled Collisions of digit sums in bases 2 and 3, by Lukas Spiegelhofer

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: We prove a folklore conjecture concerning the sum-of-digits functions in bases two and three: there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that the sum of the binary digits of $n$ equals the sum of the ternary digits of $n$.
