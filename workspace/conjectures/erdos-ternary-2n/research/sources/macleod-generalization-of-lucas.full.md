## Abstract

Core share and HTML view are not available for this content. However, as you have access to this content, a full PDF is available via the 'Save PDF' action button.

A well-known result of E. Lucas enables one to obtain the residue modulo p of in terms of the base-*p*digits of *M*and *N*. Using a recent result of P. W. Haggard and J. O. Kiltenen, a proof of N. J. Fine has been adapted to yield the corresponding residue modulo *p r*.
