# Title: $p$-adic Ducci Sequences: a short note

Authors: [Piero Giacomelli][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled $p$-adic Ducci Sequences: a short note, by Piero Giacomelli

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: In this short note we formalized the definition for the Ducci operator $D$ in the context of the $p$-adic field $\mathbb{Q}_p$ as a natural extension of the classical one. Moreover we will describe the behavior of the operator and will provide some simple results as a counterpart to the classical one.
