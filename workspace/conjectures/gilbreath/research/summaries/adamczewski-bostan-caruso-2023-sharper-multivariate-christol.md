# Adamczewski, Bostan, Caruso — "A sharper multivariate Christol's theorem" (2023)

Boris Adamczewski, Alin Bostan, Xavier Caruso. *A sharper multivariate Christol's
theorem with applications to diagonals and Hadamard products.*
arXiv:2306.02640 (2023). Full text:
`research/sources/adamczewski-bostan-caruso-2023-sharper-multivariate-christol.full.md`

## What it establishes

A new, elementary, effective and sharper proof of the **multivariate** version of
Christol's theorem, and its extension to perfect ground fields of positive
characteristic (Denef–Lipshitz, Sharif–Woodcock, Harase):

> **Theorem A (Christol, univariate).** A power series Σ_n a(n) t^n ∈ F_q[[t]] is
> algebraic over F_q(t) **iff** its coefficient sequence (a(n)) is q-automatic
> (generated by a finite q-automaton reading base-q expansions of n).

The paper's Theorem 1.2 family gives quantitative bounds on the size of the
automaton / dimension of the invariant section-operator space W ⊂ K from the degree
and height of the minimal polynomial — state complexity bounds in Christol's theorem
(Section 4). Applications: diagonals of multivariate rational/algebraic series stay
algebraic (Furstenberg–Deligne style, Section 5); multivariate algebraic power series
are closed under Hadamard product (Section 6).
