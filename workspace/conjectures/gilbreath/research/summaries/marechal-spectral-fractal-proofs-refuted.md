# Maréchal "spectral-fractal proofs" (Zenodo 2025) — refuted as crank

<!-- https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15116867 (Gilbreath); see also 14956918 (RH), 15120119 (PNT), 15116796 (Erdős–Graham), 15116806 (Baum–Connes) -->

A "complete spectral-fractal proof of Gilbreath's conjecture" (Maréchal, Zenodo, Apr 2025)
surfaced in search. It is the same author's series of near-identical "universal
spectral-fractal framework with characteristic dimension √2" preprints claiming complete
proofs of **Gilbreath, the Riemann Hypothesis, the Prime Number Theorem, Erdős–Graham, and
Baum–Connes — all published within one week (Mar–Apr 2025), all by an independent
researcher with 0 citations, none peer-reviewed.**

## Why it is refuted as a source

- **Same template, five different millennium-level/celebrated results.** A framework that
  "completely proves" RH, PNT, Gilbreath, Erdős–Graham, and Baum–Connes in seven days with
  no institutional peer review is not a mathematical breakthrough; it is the signature of a
  crank generating papers from a single template.
- **No mechanism that could be checked here.** The arguments are entirely about a
  self-adjoint Sturm–Liouville operator with a "fractal potential" whose spectrum "encodes"
  the primes. There is no statement of a computable hypothesis the run could verify, and no
  derivation of any of the run's quantities (block lengths, {0,2} regime, second entry).
- **The Erdős–Graham entry is not even a conjecture** (it is a 2003 theorem of
  ESW), which shows the author is not conversant enough with the field for the claims to be
  load-bearing.

## Status

**Refuted as a source, recorded so nobody re-searches or cites it.** The conjecture is not
proved by this author. (This is the correct handling of a "proof" claim on an open
problem: record the refutation rather than carry it as a lead.)
