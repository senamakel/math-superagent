## Classification: NOT LOAD-BEARING — crank family, do not cite

A Zenodo preprint (v1, 1 Aug 2025) by Hanyelichukwu Paul Okolo claiming "a complete
and unconditional proof" of Gilbreath's conjecture from an "Organized Complexity (OC)"
framework, allegedly derived from the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic. The "proof"
rides on axioms called "Global Harmony" and a "Principle of Invariant Dissipation"
purportedly with "profound implications for cosmology, quantum information, and genetics."

This is the same claim-profile as the run's other classified non-proofs:
Maréchal 2025 (spectral-fractal), Granville 2026 (sieving/fintech), ZARKOUNA 2026
(all claim a "complete/unconditional proof" via a bespoke universal framework and
generalise the relevance far outside number theory). None of them has a checkable
mathematical statement, none is peer-reviewed, and none supplies a mechanism this run
can test. The single precedent for the wrongness of the whole family is that the actual
conjecture is open after ~150 years and all severe attack directions are either refuted
(in this run's ledger) or equivalent to the conjecture itself; a one-page "axiom of
Global Harmony" forbidding the counterexample is not an argument.
