## What it establishes — the load-bearing theorem

This is a canonical, open-access primary statement of **Christol's theorem**, quoted
explicitly as **Theorem 4.1.1**:

> Let q be a power of the prime p, and let {a_i}_{i≥0} be a sequence over F_q.
> Then the power series Σ_{i≥0} a_i t^i ∈ F_q[[t]] is **algebraic over F_q(t)**
> if and only if the sequence {a_i} is **p-automatic**.

It generalizes Christol's theorem to generalized Laurent series (supports well-ordered
in ℚ): such a series is algebraic over F_q(t) iff it is **p-quasi-automatic**
(Theorem 4.1.3), with two proofs of the algebraic⟹automatic direction and an outline of
the automatic⟹algebraic direction. It also characterize the well-ordered sets of
rationals whose base-p expansions are generated by a finite automaton.
