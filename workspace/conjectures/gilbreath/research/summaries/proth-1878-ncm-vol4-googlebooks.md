# Proth 1878 — Google Books volume 4 record (Nouv. Corresp. Math.)

**Full text:** `research/sources/proth-1878-ncm-vol4-googlebooks.full.md` [[proth-1878-ncm-vol4-googlebooks.full]]
**Source URL:** https://books.google.com/books?id=10A0AQAAMAAJ

## What it establishes

The Google Books capture of *Nouvelle Correspondance Mathématique*, vol. 4
(1878, F. Hayez, Brussels; editors E. Catalan and P. Mansion). The conversion
yielded only: the volume metadata (LCCN ca96000413), a **table of contents**
(listing articles by Mansion, Catalan, Brocard, Le Paige, Lucas's functions,
Cesàro, etc. — a general mathematical journal), and a search-word cloud
(angles, Catalan, Fermat, Lucas, nombres premiers, questions proposées, ...).
**No page text of Proth's article was converted** — the page images did not OCR
to text in this capture.

## What it does not establish

Nothing about the content of Proth's "Sur la série des nombres premiers"
(pp. 236–240). It does confirm the journal volume exists, is edited by
Catalan/Mansion, and contains essays on primes ("nombres premiers" appears in
the word cloud), consistent with the bibliographic data already held. The
volume's ToC does not index the article by page in this capture.

## Hypotheses / bearing

Provenance/metadata record only. The content of Proth's article is covered by
the two reader accounts already in the library (Arias de Reyna 2020; Chase 2024
§7) plus the retraction (Williams 2020). Anyone seeking primary granularity
(the exact consequences Proth drew, Catalan's note wording) must use the GDZ
scan with a JS-capable browser: `https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN598948236_0004`.
**No help for content.**
