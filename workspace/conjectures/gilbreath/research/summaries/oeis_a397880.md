# A397880 — numerators of c(n), the expected depth-n entry in the CHT continuous Gilbreath model

**Source:** https://oeis.org/A397880 (OEIS catalogue; read this run).
**Companion:** A395556 = denominators of the same c(n). The OEIS entry itself IS

## What it establishes

The OEIS catalogue independently lists the numerators of the CHT/Ross exact
rationals `c(n) = E[a(n,j)]` for the stationary continuous Gilbreath model
(top row iid standard exponentials; array `a(i+1,j)=|a(i,j)-a(i,j+1)|`):

| n | c_n (exact) | A397880 numerator | A395556 denominator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 7/9 | 7 | 9 |
| 3 | 227/288 | 227 | 288 |
| 4 | 778959731701/1447295850000 | 778959731701 | 1447295850000 |

The n=2,3 values are CHT's proven exact values; the n=4 value is Ross's
sign-cone computation (claim `ross-2026-decay-constants`, fraction
778959731701/1447295850000). **The OEIS entry carries exactly the same
numerators** — a third, independent source (neither CHT-authored, Ross, nor
this run) that agrees with both.

The entry also mirrors already-recorded facts: `Sum_{i<=n} c_i >= log(n+e)`
(CHT, so c_n cannot decay exponentially); whether c_n → 0 or is even bounded
is open; the sequence is non-monotone with the digit-sum sawtooth
`c_2<c_3>c_4<c_5>c_6` tracking A000120; empirically
`c_n ≈ C·λ^{A000120(n)}/n`, λ≈1.14–1.20 (Ross). Formula:
`c(n) = (n+1)·E[b(n,1)]` where `(b_1..b_{n+1})` is uniform on the standard
simplex and `b(n,1)` is its absolute-difference apex (per CHT).
