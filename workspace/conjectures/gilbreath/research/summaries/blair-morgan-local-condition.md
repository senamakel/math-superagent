# Reducing Gilbreath's Conjecture to a Local Condition: How much of the triangle do we really need to watch?

### Authors/Creators

- [Morgan, Blair (Researcher)][3][image: ORCID icon] [4]

## Description

We present a reduction of Gilbreath&rsquo;s Conjecture to an explicit local bound. Specifically, we show that the conjecture follows from the condition

|Gᵣ[2] − Gᵣ[1]| ≤ 2 for all r ≥ 1.

We state four supporting lemmas used by the reduction, describe a minimal preimage structure associated with a model minimal violating configuration, and report computational verification of the local bound through 100,000 rows. The full conjecture remains open, but the remaining target is now clearly isolated.
