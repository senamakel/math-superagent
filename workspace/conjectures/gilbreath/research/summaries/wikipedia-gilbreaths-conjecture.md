## What it claims

**Gilbreath's conjecture**is a [conjecture][1] in [number theory][2] regarding the [sequences][3] generated by applying the [forward difference operator][4] to consecutive [prime numbers][5] and leaving the results unsigned, and then repeating this process on consecutive terms in the resulting sequence, and so forth. The statement is named after [Norman L. Gilbreath][6] who, in 1958, presented it to the mathematical community after observing the pattern by chance while doing arithmetic on a napkin. [1] In 1878, eighty years before Gilbreath's discovery, [François Proth][7] had published the same observations. [1] [2]
