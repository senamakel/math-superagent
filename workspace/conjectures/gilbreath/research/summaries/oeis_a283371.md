## Formula

- a(n) >> n/log n. In particular, lim inf a(n) * (log n)/n >= 1/2. - _Charles R Greathouse IV_, Mar 06 2017

## Comments

- Maximum number of different ways of expressing a positive number as a difference of two distinct primes less than or equal to prime(n).
- Is there any n such that a(n+1) - a(n) > 1?
- What is the asymptotic behavior of a(n)?
- To answer the first question: for all n, either a(n+1) = a(n) or a(n+1) = a(n) + 1. - _Charles R Greathouse IV_, Mar 06 2017
