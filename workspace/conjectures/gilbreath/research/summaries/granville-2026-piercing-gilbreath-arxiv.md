## Its claims (from the abstract)

- "A new methodology to attack" Gilbreath's conjecture, based on "sieving",
  "reverse sieving", "magic primes", "forbidden prime number constellations",
  "cellular automata", "reduction via classes of equivalent sequences".
- Promises "a number of new results with proof, a precise path to the solution"
  — and, tellingly, applications to "testing randomness, pattern and fraud
  detection, cybersecurity, synthetic data ... chaos in time series including
  Brownian motions".
