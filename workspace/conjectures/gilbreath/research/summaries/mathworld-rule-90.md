## What it establishes

- Rule 90 is an **additive elementary cellular automaton** introduced by Wolfram
  1983; next cell state is the **XOR (sum mod 2) of the two neighbours**
  (`Mod[p + r, 2]`); for two-colour automata equivalent to XOR of the left and
  right neighbours.
- Started from a single black cell, successive generations read as binary numbers
  1, 101, 10001, 1010101, 100000001, ... (OEIS A038183 / A070886) — the Sierpiński
  sieve.
- **The fractal is the Sierpiński gasket/sieve**, described by Sierpiński in 1915;
  generated by the **binomial coefficient mod 2** (odd = black via XOR), i.e.
  Pascal's triangle mod 2. Rules 60, 90 and 102 (omitting trailing zeros) also
  produce the Sierpiński sieve (Wolfram 2002, p. 870).
- **Additivity:** an additive CA's evolution for an arbitrary initial condition
  can be computed by convolving the single-cell evolution — the superposition
  property the interior identification uses.
- Rule 90 is amphichiral; its complement is Rule 165.
