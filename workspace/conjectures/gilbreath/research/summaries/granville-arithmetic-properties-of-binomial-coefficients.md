# Arithmetic properties of Binomial Coefficients

---

*Binomial Coefficients have many remarkable arithmetic properties. In this e-survey we introduce and explain some of what makes binomial coefficients so fascinating. This e-survey is `dynamic' so that it can be edited as soon as new developments occur: if you know of something that you believe should be included please let [us][1] know. *
