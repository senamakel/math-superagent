Concretely: x majorizes y (x ≻ y) iff y = Dx for a doubly stochastic D (Birkhoff 1946), iff Σ φ(y_i) ≤ Σ φ(x_i) for every convex φ (Karamata/Hardy–Littlewood–Pólya). Because ≻ is a partial order on the sorted vector, it can be monotone where scalar functionals are not, and the flat stratum ({0,1} strings after halving) is a *lattice* closed under the map (Rule-90/XOR), which is the natural absorbing set for a Schur-order argument.
