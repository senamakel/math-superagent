# Comparison / ordering cellular automaton

```approach
idea: View the Gilbreath triangle through the ORDERING of adjacent entries — the comparison word c_k(i) = [A_k(i+1) >= A_k(i)] plus the midpoint/convexity bits — which evolves under a finite-radius LOCAL rule; A_k(1) ∈ {0,2} becomes a left-boundary pattern-avoidance condition in this symbolic system.
mechanism: Write a = A_k(i), b = A_k(i+1), c = A_k(i+2). The new cell at the next row is d = |a-b|, d' = |b-c|, and its orientation is governed by d' > d ⟺ |b-c| > |a-b| ⟺ (b-c)^2 > (a-b)^2 ⟺ (a-c)(a+c-2b) < 0. So the sign of the new adjacent difference is determined by exactly two comparison predicates on the previous row: sign(a-c) (is a bigger than c) and sign(a+c-2b) (does b sit above or below the midpoint of a,c). The orientation word and the convexity word of row k therefore determine the orientation word of row k+1 through a finite-radius rule (Hedlund's theorem: local rule ⟺ continuous shift-commuting map, i.e. a one-dimensional cellular automaton). This is a genuinely different object from everything on disk: every prior approach tracks magnitudes, blocks, scalar potentials, flows, or networks; none attacks the ORDERING/alternation structure as a symbolic dynamical system in its own right. The second-entry claim A_k(1) ∈ {0,2} is exactly a constraint on the leftmost cell's magnitude, which is a fixed function of the first few comparison/convexity bits of row k-1. If the set of comparison words actually realised by the prime triangle forms a shift of finite type (or sofic shift) with the left boundary pinned by the leading 1, the conjecture is a trapping statement checkable on the finite transition graph. Speculative: whether the realised comparison words are a finite subshift, and whether A_k(1) >= 4 corresponds to an explicit finite forbidden word near the left edge, are both open and are exactly what the first step measures.
status: refuted
first-step: From the oracle rows (witnesses.json depth 600; blocks_depth1000.json depth 1000, exact integers) compute the comparison word c_k(i) = [A_k(i+1) >= A_k(i)] and the convexity bit v_k(i) = [A_k(i) + A_k(i+2) >= 2 A_k(i+1)] for every row, and machine-verify the exact transition formula sign(A_{k+1}(i+1) - A_{k+1}(i)) = sign((a-c)(a+c-2b)) on all triples. Then measure whether the realised comparison words have finite memory (do they form a subshift of finite type?) and whether A_k(1) >= 4 correlates with a specific forbidden word at the left edge. Cost O(depth x width), one row live, trivially bounded.
killed-by: SCALE-INVARIANCE — the symbolic system is structurally blind to the magnitude it must certify. The comparison word c_k(i) = [A_k(i+1) >= A_k(i)] and the convexity bit v_k(i) = [A_k(i)+A_k(i+2) >= 2 A_k(i+1)] are both PRESERVED under any positive scaling A_k -> lambda*A_k (lambda > 0): multiplying every entry by lambda preserves every > / >= comparison. The second entry A_k(1) scales with lambda. Concretely, the even row r = (1,2,0,4,6,2,...) (second entry 2 in {0,2}) and its double 2r = (1,4,0,8,12,4,...) (second entry 4 NOT in {0,2}) have IDENTICAL comparison and convexity words, and both are valid even rows of a 2-then-odds Gilbreath triangle, so both are realised (their full triangles are the same symbolic system scaled by 2 — |lambda a - lambda b| = lambda|a-b| is homogeneous). Hence NO pattern-avoidance condition over the orientation/convexity word can ever distinguish A_k(1)=2 from A_k(1)=4, i.e. cannot express the conjecture's conclusion A_k(1) in {0,2}. The local rule itself is real and correctly derived — verified by hand: |b-c|>|a-b| ⟺ (b-c)^2-(a-b)^2 > 0 ⟺ (2b-a-c)(a-c) > 0 ⟺ (a-c)(a+c-2b) < 0 (since 2b-a-c = -(a+c-2b)) — and Hedlund's theorem does make it a genuine 1D CA, so the "local CA" half of the claim is grounded. But the CA is a system over {0,1}-valued comparisons whose evolved state carries NO absolute-scale information, and the conjecture is exactly an absolute-scale statement about the leftmost cell. Pattern-avoidance in a scale-invariant symbolic system cannot certify a scale-sensitive conclusion.
precedent: The local-rule identity is elementary algebra (verified by hand above, no source needed). Hedlund (1969) "Endomorphisms and automorphisms of the shift dynamical system" Math. Systems Theory 3:320-375 — the local-rule ⟺ continuous shift-commuting map theorem. The scale-invariance kill is original to this run. Claim note: `research/notes/scale-invariance-kills-ordering-system.md` (to write). No prior Gilbreath work in the library or the held corpus (Odlyzko 1993, Killgrove-Ralston, Chase 2024, CHT 2026, Eppstein, Gatti, Muney) attacks the ordering word as a CA; the closest held analogue is the Rule-90 interior (which IS a real magnitude-blind additive CA over {0,2} — but that one is used only to track the {0,2} block that is ALREADY the target, not to force it into existence, mirroring this failure).
```

**Research verdict: refuted.** The finite-radius local rule and its status as a 1D cellular automaton (via Hedlund) are real, and the identity checks. But the whole programme — "A_k(1) ∈ {0,2} becomes a left-edge pattern-avoidance statement in this symbolic system" — is false: the ordering/convexity system is positively homogeneous in the row, so it has no information about absolute magnitude, and the conjecture's conclusion is precisely a statement about the absolute magnitude of the second entry. Row r and row 2r give the same symbolic system with different second entries, so no word pattern can force the {0,2} value. This is a cousin of the run's "no scalar Lyapunov survived" lesson: any representation that discards magnitude discards the conjecture. Do not re-propose. (The identity itself survives as a useful algebraic fact: the orientation word evolves locally — but that is a statement about the orientation word, which is not the conjecture.)
