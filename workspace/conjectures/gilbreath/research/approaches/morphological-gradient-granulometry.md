# Morphological gradient / granulometry

```approach
idea: The row map (a,b) -> |a-b| is the one-dimensional MORPHOLOGICAL GRADIENT (dilation minus erosion over a 2-point window) of mathematical morphology (Matheron, Serra); the whole Gilbreath triangle is the iterated morphological gradient, and the erosion/regeneration laws are statements in the granulometry / root-signal algebra.
mechanism: With dilation d(a,b) = max(a,b) and erosion e(a,b) = min(a,b) over a 2-point structuring element, the morphological gradient grad = d - e = |a-b| is exactly the Gilbreath cell map, so A_{k+1} = grad(A_k) and the triangle is the iterated gradient. The established facts translate verbatim: the leading {0,2} block is the set of positions where grad <= 2 (a flat / 1-Lipschitz prefix — the run's own characterization, cited not re-derived); the block lemma's exact erosion (one position per row) is the statement that the gradient of a flat region stays flat while the flat region shrinks by one window; regeneration is the re-formation of a flat prefix. The NEW claim is that the morphology toolbox supplies the RATE, which no scalar potential could: granulometries are one-parameter families of openings gamma_s indexed by size that are MONOTONE and (anti-)extensive in s, and iterated morphological filters converge to root signals (opening/closing are idempotent). Concretely: find an opening gamma_s (opening by a flat segment of length s) such that the size of the maximal flat prefix — i.e. the block length b_k — is a monotone functional under the gradient map. That would turn the open regeneration-rate question into a monotonicity certificate in an order-theoretic, level-set-based algebra rather than a scalar Lyapunov (all of which were machine-refuted). Kill-risk to be checked by research: granulometries are built on threshold sets (level sets), and the run already refuted the level-set approach because S_4 = {A >= 4} is NOT monotone under the cell rule; research must determine whether that transfers to kill the granulometry, or whether the gradient-specific (dilation-minus-erosion) algebra survives the non-monotonicity.

(b) Test the one new hypothesis: for the opening gamma_s by a flat segment of length s on the halved rows, check whether s_k = max{s : gamma_s leaves the first b_k positions unchanged} is monotone across rows on the depth-1000 record. Cost O(depth x width), one row live.
killed-by: TWO grounds, both decisive. (1) The granulometry monotonicity certificate cannot transfer because the Gilbreath cell map is NOT an increasing (monotone) operator, and increasing+idempotent is exactly why granulometries (size distributions) are monotone. An opening gamma is an INCREASING, anti-extensive, idempotent filter — its monotone granulometry theory (Matheron 1975, Serra 1982, "Image Analysis and Mathematical Morphology"; the root-signal characterization of iterated openings, like the root-signal literature found here, e.g. P. Maragos "Pattern spectrum and multiscale shape representation", IEEE TPAMI 11 (1989); the shape-only granulometry literature, URBACH & WILKINSON 2002 "Shape-only granulometries...", Comput. Vis. Image Underst. 92 (2003)) is built on the increasing property. The Gilbreath map T(f)(i) = |f(i)-f(i+1)| is NOT increasing: the run has already established |a-4| is not monotone in a (level-set refutation, S_4 = {A >= 4} not a monotone cluster — claim level-set-percolation-contact-process refuted). So T is not an increasing/idempotent morphological filter and the monotonicity theorems of granulometry do not apply — this is EXACTLY the level-set non-monotonicity the candidate flagged as kill-risk, and it transfers verbatim: a granulometry is a level-set (threshold) construct, and the level predicate is the non-monotone one. (2) The specific functional the approach wants to certify — the maximal flat prefix (block length b_k) — is itself NOT monotone under the gradient map. The run's PROVED step law is b_{k+1} = b_k - 1 on erosion (b_{k+1} < b_k): the flat prefix SHRINKS by one window per erosion row. So "the size of the maximal flat prefix is a monotone functional under the gradient map" is refuted by the step law on the very rows where the gradient map is pure |a-b| (no regeneration): the block length strictly decreases. A monotone certificate for a quantity that provably decreases is a contradiction in terms. (The morphological DICTIONARY — grad = |a-b| = d - e, the flat-prefix/1-Lipschitz view — is exact and survives as a useful re-description; it is the run's own block characterization. What is refuted is every use of the granulometry monotonicity algebra to certify block-length growth.)

The non-monotonicity of the cell map is the run's own established fact (level-set refutation). Kill is by the increasing-filter hypothesis failing + the step law contradicting monotonicity of b_k. **Research verdict: refuted.** The morphological dictionary is exact — `|a−b| = max − min` is the 2-point morphological gradient, and that half is grounded in standard morphology and the run's own block characterization. But the whole point of the approach — using granulometry's monotone, order-theoretic size distributions as a block-length certificate — fails on both decisive grounds the candidate itself anticipated: (1) the Gilbreath map is not an *increasing* operator, and increasing+idempotent is precisely the hypothesis carrying granulometry monotonicity, so the level-set refutation transfers; and (2) the quantity to be certified, the block length `b_k`, provably decreases on erosion (step law `b_{k+1}=b_k−1`), so no monotone certificate can exist for it. Do not re-propose the monotonicity move. (The dictionary is a re-description, not a route.)
