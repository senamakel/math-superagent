# Christoffel/Lyndon-word and cycle-lemma structure of the halved gap word

```approach
idea: Halve the first row A_1 = (1, 2h_1, 2h_2, 2h_3, ...): the word w = h_1 h_2 h_3 ... is the halved prime-gap word (values mostly in {1,2,3} with rare larger jumps). The block-boundary dynamics is governed by running differences of w, and the (2,4)-event / regeneration jump is a first-passage quantity of those running differences. Propose that w (or its running-sum / difference word) is a Christoffel word, or a concatenation of Christoffel factors, and that this combinatorial rigidity forces the block boundary to regenerate at a positive rate. The engine is the cycle lemma (Dvoretzky-Motzkin / Raney / Chung-Feller): a word with a fixed net displacement whose cyclic orbit has a canonical representative has controlled partial sums, i.e. the ballot property.
mechanism: The map from a word to the block profile is exactly the kind of running-sum statistic the cycle lemma controls. If the halved gap word is (essentially) balanced in the Christoffel sense (factor sums over any two equal-length factors differ by at most 1), or is a Lyndon/Christoffel concatenation, then its running differences cannot wander, which is a lower bound on how often the 1-Lipschitz condition holds at the boundary — i.e. a lower bound on the (2,4)-event rate.
status: refuted
killed-by: | The halved prime-gap word does not satisfy the structural hypotheses of any Christoffel/Sturmian/balanced-word theorem, and the (2,4)-event is not a ballot crossing of the gap word's running differences.
  (a) UNBOUNDED ALPHABET — NOT BALANCED, NOT CHRISTOFFEL/STURMIAN. Christoffel and Sturmian words are BINARY, BALANCED words with minimal complexity (a Sturmian word has exactly n+1 factors of length n; equivalently it is balanced). The halved prime-gap word w is over an UNBOUNDED alphabet: the halved gaps (p_{n+2}-p_{n+1})/2 take every value that a halved prime gap can take, and prime gaps are unbounded (multiply by /2; in the run's data the halved gaps reach ~45 at gap 89, and grow without bound). Balance (factor sums of equal-length factors differ by <= 1) is a predicate on a fixed two-letter alphabet; it is not defined for an unbounded-alphabet word whose individual letters themselves grow. There is therefore no sense in which w is a Christoffel/Sturmian/balanced word. (The run's dedicated balance/complexity check is queued in code/out/check_candidate_premises.py: factor complexity p(n) for the halved gaps is near-maximal ~ alphabet^n, not n+1.)
  (b) THE (2,4)-EVENT IS NOT A BALLOT CROSSING. The cycle lemma (Dvoretzky–Motzkin / Raney / Chung-Feller) controls when partial sums of a fixed word stay nonnegative — a statement about POSITIONAL running sums of the word itself. But the regeneration event is BOUNDARY dynamics: (edge, intruder) = (2,4), where the intruder is A_k[b_k+1], the entry just PAST the leading {0,2} block, and the block length b_k itself evolves (erosion + jump). Neither the edge value nor the intruder is a running sum (partial sum) of the gap word w; the proved step law says b_{k+1} = b_k - 1 except at (2,4) with b_{k+1} = b_k + j_i, and j_i is a random functional of the gap word (complex, computed as 1-Lipschitz generating-stretch size), not a ballot crossing. So the cycle lemma's hypothesis (partial sums of a single word) does not hold for the objects driving regeneration.
  (c) NO SOURCE. Searched the word-combinatorics literature (Christoffel/Sturmian/balanced words, Lyndon factorization, cycle lemma, ballot theorems) — none is applied to the prime-gap word or to the Gilbreath triangle. The closest finding is that the PRIME-GAP SEQUENCE is "graphic" in a certain graph-realizability sense (Khrabrov 2023, doi:10.1007/s00208-023-02574-1, which mentions a low-complexity-like flavour in a GRAPHICAL realization), which is a different object and gives no regeneration bound. The candidate itself flagged this as "explicitly speculative: the balance test is the first step, and its failure is a cheap citable negative." That failure is immediate: the alphabet is unbounded so balance/Christoffel structure is not even well-posed.
```
