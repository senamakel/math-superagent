# Level-set percolation / contact-process representation of the >=4 region

```approach
idea: Represent the triangle by its level sets S_m = {(k,i) : A_k(i) >= m}. The conjecture A_k(1) in {0,2} is equivalent to (k,1) notin S_4 for every k, i.e. the ">=4 region" never reaches the left column. The recurrence A_{k+1}(i) = |A_k(i)-A_k(i+1)| gives S_m an exact monotone local rule: A_{k+1}(i) >= m iff one parent exceeds the other by >= m. So S_4 is a deterministically growing cluster in a half-plane, and the {0,2} block is the immune region that keeps it off the left edge.
mechanism: The parity shape and the proved Rule-90 structure pin down the geometry of S_2 (the level-2 set). The leftward advance of S_4 is exactly erosion (the block loses one position per row); a (2,4)-event is exactly the recovery event that pushes S_4 rightward by j_i. Thus the conjecture is the subcriticality of S_4: an infection process that never percolates to column 1. Attack by a comparison theorem coupling S_4 to a subcritical oriented site percolation / 1D contact process, using the discrete maximum principle max(A_{k+1}) <= max(A_k) and {0,2}-closure as the immune barrier. The classical theorem "subcritical oriented percolation has no infinite cluster touching a fixed boundary line" then certifies non-percolation if a comparison kernel can be exhibited. This is an invariant-of-the-level-set route, not a scalar potential (all of which are refuted).
status: refuted
killed-by: | The load-bearing premise is FALSE: the level predicate is NOT monotone, so no subcritical-oriented-percolation / contact-process comparison theorem applies, and the "immune barrier" is not immune in the general class.
  (a) NON-MONOTONICITY of the cell rule. A' = |a-b| is not monotone in either parent with the other fixed: with b=4, |a-4| takes values 4,2,0,2,4 as a runs 0,2,4,6,8 — it decreases then increases. Hence "A_{k+1}(i) >= 4 iff the parents differ by >= 4" is a NON-monotone predicate: a cell already in S_4 can have a descendant in S_4 or out of it regardless of one parent's membership (e.g. parents (4,4) -> child 0, out of S_4, though both parents are in S_4; parents (4,0) -> child 4, in S_4). So S_4 membership is not hereditary and the "cluster" is not monotonically growing — cells leave S_4 as well as enter it. Comparison theorems for subcritical oriented percolation and contact processes (Durrett 1984; Grimmett 1999) are theorems about MONOTONE (attractive) percolation processes on i.i.d. sites/bonds; without a monotone coupling the whole comparison machinery has nothing to bite on. The maximum principle max(A_{k+1}) <= max(A_k) is a global statement and does not prevent a localized S_4 cluster from touching column 1 — indeed "S_4 never touches column 1" IS the conjecture, so the comparison would have to prove exactly the open content.
  (b) THE BARRIER IS NOT IMMUNE IN THE GENERAL CLASS. Eppstein 2011 (anti-gilbreath-construction) builds 2-then-odds sequences with gaps <= f(n) whose right edge leaves and re-enters 1 infinitely often; CHT 2026 Theorem 1.6 isolates long zero-blocks and long shallow {0,d}-blocks (d >= 2) as the ONLY obstructions to decay, and Lemma 3.7(iii) shows a {0,d}-valued block persists in all descendants without decrease. So the {0,2} "immune region" cannot be proved immune by any uniform barrier argument in the 2-then-odds class; primes can only differ via the unproved CHT non-concentration hypothesis. The percolation framing re-describes the regeneration obstruction, it does not resolve it.
  No published source applies oriented percolation, a contact process, or a level-set percolation model to the iterated absolute-difference triangle (searched: level-set/iterative/triangle percolation, contact processes, first-passage percolation; none touches Gilbreath). The name is real; the mechanism (monotone comparison to subcritical percolation) is impossible.
precedent: |
  Oriented percolation / contact-process comparison theorems require monotone dynamics on i.i.d. sites: Durrett, "Oriented percolation in two dimensions" (Ann. Probab. 1984); R.   Eppstein 2011 anti-Gilbreath (claim anti-gilbreath-construction): the right edge escapes to non-1 and re-enters 1 infinitely often for bounded-gap 2-then-odds sequences.
  No claim id: nothing in the library establishes any percolation representation of the Gilbreath triangle.
  Status decided on (a) non-monotonicity (a mathematical fact) and (b) the Eppstein/CHT barrier non-immunity, both stronger than absence-of-precedent.
first-step: (superseded by refutation) The falsifier the candidate itself proposed ((b)) decides the matter: S_4 is not even a monotone cluster, so there is no comparison kernel to exhibit. A check of the non-monotonicity is recorded in code/out/check_candidate_premises.py.
side: general-class; targets regeneration (the open content).
```
