| `halfstep_depthbound.py` | Dyadic half-step fold characterization (Directive 68 TASK A+B). Computes wt(Phi h) and the full fold image for the half-step family h=1^k0^k (k=2..64, m=2k) plus other block structures, using the verified closed form fold_bit(K) = #{i in [0,delta] : i&(k+delta)==i} mod 2. Prints the depth/width bound m explicitly (Directive 67 rule 3), streams one row at a time via lib.gilbreath.rows_generator, never materialises the full triangle, exact integer arithmetic. Establishes: (a) wt(Phi h)=1 iff k a power of 2 (verified k=1..1024), wt always a power of two; (b) fold image is a single row at k=m/2+1 for power-of-2 k; (c) the half-step is balanced & max-anti-dyadic yet collapses — the sharp counterexample to the F2 transfer converse. Cross-checked against direct triangle nu2 (m<=16) and direct-vs-closed row sets (0 mismatches k=1..200). |
