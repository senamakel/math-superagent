## Literature verdict (this round)

**The individual tools are all real, proved, citable, and squarely in the technique tier the run already holds.** The Iwasawa/theory backbone is uncontested:

- **Herbrand's theorem**: if the ω^{1−k} eigenspace of Cl(Q(ζ_p))[p] is nonzero, then p | B_k (for even k, 2 ≤ k ≤ p−2). **Ribet's converse** (1976): if p | B_k, then that eigenspace is nonzero. Together, for a single prime p and even k:
  `Cl(Q(ζ_p))[ω^{1−k}] ≠ 0  ⟺  p | B_k`.
- **Kummer's criterion** (equivalent, conceptually): p is regular ⇔ p divides none of B_2,…,B_{p−3}; p irregular ⇔ p divides some even B_k, and the irregular pairs (p,k) are exactly the nontrivial minus-class eigenspaces.
- **Main Conjecture of Iwasawa theory** (Mazur–Wiles 1984): the p-adic L-function side equals the Iwasawa/class-group side, from which Herbrand–Ribet follows. All confirmed by the retrieved sources (Lozano-Robledo 2007 survey; the HAL abelian Main Conjecture notes; the function-field and local-origin papers as cross-checks that frame the classical statement identically).
- **The minus class number is computable** by the Bernoulli formula the run already holds (`minus-class-number-formula`, checked at p=3,5; `minus-class-computable-plus-not`), with no dependence on the unknown h^+.

**The honest gap, stated precisely.** The approach *does not itself establish* the deep forcing step — that a solution of x^p − y^q = 1 forces a concrete class-group divisibility in a *named* eigenspace. That is the `exact-statement-mihăilescu-bbf8` request, still open. More importantly, there is a **hypothesis-subtlety** the frames must respect:

- **Classical Herbrand–Ribet is a same-prime statement**: it concerns the **p**-torsion of Cl(Q(ζ_p)) (i.e. p | B_k ⟺ the p-part). The descent's hypothetical divisibility here is a **cross-prime** one: "q | h^−(Q(ζ_p))" with q ≠ p. For a cross-prime divisibility, the correct instrument is the **analytic class-number formula** (the minus Bernoulli product already held), *not* Herbrand–Ribet literally — the q-torsion of Cl(Q(ζ_p)) is not governed by the p-adic/L-functions of the Main Conjecture at prime q. So the approach's mechanism as literally written ("convert q | h^−(Q(ζ_p)) into q | B_k via Herbrand–Ribet") is **not directly valid** for the cross-prime case; what IS valid is "q | h^−(Q(ζ_p)) is decided by the Bernoulli product, which is fully computable and eigenspace-decomposed." The eigenspace decomposition thinking carries over, but the Herbrand–Ribet *name* attaches to same-prime divisibility.

**What this grounds.** The Bernoulli/eigenspace machinery is genuinely applicable and gives a *computable, h^+-independent* route to test the descent's class-group obstruction **once the forcing statement is supplied**. The run already holds the exact analytic formula; this approach's concrete, immediate value is that it frames the obstruction as an eigenspace-and-Bernoulli question rather than a whole-class-number one, which is exactly the shape `check_conditions(p,q)` can evaluate and `parallel_map` can sweep. As a *route to the forcing statement itself*, it is conditional on `exact-statement-mihăilescu-bbf8` — the approach is honest about this and does not claim otherwise.

**Known-solution placement.** The falsifier holds: (3,2,2,3) has p=2 even, outside the odd-prime hypothesis of every lemma here; no even-k eigenspaces exist for p=2, so the Bernoulli condition is vacuously satisfied and the known solution is never excluded. Nothing here over-proves to "no solution at all".

**Recommendation.** **Grounded** — but with the same-prime/cross-prime subtlety logged. The Herbrand–Ribet / Kummer / Main-Conjecture toolkit is real and the minus-class-number formula is held; the productive direction is (a) recompute the irregular-pair structure (p=37,59,67) exactly to calibrate the eigenspace machinery, and (b) pin the forcing statement (which named divisibility the descent produces) before invoking any Bernoulli conclusion. Mark the cross-prime-to-same-prime distinction as the thing to respect; do not cite Herbrand–Ribet for q | h^−(Q(ζ_p)) with q≠p.
