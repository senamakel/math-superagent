## Why this source is load-bearing

The problem's core machinery is the arithmetic of the ring of integers `Z[ζ_p]` of the p-th cyclotomic field `Q(ζ_p)`. This is exactly the machinery the library previously held only at summary level (Milne, Washington, Nguyen — all partial or blocked). This handout states and proves the two foundational facts: `Z[ζ_p]` **is** the full ring of integers (monogenic), and how `p` ramifies. Both are prerequisite to the ideal factorisation `x^p - 1 = ∏(x - ζ_p^i)` that the whole cyclotomic approach relies on.

## Exact statements captured

1. **`Z[ζ_n]` is the ring of integers of `Q(ζ_n)` when `n` is a prime power**, and in fact the equality `Z[ζ_n] = O_K` holds in general, with `disc(K/Q)` divisible by exactly the primes dividing `n`. So the primes of `Q` that ramify in `K` are precisely the prime divisors of `n`.

   > "We have proved earlier that Z[ζn] = O_K if n is a prime power, and we will soon prove that this equality holds in general, with disc(K/Q) divisible by exactly the primes that divide n (so the primes of Q that ramify in K are precisely the prime factors of n...)."

2. **Ramification of `p` in `Q(ζ_{p^e})`:** For `n = p^e`, the ideal `pZ[ζ_{p^e}]` factors as `P^{e(p-1)}` with `P = (ζ_{p^e} - 1)`, where the ramification index is `e(P | pZ) = p^{e-1}(p-1)` and the residue degree is `f(P | pZ) = 1`.

   The derivation: `Φ_{p^e} ≡ (X-1)^{p^{e-1}(p-1)} mod p` (from `X^{p^e} - 1 = Φ_{p^e}(X^{p^{e-1}} - 1)` reduced mod p). Then `pZ[ζ_{p^e}] = P^{p^{e-1}(p-1)}` with `P = (p, ζ_{p^e} - 1)`.

3. **`P` is principal, generated by `ζ_{p^e} - 1`:** i.e. `p` itself lies in the principal ideal `(ζ_{p^e} - 1)`. Proof: `Z[ζ_{p^e}]/(ζ_{p^e} - 1) ≅ Z[X]/(Φ_{p^e}, X-1) ≅ Z/(Φ_{p^e}(1)) = Z/pZ` since `Φ_{p^e}(1) = p`.

   For `e = 1` (the case the problem cares about, `p` prime): `P = (1 - ζ_p)` is the unique prime of `Q(ζ_p)` over `p`, with `e(P|p) = p-1`, `f = 1`, and `(p) = (1-ζ_p)^{p-1}`.

## Relation to the known solution

The known solution `(x,p,y,q) = (3,2,2,3)`: for `p` odd prime the ramification statement is about `Q(ζ_p)`. It does not reference `p=2`, so no exclusion of the known solution arises from these ring-theoretic facts. These are structural givens, not counterexample-killers.
