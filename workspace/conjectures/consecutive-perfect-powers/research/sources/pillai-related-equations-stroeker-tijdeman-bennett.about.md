# Source: Pillai / related-equations tier

Holds the primary statements of the adjacent family `a^x - b^y = c`
(differences between perfect powers equal to a fixed integer): the
Stroeker–Tijdeman theorem (`c_0(3,2)=13`), Bennett's at-most-two and
at-most-one results, and Scott–Styer on the generalised Pillai equation.
Full source URLs and verbatim quotes are inside the document. Retrieved
server-side via read_sources; no publisher text is hosted locally (network
boundary), so the statements recorded are the exact quotes the readout
returned, not a stored paper.
