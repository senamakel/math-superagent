This is **the** canonical reference for the machinery the problem lives in: p-adic L-functions, class numbers of cyclotomic fields, cyclotomic units, the Stickelberger ideal, Iwasawa theory, and the split of the class number into its two factors. ## Verified contents (from the Springer/metadata pages)

- GTM 83; 490 pages; covers p-adic L-functions, class numbers, cyclotomic units, Fermat's Last Theorem, Iwasawa's theory of Z_p-extensions.
- 2nd edition adds a chapter on the work of Thaine, Kolyvagin, and Rubin including a proof of the Iwasawa Main Conjecture, plus Jacobi sums, primality testing, and Sinnott's proof of the vanishing of Iwasawa's f-invariant.
- The error page is at https://math.umd.edu/~lcw/cyclo.html.

## Relevance

The run's open content (both exponents odd prime) is contolled by the class group of Q(zeta_p) and the cyclotomic units. Washington's book is the source for:
- the class number formula, and the split h = h^+ * h^- into plus and minus factors;
- the cyclotomic units and their index in the full unit group (relating to h^- and Sinnott's results);
- the Stickelberger ideal and Stickelberger's theorem (annihilators of the minus class group);
- the analytic class number formula and its p-adic analogues (relevant to the divisibility conditions / double-Wieferich structure).
