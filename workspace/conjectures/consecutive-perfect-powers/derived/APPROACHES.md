## What closed, and why

Do not propose these again. A reason stated precisely is what makes that possible; one left blank makes this row worthless.

- [[fermat-jacobian-chabauty]] (refuted): The Catalan curve X^p - Y^q = 1 (gcd(p,q)=1) is NOT the Fermat curve X^N + Y^N = Z^N. Multiple independent sources (Hazama, Goodson, Murabayashi, Dogra) state it is a superelliptic/cyclic cover, y^q = x^p - 1 (a (p,q)-twist of a hyperelliptic/cyclic cover), whose Jacobian has CM-type by Q(zeta_p, zeta_q) — real and named, but not "the Fermat Jacobian", so Weil's CM decomposition of the Fermat Jacobian does NOT apply as literally stated. Moreover the method is conditional on rank(J(Q)) < g, and no general Mordell-Weil rank formula is known for these Jacobians (Murabayashi, Goodson): the rank bound is exactly as hard as the class-group obstruction it was meant to avoid. The corrected statement (genus (p-1)(q-1)/2 holds; a CM JP of dimension g with CM by Q(zeta_p,zeta_q) exists; Chabauty applies iff rank < g) is grounded, but as a route to a proof it is a conditional theorem whose hypothesis is the original obstruction.
- [[iwasawa-herbrand-ribet-classgroup]] (refuted): cross-prime-versus-same-prime; the literal mechanism (Herbrand–Ribet converting q | h^−(Q(ζ_p)) into q | B_k) is invalid for q ≠ p — Herbrand–Ribet is a same-prime (p-torsion) statement about Cl(Q(ζ_p))[p], while the descent's divisibility is cross-prime. Superseded by minus-classnumber-crossprime-bernoulli, which keeps the valid Bernoulli/eigenspace framing but uses the analytic class-number formula for the q ≠ p case.
- [[kim-nonabelian-chabauty]] (refuted): finiteness-only-no-effective-determination (the genus-≥4 obstruction the proposal guessed is refuted by Ellenberg–Hast; the real obstruction is that CK gives finiteness, not a computable ruling-out, and effective CK at varying genus ≥ 4 is infeasible in the same orders of magnitude already ruled out for effective bounds)
- [[kummer-descent-local-symbols]] (refuted): local-global-does-not-detect-class-group (the Cl/qCl obstruction is the non-local part; local Hilbert symbols cannot force q|h^-(Q(ζ_p)))
- [[modular-method-gfe]] (refuted): The second term of the honest dichotomy closes: r = 1 is a named, citable obstruction. The modular method for generalised Fermat equations is set up for signatures (p,q,r) in the hyperbolic regime 1/p + 1/q + 1/r < 1, where Darmon-Granville prove finiteness and where a Frey curve/abelian variety has conductor controlled by the (prime divisors of) A,B,C and the small primes. For Catalan x^p - y^q = 1 normalised as Ax^p + By^q = Cz^r with r = 1, we have 1/p + 1/q + 1/r = 1/p + 1/q + 1 > 1: NOT hyperbolic, Darmon-Granville finiteness does not apply, and the entire modular literature (Darmon's program, Freitas-Siksek, Billerey-Chen-Dieulefait-Freitas 2025, Azon 2025) treats r >= 2 only. No Frey curve with conductor bounded in terms of ABC (here A=B=C=1) exists for the r=1 degeneration -- the conductor would depend on the free third term. Recording this is the result the file set out to produce: it closes the line.
- [[pade-hypergeometric-bennett]] (refuted): theorem-form-mismatch-plus-astronomical-constants; Bennett's direct results are fixed-base (2008: at most one solution to |a^x − b^y| for fixed a,b) or fixed-equal-exponent (2007: |x|^k − |y|^k = 1), neither of which matches the variable-base, variable-exponent form x^p − y^q = 1 with p ≠ q. The only matching framing (superelliptic y^q = x^p − 1) has effective constants that are the Baker linear-forms-in-logarithms bounds already ruled astronomically infeasible (GOAL.md), and it yields no uniform statement over (p,q). Not false technique; closed as the adopted line because its decisive constant C(p,q) is the same infeasible effective bound the run has already ruled out.
- [[subspace-theorem-sunit]] (refuted): ineffectivity-of-subspace-machinery; growing-S-set-blocks-fixed-S-theorem
- [[vandiver-reflection-plus-part]] (refuted): same-prime-not-cross-prime (the Spiegelungssatz is a same-conductor-prime statement relating eigenspaces of Cl(Q(ζ_p)) under ω mod p; it does not transfer torsion across two different fields Q(ζ_p), Q(ζ_q), so it cannot deliver the cross-prime forcing q|h^-(Q(ζ_p)) — the very gap `crossprime-q-hminus-not-sourced` it was meant to fill)
