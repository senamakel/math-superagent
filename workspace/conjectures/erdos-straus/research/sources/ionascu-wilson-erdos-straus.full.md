# Title: On the Erdos-Straus conjecture

Authors: [Eugen J. Ionascu][2], [Andrew Wilson][3]

View a PDF of the paper titled On the Erdos-Straus conjecture, by Eugen J. Ionascu and Andrew Wilson

[View PDF][4] [HTML (experimental)][5]

Abstract: Paul Erdos conjectured that for every n in N, n>1, there exist a, b, c natural numbers, not necessarily distinct, so that 4/n=1/a+1/b+1/c (see \cite{rg}). In this paper we prove an extension of Mordell's theorem and formulate a conjecture which is stronger than Erdos' conjecture.
