## What it establishes (sourced, tertiary)

- Statement: 4/n = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z is conjectured solvable in positive integers
  for every integer n ≥ 2 (the page writes 4/n; the equivalent m=4 case).
- History: Obláth (1950), Rosati (1954), Bernstein (1962), Yamamoto (1965),
  Vaughan (1970), Guy (1994, Unsolved Problems §D11). Notes Swett's 1999
  verification (the page predates 10^17).
- Bibliography anchors: Obláth's Mathesis 59 (1950) 308–316; Rosati Boll.
  Un. Mat. Ital. 9 (1954) 59–63; Bernstein J. reine angew. Math. 211 (1962);
  Vaughan Mathematika 17 (1970) 193–198.
