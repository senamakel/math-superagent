# Erdős-Straus Conjecture

---

A conjecture due to Paul Erdős and E. G. Straus that the [Diophantine equation][1]

[image:  4/n=1/a+1/b+1/c ] |

involving [Egyptian fractions][2] always can be solved (Obl&aacute;th 1950, Rosati 1954, Bernstein 1962, Yamamoto 1965, Vaughan 1970, Guy 1994). Swett has established validity of the conjecture for all [image: n<=10^(14)].
