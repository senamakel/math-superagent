# Ionascu & Wilson, "On the Erdős–Straus conjecture"

Source: arXiv:1001.1100 (Rev. Roumaine Math. Pures Appl. 56 (2011) 21–30).
Full text: `research/sources/ionascu-wilson-erdos-straus.full.md`

## What it establishes (sourced)

- Gives explicit parametric solutions / families and records the conjecture's
  status around 2010 (pre-Salez-10^17).
- Relevant: the equivalence `4/n = 1/x+1/y+1/z` solutions for `n ≡ -c` or
  `n ≡ -1/c mod 4ab` with `a,b` coprime, `c` odd factor of `a+b`
  (the same family Elsholtz–Tao/Wikipedia cite; setting `a=b=1` gives
  `n ≡ 3 mod 4`, one of Mordell's identities).

## Implication

One more formulation of the modular families; confirms the `-1/c mod 4ab`
shape available for Type II.
