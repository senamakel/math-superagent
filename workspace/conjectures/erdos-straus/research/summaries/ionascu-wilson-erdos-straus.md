## Correction record

The earlier `unobtained-sources.md` note wrongly claimed this paper was
"Bello-Hernández, Benito & Fernández" — it is not. It is Ionascu & Wilson's
paper "On the Erdos-Straus conjecture". The filename `ionascu-wilson` is
correct; the earlier note ("only the author attribution in the name is
wrong") was itself wrong. See the corrected passage in
`research/summaries/unobtained-sources.md`. The Bello–Benito–Fernández paper
is a different one, arXiv:1010.2035, now also in the library.

## What it establishes (sourced)

- Proves an **extension of Mordell's theorem** and formulates a conjecture
  stronger than the Erdős–Straus conjecture.
- The abstract (on file) states Paul Erdős conjectured for every n > 1 that
  4/n is a sum of three unit fractions; the paper's title/abstract are the
  source of this file's 9 KB text.
- The summary above (parametric families `n ≡ -c` or `n ≡ -1/c mod 4ab` with
  a,b coprime, c odd factor of a+b) is **from the run's earlier reading** of
  the full text and matches {Elsholtz–Tao, Wikipedia}; it is marked as the
  same family giving Mordell's `n ≡ 3 mod 4` at a=b=1. (Recorded here
  without re-reading the PDF text; the claim is the standard one.)
