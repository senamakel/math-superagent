# Title: An elementary recursive bound for effective Positivstellensatz and Hilbert 17-th problem

Authors: [Henri Lombardi][3], [Daniel Perrucci][4], [Marie-Françoise Roy][5]

View a PDF of the paper titled An elementary recursive bound for effective Positivstellensatz and Hilbert 17-th problem, by Henri Lombardi and 2 other authors

[View PDF][6] [HTML (experimental)][7]

Abstract: We prove elementary recursive bounds in the degrees for Positivstellensatz and Hilbert 17-th problem, which is the expression of a nonnegative polynomial as a sum of squares of rational functions. We obtain a tower of five exponentials. A precise bound in terms of the number and degree of the polynomials and their number of variables is provided in the paper.
