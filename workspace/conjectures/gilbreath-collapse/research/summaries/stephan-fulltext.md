# On Stephan's conjectures concerning Pascal mod 2 — full text (Shevelev), HTML companion

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/1011.6083v4 (HTML version of https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.6083)

Same Shevelev paper as `stephan-conjectures-pascal-mod2-shevelev.md` (which see for the
summary and bearing), fetched in HTML full-text form. It carries the proofs of Stephan's
Conjectures 1–4, the study of A001317, Theorem 1 and its corollaries, the key identity
(4.3) with two proofs, and the polynomial generalization (Theorem 2), plus the
orthogonality-of-nonnegative-integers definition used to prove it. Relevant to the exact
description of the down-set sequences `M_d` and their closed forms.
