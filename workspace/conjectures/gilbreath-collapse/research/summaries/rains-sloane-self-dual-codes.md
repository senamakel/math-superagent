## What it establishes (why it does not help here)

A survey of **self-dual codes** over F2, F3, F4, Fq, Z4, Zm: weight enumerators,
MacWilliams/Gleason theorems, Gleason–Pierce classification, shadow codes, mass
formulae, extremal codes, bounds, invariant theory.

## Bearing on this problem — **none, do not read again**

This collection's abstract promised a "Walsh/weight-enumerator side" of the S² sum,
but nothing here describes the fold matrix `Φ_n`, the down-set symmetric differences
`M_d △ M_{d'}`, or the Walsh-support multiset that COLLAPSE is about. Self-dual-code
weight enumerators are a different object (the full code's distance distribution) from
the index multiset of one quadratic form's S² expansion. It does not help this
investigation.

## What it would take to matter

A result tying the distance distribution of the (n−2)-dimensional fold code to the
Walsh support of one `h` — not present. Marked low-priority; do not re-open.
