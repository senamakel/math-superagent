# Lucas' theorem survey — full text (Meštrović), HTML companion

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/1409.3820v1 (HTML version of https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.3820)

This is the same Meštrović survey as `lucas-theorem-survey-mestrovic.md` (which see for
the summary and bearing), fetched in its 145 KB HTML full-text form to complement the
PDF. It carries the full six-section treatment: Lucas' theorem and variations (Sections
1–2), prime-power congruences / Wolstenholme (3), the Lucas and double-Lucas properties
with their sequences (4), generalized-binomial Lucas theorems (5), and applications (6).
Key for this problem: Section 2's consequence that `C(n,m) ≡ 0 (mod 2)` iff `m` is not
a binary submask of `n`, which is the definitional basis of the fold matrix `Φ_n`.
