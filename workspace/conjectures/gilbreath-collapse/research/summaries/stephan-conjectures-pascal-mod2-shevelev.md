## What it establishes

Proves a series of Stephan's conjectures about Pascal's triangle mod 2 (the
Sierpinski-gasket pattern that is exactly the fold matrix), plus a polynomial
generalization. Key device: the **Fermat factorization** of the row values `c(n)`
(read as binary integers, sequence A001317): if `n = Σ 2^{k_i}` then
`d(n) = ∏ F(k_i)` where `F(k) = 2^{2^k} + 1` is the Fermat number. So each row mod 2
(read as an integer) factors into distinct Fermat numbers; the number of factors is
`popcount(n)`. Theorems proved in two ways (an orthogonality of nonnegative integers;
direct manipulation of mod-2 row structure).

## Bearing on this problem

Primary treatment of the *arithmetic* of the specific integer sequences generated by
the rows `M_d`. `|M_d| = 2^{popcount(d)}` is the same count as the number of Fermat
factors of the row. Self-similarity/orthogonality of the rows = the finite
self-similar structure governing `M_d`. Useful for the run/self-similar viewpoint but
does **not** describe `M_d △ M_{d'}` with multiplicities.
