Write the Lean 4 statement, and only the statement, of this fact about perfect
out-shuffles:

> For a deck of `n` cards with `n` even and `n ≥ 4`, applying the perfect
> out-shuffle `s` times returns the deck to its original order exactly when
> `n - 1` divides `2^s - 1`.

You must define the shuffle to state it. Give the definition and the theorem.
End the theorem in `:= by sorry`. Do not attempt a proof.
