Write the Lean 4 statement, and only the statement, of Mihăilescu's theorem:

> The only solution in natural numbers of `x^a - y^b = 1` with `x, y, a, b > 1`
> is `3^2 - 2^3 = 1`.

Give a single `theorem` whose conclusion pins down all four values. End it in
`:= by sorry`. Do not attempt a proof. Import only the Mathlib modules you need.
