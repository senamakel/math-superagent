Write the Lean 4 statement, and only the statement, of the Erdős–Gyárfás
conjecture:

> Every simple graph in which every vertex has degree at least 3 contains a
> cycle whose length is a power of 2.

Use Mathlib's `SimpleGraph` and its cycle machinery rather than inventing your
own definitions. End it in `:= by sorry`. Do not attempt a proof.
